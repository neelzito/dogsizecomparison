Shih Tzu vs Maltese: size comparison

Shih Tzu or Maltese? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

Average male and female of each breed, drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person. Solid line = male, dashed = female.

How the two breeds compare

The Shih Tzu is the taller dog. An average male stands 25 cm (9.7 in) at the shoulder, 4.3 cm (1.7 in) more than an average male Maltese at 21 cm (8.1 in).

By weight, the Shih Tzu is about 2.3× as heavy: 5.7 kg (13 lb) for an average male against 2.5 kg (5.5 lb) for the Maltese.

Both are in the Toy group.

Their life expectancy is similar (10–18 years and 12–15 years).

For air travel, the Maltese fits the 8 kg cabin limit for dog and carrier; the Shih Tzu only sometimes does.

Shih Tzu vs Maltese: questions

Which is bigger, the Shih Tzu or the Maltese?

The Shih Tzu is the taller dog. An average male stands 25 cm (9.7 in) at the shoulder, 4.3 cm (1.7 in) more than an average male Maltese at 21 cm (8.1 in).

How much does a Shih Tzu weigh compared with a Maltese?

A male Shih Tzu weighs 4.1–7.3 kg (9–16 lb) and a female 4.1–7.3 kg (9–16 lb). A male Maltese weighs 1.8–3.2 kg (4–7.1 lb) and a female 1.8–3.2 kg (4–7.1 lb).
