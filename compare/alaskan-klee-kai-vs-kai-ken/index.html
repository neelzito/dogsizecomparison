Alaskan Klee Kai vs Kai Ken: size comparison

Kai Ken or Alaskan Klee Kai? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

How the two breeds compare

The Kai Ken is the taller dog. An average male stands 48 cm (19 in) at the shoulder, 11.5 cm (4.5 in) more than an average male Alaskan Klee Kai at 37 cm (14.5 in), about 1.3× the height.

By weight, the Kai Ken is about 2.2× as heavy: 16 kg (35 lb) for an average male against 7.1 kg (16 lb) for the Alaskan Klee Kai.

Both are in the Working group.

The Alaskan Klee Kai tends to live longer: 13–16 years against 12–15 years.

For air travel, the Alaskan Klee Kai can sometimes fit the 8 kg cabin limit for dog and carrier; the Kai Ken does not.

Alaskan Klee Kai vs Kai Ken: questions

Which is bigger, the Alaskan Klee Kai or the Kai Ken?

The Kai Ken is the taller dog. An average male stands 48 cm (19 in) at the shoulder, 11.5 cm (4.5 in) more than an average male Alaskan Klee Kai at 37 cm (14.5 in), about 1.3× the height.

How much does an Alaskan Klee Kai weigh compared with a Kai Ken?

A male Alaskan Klee Kai weighs 2.7–12 kg (6–25 lb) and a female 2.7–12 kg (6–25 lb). A male Kai Ken weighs 14–18 kg (30–40 lb) and a female 12–16 kg (25–35 lb).
