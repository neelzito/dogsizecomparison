Doberman Pinscher vs German Shepherd Dog: size comparison

Doberman Pinscher or German Shepherd Dog? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

How the two breeds compare

The Doberman Pinscher is the taller dog. An average male stands 69 cm (27 in) at the shoulder, 5 cm (2 in) more than an average male German Shepherd Dog at 64 cm (25 in).

By weight, the Doberman Pinscher is about 13% heavier: 40 kg (88 lb) for an average male against 35 kg (78 lb) for the German Shepherd Dog.

The Doberman Pinscher belongs to the Working group and comes from Germany; the German Shepherd Dog is in the Herding group, from Germany.

The German Shepherd Dog tends to live longer: 12–14 years against 10–12 years.

Doberman Pinscher vs German Shepherd Dog: questions

Which is bigger, the Doberman Pinscher or the German Shepherd Dog?

The Doberman Pinscher is the taller dog. An average male stands 69 cm (27 in) at the shoulder, 5 cm (2 in) more than an average male German Shepherd Dog at 64 cm (25 in).

How much does a Doberman Pinscher weigh compared with a German Shepherd Dog?

A male Doberman Pinscher weighs 34–46 kg (75–100 lb) and a female 27–41 kg (60–90 lb). A male German Shepherd Dog weighs 30–41 kg (65–90 lb) and a female 23–32 kg (50–71 lb).
