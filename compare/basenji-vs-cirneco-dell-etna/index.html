Basenji vs Cirneco dell'Etna: size comparison

Cirneco dell'Etna or Basenji? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

How the two breeds compare

The Cirneco dell'Etna is the taller dog. An average male stands 48 cm (19 in) at the shoulder, 5.3 cm (2.1 in) more than an average male Basenji at 43 cm (16.9 in).

They weigh about the same: 11 kg (24 lb) for an average male Basenji and 11 kg (24 lb) for a Cirneco dell'Etna.

Both are in the Hound group.

The Cirneco dell'Etna tends to live longer: 15–16 years against 13–14 years.

Basenji vs Cirneco dell'Etna: questions

Which is bigger, the Basenji or the Cirneco dell'Etna?

The Cirneco dell'Etna is the taller dog. An average male stands 48 cm (19 in) at the shoulder, 5.3 cm (2.1 in) more than an average male Basenji at 43 cm (16.9 in).

How much does a Basenji weigh compared with a Cirneco dell'Etna?

A male Basenji weighs 11 kg (24 lb) and a female 10 kg (22 lb). A male Cirneco dell'Etna weighs 9.1–13 kg (20–28 lb) and a female 8.6–12 kg (19–25 lb).
