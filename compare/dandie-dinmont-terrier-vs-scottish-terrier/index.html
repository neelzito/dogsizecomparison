Dandie Dinmont Terrier vs Scottish Terrier: size comparison

Scottish Terrier or Dandie Dinmont Terrier? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

How the two breeds compare

The Dandie Dinmont Terrier and the Scottish Terrier are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Dandie Dinmont Terrier stands 24 cm (9.5 in) and an average male Scottish Terrier 26 cm (10 in).

They weigh about the same: 9.6 kg (21 lb) for an average male Dandie Dinmont Terrier and 9.3 kg (21 lb) for a Scottish Terrier.

Both are in the Terrier group and come from Scotland.

The Dandie Dinmont Terrier tends to live longer: 12–15 years against 12 years.

Dandie Dinmont Terrier vs Scottish Terrier: questions

Which is bigger, the Dandie Dinmont Terrier or the Scottish Terrier?

The Dandie Dinmont Terrier and the Scottish Terrier are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Dandie Dinmont Terrier stands 24 cm (9.5 in) and an average male Scottish Terrier 26 cm (10 in).

How much does a Dandie Dinmont Terrier weigh compared with a Scottish Terrier?

A male Dandie Dinmont Terrier weighs 8.2–11 kg (18–24 lb) and a female 8.2–11 kg (18–24 lb). A male Scottish Terrier weighs 8.6–10 kg (19–22 lb) and a female 8.2–9.5 kg (18–21 lb).
