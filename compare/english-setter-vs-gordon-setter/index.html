English Setter vs Gordon Setter: size comparison

English Setter or Gordon Setter? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

Average male and female of each breed, drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person. Solid line = male, dashed = female.

How the two breeds compare

The English Setter and the Gordon Setter are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male English Setter stands 66 cm (26 in) and an average male Gordon Setter 65 cm (25.5 in).

They weigh about the same: 33 kg (73 lb) for an average male English Setter and 31 kg (68 lb) for a Gordon Setter.

Both are in the Sporting group.

Their life expectancy is similar (12 years and 12–13 years).

English Setter vs Gordon Setter: questions

Which is bigger, the English Setter or the Gordon Setter?

The English Setter and the Gordon Setter are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male English Setter stands 66 cm (26 in) and an average male Gordon Setter 65 cm (25.5 in).

How much does an English Setter weigh compared with a Gordon Setter?

A male English Setter weighs 30–37 kg (65–80 lb) and a female 21–25 kg (45–55 lb). A male Gordon Setter weighs 25–37 kg (55–80 lb) and a female 21–32 kg (45–71 lb).
