Tibetan Mastiff vs Caucasian Shepherd Dog: size comparison

Tibetan Mastiff or Caucasian Shepherd Dog? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

Average male and female of each breed, drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person. Solid line = male, dashed = female.

How the two breeds compare

The Tibetan Mastiff is the taller dog. An average male stands 71 cm (28 in) at the shoulder, 3.8 cm (1.5 in) more than an average male Caucasian Shepherd Dog at 67 cm (26.5 in).

By weight, the Caucasian Shepherd Dog is about 12% heavier: 61 kg (134 lb) for an average male against 55 kg (120 lb) for the Tibetan Mastiff.

Both are in the Working group.

Their life expectancy is similar (10–12 years and 10–12 years).

Tibetan Mastiff vs Caucasian Shepherd Dog: questions

Which is bigger, the Tibetan Mastiff or the Caucasian Shepherd Dog?

The Tibetan Mastiff is the taller dog. An average male stands 71 cm (28 in) at the shoulder, 3.8 cm (1.5 in) more than an average male Caucasian Shepherd Dog at 67 cm (26.5 in).

How much does a Tibetan Mastiff weigh compared with a Caucasian Shepherd Dog?

A male Tibetan Mastiff weighs 41–68 kg (90–150 lb) and a female 32–55 kg (71–120 lb). A male Caucasian Shepherd Dog weighs 45–77 kg (99–170 lb) and a female 45–77 kg (99–170 lb).
