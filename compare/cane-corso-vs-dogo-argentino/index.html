Cane Corso vs Dogo Argentino: size comparison

Cane Corso or Dogo Argentino? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

Average male and female of each breed, drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person. Solid line = male, dashed = female.

How the two breeds compare

The Cane Corso is the taller dog. An average male stands 67 cm (26.3 in) at the shoulder, 2.5 cm (1 in) more than an average male Dogo Argentino at 64 cm (25.3 in).

By weight, the Cane Corso is about 16% heavier: 48 kg (105 lb) for an average male against 41 kg (90 lb) for the Dogo Argentino.

Both are in the Working group.

The Dogo Argentino tends to live longer: 9–15 years against 9–12 years.

Cane Corso vs Dogo Argentino: questions

Which is bigger, the Cane Corso or the Dogo Argentino?

The Cane Corso is the taller dog. An average male stands 67 cm (26.3 in) at the shoulder, 2.5 cm (1 in) more than an average male Dogo Argentino at 64 cm (25.3 in).

How much does a Cane Corso weigh compared with a Dogo Argentino?

A male Cane Corso weighs 45–50 kg (99–110 lb) and a female 40–45 kg (88–99 lb). A male Dogo Argentino weighs 37–46 kg (80–100 lb) and a female 37–46 kg (80–100 lb).
