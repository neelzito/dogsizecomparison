Dogo Argentino vs wolf: size comparison

How does the Dogo Argentino measure up to a gray wolf? Shoulder heights and weights against the Eurasian wolf and the larger Northwestern wolf, drawn to scale next to a person.

Average male and female Dogo Argentino next to average male wolves, drawn to scale with a 175 cm (5′9″) person. Solid line = male, dashed = female.

Open in the comparison tool →

Is the Dogo Argentino bigger than a wolf?

An average male Dogo Argentino is shorter than both wolves. By weight, it is lighter than both.

An average male Eurasian wolf, at 78 cm (30.5 in) and 47 kg (103 lb), is 13.3 cm (5.2 in) taller than an average male Dogo Argentino (64 cm, 41 kg) and is about 13% heavier.

An average male Northwestern wolf, at 84 cm (32.9 in) and 55 kg (121 lb), is 19.3 cm (7.6 in) taller than an average male Dogo Argentino (64 cm, 41 kg) and is about 34% heavier.

Wolves are built for travelling long distances: compared with most dogs of the same shoulder height they have longer legs, a narrower chest and bigger feet, so they often look larger than the numbers suggest. Wolf sizes also vary a lot by region; northern wolves are the biggest.

Dogo Argentino vs wolf: questions

Is a Dogo Argentino bigger than a wolf?

An average male Dogo Argentino is shorter than both wolves. By weight, it is lighter than both. An average male Eurasian wolf, at 78 cm (30.5 in) and 47 kg (103 lb), is 13.3 cm (5.2 in) taller than an average male Dogo Argentino (64 cm, 41 kg) and is about 13% heavier.

How much does a Dogo Argentino weigh compared with a wolf?

A male Dogo Argentino weighs 37–46 kg (80–100 lb) and a female 37–46 kg (80–100 lb). A male Eurasian wolf weighs 38–55 kg (84–121 lb) and a male Northwestern wolf 45–65 kg (99–143 lb).
