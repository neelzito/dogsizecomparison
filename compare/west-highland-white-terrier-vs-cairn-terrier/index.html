West Highland White Terrier vs Cairn Terrier: size comparison

How the two breeds compare

The West Highland White Terrier is the taller dog. An average male stands 28 cm (11 in) at the shoulder, 2.5 cm (1 in) more than an average male Cairn Terrier at 26 cm (10 in).

By weight, the West Highland White Terrier is about 24% heavier: 8 kg (18 lb) for an average male against 6.4 kg (14 lb) for the Cairn Terrier.

Both are in the Terrier group and come from Scotland.

Their life expectancy is similar (13–15 years and 13–15 years).

For air travel, the Cairn Terrier fits the 8 kg cabin limit for dog and carrier; the West Highland White Terrier does not.

West Highland White Terrier vs Cairn Terrier: questions

Which is bigger, the West Highland White Terrier or the Cairn Terrier?

The West Highland White Terrier is the taller dog. An average male stands 28 cm (11 in) at the shoulder, 2.5 cm (1 in) more than an average male Cairn Terrier at 26 cm (10 in).

How much does a West Highland White Terrier weigh compared with a Cairn Terrier?

A male West Highland White Terrier weighs 6.8–9.1 kg (15–20 lb) and a female 6.8–9.1 kg (15–20 lb). A male Cairn Terrier weighs 6.4 kg (14 lb) and a female 5.9 kg (13 lb).
