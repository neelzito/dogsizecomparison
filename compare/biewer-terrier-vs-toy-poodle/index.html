Biewer Terrier vs Toy Poodle: size comparison

Biewer Terrier or Toy Poodle? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

Average male and female of each breed, drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person. Solid line = male, dashed = female.

How the two breeds compare

The Biewer Terrier and the Toy Poodle are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Biewer Terrier stands 23 cm (9.1 in) and an average male Toy Poodle 23 cm (9.1 in).

By weight, the Biewer Terrier is about 20% heavier: 2.7 kg (6 lb) for an average male against 2.3 kg (5 lb) for the Toy Poodle.

Both are in the Toy group.

The Biewer Terrier tends to live longer: 16 years against 10–18 years.

Biewer Terrier vs Toy Poodle: questions

Which is bigger, the Biewer Terrier or the Toy Poodle?

The Biewer Terrier and the Toy Poodle are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Biewer Terrier stands 23 cm (9.1 in) and an average male Toy Poodle 23 cm (9.1 in).

How much does a Biewer Terrier weigh compared with a Toy Poodle?

A male Biewer Terrier weighs 1.8–3.6 kg (4–7.9 lb) and a female 1.8–3.6 kg (4–7.9 lb). A male Toy Poodle weighs 1.8–2.7 kg (4–6 lb) and a female 1.8–2.7 kg (4–6 lb).
