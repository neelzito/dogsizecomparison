Goldendoodle (Standard) vs Sheepadoodle (Standard): size comparison

Sheepadoodle (Standard) or Goldendoodle (Standard)? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

How the two breeds compare

The Sheepadoodle (Standard) is the taller dog. An average male stands 61 cm (24 in) at the shoulder, 3 cm (1.2 in) more than an average male Goldendoodle (Standard) at 58 cm (22.8 in).

By weight, the Sheepadoodle (Standard) is about 11% heavier: 32 kg (69 lb) for an average male against 29 kg (63 lb) for the Goldendoodle (Standard).

Both are in the designer & mixed category and come from the United States.

The Sheepadoodle (Standard) tends to live longer: 12–15 years against 10–15 years.

Goldendoodle (Standard) vs Sheepadoodle (Standard): questions

Which is bigger, the Goldendoodle (Standard) or the Sheepadoodle (Standard)?

The Sheepadoodle (Standard) is the taller dog. An average male stands 61 cm (24 in) at the shoulder, 3 cm (1.2 in) more than an average male Goldendoodle (Standard) at 58 cm (22.8 in).

How much does a Goldendoodle (Standard) weigh compared with a Sheepadoodle (Standard)?

A male Goldendoodle (Standard) weighs 23–34 kg (51–75 lb) and a female 20–29 kg (44–64 lb). A male Sheepadoodle (Standard) weighs 27–36 kg (60–79 lb) and a female 23–32 kg (51–71 lb).
