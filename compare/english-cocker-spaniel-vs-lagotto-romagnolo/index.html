English Cocker Spaniel vs Lagotto Romagnolo: size comparison

Lagotto Romagnolo or English Cocker Spaniel? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

How the two breeds compare

The Lagotto Romagnolo is the taller dog. An average male stands 46 cm (18 in) at the shoulder, 4 cm (1.6 in) more than an average male English Cocker Spaniel at 42 cm (16.4 in).

They weigh about the same: 14 kg (31 lb) for an average male English Cocker Spaniel and 15 kg (32 lb) for a Lagotto Romagnolo.

Both are in the Sporting group.

The Lagotto Romagnolo tends to live longer: 15–17 years against 12–14 years.

English Cocker Spaniel vs Lagotto Romagnolo: questions

Which is bigger, the English Cocker Spaniel or the Lagotto Romagnolo?

The Lagotto Romagnolo is the taller dog. An average male stands 46 cm (18 in) at the shoulder, 4 cm (1.6 in) more than an average male English Cocker Spaniel at 42 cm (16.4 in).

How much does an English Cocker Spaniel weigh compared with a Lagotto Romagnolo?

A male English Cocker Spaniel weighs 13–16 kg (28–34 lb) and a female 12–15 kg (26–32 lb). A male Lagotto Romagnolo weighs 13–16 kg (29–35 lb) and a female 11–14 kg (24–31 lb).
