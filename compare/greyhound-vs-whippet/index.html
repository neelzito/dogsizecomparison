Greyhound vs Whippet: size comparison

Greyhound or Whippet? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

Average male and female of each breed, drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person. Solid line = male, dashed = female.

How the two breeds compare

The Greyhound is the taller dog. An average male stands 74 cm (28.9 in) at the shoulder, 21.3 cm (8.4 in) more than an average male Whippet at 52 cm (20.6 in), about 1.4× the height.

By weight, the Greyhound is about 2.1× as heavy: 31 kg (68 lb) for an average male against 15 kg (33 lb) for the Whippet.

Both are in the Hound group and come from England.

The Whippet tends to live longer: 12–15 years against 10–13 years.

Greyhound vs Whippet: questions

Which is bigger, the Greyhound or the Whippet?

The Greyhound is the taller dog. An average male stands 74 cm (28.9 in) at the shoulder, 21.3 cm (8.4 in) more than an average male Whippet at 52 cm (20.6 in), about 1.4× the height.

How much does a Greyhound weigh compared with a Whippet?

A male Greyhound weighs 30–32 kg (65–71 lb) and a female 27–30 kg (60–65 lb). A male Whippet weighs 12–18 kg (25–40 lb) and a female 12–18 kg (25–40 lb).
