Rottweiler vs wolf: size comparison

How does the Rottweiler measure up to a gray wolf? Shoulder heights and weights against the Eurasian wolf and the larger Northwestern wolf, drawn to scale next to a person.

Average male and female Rottweiler next to average male wolves, drawn to scale with a 175 cm (5′9″) person. Solid line = male, dashed = female.

Open in the comparison tool →

Is the Rottweiler bigger than a wolf?

An average male Rottweiler is shorter than both wolves. By weight, it is heavier than a Eurasian wolf and lighter than a Northwestern wolf.

An average male Eurasian wolf, at 78 cm (30.5 in) and 47 kg (103 lb), is 12.8 cm (5 in) taller than an average male Rottweiler (65 cm, 52 kg) and is about 11% lighter.

An average male Northwestern wolf, at 84 cm (32.9 in) and 55 kg (121 lb), is 18.8 cm (7.4 in) taller than an average male Rottweiler (65 cm, 52 kg) and weighs about the same.

Wolves are built for travelling long distances: compared with most dogs of the same shoulder height they have longer legs, a narrower chest and bigger feet, so they often look larger than the numbers suggest. Wolf sizes also vary a lot by region; northern wolves are the biggest.

Rottweiler vs wolf: questions

Is a Rottweiler bigger than a wolf?

An average male Rottweiler is shorter than both wolves. By weight, it is heavier than a Eurasian wolf and lighter than a Northwestern wolf. An average male Eurasian wolf, at 78 cm (30.5 in) and 47 kg (103 lb), is 12.8 cm (5 in) taller than an average male Rottweiler (65 cm, 52 kg) and is about 11% lighter.

How much does a Rottweiler weigh compared with a wolf?

A male Rottweiler weighs 43–61 kg (95–134 lb) and a female 37–46 kg (80–100 lb). A male Eurasian wolf weighs 38–55 kg (84–121 lb) and a male Northwestern wolf 45–65 kg (99–143 lb).
