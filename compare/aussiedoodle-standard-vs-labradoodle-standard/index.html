Aussiedoodle (Standard) vs Labradoodle (Standard): size comparison

Labradoodle (Standard) or Aussiedoodle (Standard)? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

How the two breeds compare

The Labradoodle (Standard) is the taller dog. An average male stands 57 cm (22.4 in) at the shoulder, 5 cm (2 in) more than an average male Aussiedoodle (Standard) at 52 cm (20.5 in).

They weigh about the same: 25 kg (55 lb) for an average male Aussiedoodle (Standard) and 27 kg (58 lb) for a Labradoodle (Standard).

Both are in the designer & mixed category.

Their life expectancy is similar (12–15 years and 12–14 years).

Aussiedoodle (Standard) vs Labradoodle (Standard): questions

Which is bigger, the Aussiedoodle (Standard) or the Labradoodle (Standard)?

The Labradoodle (Standard) is the taller dog. An average male stands 57 cm (22.4 in) at the shoulder, 5 cm (2 in) more than an average male Aussiedoodle (Standard) at 52 cm (20.5 in).

How much does an Aussiedoodle (Standard) weigh compared with a Labradoodle (Standard)?

A male Aussiedoodle (Standard) weighs 18–32 kg (40–71 lb) and a female 16–27 kg (35–60 lb). A male Labradoodle (Standard) weighs 23–30 kg (51–66 lb) and a female 18–25 kg (40–55 lb).
