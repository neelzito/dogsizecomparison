Estrela Mountain Dog vs Tibetan Mastiff: size comparison

Tibetan Mastiff or Estrela Mountain Dog? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

Average male and female of each breed, drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person. Solid line = male, dashed = female.

How the two breeds compare

The Tibetan Mastiff is the taller dog. An average male stands 71 cm (28 in) at the shoulder, 3.3 cm (1.3 in) more than an average male Estrela Mountain Dog at 68 cm (26.7 in).

By weight, the Tibetan Mastiff is about 15% heavier: 55 kg (120 lb) for an average male against 48 kg (105 lb) for the Estrela Mountain Dog.

Both are in the Working group.

The Estrela Mountain Dog tends to live longer: 10–14 years against 10–12 years.

Estrela Mountain Dog vs Tibetan Mastiff: questions

Which is bigger, the Estrela Mountain Dog or the Tibetan Mastiff?

The Tibetan Mastiff is the taller dog. An average male stands 71 cm (28 in) at the shoulder, 3.3 cm (1.3 in) more than an average male Estrela Mountain Dog at 68 cm (26.7 in).

How much does an Estrela Mountain Dog weigh compared with a Tibetan Mastiff?

A male Estrela Mountain Dog weighs 35–60 kg (77–132 lb) and a female 35–60 kg (77–132 lb). A male Tibetan Mastiff weighs 41–68 kg (90–150 lb) and a female 32–55 kg (71–120 lb).
