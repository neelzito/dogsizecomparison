Barbet vs Small Münsterländer: size comparison

Barbet or Small Münsterländer? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

Average male and female of each breed, drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person. Solid line = male, dashed = female.

How the two breeds compare

The Barbet is the taller dog. An average male stands 55 cm (21.8 in) at the shoulder, 2.5 cm (1 in) more than an average male Small Münsterländer at 53 cm (20.8 in).

They weigh about the same: 23 kg (50 lb) for an average male Barbet and 23 kg (50 lb) for a Small Münsterländer.

Both are in the Sporting group.

Their life expectancy is similar (12–14 years and 12–14 years).

Barbet vs Small Münsterländer: questions

Which is bigger, the Barbet or the Small Münsterländer?

The Barbet is the taller dog. An average male stands 55 cm (21.8 in) at the shoulder, 2.5 cm (1 in) more than an average male Small Münsterländer at 53 cm (20.8 in).

How much does a Barbet weigh compared with a Small Münsterländer?

A male Barbet weighs 16–30 kg (35–65 lb) and a female 16–30 kg (35–65 lb). A male Small Münsterländer weighs 18–27 kg (40–60 lb) and a female 18–27 kg (40–60 lb).
