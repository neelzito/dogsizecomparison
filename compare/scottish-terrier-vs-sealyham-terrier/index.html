Scottish Terrier vs Sealyham Terrier: size comparison

Sealyham Terrier or Scottish Terrier? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

How the two breeds compare

The Scottish Terrier and the Sealyham Terrier are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Scottish Terrier stands 26 cm (10 in) and an average male Sealyham Terrier 27 cm (10.4 in).

By weight, the Sealyham Terrier is about 16% heavier: 11 kg (24 lb) for an average male against 9.3 kg (21 lb) for the Scottish Terrier.

Both are in the Terrier group.

The Sealyham Terrier tends to live longer: 12–14 years against 12 years.

Scottish Terrier vs Sealyham Terrier: questions

Which is bigger, the Scottish Terrier or the Sealyham Terrier?

The Scottish Terrier and the Sealyham Terrier are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Scottish Terrier stands 26 cm (10 in) and an average male Sealyham Terrier 27 cm (10.4 in).

How much does a Scottish Terrier weigh compared with a Sealyham Terrier?

A male Scottish Terrier weighs 8.6–10 kg (19–22 lb) and a female 8.2–9.5 kg (18–21 lb). A male Sealyham Terrier weighs 11 kg (23–24 lb) and a female 8–10 kg (18–22 lb).
