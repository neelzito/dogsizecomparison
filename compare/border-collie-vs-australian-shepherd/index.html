Border Collie vs Australian Shepherd: size comparison

Australian Shepherd or Border Collie? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

Average male and female of each breed, drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person. Solid line = male, dashed = female.

How the two breeds compare

The Australian Shepherd is the taller dog. An average male stands 55 cm (21.6 in) at the shoulder, 2.5 cm (1 in) more than an average male Border Collie at 52 cm (20.6 in).

By weight, the Australian Shepherd is about 35% heavier: 26 kg (57 lb) for an average male against 19 kg (42 lb) for the Border Collie.

Both are in the Herding group.

Their life expectancy is similar (12–15 years and 12–15 years).

Border Collie vs Australian Shepherd: questions

Which is bigger, the Border Collie or the Australian Shepherd?

The Australian Shepherd is the taller dog. An average male stands 55 cm (21.6 in) at the shoulder, 2.5 cm (1 in) more than an average male Border Collie at 52 cm (20.6 in).

How much does a Border Collie weigh compared with an Australian Shepherd?

A male Border Collie weighs 14–25 kg (30–55 lb) and a female 14–25 kg (30–55 lb). A male Australian Shepherd weighs 23–30 kg (50–65 lb) and a female 18–25 kg (40–55 lb).
