Pomsky vs Puggle: size comparison

Pomsky or Puggle? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

Average male and female of each breed, drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person. Solid line = male, dashed = female.

How the two breeds compare

The Pomsky and the Puggle are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Pomsky stands 32 cm (12.4 in) and an average male Puggle 32 cm (12.4 in).

By weight, the Pomsky is about 13% heavier: 12 kg (25 lb) for an average male against 10 kg (22 lb) for the Puggle.

Both are in the designer & mixed category and come from the United States.

The Pomsky tends to live longer: 13–15 years against 10–15 years.

For air travel, the Puggle can sometimes fit the 8 kg cabin limit for dog and carrier; the Pomsky does not.

Pomsky vs Puggle: questions

Which is bigger, the Pomsky or the Puggle?

The Pomsky and the Puggle are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Pomsky stands 32 cm (12.4 in) and an average male Puggle 32 cm (12.4 in).

How much does a Pomsky weigh compared with a Puggle?

A male Pomsky weighs 9–14 kg (20–31 lb) and a female 7–12 kg (15–26 lb). A male Puggle weighs 6.8–14 kg (15–30 lb) and a female 6.5–12 kg (14–26 lb).
