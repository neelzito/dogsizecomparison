Pomsky vs Pomeranian: size comparison

Pomsky or Pomeranian? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

Average male and female of each breed, drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person. Solid line = male, dashed = female.

How the two breeds compare

The Pomsky is the taller dog. An average male stands 32 cm (12.4 in) at the shoulder, 15 cm (5.9 in) more than an average male Pomeranian at 16.5 cm (6.5 in), about 1.9× the height.

By weight, the Pomsky is about 5× as heavy: 12 kg (25 lb) for an average male against 2.3 kg (5.1 lb) for the Pomeranian.

The Pomsky belongs to the designer & mixed category and comes from the United States; the Pomeranian is in the Toy group, from Germany/Poland.

Their life expectancy is similar (13–15 years and 12–16 years).

For air travel, the Pomeranian fits the 8 kg cabin limit for dog and carrier; the Pomsky does not.

Pomsky vs Pomeranian: questions

Which is bigger, the Pomsky or the Pomeranian?

The Pomsky is the taller dog. An average male stands 32 cm (12.4 in) at the shoulder, 15 cm (5.9 in) more than an average male Pomeranian at 16.5 cm (6.5 in), about 1.9× the height.

How much does a Pomsky weigh compared with a Pomeranian?

A male Pomsky weighs 9–14 kg (20–31 lb) and a female 7–12 kg (15–26 lb). A male Pomeranian weighs 1.4–3.2 kg (3.1–7.1 lb) and a female 1.4–3.2 kg (3.1–7.1 lb).
