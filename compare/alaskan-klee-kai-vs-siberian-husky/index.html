Alaskan Klee Kai vs Siberian Husky: size comparison

Siberian Husky or Alaskan Klee Kai? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

How the two breeds compare

The Siberian Husky is the taller dog. An average male stands 57 cm (22.2 in) at the shoulder, 19.8 cm (7.8 in) more than an average male Alaskan Klee Kai at 37 cm (14.5 in), about 1.5× the height.

By weight, the Siberian Husky is about 3.3× as heavy: 24 kg (52 lb) for an average male against 7.1 kg (16 lb) for the Alaskan Klee Kai.

Both are in the Working group.

The Alaskan Klee Kai tends to live longer: 13–16 years against 12–14 years.

For air travel, the Alaskan Klee Kai can sometimes fit the 8 kg cabin limit for dog and carrier; the Siberian Husky does not.

Alaskan Klee Kai vs Siberian Husky: questions

Which is bigger, the Alaskan Klee Kai or the Siberian Husky?

The Siberian Husky is the taller dog. An average male stands 57 cm (22.2 in) at the shoulder, 19.8 cm (7.8 in) more than an average male Alaskan Klee Kai at 37 cm (14.5 in), about 1.5× the height.

How much does an Alaskan Klee Kai weigh compared with a Siberian Husky?

A male Alaskan Klee Kai weighs 2.7–12 kg (6–25 lb) and a female 2.7–12 kg (6–25 lb). A male Siberian Husky weighs 21–27 kg (45–60 lb) and a female 16–23 kg (35–50 lb).
