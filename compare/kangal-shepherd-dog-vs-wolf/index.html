Kangal Shepherd Dog vs wolf: size comparison

How does the Kangal Shepherd Dog measure up to a gray wolf? Shoulder heights and weights against the Eurasian wolf and the larger Northwestern wolf, drawn to scale next to a person.

Average male and female Kangal Shepherd Dog next to average male wolves, drawn to scale with a 175 cm (5′9″) person. Solid line = male, dashed = female.

Open in the comparison tool →

Is the Kangal Shepherd Dog bigger than a wolf?

An average male Kangal Shepherd Dog is shorter than both wolves. By weight, it is heavier than a Eurasian wolf and about as heavy as a Northwestern wolf.

An average male Eurasian wolf, at 78 cm (30.5 in) and 47 kg (103 lb), is 2.5 cm (1 in) taller than an average male Kangal Shepherd Dog (75 cm, 54 kg) and is about 14% lighter.

An average male Northwestern wolf, at 84 cm (32.9 in) and 55 kg (121 lb), is 8.5 cm (3.3 in) taller than an average male Kangal Shepherd Dog (75 cm, 54 kg) and weighs about the same.

Wolves are built for travelling long distances: compared with most dogs of the same shoulder height they have longer legs, a narrower chest and bigger feet, so they often look larger than the numbers suggest. Wolf sizes also vary a lot by region; northern wolves are the biggest.

Is a Kangal Shepherd Dog bigger than a wolf?

How much does a Kangal Shepherd Dog weigh compared with a wolf?
