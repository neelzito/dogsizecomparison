Clumber Spaniel vs Labrador Retriever: size comparison

Labrador Retriever or Clumber Spaniel? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

Average male and female of each breed, drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person. Solid line = male, dashed = female.

How the two breeds compare

The Labrador Retriever is the taller dog. An average male stands 60 cm (23.4 in) at the shoulder, 11.3 cm (4.4 in) more than an average male Clumber Spaniel at 48 cm (19 in).

They weigh about the same: 35 kg (78 lb) for an average male Clumber Spaniel and 33 kg (73 lb) for a Labrador Retriever.

Both are in the Sporting group.

The Labrador Retriever tends to live longer: 11–13 years against 10–12 years.

Clumber Spaniel vs Labrador Retriever: questions

Which is bigger, the Clumber Spaniel or the Labrador Retriever?

The Labrador Retriever is the taller dog. An average male stands 60 cm (23.4 in) at the shoulder, 11.3 cm (4.4 in) more than an average male Clumber Spaniel at 48 cm (19 in).

How much does a Clumber Spaniel weigh compared with a Labrador Retriever?

A male Clumber Spaniel weighs 32–39 kg (71–85 lb) and a female 25–32 kg (55–71 lb). A male Labrador Retriever weighs 30–37 kg (65–80 lb) and a female 25–32 kg (55–71 lb).
