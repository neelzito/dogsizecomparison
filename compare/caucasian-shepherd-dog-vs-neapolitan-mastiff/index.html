Caucasian Shepherd Dog vs Neapolitan Mastiff: size comparison

Neapolitan Mastiff or Caucasian Shepherd Dog? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

How the two breeds compare

The Neapolitan Mastiff is the taller dog. An average male stands 72 cm (28.4 in) at the shoulder, 5 cm (2 in) more than an average male Caucasian Shepherd Dog at 67 cm (26.5 in).

By weight, the Neapolitan Mastiff is about 11% heavier: 68 kg (150 lb) for an average male against 61 kg (134 lb) for the Caucasian Shepherd Dog.

Both are in the Working group.

The Caucasian Shepherd Dog tends to live longer: 10–12 years against 7–9 years.

Caucasian Shepherd Dog vs Neapolitan Mastiff: questions

Which is bigger, the Caucasian Shepherd Dog or the Neapolitan Mastiff?

The Neapolitan Mastiff is the taller dog. An average male stands 72 cm (28.4 in) at the shoulder, 5 cm (2 in) more than an average male Caucasian Shepherd Dog at 67 cm (26.5 in).

How much does a Caucasian Shepherd Dog weigh compared with a Neapolitan Mastiff?

A male Caucasian Shepherd Dog weighs 45–77 kg (99–170 lb) and a female 45–77 kg (99–170 lb). A male Neapolitan Mastiff weighs 68 kg (150 lb) and a female 50 kg (110 lb).
