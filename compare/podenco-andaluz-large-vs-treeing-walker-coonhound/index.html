Podenco Andaluz (Large) vs Treeing Walker Coonhound: size comparison

How the two breeds compare

The Treeing Walker Coonhound is the taller dog. An average male stands 62 cm (24.5 in) at the shoulder, 3.3 cm (1.3 in) more than an average male Podenco Andaluz (Large) at 59 cm (23.2 in).

They weigh about the same: 27 kg (60 lb) for an average male Podenco Andaluz (Large) and 27 kg (60 lb) for a Treeing Walker Coonhound.

Both are in the Hound group.

Their life expectancy is similar (12–14 years and 12–13 years).

Podenco Andaluz (Large) vs Treeing Walker Coonhound: questions

Which is bigger, the Podenco Andaluz (Large) or the Treeing Walker Coonhound?

The Treeing Walker Coonhound is the taller dog. An average male stands 62 cm (24.5 in) at the shoulder, 3.3 cm (1.3 in) more than an average male Podenco Andaluz (Large) at 59 cm (23.2 in).

How much does a Podenco Andaluz (Large) weigh compared with a Treeing Walker Coonhound?

A male Podenco Andaluz (Large) weighs 21–33 kg (46–73 lb) and a female 21–33 kg (46–73 lb). A male Treeing Walker Coonhound weighs 23–32 kg (50–71 lb) and a female 23–32 kg (50–71 lb).
