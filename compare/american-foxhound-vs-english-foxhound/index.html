American Foxhound vs English Foxhound: size comparison

English Foxhound or American Foxhound? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

Average male and female of each breed, drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person. Solid line = male, dashed = female.

How the two breeds compare

The American Foxhound and the English Foxhound are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male American Foxhound stands 60 cm (23.5 in) and an average male English Foxhound 61 cm (24 in).

They weigh about the same: 31 kg (68 lb) for an average male American Foxhound and 31 kg (67 lb) for a English Foxhound.

Both are in the Hound group.

Their life expectancy is similar (11–13 years and 10–13 years).

American Foxhound vs English Foxhound: questions

Which is bigger, the American Foxhound or the English Foxhound?

The American Foxhound and the English Foxhound are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male American Foxhound stands 60 cm (23.5 in) and an average male English Foxhound 61 cm (24 in).

How much does an American Foxhound weigh compared with an English Foxhound?

A male American Foxhound weighs 30–32 kg (65–71 lb) and a female 27–30 kg (60–65 lb). A male English Foxhound weighs 27–34 kg (60–75 lb) and a female 27–34 kg (60–75 lb).
