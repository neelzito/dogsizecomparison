Pembroke Welsh Corgi vs Cardigan Welsh Corgi: size comparison

Cardigan Welsh Corgi or Pembroke Welsh Corgi? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

How the two breeds compare

The Pembroke Welsh Corgi and the Cardigan Welsh Corgi are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Pembroke Welsh Corgi stands 28 cm (11 in) and an average male Cardigan Welsh Corgi 29 cm (11.5 in).

By weight, the Cardigan Welsh Corgi is about 24% heavier: 15 kg (34 lb) for an average male against 12 kg (27 lb) for the Pembroke Welsh Corgi.

Both are in the Herding group and come from Wales.

The Cardigan Welsh Corgi tends to live longer: 12–15 years against 12–13 years.

Pembroke Welsh Corgi vs Cardigan Welsh Corgi: questions

Which is bigger, the Pembroke Welsh Corgi or the Cardigan Welsh Corgi?

The Pembroke Welsh Corgi and the Cardigan Welsh Corgi are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Pembroke Welsh Corgi stands 28 cm (11 in) and an average male Cardigan Welsh Corgi 29 cm (11.5 in).

How much does a Pembroke Welsh Corgi weigh compared with a Cardigan Welsh Corgi?

A male Pembroke Welsh Corgi weighs 11–14 kg (24–30 lb) and a female 10–13 kg (22–28 lb). A male Cardigan Welsh Corgi weighs 14–17 kg (30–37 lb) and a female 12–16 kg (25–34 lb).
