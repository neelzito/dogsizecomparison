How the two breeds compare

The Black and Tan Coonhound and the Rhodesian Ridgeback are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Black and Tan Coonhound stands 66 cm (26 in) and an average male Rhodesian Ridgeback 66 cm (26 in).

They weigh about the same: 40 kg (88 lb) for an average male Black and Tan Coonhound and 39 kg (85 lb) for a Rhodesian Ridgeback.

Both are in the Hound group.

Their life expectancy is similar (10–12 years and 10–12 years).

Which is bigger, the Black and Tan Coonhound or the Rhodesian Ridgeback?

How much does a Black and Tan Coonhound weigh compared with a Rhodesian Ridgeback?
