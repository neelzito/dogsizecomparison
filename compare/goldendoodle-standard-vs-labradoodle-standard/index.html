How the two breeds compare

The Goldendoodle (Standard) and the Labradoodle (Standard) are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Goldendoodle (Standard) stands 58 cm (22.8 in) and an average male Labradoodle (Standard) 57 cm (22.4 in).

They weigh about the same: 29 kg (63 lb) for an average male Goldendoodle (Standard) and 27 kg (58 lb) for a Labradoodle (Standard).

Both are in the designer & mixed category.

Their life expectancy is similar (10–15 years and 12–14 years).

Goldendoodle (Standard) vs Labradoodle (Standard): questions

Which is bigger, the Goldendoodle (Standard) or the Labradoodle (Standard)?

The Goldendoodle (Standard) and the Labradoodle (Standard) are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Goldendoodle (Standard) stands 58 cm (22.8 in) and an average male Labradoodle (Standard) 57 cm (22.4 in).

How much does a Goldendoodle (Standard) weigh compared with a Labradoodle (Standard)?

A male Goldendoodle (Standard) weighs 23–34 kg (51–75 lb) and a female 20–29 kg (44–64 lb). A male Labradoodle (Standard) weighs 23–30 kg (51–66 lb) and a female 18–25 kg (40–55 lb).
