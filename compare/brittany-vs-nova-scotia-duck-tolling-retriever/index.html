Brittany vs Nova Scotia Duck Tolling Retriever: size comparison

Nova Scotia Duck Tolling Retriever or Brittany? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

How the two breeds compare

The Brittany and the Nova Scotia Duck Tolling Retriever are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Brittany stands 48 cm (19 in) and an average male Nova Scotia Duck Tolling Retriever 50 cm (19.5 in).

By weight, the Nova Scotia Duck Tolling Retriever is about 22% heavier: 19 kg (42 lb) for an average male against 16 kg (35 lb) for the Brittany.

Both are in the Sporting group.

Their life expectancy is similar (12–14 years and 12–14 years).
