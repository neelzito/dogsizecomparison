How the two breeds compare

The American English Coonhound and the Portuguese Podengo (Large) are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male American English Coonhound stands 64 cm (25 in) and an average male Portuguese Podengo (Large) 63 cm (24.6 in).

They weigh about the same: 25 kg (55 lb) for an average male American English Coonhound and 25 kg (55 lb) for a Portuguese Podengo (Large).

Both are in the Hound group.

The Portuguese Podengo (Large) tends to live longer: 12–15 years against 11–12 years.
