Jack Russell Terrier vs Russell Terrier: size comparison

Russell Terrier or Jack Russell Terrier? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

How the two breeds compare

The Jack Russell Terrier and the Russell Terrier are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Jack Russell Terrier stands 28 cm (10.8 in) and an average male Russell Terrier 28 cm (11 in).

They weigh about the same: 5.5 kg (12 lb) for an average male Jack Russell Terrier and 5.4 kg (12 lb) for a Russell Terrier.

Both are in the Terrier group and come from England.

The Jack Russell Terrier tends to live longer: 13–16 years against 12–14 years.

For air travel, the Jack Russell Terrier fits the 8 kg cabin limit for dog and carrier; the Russell Terrier only sometimes does.

Jack Russell Terrier vs Russell Terrier: questions

Which is bigger, the Jack Russell Terrier or the Russell Terrier?

The Jack Russell Terrier and the Russell Terrier are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Jack Russell Terrier stands 28 cm (10.8 in) and an average male Russell Terrier 28 cm (11 in).

How much does a Jack Russell Terrier weigh compared with a Russell Terrier?

A male Jack Russell Terrier weighs 5–6 kg (11–13 lb) and a female 5–6 kg (11–13 lb). A male Russell Terrier weighs 4.1–6.8 kg (9–15 lb) and a female 4.1–6.8 kg (9–15 lb).
