Norwegian Lundehund vs Podenco Andaluz (Small): size comparison

Podenco Andaluz (Small) or Norwegian Lundehund? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

How the two breeds compare

The Podenco Andaluz (Small) is the taller dog. An average male stands 39 cm (15.2 in) at the shoulder, 3 cm (1.2 in) more than an average male Norwegian Lundehund at 36 cm (14 in).

They weigh about the same: 7.5 kg (17 lb) for an average male Norwegian Lundehund and 8 kg (18 lb) for a Podenco Andaluz (Small).

Both are in the Hound group.

Their life expectancy is similar (12–15 years and 12–14 years).

Norwegian Lundehund vs Podenco Andaluz (Small): questions

Which is bigger, the Norwegian Lundehund or the Podenco Andaluz (Small)?

The Podenco Andaluz (Small) is the taller dog. An average male stands 39 cm (15.2 in) at the shoulder, 3 cm (1.2 in) more than an average male Norwegian Lundehund at 36 cm (14 in).

How much does a Norwegian Lundehund weigh compared with a Podenco Andaluz (Small)?

A male Norwegian Lundehund weighs 6.8–8.2 kg (15–18 lb) and a female 5.4–6.8 kg (12–15 lb). A male Podenco Andaluz (Small) weighs 5–11 kg (11–24 lb) and a female 5–11 kg (11–24 lb).
