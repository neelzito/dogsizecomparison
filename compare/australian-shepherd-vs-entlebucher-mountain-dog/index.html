Australian Shepherd vs Entlebucher Mountain Dog: size comparison

Australian Shepherd or Entlebucher Mountain Dog? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

How the two breeds compare

The Australian Shepherd is the taller dog. An average male stands 55 cm (21.6 in) at the shoulder, 6.5 cm (2.6 in) more than an average male Entlebucher Mountain Dog at 48 cm (19 in).

They weigh about the same: 26 kg (57 lb) for an average male Australian Shepherd and 26 kg (57 lb) for a Entlebucher Mountain Dog.

Both are in the Herding group.

The Australian Shepherd tends to live longer: 12–15 years against 11–13 years.

Australian Shepherd vs Entlebucher Mountain Dog: questions

Which is bigger, the Australian Shepherd or the Entlebucher Mountain Dog?

The Australian Shepherd is the taller dog. An average male stands 55 cm (21.6 in) at the shoulder, 6.5 cm (2.6 in) more than an average male Entlebucher Mountain Dog at 48 cm (19 in).

How much does an Australian Shepherd weigh compared with an Entlebucher Mountain Dog?

A male Australian Shepherd weighs 23–30 kg (50–65 lb) and a female 18–25 kg (40–55 lb). A male Entlebucher Mountain Dog weighs 23–30 kg (50–65 lb) and a female 18–25 kg (40–55 lb).
