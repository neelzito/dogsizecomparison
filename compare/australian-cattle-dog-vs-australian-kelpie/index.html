Australian Cattle Dog vs Australian Kelpie: size comparison

Australian Cattle Dog or Australian Kelpie? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

How the two breeds compare

The Australian Cattle Dog and the Australian Kelpie are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Australian Cattle Dog stands 48 cm (19 in) and an average male Australian Kelpie 47 cm (18.5 in).

They weigh about the same: 19 kg (42 lb) for an average male Australian Cattle Dog and 19 kg (42 lb) for a Australian Kelpie.

Both are in the Herding group and come from Australia.

The Australian Cattle Dog tends to live longer: 12–16 years against 10–13 years.
