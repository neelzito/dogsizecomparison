Golden Retriever vs Goldendoodle (Standard): size comparison

How the two breeds compare

The Golden Retriever and the Goldendoodle (Standard) are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Golden Retriever stands 60 cm (23.5 in) and an average male Goldendoodle (Standard) 58 cm (22.8 in).

By weight, the Golden Retriever is about 11% heavier: 32 kg (70 lb) for an average male against 29 kg (63 lb) for the Goldendoodle (Standard).

The Golden Retriever belongs to the Sporting group and comes from Scotland; the Goldendoodle (Standard) is in the designer & mixed category, from the United States.

The Goldendoodle (Standard) tends to live longer: 10–15 years against 10–12 years.

Golden Retriever vs Goldendoodle (Standard): questions

Which is bigger, the Golden Retriever or the Goldendoodle (Standard)?

The Golden Retriever and the Goldendoodle (Standard) are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Golden Retriever stands 60 cm (23.5 in) and an average male Goldendoodle (Standard) 58 cm (22.8 in).

How much does a Golden Retriever weigh compared with a Goldendoodle (Standard)?

A male Golden Retriever weighs 30–34 kg (65–75 lb) and a female 25–30 kg (55–65 lb). A male Goldendoodle (Standard) weighs 23–34 kg (51–75 lb) and a female 20–29 kg (44–64 lb).
