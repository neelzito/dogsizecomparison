American Pit Bull Terrier vs wolf: size comparison

How does the American Pit Bull Terrier measure up to a gray wolf? Shoulder heights and weights against the Eurasian wolf and the larger Northwestern wolf, drawn to scale next to a person.

Average male and female American Pit Bull Terrier next to average male wolves, drawn to scale with a 175 cm (5′9″) person. Solid line = male, dashed = female.

Is the American Pit Bull Terrier bigger than a wolf?

An average male American Pit Bull Terrier is shorter than both wolves. By weight, it is lighter than both.

An average male Eurasian wolf, at 78 cm (30.5 in) and 47 kg (103 lb), is 28 cm (11 in) taller than an average male American Pit Bull Terrier (50 cm, 22 kg) and is about 2.2× as heavy.

An average male Northwestern wolf, at 84 cm (32.9 in) and 55 kg (121 lb), is 34 cm (13.4 in) taller than an average male American Pit Bull Terrier (50 cm, 22 kg) and is about 2.6× as heavy.

Wolves are built for travelling long distances: compared with most dogs of the same shoulder height they have longer legs, a narrower chest and bigger feet, so they often look larger than the numbers suggest. Wolf sizes also vary a lot by region; northern wolves are the biggest.

American Pit Bull Terrier vs wolf: questions

Is a American Pit Bull Terrier bigger than a wolf?

An average male American Pit Bull Terrier is shorter than both wolves. By weight, it is lighter than both. An average male Eurasian wolf, at 78 cm (30.5 in) and 47 kg (103 lb), is 28 cm (11 in) taller than an average male American Pit Bull Terrier (50 cm, 22 kg) and is about 2.2× as heavy.

How much does a American Pit Bull Terrier weigh compared with a wolf?

A male American Pit Bull Terrier weighs 16–27 kg (35–60 lb) and a female 14–23 kg (30–50 lb). A male Eurasian wolf weighs 38–55 kg (84–121 lb) and a male Northwestern wolf 45–65 kg (99–143 lb).
