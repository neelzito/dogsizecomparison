Australian Shepherd vs Bearded Collie: size comparison

Australian Shepherd or Bearded Collie? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

How the two breeds compare

The Australian Shepherd and the Bearded Collie are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Australian Shepherd stands 55 cm (21.6 in) and an average male Bearded Collie 55 cm (21.6 in).

By weight, the Australian Shepherd is about 14% heavier: 26 kg (57 lb) for an average male against 23 kg (50 lb) for the Bearded Collie.

Both are in the Herding group.

Their life expectancy is similar (12–15 years and 12–14 years).

Australian Shepherd vs Bearded Collie: questions

Which is bigger, the Australian Shepherd or the Bearded Collie?

The Australian Shepherd and the Bearded Collie are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Australian Shepherd stands 55 cm (21.6 in) and an average male Bearded Collie 55 cm (21.6 in).

How much does an Australian Shepherd weigh compared with a Bearded Collie?

A male Australian Shepherd weighs 23–30 kg (50–65 lb) and a female 18–25 kg (40–55 lb). A male Bearded Collie weighs 21–25 kg (45–55 lb) and a female 21–25 kg (45–55 lb).
