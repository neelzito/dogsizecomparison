Bracco Italiano vs Spinone Italiano: size comparison

Spinone Italiano or Bracco Italiano? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

Average male and female of each breed, drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person. Solid line = male, dashed = female.

How the two breeds compare

The Spinone Italiano is the taller dog. An average male stands 64 cm (25 in) at the shoulder, 2.5 cm (1 in) more than an average male Bracco Italiano at 61 cm (24 in).

By weight, the Spinone Italiano is about 11% heavier: 37 kg (80 lb) for an average male against 33 kg (73 lb) for the Bracco Italiano.

Both are in the Sporting group and come from Italy.

The Bracco Italiano tends to live longer: 10–14 years against 10–12 years.

Bracco Italiano vs Spinone Italiano: questions

Which is bigger, the Bracco Italiano or the Spinone Italiano?

The Spinone Italiano is the taller dog. An average male stands 64 cm (25 in) at the shoulder, 2.5 cm (1 in) more than an average male Bracco Italiano at 61 cm (24 in).

How much does a Bracco Italiano weigh compared with a Spinone Italiano?

A male Bracco Italiano weighs 25–41 kg (55–90 lb) and a female 25–41 kg (55–90 lb). A male Spinone Italiano weighs 34–39 kg (75–86 lb) and a female 28–32 kg (62–71 lb).
