Briard vs White Swiss Shepherd Dog: size comparison

Briard or White Swiss Shepherd Dog? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

Average male and female of each breed, drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person. Solid line = male, dashed = female.

How the two breeds compare

The Briard and the White Swiss Shepherd Dog are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Briard stands 64 cm (25 in) and an average male White Swiss Shepherd Dog 63 cm (24.8 in).

They weigh about the same: 35 kg (78 lb) for an average male Briard and 35 kg (77 lb) for a White Swiss Shepherd Dog.

Both are in the Herding group.

The White Swiss Shepherd Dog tends to live longer: 12–14 years against 12 years.

Briard vs White Swiss Shepherd Dog: questions

Which is bigger, the Briard or the White Swiss Shepherd Dog?

The Briard and the White Swiss Shepherd Dog are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Briard stands 64 cm (25 in) and an average male White Swiss Shepherd Dog 63 cm (24.8 in).

How much does a Briard weigh compared with a White Swiss Shepherd Dog?

A male Briard weighs 25–46 kg (55–100 lb) and a female 25–46 kg (55–100 lb). A male White Swiss Shepherd Dog weighs 30–40 kg (66–88 lb) and a female 25–35 kg (55–77 lb).
