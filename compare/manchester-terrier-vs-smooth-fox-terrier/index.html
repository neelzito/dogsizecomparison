Manchester Terrier vs Smooth Fox Terrier: size comparison

Smooth Fox Terrier or Manchester Terrier? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

Average male and female of each breed, drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person. Solid line = male, dashed = female.

How the two breeds compare

The Manchester Terrier and the Smooth Fox Terrier are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Manchester Terrier stands 39 cm (15.5 in) and an average male Smooth Fox Terrier 40 cm (15.6 in).

They weigh about the same: 7.7 kg (17 lb) for an average male Manchester Terrier and 8.2 kg (18 lb) for a Smooth Fox Terrier.

Both are in the Terrier group and come from England.

The Manchester Terrier tends to live longer: 15–17 years against 12–15 years.

For air travel, the Manchester Terrier can sometimes fit the 8 kg cabin limit for dog and carrier; the Smooth Fox Terrier does not.

Manchester Terrier vs Smooth Fox Terrier: questions

Which is bigger, the Manchester Terrier or the Smooth Fox Terrier?

The Manchester Terrier and the Smooth Fox Terrier are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Manchester Terrier stands 39 cm (15.5 in) and an average male Smooth Fox Terrier 40 cm (15.6 in).

How much does a Manchester Terrier weigh compared with a Smooth Fox Terrier?

A male Manchester Terrier weighs 5.4–10 kg (12–22 lb) and a female 5.4–10 kg (12–22 lb). A male Smooth Fox Terrier weighs 8.2 kg (18 lb) and a female 6.8–7.7 kg (15–17 lb).
