Chiweenie vs Maltipoo: size comparison

Maltipoo or Chiweenie? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

Average male and female of each breed, drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person. Solid line = male, dashed = female.

How the two breeds compare

The Maltipoo is the taller dog. An average male stands 25 cm (9.8 in) at the shoulder, 5 cm (2 in) more than an average male Chiweenie at 20 cm (7.9 in), about 1.3× the height.

By weight, the Maltipoo is about 18% heavier: 4.6 kg (10 lb) for an average male against 3.9 kg (8.5 lb) for the Chiweenie.

Both are in the designer & mixed category and come from the United States.

The Chiweenie tends to live longer: 12–16 years against 10–15 years.

For air travel, the Chiweenie fits the 8 kg cabin limit for dog and carrier; the Maltipoo only sometimes does.

Chiweenie vs Maltipoo: questions

Which is bigger, the Chiweenie or the Maltipoo?

The Maltipoo is the taller dog. An average male stands 25 cm (9.8 in) at the shoulder, 5 cm (2 in) more than an average male Chiweenie at 20 cm (7.9 in), about 1.3× the height.

How much does a Chiweenie weigh compared with a Maltipoo?

A male Chiweenie weighs 2.3–5.4 kg (5.1–12 lb) and a female 2.3–5 kg (5.1–11 lb). A male Maltipoo weighs 2.3–6.8 kg (5.1–15 lb) and a female 2.3–6 kg (5.1–13 lb).
