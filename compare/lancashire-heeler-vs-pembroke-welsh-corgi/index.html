Lancashire Heeler vs Pembroke Welsh Corgi: size comparison

How the two breeds compare

The Lancashire Heeler and the Pembroke Welsh Corgi are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Lancashire Heeler stands 28 cm (11 in) and an average male Pembroke Welsh Corgi 28 cm (11 in).

By weight, the Pembroke Welsh Corgi is about 2.1× as heavy: 12 kg (27 lb) for an average male against 5.9 kg (13 lb) for the Lancashire Heeler.

Both are in the Herding group.

The Lancashire Heeler tends to live longer: 12–15 years against 12–13 years.

For air travel, the Lancashire Heeler can sometimes fit the 8 kg cabin limit for dog and carrier; the Pembroke Welsh Corgi does not.

Lancashire Heeler vs Pembroke Welsh Corgi: questions

Which is bigger, the Lancashire Heeler or the Pembroke Welsh Corgi?

The Lancashire Heeler and the Pembroke Welsh Corgi are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Lancashire Heeler stands 28 cm (11 in) and an average male Pembroke Welsh Corgi 28 cm (11 in).

How much does a Lancashire Heeler weigh compared with a Pembroke Welsh Corgi?

A male Lancashire Heeler weighs 4.1–7.7 kg (9–17 lb) and a female 4.1–7.7 kg (9–17 lb). A male Pembroke Welsh Corgi weighs 11–14 kg (24–30 lb) and a female 10–13 kg (22–28 lb).
