Great Pyrenees vs Kangal Shepherd Dog: size comparison

Great Pyrenees or Kangal Shepherd Dog? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

How the two breeds compare

The Great Pyrenees and the Kangal Shepherd Dog are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Great Pyrenees stands 75 cm (29.5 in) and an average male Kangal Shepherd Dog 75 cm (29.5 in).

They weigh about the same: 51 kg (113 lb) for an average male Great Pyrenees and 54 kg (119 lb) for a Kangal Shepherd Dog.

Both are in the Working group.

The Kangal Shepherd Dog tends to live longer: 12–15 years against 10–12 years.

Great Pyrenees vs Kangal Shepherd Dog: questions

Which is bigger, the Great Pyrenees or the Kangal Shepherd Dog?

The Great Pyrenees and the Kangal Shepherd Dog are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Great Pyrenees stands 75 cm (29.5 in) and an average male Kangal Shepherd Dog 75 cm (29.5 in).

How much does a Great Pyrenees weigh compared with a Kangal Shepherd Dog?

A male Great Pyrenees weighs 46–57 kg (100–126 lb) and a female 39–50 kg (85–110 lb). A male Kangal Shepherd Dog weighs 48–60 kg (106–132 lb) and a female 40–50 kg (88–110 lb).
