Chihuahua vs Pomeranian: size comparison

Chihuahua or Pomeranian? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

Average male and female of each breed, drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person. Solid line = male, dashed = female.

How the two breeds compare

The Chihuahua and the Pomeranian are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Chihuahua stands 16.5 cm (6.5 in) and an average male Pomeranian 16.5 cm (6.5 in).

By weight, the Pomeranian is about 12% heavier: 2.3 kg (5.1 lb) for an average male against 2.1 kg (4.5 lb) for the Chihuahua.

Both are in the Toy group.

The Chihuahua tends to live longer: 14–16 years against 12–16 years.

Chihuahua vs Pomeranian: questions

Which is bigger, the Chihuahua or the Pomeranian?

The Chihuahua and the Pomeranian are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Chihuahua stands 16.5 cm (6.5 in) and an average male Pomeranian 16.5 cm (6.5 in).

How much does a Chihuahua weigh compared with a Pomeranian?

A male Chihuahua weighs 1.4–2.7 kg (3.1–6 lb) and a female 1.4–2.7 kg (3.1–6 lb). A male Pomeranian weighs 1.4–3.2 kg (3.1–7.1 lb) and a female 1.4–3.2 kg (3.1–7.1 lb).
