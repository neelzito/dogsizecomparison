Beagle vs Basset Hound: size comparison

Beagle or Basset Hound? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

How the two breeds compare

The Beagle and the Basset Hound are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Beagle stands 36 cm (14 in) and an average male Basset Hound 36 cm (14 in).

By weight, the Basset Hound is about 2.1× as heavy: 24 kg (52 lb) for an average male against 12 kg (25 lb) for the Beagle.

Both are in the Hound group.

Their life expectancy is similar (10–15 years and 12–13 years).

Beagle vs Basset Hound: questions

Which is bigger, the Beagle or the Basset Hound?

The Beagle and the Basset Hound are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Beagle stands 36 cm (14 in) and an average male Basset Hound 36 cm (14 in).

How much does a Beagle weigh compared with a Basset Hound?

A male Beagle weighs 9.5–14 kg (21–30 lb) and a female 9–14 kg (20–30 lb). A male Basset Hound weighs 18–30 kg (40–65 lb) and a female 18–30 kg (40–65 lb).
