Basset Hound vs Bavarian Mountain Scent Hound: size comparison

Bavarian Mountain Scent Hound or Basset Hound? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

How the two breeds compare

The Bavarian Mountain Scent Hound is the taller dog. An average male stands 48 cm (18.7 in) at the shoulder, 12 cm (4.7 in) more than an average male Basset Hound at 36 cm (14 in), about 1.3× the height.

They weigh about the same: 24 kg (52 lb) for an average male Basset Hound and 24 kg (52 lb) for a Bavarian Mountain Scent Hound.

Both are in the Hound group.

The Bavarian Mountain Scent Hound tends to live longer: 12–15 years against 12–13 years.

Basset Hound vs Bavarian Mountain Scent Hound: questions

Which is bigger, the Basset Hound or the Bavarian Mountain Scent Hound?

The Bavarian Mountain Scent Hound is the taller dog. An average male stands 48 cm (18.7 in) at the shoulder, 12 cm (4.7 in) more than an average male Basset Hound at 36 cm (14 in), about 1.3× the height.

How much does a Basset Hound weigh compared with a Bavarian Mountain Scent Hound?

A male Basset Hound weighs 18–30 kg (40–65 lb) and a female 18–30 kg (40–65 lb). A male Bavarian Mountain Scent Hound weighs 17–30 kg (37–66 lb) and a female 17–30 kg (37–66 lb).
