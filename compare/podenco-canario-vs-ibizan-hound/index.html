Podenco Canario vs Ibizan Hound: size comparison

Ibizan Hound or Podenco Canario? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

How the two breeds compare

The Ibizan Hound is the taller dog. An average male stands 65 cm (25.5 in) at the shoulder, 5.3 cm (2.1 in) more than an average male Podenco Canario at 60 cm (23.4 in).

They weigh about the same: 23 kg (50 lb) for an average male Podenco Canario and 23 kg (50 lb) for a Ibizan Hound.

Both are in the Hound group and come from Spain.

Their life expectancy is similar (12–14 years and 11–14 years).

Podenco Canario vs Ibizan Hound: questions

Which is bigger, the Podenco Canario or the Ibizan Hound?

The Ibizan Hound is the taller dog. An average male stands 65 cm (25.5 in) at the shoulder, 5.3 cm (2.1 in) more than an average male Podenco Canario at 60 cm (23.4 in).

How much does a Podenco Canario weigh compared with an Ibizan Hound?

A male Podenco Canario weighs 20–25 kg (44–55 lb) and a female 17–21 kg (37–46 lb). A male Ibizan Hound weighs 23 kg (50 lb) and a female 21 kg (45 lb).
