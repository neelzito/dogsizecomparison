Smooth Fox Terrier vs Wire Fox Terrier: size comparison

Smooth Fox Terrier or Wire Fox Terrier? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

How the two breeds compare

The Smooth Fox Terrier and the Wire Fox Terrier are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Smooth Fox Terrier stands 40 cm (15.6 in) and an average male Wire Fox Terrier 40 cm (15.6 in).

They weigh about the same: 8.2 kg (18 lb) for an average male Smooth Fox Terrier and 8.2 kg (18 lb) for a Wire Fox Terrier.

Both are in the Terrier group and come from England.

Their life expectancy is similar (12–15 years and 12–15 years).
