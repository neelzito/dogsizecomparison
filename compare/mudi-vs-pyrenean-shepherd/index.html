Mudi vs Pyrenean Shepherd: size comparison

Pyrenean Shepherd or Mudi? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

Average male and female of each breed, drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person. Solid line = male, dashed = female.

How the two breeds compare

The Pyrenean Shepherd is the taller dog. An average male stands 47 cm (18.3 in) at the shoulder, 4 cm (1.6 in) more than an average male Mudi at 43 cm (16.7 in).

They weigh about the same: 11 kg (23 lb) for an average male Mudi and 10 kg (22 lb) for a Pyrenean Shepherd.

Both are in the Herding group.

The Pyrenean Shepherd tends to live longer: 17–19 years against 12–14 years.

Mudi vs Pyrenean Shepherd: questions

Which is bigger, the Mudi or the Pyrenean Shepherd?

The Pyrenean Shepherd is the taller dog. An average male stands 47 cm (18.3 in) at the shoulder, 4 cm (1.6 in) more than an average male Mudi at 43 cm (16.7 in).

How much does a Mudi weigh compared with a Pyrenean Shepherd?

A male Mudi weighs 8.2–13 kg (18–29 lb) and a female 8.2–13 kg (18–29 lb). A male Pyrenean Shepherd weighs 6.8–14 kg (15–30 lb) and a female 6.8–14 kg (15–30 lb).
