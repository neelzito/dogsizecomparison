Siberian Husky vs Alaskan Malamute: size comparison

Alaskan Malamute or Siberian Husky? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

How the two breeds compare

The Alaskan Malamute is the taller dog. An average male stands 64 cm (25 in) at the shoulder, 7 cm (2.8 in) more than an average male Siberian Husky at 57 cm (22.2 in).

By weight, the Alaskan Malamute is about 62% heavier: 39 kg (85 lb) for an average male against 24 kg (52 lb) for the Siberian Husky.

Both are in the Working group.

The Siberian Husky tends to live longer: 12–14 years against 10–14 years.

Siberian Husky vs Alaskan Malamute: questions

Which is bigger, the Siberian Husky or the Alaskan Malamute?

The Alaskan Malamute is the taller dog. An average male stands 64 cm (25 in) at the shoulder, 7 cm (2.8 in) more than an average male Siberian Husky at 57 cm (22.2 in).

How much does a Siberian Husky weigh compared with an Alaskan Malamute?

A male Siberian Husky weighs 21–27 kg (45–60 lb) and a female 16–23 kg (35–50 lb). A male Alaskan Malamute weighs 39 kg (85 lb) and a female 34 kg (75 lb).
