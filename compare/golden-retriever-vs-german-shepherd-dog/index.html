Golden Retriever vs German Shepherd Dog: size comparison

German Shepherd Dog or Golden Retriever? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

Average male and female of each breed, drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person. Solid line = male, dashed = female.

How the two breeds compare

The German Shepherd Dog is the taller dog. An average male stands 64 cm (25 in) at the shoulder, 3.8 cm (1.5 in) more than an average male Golden Retriever at 60 cm (23.5 in).

By weight, the German Shepherd Dog is about 11% heavier: 35 kg (78 lb) for an average male against 32 kg (70 lb) for the Golden Retriever.

The Golden Retriever belongs to the Sporting group and comes from Scotland; the German Shepherd Dog is in the Herding group, from Germany.

The German Shepherd Dog tends to live longer: 12–14 years against 10–12 years.

Golden Retriever vs German Shepherd Dog: questions

Which is bigger, the Golden Retriever or the German Shepherd Dog?

The German Shepherd Dog is the taller dog. An average male stands 64 cm (25 in) at the shoulder, 3.8 cm (1.5 in) more than an average male Golden Retriever at 60 cm (23.5 in).

How much does a Golden Retriever weigh compared with a German Shepherd Dog?

A male Golden Retriever weighs 30–34 kg (65–75 lb) and a female 25–30 kg (55–65 lb). A male German Shepherd Dog weighs 30–41 kg (65–90 lb) and a female 23–32 kg (50–71 lb).
