How the two breeds compare

The Portuguese Podengo Pequeno is the taller dog. An average male stands 26 cm (10 in) at the shoulder, 11.8 cm (4.6 in) more than an average male Dachshund (Miniature) at 13.8 cm (5.4 in), about 1.9× the height.

By weight, the Portuguese Podengo Pequeno is about 18% heavier: 5 kg (11 lb) for an average male against 4.3 kg (9.4 lb) for the Dachshund (Miniature).

Both are in the Hound group.

Their life expectancy is similar (12–16 years and 12–15 years).

Dachshund (Miniature) vs Portuguese Podengo Pequeno: questions

Which is bigger, the Dachshund (Miniature) or the Portuguese Podengo Pequeno?

The Portuguese Podengo Pequeno is the taller dog. An average male stands 26 cm (10 in) at the shoulder, 11.8 cm (4.6 in) more than an average male Dachshund (Miniature) at 13.8 cm (5.4 in), about 1.9× the height.

How much does a Dachshund (Miniature) weigh compared with a Portuguese Podengo Pequeno?

A male Dachshund (Miniature) weighs 3.5–5 kg (7.7–11 lb) and a female 3.2–5 kg (7.1–11 lb). A male Portuguese Podengo Pequeno weighs 4.1–5.9 kg (9–13 lb) and a female 4.1–5.9 kg (9–13 lb).
