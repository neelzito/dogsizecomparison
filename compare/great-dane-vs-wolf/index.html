Great Dane vs wolf: size comparison

How does the Great Dane measure up to a gray wolf? Shoulder heights and weights against the Eurasian wolf and the larger Northwestern wolf, drawn to scale next to a person.

Average male and female Great Dane next to average male wolves, drawn to scale with a 175 cm (5′9″) person. Solid line = male, dashed = female.

Is the Great Dane bigger than a wolf?

An average male Great Dane is about as tall as a Eurasian wolf. By weight, it is heavier than both.

An average male Eurasian wolf, at 78 cm (30.5 in) and 47 kg (103 lb), is about the same height as an average male Great Dane (79 cm, 72 kg) and is about 35% lighter.

An average male Northwestern wolf, at 84 cm (32.9 in) and 55 kg (121 lb), is 4.8 cm (1.9 in) taller than an average male Great Dane (79 cm, 72 kg) and is about 23% lighter.

Wolves are built for travelling long distances: compared with most dogs of the same shoulder height they have longer legs, a narrower chest and bigger feet, so they often look larger than the numbers suggest. Wolf sizes also vary a lot by region; northern wolves are the biggest.

Great Dane vs wolf: questions

Is a Great Dane bigger than a wolf?

An average male Great Dane is about as tall as a Eurasian wolf. By weight, it is heavier than both. An average male Eurasian wolf, at 78 cm (30.5 in) and 47 kg (103 lb), is about the same height as an average male Great Dane (79 cm, 72 kg) and is about 35% lighter.

How much does a Great Dane weigh compared with a wolf?

A male Great Dane weighs 64–80 kg (140–175 lb) and a female 50–64 kg (110–140 lb). A male Eurasian wolf weighs 38–55 kg (84–121 lb) and a male Northwestern wolf 45–65 kg (99–143 lb).
