Boston Terrier vs Shiba Inu: size comparison

How the two breeds compare

The Boston Terrier and the Shiba Inu are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Boston Terrier stands 41 cm (15.9 in) and an average male Shiba Inu 40 cm (15.6 in).

By weight, the Shiba Inu is about 24% heavier: 11 kg (23 lb) for an average male against 8.5 kg (19 lb) for the Boston Terrier.

Both are in the Non-Sporting group.

The Shiba Inu tends to live longer: 13–16 years against 11–13 years.

For air travel, the Boston Terrier can sometimes fit the 8 kg cabin limit for dog and carrier; the Shiba Inu does not.

Boston Terrier vs Shiba Inu: questions

Which is bigger, the Boston Terrier or the Shiba Inu?

The Boston Terrier and the Shiba Inu are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Boston Terrier stands 41 cm (15.9 in) and an average male Shiba Inu 40 cm (15.6 in).

How much does a Boston Terrier weigh compared with a Shiba Inu?

A male Boston Terrier weighs 5.4–12 kg (12–25 lb) and a female 5.4–12 kg (12–25 lb). A male Shiba Inu weighs 11 kg (23 lb) and a female 7.7 kg (17 lb).
