Miniature Schnauzer vs Parson Russell Terrier: size comparison

Parson Russell Terrier or Miniature Schnauzer? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

Average male and female of each breed, drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person. Solid line = male, dashed = female.

How the two breeds compare

The Parson Russell Terrier is the taller dog. An average male stands 36 cm (14 in) at the shoulder, 2.5 cm (1 in) more than an average male Miniature Schnauzer at 33 cm (13 in).

They weigh about the same: 7.1 kg (16 lb) for an average male Miniature Schnauzer and 6.8 kg (15 lb) for a Parson Russell Terrier.

Both are in the Terrier group.

Their life expectancy is similar (12–15 years and 13–15 years).

Miniature Schnauzer vs Parson Russell Terrier: questions

Which is bigger, the Miniature Schnauzer or the Parson Russell Terrier?

The Parson Russell Terrier is the taller dog. An average male stands 36 cm (14 in) at the shoulder, 2.5 cm (1 in) more than an average male Miniature Schnauzer at 33 cm (13 in).

How much does a Miniature Schnauzer weigh compared with a Parson Russell Terrier?

A male Miniature Schnauzer weighs 5–9.1 kg (11–20 lb) and a female 5–9.1 kg (11–20 lb). A male Parson Russell Terrier weighs 5.9–7.7 kg (13–17 lb) and a female 5.9–7.7 kg (13–17 lb).
