Bloodhound vs Otterhound: size comparison

Otterhound or Bloodhound? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

Average male and female of each breed, drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person. Solid line = male, dashed = female.

How the two breeds compare

The Otterhound is the taller dog. An average male stands 69 cm (27 in) at the shoulder, 2.5 cm (1 in) more than an average male Bloodhound at 66 cm (26 in).

By weight, the Otterhound is about 14% heavier: 52 kg (115 lb) for an average male against 46 kg (100 lb) for the Bloodhound.

Both are in the Hound group.

Their life expectancy is similar (10–12 years and 10–13 years).

Bloodhound vs Otterhound: questions

Which is bigger, the Bloodhound or the Otterhound?

The Otterhound is the taller dog. An average male stands 69 cm (27 in) at the shoulder, 2.5 cm (1 in) more than an average male Bloodhound at 66 cm (26 in).

How much does a Bloodhound weigh compared with an Otterhound?

A male Bloodhound weighs 41–50 kg (90–110 lb) and a female 37–46 kg (80–100 lb). A male Otterhound weighs 52 kg (115 lb) and a female 37 kg (80 lb).
