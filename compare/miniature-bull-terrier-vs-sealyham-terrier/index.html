Miniature Bull Terrier vs Sealyham Terrier: size comparison

Miniature Bull Terrier or Sealyham Terrier? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

Average male and female of each breed, drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person. Solid line = male, dashed = female.

How the two breeds compare

The Miniature Bull Terrier is the taller dog. An average male stands 31 cm (12 in) at the shoulder, 4 cm (1.6 in) more than an average male Sealyham Terrier at 27 cm (10.4 in).

They weigh about the same: 10 kg (23 lb) for an average male Miniature Bull Terrier and 11 kg (24 lb) for a Sealyham Terrier.

Both are in the Terrier group.

The Sealyham Terrier tends to live longer: 12–14 years against 11–13 years.

Miniature Bull Terrier vs Sealyham Terrier: questions

Which is bigger, the Miniature Bull Terrier or the Sealyham Terrier?

The Miniature Bull Terrier is the taller dog. An average male stands 31 cm (12 in) at the shoulder, 4 cm (1.6 in) more than an average male Sealyham Terrier at 27 cm (10.4 in).

How much does a Miniature Bull Terrier weigh compared with a Sealyham Terrier?

A male Miniature Bull Terrier weighs 8.2–13 kg (18–28 lb) and a female 8.2–13 kg (18–28 lb). A male Sealyham Terrier weighs 11 kg (23–24 lb) and a female 8–10 kg (18–22 lb).
