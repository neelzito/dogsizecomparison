Polish Lowland Sheepdog vs Spanish Water Dog: size comparison

Polish Lowland Sheepdog or Spanish Water Dog? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

Average male and female of each breed, drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person. Solid line = male, dashed = female.

How the two breeds compare

The Polish Lowland Sheepdog and the Spanish Water Dog are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Polish Lowland Sheepdog stands 48 cm (19 in) and an average male Spanish Water Dog 47 cm (18.6 in).

By weight, the Spanish Water Dog is about 11% heavier: 20 kg (44 lb) for an average male against 18 kg (40 lb) for the Polish Lowland Sheepdog.

Both are in the Herding group.

Their life expectancy is similar (12–14 years and 12–14 years).

Polish Lowland Sheepdog vs Spanish Water Dog: questions

Which is bigger, the Polish Lowland Sheepdog or the Spanish Water Dog?

The Polish Lowland Sheepdog and the Spanish Water Dog are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Polish Lowland Sheepdog stands 48 cm (19 in) and an average male Spanish Water Dog 47 cm (18.6 in).

How much does a Polish Lowland Sheepdog weigh compared with a Spanish Water Dog?

A male Polish Lowland Sheepdog weighs 14–23 kg (30–50 lb) and a female 14–23 kg (30–50 lb). A male Spanish Water Dog weighs 18–22 kg (40–49 lb) and a female 14–18 kg (31–40 lb).
