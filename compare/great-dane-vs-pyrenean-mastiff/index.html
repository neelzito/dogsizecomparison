Great Dane vs Pyrenean Mastiff: size comparison

Great Dane or Pyrenean Mastiff? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

How the two breeds compare

The Great Dane and the Pyrenean Mastiff are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Great Dane stands 79 cm (31 in) and an average male Pyrenean Mastiff 77 cm (30.4 in).

They weigh about the same: 72 kg (158 lb) for an average male Great Dane and 70 kg (155 lb) for a Pyrenean Mastiff.

Both are in the Working group.

The Pyrenean Mastiff tends to live longer: 10–12 years against 7–10 years.

Great Dane vs Pyrenean Mastiff: questions

Which is bigger, the Great Dane or the Pyrenean Mastiff?

The Great Dane and the Pyrenean Mastiff are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Great Dane stands 79 cm (31 in) and an average male Pyrenean Mastiff 77 cm (30.4 in).

How much does a Great Dane weigh compared with a Pyrenean Mastiff?

A male Great Dane weighs 64–80 kg (140–175 lb) and a female 50–64 kg (110–140 lb). A male Pyrenean Mastiff weighs 55–86 kg (120–190 lb) and a female 55–86 kg (120–190 lb).
