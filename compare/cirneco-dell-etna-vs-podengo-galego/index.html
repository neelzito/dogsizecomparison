Cirneco dell'Etna vs Podengo Galego: size comparison

Podengo Galego or Cirneco dell'Etna? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

How the two breeds compare

The Cirneco dell'Etna and the Podengo Galego are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Cirneco dell'Etna stands 48 cm (19 in) and an average male Podengo Galego 49 cm (19.3 in).

By weight, the Podengo Galego is about 16% heavier: 13 kg (28 lb) for an average male against 11 kg (24 lb) for the Cirneco dell'Etna.

Both are in the Hound group.

The Cirneco dell'Etna tends to live longer: 15–16 years against 12–14 years.

Cirneco dell'Etna vs Podengo Galego: questions

Which is bigger, the Cirneco dell'Etna or the Podengo Galego?

The Cirneco dell'Etna and the Podengo Galego are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Cirneco dell'Etna stands 48 cm (19 in) and an average male Podengo Galego 49 cm (19.3 in).

How much does a Cirneco dell'Etna weigh compared with a Podengo Galego?

A male Cirneco dell'Etna weighs 9.1–13 kg (20–28 lb) and a female 8.6–12 kg (19–25 lb). A male Podengo Galego weighs 10–15 kg (22–33 lb) and a female 10–15 kg (22–33 lb).
