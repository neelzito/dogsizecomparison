Azawakh vs Ibizan Hound: size comparison

Azawakh or Ibizan Hound? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

Average male and female of each breed, drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person. Solid line = male, dashed = female.

How the two breeds compare

The Azawakh is the taller dog. An average male stands 69 cm (27 in) at the shoulder, 3.8 cm (1.5 in) more than an average male Ibizan Hound at 65 cm (25.5 in).

They weigh about the same: 23 kg (50 lb) for an average male Azawakh and 23 kg (50 lb) for a Ibizan Hound.

Both are in the Hound group.

The Azawakh tends to live longer: 12–15 years against 11–14 years.

Azawakh vs Ibizan Hound: questions

Which is bigger, the Azawakh or the Ibizan Hound?

The Azawakh is the taller dog. An average male stands 69 cm (27 in) at the shoulder, 3.8 cm (1.5 in) more than an average male Ibizan Hound at 65 cm (25.5 in).

How much does an Azawakh weigh compared with an Ibizan Hound?

A male Azawakh weighs 20–25 kg (44–55 lb) and a female 15–20 kg (33–44 lb). A male Ibizan Hound weighs 23 kg (50 lb) and a female 21 kg (45 lb).
