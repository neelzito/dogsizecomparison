Miniature Poodle vs Standard Poodle: size comparison

Standard Poodle or Miniature Poodle? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

Average male and female of each breed, drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person. Solid line = male, dashed = female.

How the two breeds compare

The Standard Poodle is the taller dog. An average male stands 58 cm (22.6 in) at the shoulder, 25.8 cm (10.1 in) more than an average male Miniature Poodle at 32 cm (12.5 in), about 1.8× the height.

By weight, the Standard Poodle is about 5.2× as heavy: 30 kg (65 lb) for an average male against 5.7 kg (12 lb) for the Miniature Poodle.

Both are in the Non-Sporting group and come from France.

Their life expectancy is similar (10–18 years and 10–18 years).

For air travel, the Miniature Poodle can sometimes fit the 8 kg cabin limit for dog and carrier; the Standard Poodle does not.

Miniature Poodle vs Standard Poodle: questions

Which is bigger, the Miniature Poodle or the Standard Poodle?

The Standard Poodle is the taller dog. An average male stands 58 cm (22.6 in) at the shoulder, 25.8 cm (10.1 in) more than an average male Miniature Poodle at 32 cm (12.5 in), about 1.8× the height.

How much does a Miniature Poodle weigh compared with a Standard Poodle?

A male Miniature Poodle weighs 4.5–6.8 kg (9.9–15 lb) and a female 4.5–6.8 kg (9.9–15 lb). A male Standard Poodle weighs 27–32 kg (60–71 lb) and a female 18–23 kg (40–50 lb).
