Weimaraner vs Vizsla: size comparison

Weimaraner or Vizsla? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

Average male and female of each breed, drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person. Solid line = male, dashed = female.

How the two breeds compare

The Weimaraner is the taller dog. An average male stands 66 cm (26 in) at the shoulder, 7.5 cm (3 in) more than an average male Vizsla at 59 cm (23 in).

By weight, the Weimaraner is about 40% heavier: 37 kg (80 lb) for an average male against 26 kg (57 lb) for the Vizsla.

Both are in the Sporting group.

The Vizsla tends to live longer: 12–14 years against 10–13 years.

Weimaraner vs Vizsla: questions

Which is bigger, the Weimaraner or the Vizsla?

The Weimaraner is the taller dog. An average male stands 66 cm (26 in) at the shoulder, 7.5 cm (3 in) more than an average male Vizsla at 59 cm (23 in).

How much does a Weimaraner weigh compared with a Vizsla?

A male Weimaraner weighs 32–41 kg (71–90 lb) and a female 25–34 kg (55–75 lb). A male Vizsla weighs 25–27 kg (55–60 lb) and a female 20–25 kg (44–55 lb).
