Beagle vs Harrier: size comparison

Harrier or Beagle? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

Average male and female of each breed, drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person. Solid line = male, dashed = female.

How the two breeds compare

The Harrier is the taller dog. An average male stands 51 cm (20.1 in) at the shoulder, 15.5 cm (6.1 in) more than an average male Beagle at 36 cm (14 in), about 1.4× the height.

By weight, the Harrier is about 2.1× as heavy: 24 kg (52 lb) for an average male against 12 kg (25 lb) for the Beagle.

Both are in the Hound group and come from England.

The Harrier tends to live longer: 12–15 years against 10–15 years.

Beagle vs Harrier: questions

Which is bigger, the Beagle or the Harrier?

The Harrier is the taller dog. An average male stands 51 cm (20.1 in) at the shoulder, 15.5 cm (6.1 in) more than an average male Beagle at 36 cm (14 in), about 1.4× the height.

How much does a Beagle weigh compared with a Harrier?

A male Beagle weighs 9.5–14 kg (21–30 lb) and a female 9–14 kg (20–30 lb). A male Harrier weighs 21–27 kg (45–60 lb) and a female 21–27 kg (45–60 lb).
