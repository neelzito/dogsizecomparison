How the two breeds compare

The Podenco Andaluz (Medium) and the Portuguese Podengo (Medium) are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Podenco Andaluz (Medium) stands 48 cm (18.9 in) and an average male Portuguese Podengo (Medium) 47 cm (18.5 in).

By weight, the Portuguese Podengo (Medium) is about 13% heavier: 18 kg (40 lb) for an average male against 16 kg (35 lb) for the Podenco Andaluz (Medium).

Both are in the Hound group.

Their life expectancy is similar (12–14 years and 12–15 years).

How much does a Podenco Andaluz (Medium) weigh compared with a Portuguese Podengo (Medium)?
