Cane Corso vs Mastiff (English): size comparison

Mastiff (English) or Cane Corso? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

How the two breeds compare

The Mastiff (English) is the taller dog. An average male stands 84 cm (32.9 in) at the shoulder, 16.8 cm (6.6 in) more than an average male Cane Corso at 67 cm (26.3 in), about 1.3× the height.

By weight, the Mastiff (English) is about 86% heavier: 89 kg (195 lb) for an average male against 48 kg (105 lb) for the Cane Corso.

Both are in the Working group.

The Cane Corso tends to live longer: 9–12 years against 6–10 years.

Cane Corso vs Mastiff (English): questions

Which is bigger, the Cane Corso or the Mastiff (English)?

The Mastiff (English) is the taller dog. An average male stands 84 cm (32.9 in) at the shoulder, 16.8 cm (6.6 in) more than an average male Cane Corso at 67 cm (26.3 in), about 1.3× the height.

How much does a Cane Corso weigh compared with a Mastiff (English)?

A male Cane Corso weighs 45–50 kg (99–110 lb) and a female 40–45 kg (88–99 lb). A male Mastiff (English) weighs 73–105 kg (160–230 lb) and a female 55–77 kg (120–170 lb).
