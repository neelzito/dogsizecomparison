Spinone Italiano vs Weimaraner: size comparison

Weimaraner or Spinone Italiano? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

Average male and female of each breed, drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person. Solid line = male, dashed = female.

How the two breeds compare

The Weimaraner is the taller dog. An average male stands 66 cm (26 in) at the shoulder, 2.5 cm (1 in) more than an average male Spinone Italiano at 64 cm (25 in).

They weigh about the same: 37 kg (80 lb) for an average male Spinone Italiano and 37 kg (80 lb) for a Weimaraner.

Both are in the Sporting group.

Their life expectancy is similar (10–12 years and 10–13 years).

Spinone Italiano vs Weimaraner: questions

Which is bigger, the Spinone Italiano or the Weimaraner?

The Weimaraner is the taller dog. An average male stands 66 cm (26 in) at the shoulder, 2.5 cm (1 in) more than an average male Spinone Italiano at 64 cm (25 in).

How much does a Spinone Italiano weigh compared with a Weimaraner?

A male Spinone Italiano weighs 34–39 kg (75–86 lb) and a female 28–32 kg (62–71 lb). A male Weimaraner weighs 32–41 kg (71–90 lb) and a female 25–34 kg (55–75 lb).
