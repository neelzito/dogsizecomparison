How the two breeds compare

The English Springer Spaniel and the Nova Scotia Duck Tolling Retriever are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male English Springer Spaniel stands 51 cm (20.1 in) and an average male Nova Scotia Duck Tolling Retriever 50 cm (19.5 in).

By weight, the English Springer Spaniel is about 17% heavier: 23 kg (50 lb) for an average male against 19 kg (42 lb) for the Nova Scotia Duck Tolling Retriever.

Both are in the Sporting group.

Their life expectancy is similar (12–14 years and 12–14 years).
