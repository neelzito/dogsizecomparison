Keeshond vs Xoloitzcuintli (Standard): size comparison

Xoloitzcuintli (Standard) or Keeshond? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

How the two breeds compare

The Xoloitzcuintli (Standard) is the taller dog. An average male stands 52 cm (20.5 in) at the shoulder, 6.5 cm (2.6 in) more than an average male Keeshond at 46 cm (17.9 in).

They weigh about the same: 18 kg (40 lb) for an average male Keeshond and 19 kg (42 lb) for a Xoloitzcuintli (Standard).

Both are in the Non-Sporting group.

The Xoloitzcuintli (Standard) tends to live longer: 13–18 years against 12–15 years.

Keeshond vs Xoloitzcuintli (Standard): questions

Which is bigger, the Keeshond or the Xoloitzcuintli (Standard)?

The Xoloitzcuintli (Standard) is the taller dog. An average male stands 52 cm (20.5 in) at the shoulder, 6.5 cm (2.6 in) more than an average male Keeshond at 46 cm (17.9 in).

How much does a Keeshond weigh compared with a Xoloitzcuintli (Standard)?

A male Keeshond weighs 16–21 kg (35–45 lb) and a female 16–21 kg (35–45 lb). A male Xoloitzcuintli (Standard) weighs 14–25 kg (30–55 lb) and a female 14–25 kg (30–55 lb).
