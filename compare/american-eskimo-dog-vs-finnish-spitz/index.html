American Eskimo Dog vs Finnish Spitz: size comparison

Finnish Spitz or American Eskimo Dog? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

Average male and female of each breed, drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person. Solid line = male, dashed = female.

How the two breeds compare

The Finnish Spitz is the taller dog. An average male stands 48 cm (18.8 in) at the shoulder, 4.5 cm (1.8 in) more than an average male American Eskimo Dog at 43 cm (17 in).

They weigh about the same: 14 kg (30 lb) for an average male American Eskimo Dog and 13 kg (29 lb) for a Finnish Spitz.

Both are in the Non-Sporting group.

Their life expectancy is similar (13–15 years and 13–15 years).

American Eskimo Dog vs Finnish Spitz: questions

Which is bigger, the American Eskimo Dog or the Finnish Spitz?

The Finnish Spitz is the taller dog. An average male stands 48 cm (18.8 in) at the shoulder, 4.5 cm (1.8 in) more than an average male American Eskimo Dog at 43 cm (17 in).

How much does an American Eskimo Dog weigh compared with a Finnish Spitz?

A male American Eskimo Dog weighs 12–16 kg (25–35 lb) and a female 12–16 kg (25–35 lb). A male Finnish Spitz weighs 12–15 kg (25–33 lb) and a female 9.1–13 kg (20–28 lb).
