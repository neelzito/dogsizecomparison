Berger Picard vs Dutch Shepherd: size comparison

Berger Picard or Dutch Shepherd? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

Average male and female of each breed, drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person. Solid line = male, dashed = female.

How the two breeds compare

The Berger Picard is the taller dog. An average male stands 62 cm (24.5 in) at the shoulder, 4 cm (1.6 in) more than an average male Dutch Shepherd at 58 cm (22.9 in).

They weigh about the same: 27 kg (60 lb) for an average male Berger Picard and 27 kg (58 lb) for a Dutch Shepherd.

Both are in the Herding group.

Their life expectancy is similar (12–13 years and 11–14 years).

Berger Picard vs Dutch Shepherd: questions

Which is bigger, the Berger Picard or the Dutch Shepherd?

The Berger Picard is the taller dog. An average male stands 62 cm (24.5 in) at the shoulder, 4 cm (1.6 in) more than an average male Dutch Shepherd at 58 cm (22.9 in).

How much does a Berger Picard weigh compared with a Dutch Shepherd?

A male Berger Picard weighs 23–32 kg (50–71 lb) and a female 23–32 kg (50–71 lb). A male Dutch Shepherd weighs 19–34 kg (42–75 lb) and a female 19–34 kg (42–75 lb).
