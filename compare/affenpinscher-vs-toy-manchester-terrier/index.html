Affenpinscher vs Toy Manchester Terrier: size comparison

Toy Manchester Terrier or Affenpinscher? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

Average male and female of each breed, drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person. Solid line = male, dashed = female.

How the two breeds compare

The Toy Manchester Terrier is the taller dog. An average male stands 28 cm (11 in) at the shoulder, 2 cm (0.8 in) more than an average male Affenpinscher at 26 cm (10.2 in).

They weigh about the same: 3.9 kg (8.5 lb) for an average male Affenpinscher and 4.1 kg (8.9 lb) for a Toy Manchester Terrier.

Both are in the Toy group.

The Toy Manchester Terrier tends to live longer: 15–17 years against 12–15 years.

Affenpinscher vs Toy Manchester Terrier: questions

Which is bigger, the Affenpinscher or the Toy Manchester Terrier?

The Toy Manchester Terrier is the taller dog. An average male stands 28 cm (11 in) at the shoulder, 2 cm (0.8 in) more than an average male Affenpinscher at 26 cm (10.2 in).

How much does an Affenpinscher weigh compared with a Toy Manchester Terrier?

A male Affenpinscher weighs 3.2–4.5 kg (7.1–9.9 lb) and a female 3.2–4.5 kg (7.1–9.9 lb). A male Toy Manchester Terrier weighs 2.7–5.4 kg (6–12 lb) and a female 2.7–5.4 kg (6–12 lb).
