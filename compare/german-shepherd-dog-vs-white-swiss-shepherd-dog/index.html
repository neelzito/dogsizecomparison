German Shepherd Dog vs White Swiss Shepherd Dog: size comparison

German Shepherd Dog or White Swiss Shepherd Dog? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

How the two breeds compare

The German Shepherd Dog and the White Swiss Shepherd Dog are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male German Shepherd Dog stands 64 cm (25 in) and an average male White Swiss Shepherd Dog 63 cm (24.8 in).

They weigh about the same: 35 kg (78 lb) for an average male German Shepherd Dog and 35 kg (77 lb) for a White Swiss Shepherd Dog.

Both are in the Herding group.

Their life expectancy is similar (12–14 years and 12–14 years).

German Shepherd Dog vs White Swiss Shepherd Dog: questions

Which is bigger, the German Shepherd Dog or the White Swiss Shepherd Dog?

The German Shepherd Dog and the White Swiss Shepherd Dog are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male German Shepherd Dog stands 64 cm (25 in) and an average male White Swiss Shepherd Dog 63 cm (24.8 in).

How much does a German Shepherd Dog weigh compared with a White Swiss Shepherd Dog?

A male German Shepherd Dog weighs 30–41 kg (65–90 lb) and a female 23–32 kg (50–71 lb). A male White Swiss Shepherd Dog weighs 30–40 kg (66–88 lb) and a female 25–35 kg (55–77 lb).
