How the two breeds compare

The Akita Inu (Japanese) and the Akita (American) are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Akita Inu (Japanese) stands 67 cm (26.3 in) and an average male Akita (American) 69 cm (27 in).

By weight, the Akita (American) is about 65% heavier: 52 kg (115 lb) for an average male against 32 kg (70 lb) for the Akita Inu (Japanese).

Both are in the Working group.

The Akita (American) tends to live longer: 10–14 years against 10–12 years.

Akita Inu (Japanese) vs Akita (American): questions

Which is bigger, the Akita Inu (Japanese) or the Akita (American)?

The Akita Inu (Japanese) and the Akita (American) are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Akita Inu (Japanese) stands 67 cm (26.3 in) and an average male Akita (American) 69 cm (27 in).

How much does an Akita Inu (Japanese) weigh compared with an Akita (American)?

A male Akita Inu (Japanese) weighs 30–34 kg (65–75 lb) and a female 25–30 kg (55–65 lb). A male Akita (American) weighs 46–59 kg (100–130 lb) and a female 32–46 kg (71–100 lb).
