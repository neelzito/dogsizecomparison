Chinese Crested vs Italian Greyhound: size comparison

Italian Greyhound or Chinese Crested? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

Average male and female of each breed, drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person. Solid line = male, dashed = female.

How the two breeds compare

The Italian Greyhound is the taller dog. An average male stands 36 cm (14 in) at the shoulder, 5 cm (2 in) more than an average male Chinese Crested at 31 cm (12 in).

They weigh about the same: 4.5 kg (9.9 lb) for an average male Chinese Crested and 4.8 kg (11 lb) for a Italian Greyhound.

Both are in the Toy group.

The Chinese Crested tends to live longer: 13–18 years against 14–15 years.

Chinese Crested vs Italian Greyhound: questions

Which is bigger, the Chinese Crested or the Italian Greyhound?

The Italian Greyhound is the taller dog. An average male stands 36 cm (14 in) at the shoulder, 5 cm (2 in) more than an average male Chinese Crested at 31 cm (12 in).

How much does a Chinese Crested weigh compared with an Italian Greyhound?

A male Chinese Crested weighs 3.6–5.4 kg (7.9–12 lb) and a female 3.6–5.4 kg (7.9–12 lb). A male Italian Greyhound weighs 3.2–6.4 kg (7.1–14 lb) and a female 3.2–6.4 kg (7.1–14 lb).
