Glen of Imaal Terrier vs Staffordshire Bull Terrier: size comparison

Staffordshire Bull Terrier or Glen of Imaal Terrier? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

How the two breeds compare

The Staffordshire Bull Terrier is the taller dog. An average male stands 38 cm (15 in) at the shoulder, 4.3 cm (1.7 in) more than an average male Glen of Imaal Terrier at 34 cm (13.3 in).

By weight, the Glen of Imaal Terrier is about 10% heavier: 16 kg (36 lb) for an average male against 15 kg (33 lb) for the Staffordshire Bull Terrier.

Both are in the Terrier group.

The Staffordshire Bull Terrier tends to live longer: 12–15 years against 10–15 years.

Glen of Imaal Terrier vs Staffordshire Bull Terrier: questions

Which is bigger, the Glen of Imaal Terrier or the Staffordshire Bull Terrier?

The Staffordshire Bull Terrier is the taller dog. An average male stands 38 cm (15 in) at the shoulder, 4.3 cm (1.7 in) more than an average male Glen of Imaal Terrier at 34 cm (13.3 in).

How much does a Glen of Imaal Terrier weigh compared with a Staffordshire Bull Terrier?

A male Glen of Imaal Terrier weighs 15–18 kg (32–40 lb) and a female 15–18 kg (32–40 lb). A male Staffordshire Bull Terrier weighs 13–17 kg (28–37 lb) and a female 11–16 kg (24–34 lb).
