Barbet vs Irish Red and White Setter: size comparison

Irish Red and White Setter or Barbet? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

How the two breeds compare

The Irish Red and White Setter is the taller dog. An average male stands 64 cm (25.2 in) at the shoulder, 8.8 cm (3.4 in) more than an average male Barbet at 55 cm (21.8 in).

They weigh about the same: 23 kg (50 lb) for an average male Barbet and 23 kg (51 lb) for a Irish Red and White Setter.

Both are in the Sporting group.

Their life expectancy is similar (12–14 years and 11–15 years).

Barbet vs Irish Red and White Setter: questions

Which is bigger, the Barbet or the Irish Red and White Setter?

The Irish Red and White Setter is the taller dog. An average male stands 64 cm (25.2 in) at the shoulder, 8.8 cm (3.4 in) more than an average male Barbet at 55 cm (21.8 in).

How much does a Barbet weigh compared with an Irish Red and White Setter?

A male Barbet weighs 16–30 kg (35–65 lb) and a female 16–30 kg (35–65 lb). A male Irish Red and White Setter weighs 19–27 kg (42–60 lb) and a female 16–23 kg (35–50 lb).
