Kai Ken vs Standard Schnauzer: size comparison

Kai Ken or Standard Schnauzer? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

Average male and female of each breed, drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person. Solid line = male, dashed = female.

How the two breeds compare

The Kai Ken and the Standard Schnauzer are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Kai Ken stands 48 cm (19 in) and an average male Standard Schnauzer 48 cm (19 in).

By weight, the Standard Schnauzer is about 22% heavier: 19 kg (42 lb) for an average male against 16 kg (35 lb) for the Kai Ken.

Both are in the Working group.

The Standard Schnauzer tends to live longer: 13–16 years against 12–15 years.

Kai Ken vs Standard Schnauzer: questions

Which is bigger, the Kai Ken or the Standard Schnauzer?

The Kai Ken and the Standard Schnauzer are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Kai Ken stands 48 cm (19 in) and an average male Standard Schnauzer 48 cm (19 in).

How much does a Kai Ken weigh compared with a Standard Schnauzer?

A male Kai Ken weighs 14–18 kg (30–40 lb) and a female 12–16 kg (25–35 lb). A male Standard Schnauzer weighs 16–23 kg (35–50 lb) and a female 14–21 kg (30–45 lb).
