Chinese Crested vs Miniature Pinscher: size comparison

Chinese Crested or Miniature Pinscher? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

How the two breeds compare

The Chinese Crested and the Miniature Pinscher are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Chinese Crested stands 31 cm (12 in) and an average male Miniature Pinscher 29 cm (11.3 in).

By weight, the Chinese Crested is about 11% heavier: 4.5 kg (9.9 lb) for an average male against 4.1 kg (8.9 lb) for the Miniature Pinscher.

Both are in the Toy group.

The Chinese Crested tends to live longer: 13–18 years against 12–16 years.

Chinese Crested vs Miniature Pinscher: questions

Which is bigger, the Chinese Crested or the Miniature Pinscher?

The Chinese Crested and the Miniature Pinscher are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Chinese Crested stands 31 cm (12 in) and an average male Miniature Pinscher 29 cm (11.3 in).

How much does a Chinese Crested weigh compared with a Miniature Pinscher?

A male Chinese Crested weighs 3.6–5.4 kg (7.9–12 lb) and a female 3.6–5.4 kg (7.9–12 lb). A male Miniature Pinscher weighs 3.6–4.5 kg (7.9–9.9 lb) and a female 3.6–4.5 kg (7.9–9.9 lb).
