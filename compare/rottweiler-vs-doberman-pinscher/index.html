Rottweiler vs Doberman Pinscher: size comparison

Doberman Pinscher or Rottweiler? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

Average male and female of each breed, drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person. Solid line = male, dashed = female.

How the two breeds compare

The Doberman Pinscher is the taller dog. An average male stands 69 cm (27 in) at the shoulder, 3.8 cm (1.5 in) more than an average male Rottweiler at 65 cm (25.5 in).

By weight, the Rottweiler is about 31% heavier: 52 kg (115 lb) for an average male against 40 kg (88 lb) for the Doberman Pinscher.

Both are in the Working group and come from Germany.

The Doberman Pinscher tends to live longer: 10–12 years against 9–10 years.

Rottweiler vs Doberman Pinscher: questions

Which is bigger, the Rottweiler or the Doberman Pinscher?

The Doberman Pinscher is the taller dog. An average male stands 69 cm (27 in) at the shoulder, 3.8 cm (1.5 in) more than an average male Rottweiler at 65 cm (25.5 in).

How much does a Rottweiler weigh compared with a Doberman Pinscher?

A male Rottweiler weighs 43–61 kg (95–134 lb) and a female 37–46 kg (80–100 lb). A male Doberman Pinscher weighs 34–46 kg (75–100 lb) and a female 27–41 kg (60–90 lb).
