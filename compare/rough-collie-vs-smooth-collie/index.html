Rough Collie vs Smooth Collie: size comparison

Rough Collie or Smooth Collie? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

Average male and female of each breed, drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person. Solid line = male, dashed = female.

How the two breeds compare

The Rough Collie and the Smooth Collie are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Rough Collie stands 64 cm (25 in) and an average male Smooth Collie 64 cm (25 in).

They weigh about the same: 31 kg (67 lb) for an average male Rough Collie and 31 kg (67 lb) for a Smooth Collie.

Both are in the Herding group and come from Scotland.

Their life expectancy is similar (12–14 years and 12–14 years).
