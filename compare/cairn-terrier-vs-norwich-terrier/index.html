Cairn Terrier vs Norwich Terrier: size comparison

How the two breeds compare

The Cairn Terrier and the Norwich Terrier are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Cairn Terrier stands 26 cm (10 in) and an average male Norwich Terrier 26 cm (10 in).

By weight, the Cairn Terrier is about 19% heavier: 6.4 kg (14 lb) for an average male against 5.4 kg (12 lb) for the Norwich Terrier.

Both are in the Terrier group.

Their life expectancy is similar (13–15 years and 12–15 years).
