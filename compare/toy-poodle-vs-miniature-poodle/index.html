Toy Poodle vs Miniature Poodle: size comparison

How the two breeds compare

The Miniature Poodle is the taller dog. An average male stands 32 cm (12.5 in) at the shoulder, 8.8 cm (3.4 in) more than an average male Toy Poodle at 23 cm (9.1 in), about 1.4× the height.

By weight, the Miniature Poodle is about 2.5× as heavy: 5.7 kg (12 lb) for an average male against 2.3 kg (5 lb) for the Toy Poodle.

The Toy Poodle belongs to the Toy group and comes from France/Germany; the Miniature Poodle is in the Non-Sporting group, from France/Germany.

Their life expectancy is similar (10–18 years and 10–18 years).

For air travel, the Toy Poodle fits the 8 kg cabin limit for dog and carrier; the Miniature Poodle only sometimes does.

Toy Poodle vs Miniature Poodle: questions

Which is bigger, the Toy Poodle or the Miniature Poodle?

The Miniature Poodle is the taller dog. An average male stands 32 cm (12.5 in) at the shoulder, 8.8 cm (3.4 in) more than an average male Toy Poodle at 23 cm (9.1 in), about 1.4× the height.

How much does a Toy Poodle weigh compared with a Miniature Poodle?

A male Toy Poodle weighs 1.8–2.7 kg (4–6 lb) and a female 1.8–2.7 kg (4–6 lb). A male Miniature Poodle weighs 4.5–6.8 kg (9.9–15 lb) and a female 4.5–6.8 kg (9.9–15 lb).
