Russian Toy vs Toy Poodle: size comparison

Russian Toy or Toy Poodle? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

Average male and female of each breed, drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person. Solid line = male, dashed = female.

How the two breeds compare

The Russian Toy and the Toy Poodle are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Russian Toy stands 24 cm (9.5 in) and an average male Toy Poodle 23 cm (9.1 in).

They weigh about the same: 2.2 kg (4.7 lb) for an average male Russian Toy and 2.3 kg (5 lb) for a Toy Poodle.

Both are in the Toy group.

The Toy Poodle tends to live longer: 10–18 years against 12–14 years.

Russian Toy vs Toy Poodle: questions

Which is bigger, the Russian Toy or the Toy Poodle?

The Russian Toy and the Toy Poodle are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Russian Toy stands 24 cm (9.5 in) and an average male Toy Poodle 23 cm (9.1 in).

How much does a Russian Toy weigh compared with a Toy Poodle?

A male Russian Toy weighs 1.4–2.9 kg (3.1–6.4 lb) and a female 1.4–2.9 kg (3.1–6.4 lb). A male Toy Poodle weighs 1.8–2.7 kg (4–6 lb) and a female 1.8–2.7 kg (4–6 lb).
