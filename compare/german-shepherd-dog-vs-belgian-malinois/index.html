German Shepherd Dog vs Belgian Malinois: size comparison

How the two breeds compare

The German Shepherd Dog and the Belgian Malinois are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male German Shepherd Dog stands 64 cm (25 in) and an average male Belgian Malinois 64 cm (25 in).

By weight, the German Shepherd Dog is about 11% heavier: 35 kg (78 lb) for an average male against 32 kg (70 lb) for the Belgian Malinois.

Both are in the Herding group.

The Belgian Malinois tends to live longer: 14–16 years against 12–14 years.

German Shepherd Dog vs Belgian Malinois: questions

Which is bigger, the German Shepherd Dog or the Belgian Malinois?

The German Shepherd Dog and the Belgian Malinois are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male German Shepherd Dog stands 64 cm (25 in) and an average male Belgian Malinois 64 cm (25 in).

How much does a German Shepherd Dog weigh compared with a Belgian Malinois?

A male German Shepherd Dog weighs 30–41 kg (65–90 lb) and a female 23–32 kg (50–71 lb). A male Belgian Malinois weighs 27–37 kg (60–80 lb) and a female 18–27 kg (40–60 lb).
