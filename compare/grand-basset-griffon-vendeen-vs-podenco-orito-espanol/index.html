How the two breeds compare

The Podenco Orito Español is the taller dog. An average male stands 45 cm (17.7 in) at the shoulder, 2.5 cm (1 in) more than an average male Grand Basset Griffon Vendéen at 43 cm (16.7 in).

They weigh about the same: 19 kg (42 lb) for an average male Grand Basset Griffon Vendéen and 19 kg (42 lb) for a Podenco Orito Español.

Both are in the Hound group.

The Grand Basset Griffon Vendéen tends to live longer: 13–15 years against 12–14 years.

Grand Basset Griffon Vendéen vs Podenco Orito Español: questions

Which is bigger, the Grand Basset Griffon Vendéen or the Podenco Orito Español?

The Podenco Orito Español is the taller dog. An average male stands 45 cm (17.7 in) at the shoulder, 2.5 cm (1 in) more than an average male Grand Basset Griffon Vendéen at 43 cm (16.7 in).

How much does a Grand Basset Griffon Vendéen weigh compared with a Podenco Orito Español?

A male Grand Basset Griffon Vendéen weighs 18–21 kg (40–45 lb) and a female 18–21 kg (40–45 lb). A male Podenco Orito Español weighs 16–22 kg (35–49 lb) and a female 14–20 kg (31–44 lb).
