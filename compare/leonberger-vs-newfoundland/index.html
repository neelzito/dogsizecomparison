Leonberger vs Newfoundland: size comparison

Leonberger or Newfoundland? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

Average male and female of each breed, drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person. Solid line = male, dashed = female.

How the two breeds compare

The Leonberger is the taller dog. An average male stands 76 cm (29.7 in) at the shoulder, 4.5 cm (1.8 in) more than an average male Newfoundland at 71 cm (28 in).

They weigh about the same: 64 kg (140 lb) for an average male Leonberger and 64 kg (140 lb) for a Newfoundland.

Both are in the Working group.

The Newfoundland tends to live longer: 9–10 years against 7 years.

Leonberger vs Newfoundland: questions

Which is bigger, the Leonberger or the Newfoundland?

The Leonberger is the taller dog. An average male stands 76 cm (29.7 in) at the shoulder, 4.5 cm (1.8 in) more than an average male Newfoundland at 71 cm (28 in).

How much does a Leonberger weigh compared with a Newfoundland?

A male Leonberger weighs 50–77 kg (110–170 lb) and a female 41–64 kg (90–140 lb). A male Newfoundland weighs 59–68 kg (130–150 lb) and a female 46–55 kg (100–120 lb).
