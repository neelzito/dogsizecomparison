Eurasier vs Standard Poodle: size comparison

Standard Poodle or Eurasier? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

Average male and female of each breed, drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person. Solid line = male, dashed = female.

How the two breeds compare

The Standard Poodle is the taller dog. An average male stands 58 cm (22.6 in) at the shoulder, 2.8 cm (1.1 in) more than an average male Eurasier at 55 cm (21.6 in).

By weight, the Standard Poodle is about 18% heavier: 30 kg (65 lb) for an average male against 25 kg (55 lb) for the Eurasier.

Both are in the Non-Sporting group.

Their life expectancy is similar (12–16 years and 10–18 years).

Eurasier vs Standard Poodle: questions

Which is bigger, the Eurasier or the Standard Poodle?

The Standard Poodle is the taller dog. An average male stands 58 cm (22.6 in) at the shoulder, 2.8 cm (1.1 in) more than an average male Eurasier at 55 cm (21.6 in).

How much does a Eurasier weigh compared with a Standard Poodle?

A male Eurasier weighs 18–32 kg (40–71 lb) and a female 18–32 kg (40–71 lb). A male Standard Poodle weighs 27–32 kg (60–71 lb) and a female 18–23 kg (40–50 lb).
