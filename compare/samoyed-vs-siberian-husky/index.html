Samoyed vs Siberian Husky: size comparison

Samoyed or Siberian Husky? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

How the two breeds compare

The Samoyed and the Siberian Husky are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Samoyed stands 57 cm (22.2 in) and an average male Siberian Husky 57 cm (22.2 in).

They weigh about the same: 25 kg (55 lb) for an average male Samoyed and 24 kg (52 lb) for a Siberian Husky.

Both are in the Working group and come from Russia.

Their life expectancy is similar (12–14 years and 12–14 years).

Samoyed vs Siberian Husky: questions

Which is bigger, the Samoyed or the Siberian Husky?

The Samoyed and the Siberian Husky are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Samoyed stands 57 cm (22.2 in) and an average male Siberian Husky 57 cm (22.2 in).

How much does a Samoyed weigh compared with a Siberian Husky?

A male Samoyed weighs 21–30 kg (45–65 lb) and a female 16–23 kg (35–50 lb). A male Siberian Husky weighs 21–27 kg (45–60 lb) and a female 16–23 kg (35–50 lb).
