Afghan Hound vs Sloughi: size comparison

Sloughi or Afghan Hound? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

Average male and female of each breed, drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person. Solid line = male, dashed = female.

Open in the comparison tool →

How the two breeds compare

The Sloughi is the taller dog. An average male stands 70 cm (27.5 in) at the shoulder, 3.8 cm (1.5 in) more than an average male Afghan Hound at 66 cm (26 in).

They weigh about the same: 25 kg (55 lb) for an average male Afghan Hound and 24 kg (52 lb) for a Sloughi.

Both are in the Hound group.

The Afghan Hound tends to live longer: 12–18 years against 10–15 years.

Afghan Hound vs Sloughi: questions

Which is bigger, the Afghan Hound or the Sloughi?

The Sloughi is the taller dog. An average male stands 70 cm (27.5 in) at the shoulder, 3.8 cm (1.5 in) more than an average male Afghan Hound at 66 cm (26 in).

How much does an Afghan Hound weigh compared with a Sloughi?

A male Afghan Hound weighs 23–27 kg (50–60 lb) and a female 23–27 kg (50–60 lb). A male Sloughi weighs 18–30 kg (40–65 lb) and a female 18–30 kg (40–65 lb).
