How the two breeds compare

The Bernese Mountain Dog and the Bernedoodle (Standard) are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Bernese Mountain Dog stands 67 cm (26.3 in) and an average male Bernedoodle (Standard) 66 cm (26 in).

By weight, the Bernese Mountain Dog is about 21% heavier: 44 kg (98 lb) for an average male against 37 kg (80 lb) for the Bernedoodle (Standard).

The Bernese Mountain Dog belongs to the Working group and comes from Switzerland; the Bernedoodle (Standard) is in the designer & mixed category, from the United States/Canada.

The Bernedoodle (Standard) tends to live longer: 12–15 years against 7–10 years.

Bernese Mountain Dog vs Bernedoodle (Standard): questions

Which is bigger, the Bernese Mountain Dog or the Bernedoodle (Standard)?

The Bernese Mountain Dog and the Bernedoodle (Standard) are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Bernese Mountain Dog stands 67 cm (26.3 in) and an average male Bernedoodle (Standard) 66 cm (26 in).

How much does a Bernese Mountain Dog weigh compared with a Bernedoodle (Standard)?

A male Bernese Mountain Dog weighs 37–52 kg (80–115 lb) and a female 32–43 kg (71–95 lb). A male Bernedoodle (Standard) weighs 32–41 kg (71–90 lb) and a female 27–36 kg (60–79 lb).
