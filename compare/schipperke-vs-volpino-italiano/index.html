Schipperke vs Volpino Italiano: size comparison

Schipperke or Volpino Italiano? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

Average male and female of each breed, drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person. Solid line = male, dashed = female.

Open in the comparison tool →

How the two breeds compare

The Schipperke is the taller dog. An average male stands 31 cm (12 in) at the shoulder, 2 cm (0.8 in) more than an average male Volpino Italiano at 29 cm (11.2 in).

They weigh about the same: 5.9 kg (13 lb) for an average male Schipperke and 5.9 kg (13 lb) for a Volpino Italiano.

Both are in the Non-Sporting group.

Their life expectancy is similar (12–16 years and 12–15 years).

Schipperke vs Volpino Italiano: questions

Which is bigger, the Schipperke or the Volpino Italiano?

The Schipperke is the taller dog. An average male stands 31 cm (12 in) at the shoulder, 2 cm (0.8 in) more than an average male Volpino Italiano at 29 cm (11.2 in).

How much does a Schipperke weigh compared with a Volpino Italiano?

A male Schipperke weighs 4.5–7.3 kg (9.9–16 lb) and a female 4.5–7.3 kg (9.9–16 lb). A male Volpino Italiano weighs 4.5–7.3 kg (9.9–16 lb) and a female 3.6–5.4 kg (7.9–12 lb).
