Landseer (European) vs Leonberger: size comparison

Landseer (European) or Leonberger? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

Average male and female of each breed, drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person. Solid line = male, dashed = female.

How the two breeds compare

The Landseer (European) and the Leonberger are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Landseer (European) stands 76 cm (29.9 in) and an average male Leonberger 76 cm (29.7 in).

They weigh about the same: 60 kg (132 lb) for an average male Landseer (European) and 64 kg (140 lb) for a Leonberger.

Both are in the Working group and come from Germany.

The Landseer (European) tends to live longer: 10–12 years against 7 years.

Landseer (European) vs Leonberger: questions

Which is bigger, the Landseer (European) or the Leonberger?

The Landseer (European) and the Leonberger are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Landseer (European) stands 76 cm (29.9 in) and an average male Leonberger 76 cm (29.7 in).

How much does a Landseer (European) weigh compared with a Leonberger?

A male Landseer (European) weighs 50–70 kg (110–154 lb) and a female 45–60 kg (99–132 lb). A male Leonberger weighs 50–77 kg (110–170 lb) and a female 41–64 kg (90–140 lb).
