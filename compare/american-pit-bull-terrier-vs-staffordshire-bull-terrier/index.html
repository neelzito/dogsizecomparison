How the two breeds compare

The American Pit Bull Terrier is the taller dog. An average male stands 50 cm (19.5 in) at the shoulder, 11.5 cm (4.5 in) more than an average male Staffordshire Bull Terrier at 38 cm (15 in), about 1.3× the height.

By weight, the American Pit Bull Terrier is about 46% heavier: 22 kg (48 lb) for an average male against 15 kg (33 lb) for the Staffordshire Bull Terrier.

Both are in the Terrier group.

Their life expectancy is similar (12–14 years and 12–15 years).

American Pit Bull Terrier vs Staffordshire Bull Terrier: questions

Which is bigger, the American Pit Bull Terrier or the Staffordshire Bull Terrier?

The American Pit Bull Terrier is the taller dog. An average male stands 50 cm (19.5 in) at the shoulder, 11.5 cm (4.5 in) more than an average male Staffordshire Bull Terrier at 38 cm (15 in), about 1.3× the height.

How much does an American Pit Bull Terrier weigh compared with a Staffordshire Bull Terrier?

A male American Pit Bull Terrier weighs 16–27 kg (35–60 lb) and a female 14–23 kg (30–50 lb). A male Staffordshire Bull Terrier weighs 13–17 kg (28–37 lb) and a female 11–16 kg (24–34 lb).
