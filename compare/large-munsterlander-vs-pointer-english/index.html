Large Münsterländer vs Pointer (English): size comparison

How the two breeds compare

The Pointer (English) is the taller dog. An average male stands 67 cm (26.5 in) at the shoulder, 4.3 cm (1.7 in) more than an average male Large Münsterländer at 63 cm (24.8 in).

They weigh about the same: 27 kg (60 lb) for an average male Large Münsterländer and 30 kg (65 lb) for a Pointer (English).

Both are in the Sporting group.

Their life expectancy is similar (12–14 years and 12–15 years).

Large Münsterländer vs Pointer (English): questions

Which is bigger, the Large Münsterländer or the Pointer (English)?

The Pointer (English) is the taller dog. An average male stands 67 cm (26.5 in) at the shoulder, 4.3 cm (1.7 in) more than an average male Large Münsterländer at 63 cm (24.8 in).

How much does a Large Münsterländer weigh compared with a Pointer (English)?

A male Large Münsterländer weighs 23–32 kg (50–71 lb) and a female 23–32 kg (50–71 lb). A male Pointer (English) weighs 25–34 kg (55–75 lb) and a female 21–30 kg (45–65 lb).
