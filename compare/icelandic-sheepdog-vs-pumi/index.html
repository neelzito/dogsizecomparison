Icelandic Sheepdog vs Pumi: size comparison

Icelandic Sheepdog or Pumi? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

Average male and female of each breed, drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person. Solid line = male, dashed = female.

How the two breeds compare

The Icelandic Sheepdog and the Pumi are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Icelandic Sheepdog stands 46 cm (17.9 in) and an average male Pumi 44 cm (17.2 in).

They weigh about the same: 14 kg (30 lb) for an average male Icelandic Sheepdog and 13 kg (28 lb) for a Pumi.

Both are in the Herding group.

Their life expectancy is similar (12–14 years and 12–13 years).

Icelandic Sheepdog vs Pumi: questions

Which is bigger, the Icelandic Sheepdog or the Pumi?

The Icelandic Sheepdog and the Pumi are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Icelandic Sheepdog stands 46 cm (17.9 in) and an average male Pumi 44 cm (17.2 in).

How much does an Icelandic Sheepdog weigh compared with a Pumi?

A male Icelandic Sheepdog weighs 14 kg (30 lb) and a female 12 kg (25 lb). A male Pumi weighs 12–13 kg (26–29 lb) and a female 10–11 kg (22–24 lb).
