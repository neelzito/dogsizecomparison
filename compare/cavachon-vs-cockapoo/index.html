Cavachon vs Cockapoo: size comparison

Cavachon or Cockapoo? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

Average male and female of each breed, drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person. Solid line = male, dashed = female.

How the two breeds compare

The Cavachon and the Cockapoo are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Cavachon stands 32 cm (12.4 in) and an average male Cockapoo 32 cm (12.4 in).

They weigh about the same: 7.5 kg (17 lb) for an average male Cavachon and 8.2 kg (18 lb) for a Cockapoo.

Both are in the designer & mixed category and come from the United States.

The Cockapoo tends to live longer: 13–18 years against 12–15 years.

Cavachon vs Cockapoo: questions

Which is bigger, the Cavachon or the Cockapoo?

The Cavachon and the Cockapoo are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Cavachon stands 32 cm (12.4 in) and an average male Cockapoo 32 cm (12.4 in).

How much does a Cavachon weigh compared with a Cockapoo?

A male Cavachon weighs 6–9 kg (13–20 lb) and a female 5.5–8 kg (12–18 lb). A male Cockapoo weighs 5.4–11 kg (12–24 lb) and a female 5–10 kg (11–22 lb).
