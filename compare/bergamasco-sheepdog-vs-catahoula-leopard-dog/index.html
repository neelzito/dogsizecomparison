How the two breeds compare

The Bergamasco Sheepdog and the Catahoula Leopard Dog are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Bergamasco Sheepdog stands 60 cm (23.4 in) and an average male Catahoula Leopard Dog 59 cm (23 in).

They weigh about the same: 35 kg (77 lb) for an average male Bergamasco Sheepdog and 33 kg (72 lb) for a Catahoula Leopard Dog.

Both are in the Herding group.

The Bergamasco Sheepdog tends to live longer: 13–15 years against 10–14 years.
