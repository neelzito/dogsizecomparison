Norwegian Lundehund vs Portuguese Podengo Pequeno: size comparison

Norwegian Lundehund or Portuguese Podengo Pequeno? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

Average male and female of each breed, drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person. Solid line = male, dashed = female.

How the two breeds compare

The Norwegian Lundehund is the taller dog. An average male stands 36 cm (14 in) at the shoulder, 10 cm (3.9 in) more than an average male Portuguese Podengo Pequeno at 26 cm (10 in), about 1.4× the height.

By weight, the Norwegian Lundehund is about 50% heavier: 7.5 kg (17 lb) for an average male against 5 kg (11 lb) for the Portuguese Podengo Pequeno.

Both are in the Hound group.

Their life expectancy is similar (12–15 years and 12–15 years).

For air travel, the Portuguese Podengo Pequeno fits the 8 kg cabin limit for dog and carrier; the Norwegian Lundehund only sometimes does.

Norwegian Lundehund vs Portuguese Podengo Pequeno: questions

Which is bigger, the Norwegian Lundehund or the Portuguese Podengo Pequeno?

The Norwegian Lundehund is the taller dog. An average male stands 36 cm (14 in) at the shoulder, 10 cm (3.9 in) more than an average male Portuguese Podengo Pequeno at 26 cm (10 in), about 1.4× the height.

How much does a Norwegian Lundehund weigh compared with a Portuguese Podengo Pequeno?

A male Norwegian Lundehund weighs 6.8–8.2 kg (15–18 lb) and a female 5.4–6.8 kg (12–15 lb). A male Portuguese Podengo Pequeno weighs 4.1–5.9 kg (9–13 lb) and a female 4.1–5.9 kg (9–13 lb).
