How the two breeds compare

The Standard Poodle and the Labradoodle (Standard) are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Standard Poodle stands 58 cm (22.6 in) and an average male Labradoodle (Standard) 57 cm (22.4 in).

By weight, the Standard Poodle is about 11% heavier: 30 kg (65 lb) for an average male against 27 kg (58 lb) for the Labradoodle (Standard).

The Standard Poodle belongs to the Non-Sporting group and comes from France/Germany; the Labradoodle (Standard) is in the designer & mixed category, from Australia.

The Standard Poodle tends to live longer: 10–18 years against 12–14 years.

Standard Poodle vs Labradoodle (Standard): questions

Which is bigger, the Standard Poodle or the Labradoodle (Standard)?

The Standard Poodle and the Labradoodle (Standard) are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Standard Poodle stands 58 cm (22.6 in) and an average male Labradoodle (Standard) 57 cm (22.4 in).

How much does a Standard Poodle weigh compared with a Labradoodle (Standard)?

A male Standard Poodle weighs 27–32 kg (60–71 lb) and a female 18–23 kg (40–50 lb). A male Labradoodle (Standard) weighs 23–30 kg (51–66 lb) and a female 18–25 kg (40–55 lb).
