Bolognese vs Miniature Pinscher: size comparison

Miniature Pinscher or Bolognese? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

Average male and female of each breed, drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person. Solid line = male, dashed = female.

How the two breeds compare

The Bolognese and the Miniature Pinscher are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Bolognese stands 28 cm (11 in) and an average male Miniature Pinscher 29 cm (11.3 in).

By weight, the Miniature Pinscher is about 13% heavier: 4.1 kg (8.9 lb) for an average male against 3.6 kg (7.9 lb) for the Bolognese.

Both are in the Toy group.

The Miniature Pinscher tends to live longer: 12–16 years against 12–14 years.

Bolognese vs Miniature Pinscher: questions

Which is bigger, the Bolognese or the Miniature Pinscher?

The Bolognese and the Miniature Pinscher are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Bolognese stands 28 cm (11 in) and an average male Miniature Pinscher 29 cm (11.3 in).

How much does a Bolognese weigh compared with a Miniature Pinscher?

A male Bolognese weighs 2.7–4.5 kg (6–9.9 lb) and a female 2.7–4.5 kg (6–9.9 lb). A male Miniature Pinscher weighs 3.6–4.5 kg (7.9–9.9 lb) and a female 3.6–4.5 kg (7.9–9.9 lb).
