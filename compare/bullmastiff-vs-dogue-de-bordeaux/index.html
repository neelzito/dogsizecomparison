Bullmastiff vs Dogue de Bordeaux: size comparison

Bullmastiff or Dogue de Bordeaux? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

Average male and female of each breed, drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person. Solid line = male, dashed = female.

How the two breeds compare

The Bullmastiff is the taller dog. An average male stands 66 cm (26 in) at the shoulder, 2.5 cm (1 in) more than an average male Dogue de Bordeaux at 64 cm (25 in).

They weigh about the same: 55 kg (120 lb) for an average male Bullmastiff and 58 kg (127 lb) for a Dogue de Bordeaux.

Both are in the Working group.

The Bullmastiff tends to live longer: 7–9 years against 5–8 years.

Bullmastiff vs Dogue de Bordeaux: questions

Which is bigger, the Bullmastiff or the Dogue de Bordeaux?

The Bullmastiff is the taller dog. An average male stands 66 cm (26 in) at the shoulder, 2.5 cm (1 in) more than an average male Dogue de Bordeaux at 64 cm (25 in).

How much does a Bullmastiff weigh compared with a Dogue de Bordeaux?

A male Bullmastiff weighs 50–59 kg (110–130 lb) and a female 46–55 kg (100–120 lb). A male Dogue de Bordeaux weighs 50–65 kg (110–143 lb) and a female 45–57 kg (99–126 lb).
