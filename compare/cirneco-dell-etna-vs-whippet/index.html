Cirneco dell'Etna vs Whippet: size comparison

Whippet or Cirneco dell'Etna? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

Average male and female of each breed, drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person. Solid line = male, dashed = female.

How the two breeds compare

The Whippet is the taller dog. An average male stands 52 cm (20.6 in) at the shoulder, 4 cm (1.6 in) more than an average male Cirneco dell'Etna at 48 cm (19 in).

By weight, the Whippet is about 37% heavier: 15 kg (33 lb) for an average male against 11 kg (24 lb) for the Cirneco dell'Etna.

Both are in the Hound group.

The Cirneco dell'Etna tends to live longer: 15–16 years against 12–15 years.

Cirneco dell'Etna vs Whippet: questions

Which is bigger, the Cirneco dell'Etna or the Whippet?

The Whippet is the taller dog. An average male stands 52 cm (20.6 in) at the shoulder, 4 cm (1.6 in) more than an average male Cirneco dell'Etna at 48 cm (19 in).

How much does a Cirneco dell'Etna weigh compared with a Whippet?

A male Cirneco dell'Etna weighs 9.1–13 kg (20–28 lb) and a female 8.6–12 kg (19–25 lb). A male Whippet weighs 12–18 kg (25–40 lb) and a female 12–18 kg (25–40 lb).
