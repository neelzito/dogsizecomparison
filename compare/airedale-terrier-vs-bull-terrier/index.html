Airedale Terrier vs Bull Terrier: size comparison

Airedale Terrier or Bull Terrier? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

Average male and female of each breed, drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person. Solid line = male, dashed = female.

How the two breeds compare

The Airedale Terrier is the taller dog. An average male stands 59 cm (23 in) at the shoulder, 3.8 cm (1.5 in) more than an average male Bull Terrier at 55 cm (21.6 in).

They weigh about the same: 27 kg (60 lb) for an average male Airedale Terrier and 27 kg (60 lb) for a Bull Terrier.

Both are in the Terrier group and come from England.

Their life expectancy is similar (11–14 years and 12–13 years).

Airedale Terrier vs Bull Terrier: questions

Which is bigger, the Airedale Terrier or the Bull Terrier?

The Airedale Terrier is the taller dog. An average male stands 59 cm (23 in) at the shoulder, 3.8 cm (1.5 in) more than an average male Bull Terrier at 55 cm (21.6 in).

How much does an Airedale Terrier weigh compared with a Bull Terrier?

A male Airedale Terrier weighs 23–32 kg (50–71 lb) and a female 23–32 kg (50–71 lb). A male Bull Terrier weighs 23–32 kg (50–71 lb) and a female 23–32 kg (50–71 lb).
