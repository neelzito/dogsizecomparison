Flat-Coated Retriever vs Golden Retriever: size comparison

Flat-Coated Retriever or Golden Retriever? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

Average male and female of each breed, drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person. Solid line = male, dashed = female.

How the two breeds compare

The Flat-Coated Retriever and the Golden Retriever are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Flat-Coated Retriever stands 60 cm (23.7 in) and an average male Golden Retriever 60 cm (23.5 in).

They weigh about the same: 30 kg (65 lb) for an average male Flat-Coated Retriever and 32 kg (70 lb) for a Golden Retriever.

Both are in the Sporting group.

The Golden Retriever tends to live longer: 10–12 years against 8–10 years.
