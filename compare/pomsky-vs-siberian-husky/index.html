Pomsky vs Siberian Husky: size comparison

Siberian Husky or Pomsky? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

Average male and female of each breed, drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person. Solid line = male, dashed = female.

How the two breeds compare

The Siberian Husky is the taller dog. An average male stands 57 cm (22.2 in) at the shoulder, 25 cm (9.8 in) more than an average male Pomsky at 32 cm (12.4 in), about 1.8× the height.

By weight, the Siberian Husky is about 2.1× as heavy: 24 kg (52 lb) for an average male against 12 kg (25 lb) for the Pomsky.

The Pomsky belongs to the designer & mixed category and comes from the United States; the Siberian Husky is in the Working group, from Russia (Siberia).

The Pomsky tends to live longer: 13–15 years against 12–14 years.

Pomsky vs Siberian Husky: questions

Which is bigger, the Pomsky or the Siberian Husky?

The Siberian Husky is the taller dog. An average male stands 57 cm (22.2 in) at the shoulder, 25 cm (9.8 in) more than an average male Pomsky at 32 cm (12.4 in), about 1.8× the height.

How much does a Pomsky weigh compared with a Siberian Husky?

A male Pomsky weighs 9–14 kg (20–31 lb) and a female 7–12 kg (15–26 lb). A male Siberian Husky weighs 21–27 kg (45–60 lb) and a female 16–23 kg (35–50 lb).
