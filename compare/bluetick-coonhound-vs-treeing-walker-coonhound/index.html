How the two breeds compare

The Bluetick Coonhound and the Treeing Walker Coonhound are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Bluetick Coonhound stands 62 cm (24.5 in) and an average male Treeing Walker Coonhound 62 cm (24.5 in).

By weight, the Bluetick Coonhound is about 13% heavier: 31 kg (68 lb) for an average male against 27 kg (60 lb) for the Treeing Walker Coonhound.

Both are in the Hound group and come from the United States.

The Treeing Walker Coonhound tends to live longer: 12–13 years against 11–12 years.

Bluetick Coonhound vs Treeing Walker Coonhound: questions

Which is bigger, the Bluetick Coonhound or the Treeing Walker Coonhound?

The Bluetick Coonhound and the Treeing Walker Coonhound are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Bluetick Coonhound stands 62 cm (24.5 in) and an average male Treeing Walker Coonhound 62 cm (24.5 in).

How much does a Bluetick Coonhound weigh compared with a Treeing Walker Coonhound?

A male Bluetick Coonhound weighs 25–37 kg (55–80 lb) and a female 21–30 kg (45–65 lb). A male Treeing Walker Coonhound weighs 23–32 kg (50–71 lb) and a female 23–32 kg (50–71 lb).
