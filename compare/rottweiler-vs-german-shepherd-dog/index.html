Rottweiler vs German Shepherd Dog: size comparison

Rottweiler or German Shepherd Dog? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

How the two breeds compare

The Rottweiler and the German Shepherd Dog are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Rottweiler stands 65 cm (25.5 in) and an average male German Shepherd Dog 64 cm (25 in).

By weight, the Rottweiler is about 48% heavier: 52 kg (115 lb) for an average male against 35 kg (78 lb) for the German Shepherd Dog.

The Rottweiler belongs to the Working group and comes from Germany; the German Shepherd Dog is in the Herding group, from Germany.

The German Shepherd Dog tends to live longer: 12–14 years against 9–10 years.

Rottweiler vs German Shepherd Dog: questions

Which is bigger, the Rottweiler or the German Shepherd Dog?

The Rottweiler and the German Shepherd Dog are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Rottweiler stands 65 cm (25.5 in) and an average male German Shepherd Dog 64 cm (25 in).

How much does a Rottweiler weigh compared with a German Shepherd Dog?

A male Rottweiler weighs 43–61 kg (95–134 lb) and a female 37–46 kg (80–100 lb). A male German Shepherd Dog weighs 30–41 kg (65–90 lb) and a female 23–32 kg (50–71 lb).
