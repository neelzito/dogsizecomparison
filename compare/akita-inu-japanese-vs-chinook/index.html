Akita Inu (Japanese) vs Chinook: size comparison

Akita Inu (Japanese) or Chinook? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

Average male and female of each breed, drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person. Solid line = male, dashed = female.

How the two breeds compare

The Akita Inu (Japanese) is the taller dog. An average male stands 67 cm (26.3 in) at the shoulder, 3.3 cm (1.3 in) more than an average male Chinook at 64 cm (25 in).

They weigh about the same: 32 kg (70 lb) for an average male Akita Inu (Japanese) and 33 kg (73 lb) for a Chinook.

Both are in the Working group.

The Chinook tends to live longer: 12–15 years against 10–12 years.

Akita Inu (Japanese) vs Chinook: questions

Which is bigger, the Akita Inu (Japanese) or the Chinook?

The Akita Inu (Japanese) is the taller dog. An average male stands 67 cm (26.3 in) at the shoulder, 3.3 cm (1.3 in) more than an average male Chinook at 64 cm (25 in).

How much does an Akita Inu (Japanese) weigh compared with a Chinook?

A male Akita Inu (Japanese) weighs 30–34 kg (65–75 lb) and a female 25–30 kg (55–65 lb). A male Chinook weighs 25–41 kg (55–90 lb) and a female 23–30 kg (50–65 lb).
