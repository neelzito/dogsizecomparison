Australian Terrier vs Teddy Roosevelt Terrier: size comparison

Teddy Roosevelt Terrier or Australian Terrier? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

Average male and female of each breed, drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person. Solid line = male, dashed = female.

How the two breeds compare

The Australian Terrier and the Teddy Roosevelt Terrier are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Australian Terrier stands 27 cm (10.5 in) and an average male Teddy Roosevelt Terrier 28 cm (11 in).

They weigh about the same: 8 kg (18 lb) for an average male Australian Terrier and 7.5 kg (16 lb) for a Teddy Roosevelt Terrier.

Both are in the Terrier group.

The Teddy Roosevelt Terrier tends to live longer: 14–16 years against 11–15 years.

For air travel, the Teddy Roosevelt Terrier can sometimes fit the 8 kg cabin limit for dog and carrier; the Australian Terrier does not.

Australian Terrier vs Teddy Roosevelt Terrier: questions

Which is bigger, the Australian Terrier or the Teddy Roosevelt Terrier?

The Australian Terrier and the Teddy Roosevelt Terrier are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Australian Terrier stands 27 cm (10.5 in) and an average male Teddy Roosevelt Terrier 28 cm (11 in).

How much does an Australian Terrier weigh compared with a Teddy Roosevelt Terrier?

A male Australian Terrier weighs 6.8–9.1 kg (15–20 lb) and a female 6.8–9.1 kg (15–20 lb). A male Teddy Roosevelt Terrier weighs 3.6–11 kg (7.9–25 lb) and a female 3.6–11 kg (7.9–25 lb).
