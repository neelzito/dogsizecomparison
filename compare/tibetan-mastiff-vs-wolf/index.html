Tibetan Mastiff vs wolf: size comparison

How does the Tibetan Mastiff measure up to a gray wolf? Shoulder heights and weights against the Eurasian wolf and the larger Northwestern wolf, drawn to scale next to a person.

Average male and female Tibetan Mastiff next to average male wolves, drawn to scale with a 175 cm (5′9″) person. Solid line = male, dashed = female.

Open in the comparison tool →

Is the Tibetan Mastiff bigger than a wolf?

An average male Tibetan Mastiff is shorter than both wolves. By weight, it is heavier than a Eurasian wolf and about as heavy as a Northwestern wolf.

An average male Eurasian wolf, at 78 cm (30.5 in) and 47 kg (103 lb), is 6.5 cm (2.6 in) taller than an average male Tibetan Mastiff (71 cm, 55 kg) and is about 15% lighter.

An average male Northwestern wolf, at 84 cm (32.9 in) and 55 kg (121 lb), is 12.5 cm (4.9 in) taller than an average male Tibetan Mastiff (71 cm, 55 kg) and weighs about the same.

Wolves are built for travelling long distances: compared with most dogs of the same shoulder height they have longer legs, a narrower chest and bigger feet, so they often look larger than the numbers suggest. Wolf sizes also vary a lot by region; northern wolves are the biggest.

Tibetan Mastiff vs wolf: questions

Is a Tibetan Mastiff bigger than a wolf?

An average male Tibetan Mastiff is shorter than both wolves. By weight, it is heavier than a Eurasian wolf and about as heavy as a Northwestern wolf. An average male Eurasian wolf, at 78 cm (30.5 in) and 47 kg (103 lb), is 6.5 cm (2.6 in) taller than an average male Tibetan Mastiff (71 cm, 55 kg) and is about 15% lighter.

How much does a Tibetan Mastiff weigh compared with a wolf?

A male Tibetan Mastiff weighs 41–68 kg (90–150 lb) and a female 32–55 kg (71–120 lb). A male Eurasian wolf weighs 38–55 kg (84–121 lb) and a male Northwestern wolf 45–65 kg (99–143 lb).
