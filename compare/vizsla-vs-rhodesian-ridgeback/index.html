Vizsla vs Rhodesian Ridgeback: size comparison

Rhodesian Ridgeback or Vizsla? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

Average male and female of each breed, drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person. Solid line = male, dashed = female.

How the two breeds compare

The Rhodesian Ridgeback is the taller dog. An average male stands 66 cm (26 in) at the shoulder, 7.5 cm (3 in) more than an average male Vizsla at 59 cm (23 in).

By weight, the Rhodesian Ridgeback is about 48% heavier: 39 kg (85 lb) for an average male against 26 kg (57 lb) for the Vizsla.

The Vizsla belongs to the Sporting group and comes from Hungary; the Rhodesian Ridgeback is in the Hound group, from Southern Africa.

The Vizsla tends to live longer: 12–14 years against 10–12 years.

Vizsla vs Rhodesian Ridgeback: questions

Which is bigger, the Vizsla or the Rhodesian Ridgeback?

The Rhodesian Ridgeback is the taller dog. An average male stands 66 cm (26 in) at the shoulder, 7.5 cm (3 in) more than an average male Vizsla at 59 cm (23 in).

How much does a Vizsla weigh compared with a Rhodesian Ridgeback?

A male Vizsla weighs 25–27 kg (55–60 lb) and a female 20–25 kg (44–55 lb). A male Rhodesian Ridgeback weighs 39 kg (85 lb) and a female 32 kg (71 lb).
