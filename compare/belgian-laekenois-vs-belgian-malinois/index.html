Belgian Laekenois vs Belgian Malinois: size comparison

Belgian Laekenois or Belgian Malinois? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

How the two breeds compare

The Belgian Laekenois and the Belgian Malinois are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Belgian Laekenois stands 64 cm (25 in) and an average male Belgian Malinois 64 cm (25 in).

By weight, the Belgian Malinois is about 17% heavier: 32 kg (70 lb) for an average male against 27 kg (60 lb) for the Belgian Laekenois.

Both are in the Herding group and come from Belgium.

The Belgian Malinois tends to live longer: 14–16 years against 10–12 years.
