Whippet vs Italian Greyhound: size comparison

Whippet or Italian Greyhound? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

Average male and female of each breed, drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person. Solid line = male, dashed = female.

How the two breeds compare

The Whippet is the taller dog. An average male stands 52 cm (20.6 in) at the shoulder, 16.8 cm (6.6 in) more than an average male Italian Greyhound at 36 cm (14 in), about 1.5× the height.

By weight, the Whippet is about 3.1× as heavy: 15 kg (33 lb) for an average male against 4.8 kg (11 lb) for the Italian Greyhound.

The Whippet belongs to the Hound group and comes from England; the Italian Greyhound is in the Toy group, from Italy.

The Italian Greyhound tends to live longer: 14–15 years against 12–15 years.

For air travel, the Italian Greyhound fits the 8 kg cabin limit for dog and carrier; the Whippet does not.

Whippet vs Italian Greyhound: questions

Which is bigger, the Whippet or the Italian Greyhound?

The Whippet is the taller dog. An average male stands 52 cm (20.6 in) at the shoulder, 16.8 cm (6.6 in) more than an average male Italian Greyhound at 36 cm (14 in), about 1.5× the height.

How much does a Whippet weigh compared with an Italian Greyhound?

A male Whippet weighs 12–18 kg (25–40 lb) and a female 12–18 kg (25–40 lb). A male Italian Greyhound weighs 3.2–6.4 kg (7.1–14 lb) and a female 3.2–6.4 kg (7.1–14 lb).
