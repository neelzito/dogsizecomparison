German Shorthaired Pointer vs Irish Water Spaniel: size comparison

How the two breeds compare

The German Shorthaired Pointer is the taller dog. An average male stands 61 cm (24 in) at the shoulder, 2.5 cm (1 in) more than an average male Irish Water Spaniel at 59 cm (23 in).

They weigh about the same: 29 kg (63 lb) for an average male German Shorthaired Pointer and 28 kg (62 lb) for a Irish Water Spaniel.

Both are in the Sporting group.

The Irish Water Spaniel tends to live longer: 12–13 years against 10–12 years.

German Shorthaired Pointer vs Irish Water Spaniel: questions

Which is bigger, the German Shorthaired Pointer or the Irish Water Spaniel?

The German Shorthaired Pointer is the taller dog. An average male stands 61 cm (24 in) at the shoulder, 2.5 cm (1 in) more than an average male Irish Water Spaniel at 59 cm (23 in).

How much does a German Shorthaired Pointer weigh compared with an Irish Water Spaniel?

A male German Shorthaired Pointer weighs 25–32 kg (55–71 lb) and a female 21–27 kg (45–60 lb). A male Irish Water Spaniel weighs 25–31 kg (55–68 lb) and a female 21–27 kg (45–58 lb).
