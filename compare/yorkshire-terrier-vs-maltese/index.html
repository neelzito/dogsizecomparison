Yorkshire Terrier vs Maltese: size comparison

Maltese or Yorkshire Terrier? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

Average male and female of each breed, drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person. Solid line = male, dashed = female.

How the two breeds compare

The Yorkshire Terrier and the Maltese are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Yorkshire Terrier stands 19.3 cm (7.6 in) and an average male Maltese 21 cm (8.1 in).

They weigh about the same: 2.5 kg (5.5 lb) for an average male Yorkshire Terrier and 2.5 kg (5.5 lb) for a Maltese.

Both are in the Toy group.

Their life expectancy is similar (11–15 years and 12–15 years).

Yorkshire Terrier vs Maltese: questions

Which is bigger, the Yorkshire Terrier or the Maltese?

The Yorkshire Terrier and the Maltese are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Yorkshire Terrier stands 19.3 cm (7.6 in) and an average male Maltese 21 cm (8.1 in).

How much does a Yorkshire Terrier weigh compared with a Maltese?

A male Yorkshire Terrier weighs 1.8–3.2 kg (4–7.1 lb) and a female 1.8–3.2 kg (4–7.1 lb). A male Maltese weighs 1.8–3.2 kg (4–7.1 lb) and a female 1.8–3.2 kg (4–7.1 lb).
