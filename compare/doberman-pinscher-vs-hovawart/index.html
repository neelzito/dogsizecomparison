Doberman Pinscher vs Hovawart: size comparison

Doberman Pinscher or Hovawart? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

How the two breeds compare

The Doberman Pinscher is the taller dog. An average male stands 69 cm (27 in) at the shoulder, 3.8 cm (1.5 in) more than an average male Hovawart at 65 cm (25.5 in).

By weight, the Doberman Pinscher is about 13% heavier: 40 kg (88 lb) for an average male against 35 kg (78 lb) for the Hovawart.

Both are in the Working group and come from Germany.

The Hovawart tends to live longer: 10–14 years against 10–12 years.

Doberman Pinscher vs Hovawart: questions

Which is bigger, the Doberman Pinscher or the Hovawart?

The Doberman Pinscher is the taller dog. An average male stands 69 cm (27 in) at the shoulder, 3.8 cm (1.5 in) more than an average male Hovawart at 65 cm (25.5 in).

How much does a Doberman Pinscher weigh compared with a Hovawart?

A male Doberman Pinscher weighs 34–46 kg (75–100 lb) and a female 27–41 kg (60–90 lb). A male Hovawart weighs 30–41 kg (65–90 lb) and a female 30–41 kg (65–90 lb).
