Ibizan Hound vs Saluki: size comparison

Ibizan Hound or Saluki? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

How the two breeds compare

The Ibizan Hound and the Saluki are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Ibizan Hound stands 65 cm (25.5 in) and an average male Saluki 65 cm (25.5 in).

They weigh about the same: 23 kg (50 lb) for an average male Ibizan Hound and 24 kg (52 lb) for a Saluki.

Both are in the Hound group.

The Saluki tends to live longer: 10–17 years against 11–14 years.
