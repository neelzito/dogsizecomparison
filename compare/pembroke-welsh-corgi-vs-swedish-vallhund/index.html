Pembroke Welsh Corgi vs Swedish Vallhund: size comparison

Swedish Vallhund or Pembroke Welsh Corgi? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

Average male and female of each breed, drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person. Solid line = male, dashed = female.

How the two breeds compare

The Swedish Vallhund is the taller dog. An average male stands 34 cm (13.2 in) at the shoulder, 5.5 cm (2.2 in) more than an average male Pembroke Welsh Corgi at 28 cm (11 in).

They weigh about the same: 12 kg (27 lb) for an average male Pembroke Welsh Corgi and 13 kg (28 lb) for a Swedish Vallhund.

Both are in the Herding group.

The Swedish Vallhund tends to live longer: 12–15 years against 12–13 years.

Pembroke Welsh Corgi vs Swedish Vallhund: questions

Which is bigger, the Pembroke Welsh Corgi or the Swedish Vallhund?

The Swedish Vallhund is the taller dog. An average male stands 34 cm (13.2 in) at the shoulder, 5.5 cm (2.2 in) more than an average male Pembroke Welsh Corgi at 28 cm (11 in).

How much does a Pembroke Welsh Corgi weigh compared with a Swedish Vallhund?

A male Pembroke Welsh Corgi weighs 11–14 kg (24–30 lb) and a female 10–13 kg (22–28 lb). A male Swedish Vallhund weighs 9.1–16 kg (20–35 lb) and a female 9.1–16 kg (20–35 lb).
