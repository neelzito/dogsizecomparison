Papillon vs Chihuahua: size comparison

Papillon or Chihuahua? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

Average male and female of each breed, drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person. Solid line = male, dashed = female.

How the two breeds compare

The Papillon is the taller dog. An average male stands 24 cm (9.5 in) at the shoulder, 7.8 cm (3.1 in) more than an average male Chihuahua at 16.5 cm (6.5 in), about 1.5× the height.

By weight, the Papillon is about 66% heavier: 3.4 kg (7.5 lb) for an average male against 2.1 kg (4.5 lb) for the Chihuahua.

Both are in the Toy group.

Their life expectancy is similar (14–16 years and 14–16 years).

Papillon vs Chihuahua: questions

Which is bigger, the Papillon or the Chihuahua?

The Papillon is the taller dog. An average male stands 24 cm (9.5 in) at the shoulder, 7.8 cm (3.1 in) more than an average male Chihuahua at 16.5 cm (6.5 in), about 1.5× the height.

How much does a Papillon weigh compared with a Chihuahua?

A male Papillon weighs 2.3–4.5 kg (5.1–9.9 lb) and a female 2.3–4.5 kg (5.1–9.9 lb). A male Chihuahua weighs 1.4–2.7 kg (3.1–6 lb) and a female 1.4–2.7 kg (3.1–6 lb).
