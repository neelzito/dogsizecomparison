Boykin Spaniel vs English Cocker Spaniel: size comparison

Boykin Spaniel or English Cocker Spaniel? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

How the two breeds compare

The Boykin Spaniel and the English Cocker Spaniel are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Boykin Spaniel stands 43 cm (16.7 in) and an average male English Cocker Spaniel 42 cm (16.4 in).

By weight, the Boykin Spaniel is about 13% heavier: 16 kg (35 lb) for an average male against 14 kg (31 lb) for the English Cocker Spaniel.

Both are in the Sporting group.

Their life expectancy is similar (10–15 years and 12–14 years).

Boykin Spaniel vs English Cocker Spaniel: questions

Which is bigger, the Boykin Spaniel or the English Cocker Spaniel?

The Boykin Spaniel and the English Cocker Spaniel are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Boykin Spaniel stands 43 cm (16.7 in) and an average male English Cocker Spaniel 42 cm (16.4 in).

How much does a Boykin Spaniel weigh compared with an English Cocker Spaniel?

A male Boykin Spaniel weighs 14–18 kg (30–40 lb) and a female 12–16 kg (25–35 lb). A male English Cocker Spaniel weighs 13–16 kg (28–34 lb) and a female 12–15 kg (26–32 lb).
