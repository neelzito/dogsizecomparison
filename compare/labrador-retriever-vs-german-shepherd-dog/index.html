Labrador Retriever vs German Shepherd Dog: size comparison

German Shepherd Dog or Labrador Retriever? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

Average male and female of each breed, drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person. Solid line = male, dashed = female.

How the two breeds compare

The German Shepherd Dog is the taller dog. An average male stands 64 cm (25 in) at the shoulder, 4 cm (1.6 in) more than an average male Labrador Retriever at 60 cm (23.4 in).

They weigh about the same: 33 kg (73 lb) for an average male Labrador Retriever and 35 kg (78 lb) for a German Shepherd Dog.

The Labrador Retriever belongs to the Sporting group and comes from Canada/UK; the German Shepherd Dog is in the Herding group, from Germany.

The German Shepherd Dog tends to live longer: 12–14 years against 11–13 years.

Labrador Retriever vs German Shepherd Dog: questions

Which is bigger, the Labrador Retriever or the German Shepherd Dog?

The German Shepherd Dog is the taller dog. An average male stands 64 cm (25 in) at the shoulder, 4 cm (1.6 in) more than an average male Labrador Retriever at 60 cm (23.4 in).

How much does a Labrador Retriever weigh compared with a German Shepherd Dog?

A male Labrador Retriever weighs 30–37 kg (65–80 lb) and a female 25–32 kg (55–71 lb). A male German Shepherd Dog weighs 30–41 kg (65–90 lb) and a female 23–32 kg (50–71 lb).
