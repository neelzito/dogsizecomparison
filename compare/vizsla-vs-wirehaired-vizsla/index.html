Vizsla vs Wirehaired Vizsla: size comparison

Wirehaired Vizsla or Vizsla? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

Average male and female of each breed, drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person. Solid line = male, dashed = female.

How the two breeds compare

The Wirehaired Vizsla is the taller dog. An average male stands 61 cm (24 in) at the shoulder, 2.5 cm (1 in) more than an average male Vizsla at 59 cm (23 in).

They weigh about the same: 26 kg (57 lb) for an average male Vizsla and 27 kg (60 lb) for a Wirehaired Vizsla.

Both are in the Sporting group and come from Hungary.

Their life expectancy is similar (12–14 years and 12–14 years).

Vizsla vs Wirehaired Vizsla: questions

Which is bigger, the Vizsla or the Wirehaired Vizsla?

The Wirehaired Vizsla is the taller dog. An average male stands 61 cm (24 in) at the shoulder, 2.5 cm (1 in) more than an average male Vizsla at 59 cm (23 in).

How much does a Vizsla weigh compared with a Wirehaired Vizsla?

A male Vizsla weighs 25–27 kg (55–60 lb) and a female 20–25 kg (44–55 lb). A male Wirehaired Vizsla weighs 25–30 kg (55–65 lb) and a female 21–25 kg (45–55 lb).
