Australian Shepherd vs Miniature American Shepherd: size comparison

Australian Shepherd or Miniature American Shepherd? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

How the two breeds compare

The Australian Shepherd is the taller dog. An average male stands 55 cm (21.6 in) at the shoulder, 14.3 cm (5.6 in) more than an average male Miniature American Shepherd at 41 cm (15.9 in), about 1.4× the height.

By weight, the Australian Shepherd is about 92% heavier: 26 kg (57 lb) for an average male against 14 kg (30 lb) for the Miniature American Shepherd.

Both are in the Herding group and come from the United States.

The Australian Shepherd tends to live longer: 12–15 years against 12–13 years.

Australian Shepherd vs Miniature American Shepherd: questions

Which is bigger, the Australian Shepherd or the Miniature American Shepherd?

The Australian Shepherd is the taller dog. An average male stands 55 cm (21.6 in) at the shoulder, 14.3 cm (5.6 in) more than an average male Miniature American Shepherd at 41 cm (15.9 in), about 1.4× the height.

How much does an Australian Shepherd weigh compared with a Miniature American Shepherd?

A male Australian Shepherd weighs 23–30 kg (50–65 lb) and a female 18–25 kg (40–55 lb). A male Miniature American Shepherd weighs 9.1–18 kg (20–40 lb) and a female 9.1–18 kg (20–40 lb).
