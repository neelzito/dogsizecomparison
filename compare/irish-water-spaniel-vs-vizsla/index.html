Irish Water Spaniel vs Vizsla: size comparison

Irish Water Spaniel or Vizsla? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

How the two breeds compare

The Irish Water Spaniel and the Vizsla are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Irish Water Spaniel stands 59 cm (23 in) and an average male Vizsla 59 cm (23 in).

They weigh about the same: 28 kg (62 lb) for an average male Irish Water Spaniel and 26 kg (57 lb) for a Vizsla.

Both are in the Sporting group.

Their life expectancy is similar (12–13 years and 12–14 years).

Irish Water Spaniel vs Vizsla: questions

Which is bigger, the Irish Water Spaniel or the Vizsla?

The Irish Water Spaniel and the Vizsla are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Irish Water Spaniel stands 59 cm (23 in) and an average male Vizsla 59 cm (23 in).

How much does an Irish Water Spaniel weigh compared with a Vizsla?

A male Irish Water Spaniel weighs 25–31 kg (55–68 lb) and a female 21–27 kg (45–58 lb). A male Vizsla weighs 25–27 kg (55–60 lb) and a female 20–25 kg (44–55 lb).
