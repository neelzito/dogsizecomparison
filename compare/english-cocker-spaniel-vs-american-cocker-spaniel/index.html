English Cocker Spaniel vs American Cocker Spaniel: size comparison

How the two breeds compare

The English Cocker Spaniel is the taller dog. An average male stands 42 cm (16.4 in) at the shoulder, 3.5 cm (1.4 in) more than an average male American Cocker Spaniel at 38 cm (15.1 in).

By weight, the English Cocker Spaniel is about 12% heavier: 14 kg (31 lb) for an average male against 13 kg (28 lb) for the American Cocker Spaniel.

Both are in the Sporting group.

The English Cocker Spaniel tends to live longer: 12–14 years against 10–14 years.

English Cocker Spaniel vs American Cocker Spaniel: questions

Which is bigger, the English Cocker Spaniel or the American Cocker Spaniel?

The English Cocker Spaniel is the taller dog. An average male stands 42 cm (16.4 in) at the shoulder, 3.5 cm (1.4 in) more than an average male American Cocker Spaniel at 38 cm (15.1 in).

How much does an English Cocker Spaniel weigh compared with an American Cocker Spaniel?

A male English Cocker Spaniel weighs 13–16 kg (28–34 lb) and a female 12–15 kg (26–32 lb). A male American Cocker Spaniel weighs 12–14 kg (25–30 lb) and a female 9.1–12 kg (20–25 lb).
