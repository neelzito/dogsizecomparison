Miniature Poodle vs Schipperke: size comparison

Miniature Poodle or Schipperke? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

Average male and female of each breed, drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person. Solid line = male, dashed = female.

How the two breeds compare

The Miniature Poodle and the Schipperke are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Miniature Poodle stands 32 cm (12.5 in) and an average male Schipperke 31 cm (12 in).

They weigh about the same: 5.7 kg (12 lb) for an average male Miniature Poodle and 5.9 kg (13 lb) for a Schipperke.

Both are in the Non-Sporting group.

Their life expectancy is similar (10–18 years and 12–16 years).

Miniature Poodle vs Schipperke: questions

Which is bigger, the Miniature Poodle or the Schipperke?

The Miniature Poodle and the Schipperke are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Miniature Poodle stands 32 cm (12.5 in) and an average male Schipperke 31 cm (12 in).

How much does a Miniature Poodle weigh compared with a Schipperke?

A male Miniature Poodle weighs 4.5–6.8 kg (9.9–15 lb) and a female 4.5–6.8 kg (9.9–15 lb). A male Schipperke weighs 4.5–7.3 kg (9.9–16 lb) and a female 4.5–7.3 kg (9.9–16 lb).
