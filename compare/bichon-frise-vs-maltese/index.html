Bichon Frise vs Maltese: size comparison

Bichon Frise or Maltese? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

Average male and female of each breed, drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person. Solid line = male, dashed = female.

How the two breeds compare

The Bichon Frise is the taller dog. An average male stands 27 cm (10.4 in) at the shoulder, 6 cm (2.4 in) more than an average male Maltese at 21 cm (8.1 in), about 1.3× the height.

By weight, the Bichon Frise is about 2.7× as heavy: 6.8 kg (15 lb) for an average male against 2.5 kg (5.5 lb) for the Maltese.

The Bichon Frise belongs to the Non-Sporting group and comes from the Mediterranean; the Maltese is in the Toy group, from Malta.

The Bichon Frise tends to live longer: 14–15 years against 12–15 years.

For air travel, the Maltese fits the 8 kg cabin limit for dog and carrier; the Bichon Frise only sometimes does.

Bichon Frise vs Maltese: questions

Which is bigger, the Bichon Frise or the Maltese?

The Bichon Frise is the taller dog. An average male stands 27 cm (10.4 in) at the shoulder, 6 cm (2.4 in) more than an average male Maltese at 21 cm (8.1 in), about 1.3× the height.

How much does a Bichon Frise weigh compared with a Maltese?

A male Bichon Frise weighs 5.4–8.2 kg (12–18 lb) and a female 5.4–8.2 kg (12–18 lb). A male Maltese weighs 1.8–3.2 kg (4–7.1 lb) and a female 1.8–3.2 kg (4–7.1 lb).
