Black Russian Terrier vs Komondor: size comparison

Komondor or Black Russian Terrier? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

Average male and female of each breed, drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person. Solid line = male, dashed = female.

How the two breeds compare

The Komondor is the taller dog. An average male stands 75 cm (29.5 in) at the shoulder, 2.8 cm (1.1 in) more than an average male Black Russian Terrier at 72 cm (28.4 in).

By weight, the Komondor is about 10% heavier: 53 kg (116 lb) for an average male against 48 kg (105 lb) for the Black Russian Terrier.

Both are in the Working group.

Their life expectancy is similar (10–12 years and 10–12 years).

Black Russian Terrier vs Komondor: questions

Which is bigger, the Black Russian Terrier or the Komondor?

The Komondor is the taller dog. An average male stands 75 cm (29.5 in) at the shoulder, 2.8 cm (1.1 in) more than an average male Black Russian Terrier at 72 cm (28.4 in).

How much does a Black Russian Terrier weigh compared with a Komondor?

A male Black Russian Terrier weighs 37–59 kg (80–130 lb) and a female 37–59 kg (80–130 lb). A male Komondor weighs 46–60 kg (100–132 lb) and a female 37–50 kg (80–110 lb).
