Chesapeake Bay Retriever vs Labrador Retriever: size comparison

Chesapeake Bay Retriever or Labrador Retriever? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

How the two breeds compare

The Chesapeake Bay Retriever is the taller dog. An average male stands 62 cm (24.5 in) at the shoulder, 2.8 cm (1.1 in) more than an average male Labrador Retriever at 60 cm (23.4 in).

They weigh about the same: 33 kg (73 lb) for an average male Chesapeake Bay Retriever and 33 kg (73 lb) for a Labrador Retriever.

Both are in the Sporting group.

Their life expectancy is similar (10–13 years and 11–13 years).

Chesapeake Bay Retriever vs Labrador Retriever: questions

Which is bigger, the Chesapeake Bay Retriever or the Labrador Retriever?

The Chesapeake Bay Retriever is the taller dog. An average male stands 62 cm (24.5 in) at the shoulder, 2.8 cm (1.1 in) more than an average male Labrador Retriever at 60 cm (23.4 in).

How much does a Chesapeake Bay Retriever weigh compared with a Labrador Retriever?

A male Chesapeake Bay Retriever weighs 30–37 kg (65–80 lb) and a female 25–32 kg (55–71 lb). A male Labrador Retriever weighs 30–37 kg (65–80 lb) and a female 25–32 kg (55–71 lb).
