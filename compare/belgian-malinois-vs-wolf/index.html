Belgian Malinois vs wolf: size comparison

How does the Belgian Malinois measure up to a gray wolf? Shoulder heights and weights against the Eurasian wolf and the larger Northwestern wolf, drawn to scale next to a person.

Average male and female Belgian Malinois next to average male wolves, drawn to scale with a 175 cm (5′9″) person. Solid line = male, dashed = female.

Open in the comparison tool →

Is the Belgian Malinois bigger than a wolf?

An average male Belgian Malinois is shorter than both wolves. By weight, it is lighter than both.

An average male Eurasian wolf, at 78 cm (30.5 in) and 47 kg (103 lb), is 14 cm (5.5 in) taller than an average male Belgian Malinois (64 cm, 32 kg) and is about 46% heavier.

An average male Northwestern wolf, at 84 cm (32.9 in) and 55 kg (121 lb), is 20 cm (7.9 in) taller than an average male Belgian Malinois (64 cm, 32 kg) and is about 73% heavier.

Wolves are built for travelling long distances: compared with most dogs of the same shoulder height they have longer legs, a narrower chest and bigger feet, so they often look larger than the numbers suggest. Wolf sizes also vary a lot by region; northern wolves are the biggest.

Belgian Malinois vs wolf: questions

Is a Belgian Malinois bigger than a wolf?

An average male Belgian Malinois is shorter than both wolves. By weight, it is lighter than both. An average male Eurasian wolf, at 78 cm (30.5 in) and 47 kg (103 lb), is 14 cm (5.5 in) taller than an average male Belgian Malinois (64 cm, 32 kg) and is about 46% heavier.

How much does a Belgian Malinois weigh compared with a wolf?

A male Belgian Malinois weighs 27–37 kg (60–80 lb) and a female 18–27 kg (40–60 lb). A male Eurasian wolf weighs 38–55 kg (84–121 lb) and a male Northwestern wolf 45–65 kg (99–143 lb).
