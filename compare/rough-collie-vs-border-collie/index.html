Rough Collie vs Border Collie: size comparison

Rough Collie or Border Collie? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

Average male and female of each breed, drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person. Solid line = male, dashed = female.

How the two breeds compare

The Rough Collie is the taller dog. An average male stands 64 cm (25 in) at the shoulder, 11.3 cm (4.4 in) more than an average male Border Collie at 52 cm (20.6 in).

By weight, the Rough Collie is about 58% heavier: 31 kg (67 lb) for an average male against 19 kg (42 lb) for the Border Collie.

Both are in the Herding group.

Their life expectancy is similar (12–14 years and 12–15 years).

Rough Collie vs Border Collie: questions

Which is bigger, the Rough Collie or the Border Collie?

The Rough Collie is the taller dog. An average male stands 64 cm (25 in) at the shoulder, 11.3 cm (4.4 in) more than an average male Border Collie at 52 cm (20.6 in).

How much does a Rough Collie weigh compared with a Border Collie?

A male Rough Collie weighs 27–34 kg (60–75 lb) and a female 23–30 kg (50–65 lb). A male Border Collie weighs 14–25 kg (30–55 lb) and a female 14–25 kg (30–55 lb).
