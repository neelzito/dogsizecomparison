Podenco Andaluz (Medium) vs Whippet: size comparison

Whippet or Podenco Andaluz (Medium)? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

Average male and female of each breed, drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person. Solid line = male, dashed = female.

How the two breeds compare

The Whippet is the taller dog. An average male stands 52 cm (20.6 in) at the shoulder, 4.3 cm (1.7 in) more than an average male Podenco Andaluz (Medium) at 48 cm (18.9 in).

They weigh about the same: 16 kg (35 lb) for an average male Podenco Andaluz (Medium) and 15 kg (33 lb) for a Whippet.

Both are in the Hound group.

Their life expectancy is similar (12–14 years and 12–15 years).

Podenco Andaluz (Medium) vs Whippet: questions

Which is bigger, the Podenco Andaluz (Medium) or the Whippet?

The Whippet is the taller dog. An average male stands 52 cm (20.6 in) at the shoulder, 4.3 cm (1.7 in) more than an average male Podenco Andaluz (Medium) at 48 cm (18.9 in).

How much does a Podenco Andaluz (Medium) weigh compared with a Whippet?

A male Podenco Andaluz (Medium) weighs 10–22 kg (22–49 lb) and a female 10–22 kg (22–49 lb). A male Whippet weighs 12–18 kg (25–40 lb) and a female 12–18 kg (25–40 lb).
