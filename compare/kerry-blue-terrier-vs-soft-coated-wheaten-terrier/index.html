Kerry Blue Terrier vs Soft Coated Wheaten Terrier: size comparison

Kerry Blue Terrier or Soft Coated Wheaten Terrier? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

How the two breeds compare

The Kerry Blue Terrier and the Soft Coated Wheaten Terrier are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Kerry Blue Terrier stands 48 cm (18.7 in) and an average male Soft Coated Wheaten Terrier 47 cm (18.5 in).

They weigh about the same: 17 kg (36 lb) for an average male Kerry Blue Terrier and 17 kg (37 lb) for a Soft Coated Wheaten Terrier.

Both are in the Terrier group and come from Ireland.

Their life expectancy is similar (12–15 years and 12–14 years).

Kerry Blue Terrier vs Soft Coated Wheaten Terrier: questions

Which is bigger, the Kerry Blue Terrier or the Soft Coated Wheaten Terrier?

The Kerry Blue Terrier and the Soft Coated Wheaten Terrier are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Kerry Blue Terrier stands 48 cm (18.7 in) and an average male Soft Coated Wheaten Terrier 47 cm (18.5 in).

How much does a Kerry Blue Terrier weigh compared with a Soft Coated Wheaten Terrier?

A male Kerry Blue Terrier weighs 15–18 kg (33–40 lb) and a female 14–16 kg (31–35 lb). A male Soft Coated Wheaten Terrier weighs 16–18 kg (35–40 lb) and a female 14–16 kg (30–35 lb).
