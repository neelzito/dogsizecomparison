Kromfohrländer vs Staffordshire Bull Terrier: size comparison

Kromfohrländer or Staffordshire Bull Terrier? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

How the two breeds compare

The Kromfohrländer is the taller dog. An average male stands 42 cm (16.4 in) at the shoulder, 3.8 cm (1.5 in) more than an average male Staffordshire Bull Terrier at 38 cm (15 in).

By weight, the Staffordshire Bull Terrier is about 18% heavier: 15 kg (33 lb) for an average male against 13 kg (28 lb) for the Kromfohrländer.

Both are in the Terrier group.

Their life expectancy is similar (13–15 years and 12–15 years).

Kromfohrländer vs Staffordshire Bull Terrier: questions

Which is bigger, the Kromfohrländer or the Staffordshire Bull Terrier?

The Kromfohrländer is the taller dog. An average male stands 42 cm (16.4 in) at the shoulder, 3.8 cm (1.5 in) more than an average male Staffordshire Bull Terrier at 38 cm (15 in).

How much does a Kromfohrländer weigh compared with a Staffordshire Bull Terrier?

A male Kromfohrländer weighs 9.1–16 kg (20–35 lb) and a female 9.1–16 kg (20–35 lb). A male Staffordshire Bull Terrier weighs 13–17 kg (28–37 lb) and a female 11–16 kg (24–34 lb).
