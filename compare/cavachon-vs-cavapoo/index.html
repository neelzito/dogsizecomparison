Cavachon vs Cavapoo: size comparison

Cavachon or Cavapoo? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

Average male and female of each breed, drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person. Solid line = male, dashed = female.

Open in the comparison tool →

How the two breeds compare

The Cavachon is the taller dog. An average male stands 32 cm (12.4 in) at the shoulder, 2 cm (0.8 in) more than an average male Cavapoo at 30 cm (11.6 in).

They weigh about the same: 7.5 kg (17 lb) for an average male Cavachon and 7.8 kg (17 lb) for a Cavapoo.

Both are in the designer & mixed category.

Their life expectancy is similar (12–15 years and 12–15 years).

Cavachon vs Cavapoo: questions

Which is bigger, the Cavachon or the Cavapoo?

The Cavachon is the taller dog. An average male stands 32 cm (12.4 in) at the shoulder, 2 cm (0.8 in) more than an average male Cavapoo at 30 cm (11.6 in).

How much does a Cavachon weigh compared with a Cavapoo?

A male Cavachon weighs 6–9 kg (13–20 lb) and a female 5.5–8 kg (12–18 lb). A male Cavapoo weighs 4.5–11 kg (9.9–24 lb) and a female 4.5–9 kg (9.9–20 lb).
