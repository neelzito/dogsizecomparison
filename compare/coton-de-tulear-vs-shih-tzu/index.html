Coton de Tulear vs Shih Tzu: size comparison

Coton de Tulear or Shih Tzu? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

Average male and female of each breed, drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person. Solid line = male, dashed = female.

How the two breeds compare

The Coton de Tulear is the taller dog. An average male stands 27 cm (10.5 in) at the shoulder, 2 cm (0.8 in) more than an average male Shih Tzu at 25 cm (9.7 in).

They weigh about the same: 5.4 kg (12 lb) for an average male Coton de Tulear and 5.7 kg (13 lb) for a Shih Tzu.

Both are in the Toy group.

The Coton de Tulear tends to live longer: 15–19 years against 10–18 years.

Coton de Tulear vs Shih Tzu: questions

Which is bigger, the Coton de Tulear or the Shih Tzu?

The Coton de Tulear is the taller dog. An average male stands 27 cm (10.5 in) at the shoulder, 2 cm (0.8 in) more than an average male Shih Tzu at 25 cm (9.7 in).

How much does a Coton de Tulear weigh compared with a Shih Tzu?

A male Coton de Tulear weighs 4.1–6.8 kg (9–15 lb) and a female 3.6–5.9 kg (7.9–13 lb). A male Shih Tzu weighs 4.1–7.3 kg (9–16 lb) and a female 4.1–7.3 kg (9–16 lb).
