How the two breeds compare

The Podenco Andaluz (Large) is the taller dog. An average male stands 59 cm (23.2 in) at the shoulder, 11 cm (4.3 in) more than an average male Podenco Andaluz (Medium) at 48 cm (18.9 in).

By weight, the Podenco Andaluz (Large) is about 69% heavier: 27 kg (60 lb) for an average male against 16 kg (35 lb) for the Podenco Andaluz (Medium).

Both are in the Hound group and come from Spain.

Their life expectancy is similar (12–14 years and 12–14 years).

Podenco Andaluz (Medium) vs Podenco Andaluz (Large): questions

Which is bigger, the Podenco Andaluz (Medium) or the Podenco Andaluz (Large)?

The Podenco Andaluz (Large) is the taller dog. An average male stands 59 cm (23.2 in) at the shoulder, 11 cm (4.3 in) more than an average male Podenco Andaluz (Medium) at 48 cm (18.9 in).

How much does a Podenco Andaluz (Medium) weigh compared with a Podenco Andaluz (Large)?

A male Podenco Andaluz (Medium) weighs 10–22 kg (22–49 lb) and a female 10–22 kg (22–49 lb). A male Podenco Andaluz (Large) weighs 21–33 kg (46–73 lb) and a female 21–33 kg (46–73 lb).
