Shiba Inu vs Tibetan Terrier: size comparison

Shiba Inu or Tibetan Terrier? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

Average male and female of each breed, drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person. Solid line = male, dashed = female.

Open in the comparison tool →

How the two breeds compare

The Shiba Inu and the Tibetan Terrier are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Shiba Inu stands 40 cm (15.6 in) and an average male Tibetan Terrier 39 cm (15.5 in).

They weigh about the same: 11 kg (23 lb) for an average male Shiba Inu and 11 kg (24 lb) for a Tibetan Terrier.

Both are in the Non-Sporting group.

The Tibetan Terrier tends to live longer: 15–16 years against 13–16 years.

Shiba Inu vs Tibetan Terrier: questions

Which is bigger, the Shiba Inu or the Tibetan Terrier?

The Shiba Inu and the Tibetan Terrier are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Shiba Inu stands 40 cm (15.6 in) and an average male Tibetan Terrier 39 cm (15.5 in).

How much does a Shiba Inu weigh compared with a Tibetan Terrier?

A male Shiba Inu weighs 11 kg (23 lb) and a female 7.7 kg (17 lb). A male Tibetan Terrier weighs 8.2–14 kg (18–30 lb) and a female 8–12 kg (18–26 lb).
