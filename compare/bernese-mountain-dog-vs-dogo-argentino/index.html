Bernese Mountain Dog vs Dogo Argentino: size comparison

Bernese Mountain Dog or Dogo Argentino? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

How the two breeds compare

The Bernese Mountain Dog is the taller dog. An average male stands 67 cm (26.3 in) at the shoulder, 2.5 cm (1 in) more than an average male Dogo Argentino at 64 cm (25.3 in).

They weigh about the same: 44 kg (98 lb) for an average male Bernese Mountain Dog and 41 kg (90 lb) for a Dogo Argentino.

Both are in the Working group.

The Dogo Argentino tends to live longer: 9–15 years against 7–10 years.

Bernese Mountain Dog vs Dogo Argentino: questions

Which is bigger, the Bernese Mountain Dog or the Dogo Argentino?

The Bernese Mountain Dog is the taller dog. An average male stands 67 cm (26.3 in) at the shoulder, 2.5 cm (1 in) more than an average male Dogo Argentino at 64 cm (25.3 in).

How much does a Bernese Mountain Dog weigh compared with a Dogo Argentino?

A male Bernese Mountain Dog weighs 37–52 kg (80–115 lb) and a female 32–43 kg (71–95 lb). A male Dogo Argentino weighs 37–46 kg (80–100 lb) and a female 37–46 kg (80–100 lb).
