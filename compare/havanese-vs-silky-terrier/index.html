Havanese vs Silky Terrier: size comparison

Havanese or Silky Terrier? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

Average male and female of each breed, drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person. Solid line = male, dashed = female.

How the two breeds compare

The Havanese and the Silky Terrier are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Havanese stands 25 cm (9.9 in) and an average male Silky Terrier 24 cm (9.5 in).

They weigh about the same: 4.6 kg (10 lb) for an average male Havanese and 4.6 kg (10 lb) for a Silky Terrier.

Both are in the Toy group.

The Havanese tends to live longer: 14–16 years against 13–15 years.

Havanese vs Silky Terrier: questions

Which is bigger, the Havanese or the Silky Terrier?

The Havanese and the Silky Terrier are almost the same height at the shoulder: an average male Havanese stands 25 cm (9.9 in) and an average male Silky Terrier 24 cm (9.5 in).

How much does a Havanese weigh compared with a Silky Terrier?

A male Havanese weighs 3.2–5.9 kg (7.1–13 lb) and a female 3.2–5.9 kg (7.1–13 lb). A male Silky Terrier weighs 4.1–5 kg (9–11 lb) and a female 4.1–5 kg (9–11 lb).
