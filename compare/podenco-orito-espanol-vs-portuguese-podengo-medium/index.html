Podenco Orito Español vs Portuguese Podengo (Medium): size comparison

Portuguese Podengo (Medium) or Podenco Orito Español? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

How the two breeds compare

The Portuguese Podengo (Medium) is the taller dog. An average male stands 47 cm (18.5 in) at the shoulder, 2 cm (0.8 in) more than an average male Podenco Orito Español at 45 cm (17.7 in).

They weigh about the same: 19 kg (42 lb) for an average male Podenco Orito Español and 18 kg (40 lb) for a Portuguese Podengo (Medium).

Both are in the Hound group.

Their life expectancy is similar (12–14 years and 12–15 years).

Podenco Orito Español vs Portuguese Podengo (Medium): questions

Which is bigger, the Podenco Orito Español or the Portuguese Podengo (Medium)?

The Portuguese Podengo (Medium) is the taller dog. An average male stands 47 cm (18.5 in) at the shoulder, 2 cm (0.8 in) more than an average male Podenco Orito Español at 45 cm (17.7 in).

How much does a Podenco Orito Español weigh compared with a Portuguese Podengo (Medium)?

A male Podenco Orito Español weighs 16–22 kg (35–49 lb) and a female 14–20 kg (31–44 lb). A male Portuguese Podengo (Medium) weighs 16–20 kg (35–44 lb) and a female 16–20 kg (35–44 lb).
