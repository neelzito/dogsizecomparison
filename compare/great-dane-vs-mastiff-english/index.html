Great Dane vs Mastiff (English): size comparison

How the two breeds compare

The Mastiff (English) is the taller dog. An average male stands 84 cm (32.9 in) at the shoulder, 4.8 cm (1.9 in) more than an average male Great Dane at 79 cm (31 in).

By weight, the Mastiff (English) is about 24% heavier: 89 kg (195 lb) for an average male against 72 kg (158 lb) for the Great Dane.

Both are in the Working group.

Their life expectancy is similar (7–10 years and 6–10 years).

Great Dane vs Mastiff (English): questions

Which is bigger, the Great Dane or the Mastiff (English)?

The Mastiff (English) is the taller dog. An average male stands 84 cm (32.9 in) at the shoulder, 4.8 cm (1.9 in) more than an average male Great Dane at 79 cm (31 in).

How much does a Great Dane weigh compared with a Mastiff (English)?

A male Great Dane weighs 64–80 kg (140–175 lb) and a female 50–64 kg (110–140 lb). A male Mastiff (English) weighs 73–105 kg (160–230 lb) and a female 55–77 kg (120–170 lb).
