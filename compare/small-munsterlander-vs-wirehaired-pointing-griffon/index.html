Small Münsterländer vs Wirehaired Pointing Griffon: size comparison

Wirehaired Pointing Griffon or Small Münsterländer? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

How the two breeds compare

The Wirehaired Pointing Griffon is the taller dog. An average male stands 59 cm (23 in) at the shoulder, 5.8 cm (2.3 in) more than an average male Small Münsterländer at 53 cm (20.8 in).

By weight, the Wirehaired Pointing Griffon is about 21% heavier: 27 kg (60 lb) for an average male against 23 kg (50 lb) for the Small Münsterländer.

Both are in the Sporting group.

Their life expectancy is similar (12–14 years and 12–15 years).

Small Münsterländer vs Wirehaired Pointing Griffon: questions

Which is bigger, the Small Münsterländer or the Wirehaired Pointing Griffon?

The Wirehaired Pointing Griffon is the taller dog. An average male stands 59 cm (23 in) at the shoulder, 5.8 cm (2.3 in) more than an average male Small Münsterländer at 53 cm (20.8 in).

How much does a Small Münsterländer weigh compared with a Wirehaired Pointing Griffon?

A male Small Münsterländer weighs 18–27 kg (40–60 lb) and a female 18–27 kg (40–60 lb). A male Wirehaired Pointing Griffon weighs 23–32 kg (50–71 lb) and a female 16–23 kg (35–50 lb).
