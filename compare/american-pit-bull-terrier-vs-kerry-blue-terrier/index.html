American Pit Bull Terrier vs Kerry Blue Terrier: size comparison

How the two breeds compare

The American Pit Bull Terrier is the taller dog. An average male stands 50 cm (19.5 in) at the shoulder, 2 cm (0.8 in) more than an average male Kerry Blue Terrier at 48 cm (18.7 in).

By weight, the American Pit Bull Terrier is about 31% heavier: 22 kg (48 lb) for an average male against 17 kg (36 lb) for the Kerry Blue Terrier.

Both are in the Terrier group.

Their life expectancy is similar (12–14 years and 12–15 years).

American Pit Bull Terrier vs Kerry Blue Terrier: questions

Which is bigger, the American Pit Bull Terrier or the Kerry Blue Terrier?

The American Pit Bull Terrier is the taller dog. An average male stands 50 cm (19.5 in) at the shoulder, 2 cm (0.8 in) more than an average male Kerry Blue Terrier at 48 cm (18.7 in).

How much does an American Pit Bull Terrier weigh compared with a Kerry Blue Terrier?

A male American Pit Bull Terrier weighs 16–27 kg (35–60 lb) and a female 14–23 kg (30–50 lb). A male Kerry Blue Terrier weighs 15–18 kg (33–40 lb) and a female 14–16 kg (31–35 lb).
