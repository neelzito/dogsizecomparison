French Bulldog vs Boston Terrier: size comparison

Boston Terrier or French Bulldog? Heights at the shoulder, weights for males and females, and both breeds drawn to scale next to a person.

How the two breeds compare

The Boston Terrier is the taller dog. An average male stands 41 cm (15.9 in) at the shoulder, 10 cm (3.9 in) more than an average male French Bulldog at 31 cm (12 in), about 1.3× the height.

By weight, the French Bulldog is about 27% heavier: 11 kg (24 lb) for an average male against 8.5 kg (19 lb) for the Boston Terrier.

Both are in the Non-Sporting group.

The Boston Terrier tends to live longer: 11–13 years against 10–12 years.

For air travel, the Boston Terrier can sometimes fit the 8 kg cabin limit for dog and carrier; the French Bulldog does not.

French Bulldog vs Boston Terrier: questions

Which is bigger, the French Bulldog or the Boston Terrier?

The Boston Terrier is the taller dog. An average male stands 41 cm (15.9 in) at the shoulder, 10 cm (3.9 in) more than an average male French Bulldog at 31 cm (12 in), about 1.3× the height.

How much does a French Bulldog weigh compared with a Boston Terrier?

A male French Bulldog weighs 9–13 kg (20–28 lb) and a female 8–13 kg (18–28 lb). A male Boston Terrier weighs 5.4–12 kg (12–25 lb) and a female 5.4–12 kg (12–25 lb).
