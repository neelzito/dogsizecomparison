How cabin rules work

Airlines that allow pets in the cabin set two kinds of limit: a weight limit, usually for dog and carrier together, and a carrier size that fits under the seat in front. European airlines mostly cap the total at 8 kg (17.6 lb); several North American airlines set no weight limit but the dog must still fit in an under-seat carrier and be able to stand and turn around in it.

Soft carriers weigh about 1–2 kg (2–4 lb). Weigh yours with the bedding you'll use. For the full rules, see flying with a dog in the cabin.

Frequently asked questions

What is the weight limit for dogs in the airplane cabin?

Most European airlines allow 8 kg (17.6 lb) for dog and carrier together. Several US airlines set no weight limit but require the carrier to fit under the seat. Some airlines, including British Airways, Ryanair and easyJet, don't accept pets in the cabin at all, apart from assistance dogs.

Which dog breeds can fly in the cabin?

Breeds that stay under about 6.5 kg (14 lb), such as the Chihuahua, Yorkshire Terrier, Pomeranian, Maltese and Toy Poodle. Small breeds like the Shih Tzu or Miniature Dachshund often fit if the dog is on the lighter side.
