Dog years to human years

Convert your dog's age to human years using size-based ageing, plus the newer DNA-based formula.

Disclaimer: Dog-to-human age conversions are approximations for fun and general orientation, not a measure of your dog's health. Your vet can tell you how your dog is ageing.

Why "multiply by 7" is wrong

Dogs mature very fast: a one-year-old dog is roughly as developed as a 15-year-old person, and a two-year-old as a 24-year-old. After that, ageing slows, but how much depends on size. Large and giant dogs age faster in later life, which is why they have shorter lifespans.

The DNA-based formula

A 2019 study of DNA methylation in Labrador Retrievers proposed human age = 16 × ln(dog age) + 31. It ages young dogs even faster (a 1-year-old is 31) and was calibrated on one breed only, so the calculator shows it as a second opinion.

Frequently asked questions

How old is a 1-year-old dog in human years?

About 15 human years for most dogs, about 12 for giant breeds that mature more slowly.

Do small dogs age slower?

After about age 3, yes. A 10-year-old small dog is roughly 56 in human years, a 10-year-old giant breed nearer 79.
