How the estimate works

Puppies grow fast at first and then level off. Small breeds reach adult weight at around 9–12 months; giant breeds keep filling out until 18–24 months. The calculator divides your puppy's current weight by the share of adult weight a typical puppy of that size has reached at that age.

Why it is only an estimate

  • Growth comes in spurts, so a single weigh-in can mislead. Weigh weekly and use the average.
  • Mixed breeds can follow either parent. Paw size and parents' weights are as useful as any formula.
  • Under- or over-feeding changes the curve. Your vet can tell you whether your puppy's body condition is right.

Frequently asked questions

When do puppies stop growing?

Toy and small breeds reach adult height at about 6–8 months and adult weight by 9–12 months. Medium breeds finish at about 12 months, large breeds at 12–18 months and giant breeds at 18–24 months.

Is a puppy half its adult weight at 4 months?

For medium breeds that's roughly right at about 4–5 months. Small breeds are past half by 4 months; large and giant breeds reach half their adult weight later, around 5–7 months.
