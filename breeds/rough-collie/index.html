How big is a Rough Collie?

The Rough Collie is a large-sized dog breed from Scotland, in the Herding group. Males stand 61–66 cm (24–26 in) at the shoulder and weigh 27–34 kg (60–75 lb). Females stand 56–61 cm (22–24 in) and weigh 23–30 kg (50–65 lb). On average it is taller than 72% of the 268 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 74%.

Rough Collie compared to a human

Next to a 175 cm (5′9″) adult, an average male Rough Collie stands 64 cm (25 in) at the shoulder: 36% of the person's height, reaching about mid-thigh. An average female, at 59 cm (23 in), reaches about mid-thigh. In feet, males stand 2 ft to 2 ft 2 in tall at the shoulder and females 1 ft 10 in to 2 ft. Next to a 120 cm (3′11″) child, about seven years old, the shoulder comes to about hip height. The head sits higher than the shoulder, so the dog looks taller than these numbers suggest.

Typical life expectancy is 12–14 years, longer than the 12.1-year average for large breeds.

About the Rough Collie

The Rough Collie was developed from herding dogs in the Scottish Highlands. In the 19th century, Queen Victoria fell in love with Collies at Balmoral, and the breed became fashionable. Breeders refined it into a more elegant dog with a long, narrow head and a spectacular coat. In the 20th century, the Lassie books, films and TV series made the Rough Collie one of the most famous dogs in the world. Today it is a gentle, loyal and intelligent family companion.

What a Rough Collie's size means at home

Size depends on the standard. The AKC calls for males of 24–26 in (61–66 cm) at the shoulder and 60–75 lb (27–34 kg), and females of 22–24 in (56–61 cm) and 50–65 lb (22.5–29.5 kg). These are the figures on this page. A male reaches roughly mid-thigh on an adult. The UK and FCI standards are smaller, at about 56–61 cm for males and 51–56 cm for females, so European Collies are often noticeably smaller than American ones. A Rough Collie needs a large boot and usually a 48″ (122 cm) crate. Check with the crate size calculator.

Rough Collie vs Smooth, Border Collie and Sheltie

  • The Smooth Collie is the same size and build, with a short coat.
  • The Border Collie is smaller, lighter and much more energetic, with a broader head.
  • The Shetland Sheepdog looks like a miniature Rough Collie but is a separate breed.

Temperament and exercise

Rough Collies are gentle, sensitive and devoted to their families, usually excellent with children. They're intelligent and easy to train with kindness. They need daily walks and play, but they're calmer than working Border Collies. Many bark, especially when excited.

Coat care

That magnificent double coat sheds heavily, especially once or twice a year. It needs a thorough brush at least once or twice a week, paying attention to the areas behind the ears, under the legs and the "trousers", where it mats.

Health

Many Collies carry the MDR1 gene variant, which makes them dangerously sensitive to some common medicines, including certain wormers and anaesthetics. A simple DNA test tells you, and your vet should know the result. Collie eye anomaly and progressive retinal atrophy (PRA) are also known, and DNA tests exist for both. Rough Collies often live 12–14 years.

Rough Collie size: common questions

How big does a Rough Collie get?

A fully grown male Rough Collie stands 61–66 cm (24–26 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 27–34 kg (60–75 lb). Females stand 56–61 cm (22–24 in) and weigh 23–30 kg (50–65 lb).

How tall is a Rough Collie in feet?

A male Rough Collie stands 2 ft to 2 ft 2 in tall at the shoulder (61–66 cm) and a female 1 ft 10 in to 2 ft (56–61 cm). Next to a 5′9″ adult, an average male reaches about mid-thigh.

Is a Rough Collie a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 28 kg (62 lb), the Rough Collie counts as a large dog breed on this site (large = 25–45 kg (55–99 lb) on average). It is taller than 72% of the 268 breeds listed.

How long does a Rough Collie live?

Typical life expectancy is 12–14 years, longer than the 12.1-year average for large breeds.

Can a Rough Collie fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Rough Collie weighs 23–34 kg (50–75 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).

Sizes are breed-standard ranges for adult dogs; individual dogs, working lines and dogs from different countries can be bigger or smaller. Airline notes are a general guide only. Check with your airline and your vet. Disclaimer.
