How big is a Manchester Terrier?

The Manchester Terrier is a small-sized dog breed from England, in the Terrier group. Adults stand 38–41 cm (15–15.9 in) at the shoulder and weigh 5.4–10 kg (12–22 lb); the breed standard gives the same range for males and females. On average it is taller than 33% of the 256 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 20%. At the shoulder, an average Manchester Terrier reaches just below the knee on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 15–17 years, longer than the 13.7-year average for small breeds. Lighter individuals fit airline cabin limits of 8 kg (17.6 lb) for dog and carrier; heavier ones need an airline that weighs only the carrier size, or travel in the hold.

Sizes follow the American Kennel Club (AKC) breed standard.

About the Manchester Terrier

The Manchester Terrier was developed in the 19th century around Manchester, in north-west England, by crossing black-and-tan terriers with Whippets. John Hulme is credited with starting the breed. It was used to kill rats, both in homes and in the rat-baiting contests that were popular at the time. It's a sleek, elegant, black-and-tan terrier, and it's now rare: The Kennel Club lists it as a vulnerable native breed.

Standard and Toy

In the US, the AKC recognises two varieties:

  • Standard Manchester Terrier: over 12 lb up to 22 lb (5.5–10 kg)
  • Toy Manchester Terrier: 12 lb (5.5 kg) or less

The UK's English Toy Terrier is a separate breed. A typical Standard Manchester stands about 38–40.5 cm at the shoulder, just below an adult's knee. The crate size calculator suggests a 30″ (76 cm) crate for an average Standard. Toys may fit the airline cabin limit. Check with the airline cabin check.

Manchester vs Smooth Fox Terrier

The Smooth Fox Terrier is about the same size, but mostly white. The Manchester looks like a miniature Doberman, but the two aren't closely related.

Temperament and exercise

Manchesters are clever, lively and affectionate, devoted to their family. They need daily exercise and love to chase. They're quick learners.

Care and health

The short, glossy coat needs almost no grooming. Known health problems include von Willebrand's disease, eye conditions and Legg-Calvé-Perthes disease. Manchesters are long-lived, often 15–17 years.

Sources: AKC breed standard and breed history; The Kennel Club breed standard and vulnerable native breeds list.

Manchester Terrier size: common questions

How big does a Manchester Terrier get?

An adult Manchester Terrier stands 38–41 cm (15–15.9 in) tall at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 5.4–10 kg (12–22 lb).

Is a Manchester Terrier a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 7.7 kg (17 lb), the Manchester Terrier counts as a small dog breed on this site (small = 5–10 kg (11–22 lb) on average). It is taller than 33% of the 256 breeds listed.

How long does a Manchester Terrier live?

Typical life expectancy is 15–17 years, longer than the 13.7-year average for small breeds.

Can a Manchester Terrier fly in the airplane cabin?

Sometimes. Lighter individuals fit airline cabin limits of 8 kg (17.6 lb) for dog and carrier; heavier ones need an airline that weighs only the carrier size, or travel in the hold. Rules vary by airline, so check before booking.
