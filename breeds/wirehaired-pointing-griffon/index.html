How big is a Wirehaired Pointing Griffon?

The Wirehaired Pointing Griffon is a medium-sized dog breed from the Netherlands/France, in the Sporting group. Males stand 56–61 cm (22–24 in) at the shoulder and weigh 23–32 kg (50–71 lb). Females stand 51–56 cm (20.1–22 in) and weigh 16–23 kg (35–50 lb). On average it is taller than 60% of the 247 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 56%. At the shoulder, an average male Wirehaired Pointing Griffon reaches about mid-thigh on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 12–15 years, about average for medium breeds. It is too heavy for airlines that cap dog and carrier at 8 kg (17.6 lb), so it usually flies in the hold unless the airline only checks the carrier size.

Wirehaired Pointing Griffon size: common questions

How big does a Wirehaired Pointing Griffon get?

A fully grown male Wirehaired Pointing Griffon stands 56–61 cm (22–24 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 23–32 kg (50–71 lb). Females stand 51–56 cm (20.1–22 in) and weigh 16–23 kg (35–50 lb).

Is a Wirehaired Pointing Griffon a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 23 kg (51 lb), the Wirehaired Pointing Griffon counts as a medium dog breed on this site (medium = 10–25 kg (22–55 lb) on average). It is taller than 60% of the 247 breeds listed.

How long does a Wirehaired Pointing Griffon live?

Typical life expectancy is 12–15 years, about average for medium breeds.

Can a Wirehaired Pointing Griffon fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Wirehaired Pointing Griffon weighs 16–32 kg (35–71 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).
