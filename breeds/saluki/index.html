How big is a Saluki?

The Saluki is a medium-sized dog breed from the Middle East, in the Hound group. Males stand 59–71 cm (23–28 in) at the shoulder and weigh 18–30 kg (40–65 lb). Females stand 56–66 cm (22–26 in) and weigh 18–30 kg (40–65 lb). On average it is taller than 78% of the 247 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 57%. At the shoulder, an average male Saluki reaches about mid-thigh on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 10–17 years, about average for medium breeds. It is too heavy for airlines that cap dog and carrier at 8 kg (17.6 lb), so it usually flies in the hold unless the airline only checks the carrier size.

Saluki size: common questions

How big does a Saluki get?

A fully grown male Saluki stands 59–71 cm (23–28 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 18–30 kg (40–65 lb). Females stand 56–66 cm (22–26 in) and weigh 18–30 kg (40–65 lb).

Is a Saluki a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 24 kg (52 lb), the Saluki counts as a medium dog breed on this site (medium = 10–25 kg (22–55 lb) on average). It is taller than 78% of the 247 breeds listed.

How long does a Saluki live?

Typical life expectancy is 10–17 years, about average for medium breeds.

Can a Saluki fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Saluki weighs 18–30 kg (40–65 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).
