How big is a Volpino Italiano?

The Volpino Italiano is a small-sized dog breed from Italy, in the Non-Sporting group. Males stand 27–31 cm (10.4–12 in) at the shoulder and weigh 4.5–7.3 kg (9.9–16 lb). Females stand 27–31 cm (10.4–12 in) and weigh 3.6–5.4 kg (7.9–12 lb). On average it is taller than 16% of the 256 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 10%. At the shoulder, an average male Volpino Italiano reaches about mid-shin on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 12–15 years, about average for small breeds. Lighter individuals fit airline cabin limits of 8 kg (17.6 lb) for dog and carrier; heavier ones need an airline that weighs only the carrier size, or travel in the hold.

About the Volpino Italiano

The Volpino Italiano is a small Italian spitz, kept for centuries as a watchdog in palaces and on farms, especially in Tuscany. Its name means "little fox". It was so rare by the 1960s that breeders feared it had died out, but the Italian kennel club revived it in the 1980s. It's lively, alert and very vocal, usually white with a fluffy stand-off coat.

What a Volpino's size means at home

The FCI standard calls for 27–30 cm at the shoulder for males and 25–28 cm for females. Volpini weigh about 4.5–7.5 kg (10–16 lb). A Volpino reaches around mid-shin on an adult. It suits any home and usually needs a 24″ (61 cm) crate. Check with the crate size calculator. Lighter dogs may fit the cabin limit. Check with the airline cabin check.

Volpino vs Schipperke and Tibetan Spaniel

The Schipperke is a little taller and about the same weight, always black. The Tibetan Spaniel is a little shorter and lighter. The Volpino is closely related to the German Spitz.

Temperament and exercise

Volpini are cheerful, affectionate and alert, excellent watchdogs that bark at anything unusual. They need daily walks and play.

Coat and health

The double coat sheds seasonally and needs brushing a couple of times a week. Primary lens luxation is known in the breed, and a DNA test exists. Volpini often live 12–15 years.

Sources: FCI standard No. 195 (Volpino Italiano); ENCI (Italian kennel club).

Volpino Italiano size: common questions

How big does a Volpino Italiano get?

A fully grown male Volpino Italiano stands 27–31 cm (10.4–12 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 4.5–7.3 kg (9.9–16 lb). Females stand 27–31 cm (10.4–12 in) and weigh 3.6–5.4 kg (7.9–12 lb).

Is a Volpino Italiano a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 5.2 kg (11 lb), the Volpino Italiano counts as a small dog breed on this site (small = 5–10 kg (11–22 lb) on average). It is taller than 16% of the 256 breeds listed.

How long does a Volpino Italiano live?

Typical life expectancy is 12–15 years, about average for small breeds.

Can a Volpino Italiano fly in the airplane cabin?

Sometimes. Lighter individuals fit airline cabin limits of 8 kg (17.6 lb) for dog and carrier; heavier ones need an airline that weighs only the carrier size, or travel in the hold. Rules vary by airline, so check before booking.
