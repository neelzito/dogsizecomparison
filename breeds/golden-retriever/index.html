How big is a Golden Retriever?

The Golden Retriever is a large-sized dog breed from Scotland, in the Sporting group. Males stand 59–61 cm (23–24 in) at the shoulder and weigh 30–34 kg (65–75 lb). Females stand 55–57 cm (21.5–22.4 in) and weigh 25–30 kg (55–65 lb). On average it is taller than 63% of the 247 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 73%. At the shoulder, an average male Golden Retriever reaches about mid-thigh on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 10–12 years, shorter than the 12.1-year average for large breeds.

Golden Retriever size: common questions

How big does a Golden Retriever get?

A fully grown male Golden Retriever stands 59–61 cm (23–24 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 30–34 kg (65–75 lb). Females stand 55–57 cm (21.5–22.4 in) and weigh 25–30 kg (55–65 lb).

Is a Golden Retriever a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 30 kg (65 lb), the Golden Retriever counts as a large dog breed on this site (large = 25–45 kg (55–99 lb) on average). It is taller than 63% of the 247 breeds listed.

How long does a Golden Retriever live?

Typical life expectancy is 10–12 years, shorter than the 12.1-year average for large breeds.

Can a Golden Retriever fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Golden Retriever weighs 25–34 kg (55–75 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).

Sizes are breed-standard ranges for adult dogs; individual dogs, working lines and dogs from different countries can be bigger or smaller. Airline notes are a general guide only. Check with your airline and your vet. Disclaimer.
