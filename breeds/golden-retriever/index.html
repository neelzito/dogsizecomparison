How big is a Golden Retriever?

The Golden Retriever is a large-sized dog breed from Scotland, in the Sporting group. Males stand 59–61 cm (23–24 in) at the shoulder and weigh 30–34 kg (65–75 lb). Females stand 55–57 cm (21.5–22.4 in) and weigh 25–30 kg (55–65 lb). On average it is taller than 64% of the 266 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 75%. At the shoulder, an average male Golden Retriever reaches about mid-thigh on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 10–12 years, shorter than the 12.1-year average for large breeds.

About the Golden Retriever

The Golden Retriever was developed in the Scottish Highlands in the second half of the 19th century. Lord Tweedmouth wanted a gundog that could retrieve shot birds from water and rough ground on his estate, Guisachan, near Inverness. He crossed a yellow retriever with the now-extinct Tweed Water Spaniel, and later added other retrievers, a little Irish Setter and possibly Bloodhound. The result was a soft-mouthed, eager and very trainable dog. That same nature makes the Golden one of the most popular family dogs and a common guide and assistance dog today.

What a Golden's size means at home

A Golden Retriever stands about 55–61 cm at the shoulder, roughly an adult's mid-thigh, and weighs 25–34 kg (55–75 lb). It is about as tall as a Labrador but looks bigger because of its coat. It needs an estate car or SUV boot and usually a 42″ (107 cm) crate. Check with the crate size calculator. That waving, feathered tail sits at coffee-table height, so clear anything breakable. Goldens are far too heavy for an airline cabin and fly in the hold.

Golden vs Labrador

The two are often confused. They're very close in size, but the Golden has a longer double coat with feathering on the legs, chest and tail, a slightly finer head, and a plume of a tail rather than the Labrador's thick otter tail. Goldens also come in a wider range of shades, from pale cream to deep red. The cream-coloured "English" type is common in Europe, while American breeders tend to prefer darker gold.

Coat and care

That coat sheds a lot, especially twice a year when the undercoat comes out. Brush a few times a week (daily during the shedding season) to keep the house manageable and the coat free of mats behind the ears and on the trousers. Goldens love water and mud, so a towel by the door is part of the deal.

Puppies and health

Goldens grow quickly and usually reach their adult height at about 12 months, then fill out until about 2 years. The puppy weight calculator gives an estimate for your puppy. Keep your dog lean, as extra weight is hard on the hips and elbows. Cancer is the breed's biggest health concern: in the Golden Retriever Club of America's health survey, it caused about 60% of deaths. Regular vet checks and attention to any new lumps, weight loss or tiredness are especially worthwhile in this breed.

Golden Retriever size: common questions

How big does a Golden Retriever get?

A fully grown male Golden Retriever stands 59–61 cm (23–24 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 30–34 kg (65–75 lb). Females stand 55–57 cm (21.5–22.4 in) and weigh 25–30 kg (55–65 lb).

Is a Golden Retriever a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 30 kg (65 lb), the Golden Retriever counts as a large dog breed on this site (large = 25–45 kg (55–99 lb) on average). It is taller than 64% of the 266 breeds listed.

How long does a Golden Retriever live?

Typical life expectancy is 10–12 years, shorter than the 12.1-year average for large breeds.

Can a Golden Retriever fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Golden Retriever weighs 25–34 kg (55–75 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).

Sizes are breed-standard ranges for adult dogs; individual dogs, working lines and dogs from different countries can be bigger or smaller. Airline notes are a general guide only. Check with your airline and your vet. Disclaimer.
