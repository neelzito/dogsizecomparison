How big is a Schnoodle (Miniature)?

The Schnoodle (Miniature) is a small-sized dog breed from the United States, in the designer & mixed category. Males stand 30–38 cm (11.8–15 in) at the shoulder and weigh 5.4–9 kg (12–20 lb). Females stand 28–36 cm (11–14.2 in) and weigh 5–8 kg (11–18 lb). On average it is taller than 23% of the 247 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 17%. At the shoulder, an average male Schnoodle (Miniature) reaches just below the knee on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 12–15 years, about average for small breeds. Lighter individuals fit airline cabin limits of 8 kg (17.6 lb) for dog and carrier; heavier ones need an airline that weighs only the carrier size, or travel in the hold.

Schnoodle (Miniature) size: common questions

How big does a Schnoodle (Miniature) get?

A fully grown male Schnoodle (Miniature) stands 30–38 cm (11.8–15 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 5.4–9 kg (12–20 lb). Females stand 28–36 cm (11–14.2 in) and weigh 5–8 kg (11–18 lb).

Is a Schnoodle (Miniature) a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 6.9 kg (15 lb), the Schnoodle (Miniature) counts as a small dog breed on this site (small = 5–10 kg (11–22 lb) on average). It is taller than 23% of the 247 breeds listed.

How long does a Schnoodle (Miniature) live?

Typical life expectancy is 12–15 years, about average for small breeds.

Can a Schnoodle (Miniature) fly in the airplane cabin?

Sometimes. Lighter individuals fit airline cabin limits of 8 kg (17.6 lb) for dog and carrier; heavier ones need an airline that weighs only the carrier size, or travel in the hold. Rules vary by airline, so check before booking.
