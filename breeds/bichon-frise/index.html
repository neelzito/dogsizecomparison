How big is a Bichon Frise?

The Bichon Frise is a small-sized dog breed from the Mediterranean, in the Non-Sporting group. Adults stand 24–29 cm (9.4–11.4 in) at the shoulder and weigh 5.4–8.2 kg (12–18 lb); the breed standard gives the same range for males and females. On average it is taller than 12% of the 256 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 15%. At the shoulder, an average Bichon Frise reaches about mid-shin on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 14–15 years, longer than the 13.7-year average for small breeds. Lighter individuals fit airline cabin limits of 8 kg (17.6 lb) for dog and carrier; heavier ones need an airline that weighs only the carrier size, or travel in the hold.

Sizes follow the American Kennel Club (AKC) breed standard.

Bichon Frise size: common questions

How big does a Bichon Frise get?

An adult Bichon Frise stands 24–29 cm (9.4–11.4 in) tall at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 5.4–8.2 kg (12–18 lb).

Is a Bichon Frise a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 6.8 kg (15 lb), the Bichon Frise counts as a small dog breed on this site (small = 5–10 kg (11–22 lb) on average). It is taller than 12% of the 256 breeds listed.

How long does a Bichon Frise live?

Typical life expectancy is 14–15 years, longer than the 13.7-year average for small breeds.

Can a Bichon Frise fly in the airplane cabin?

Sometimes. Lighter individuals fit airline cabin limits of 8 kg (17.6 lb) for dog and carrier; heavier ones need an airline that weighs only the carrier size, or travel in the hold. Rules vary by airline, so check before booking.
