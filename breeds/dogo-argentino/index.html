How big is a Dogo Argentino?

The Dogo Argentino is a large-sized dog breed from Argentina, in the Working group. Males stand 61–68 cm (24–26.6 in) at the shoulder and weigh 37–46 kg (80–100 lb). Females stand 61–65 cm (24–25.6 in) and weigh 37–46 kg (80–100 lb). On average it is taller than 82% of the 247 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 87%. At the shoulder, an average male Dogo Argentino reaches about mid-thigh on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 9–15 years, about average for large breeds.

Dogo Argentino size: common questions

How big does a Dogo Argentino get?

A fully grown male Dogo Argentino stands 61–68 cm (24–26.6 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 37–46 kg (80–100 lb). Females stand 61–65 cm (24–25.6 in) and weigh 37–46 kg (80–100 lb).

Is a Dogo Argentino a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 41 kg (90 lb), the Dogo Argentino counts as a large dog breed on this site (large = 25–45 kg (55–99 lb) on average). It is taller than 82% of the 247 breeds listed.

How long does a Dogo Argentino live?

Typical life expectancy is 9–15 years, about average for large breeds.

Can a Dogo Argentino fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Dogo Argentino weighs 37–46 kg (80–100 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).

Sizes are breed-standard ranges for adult dogs; individual dogs, working lines and dogs from different countries can be bigger or smaller. Airline notes are a general guide only. Check with your airline and your vet. Disclaimer.
