How big is an Affenpinscher?

The Affenpinscher is a toy dog breed from Germany, in the Toy group. Adults stand 23–29 cm (9.1–11.4 in) at the shoulder and weigh 3.2–4.5 kg (7.1–9.9 lb); the breed standard gives the same range for males and females. On average it is taller than 11% of the 267 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 5%. At the shoulder, an average Affenpinscher reaches about mid-shin on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 12–15 years, about average for toy breeds. At 3.2–4.5 kg (7.1–9.9 lb), it fits even the strictest airline cabin rule, where dog and carrier together may weigh 8 kg (17.6 lb).

Sizes follow the American Kennel Club (AKC) breed standard.

About the Affenpinscher

The Affenpinscher is a small German terrier-type dog. Its name means "monkey terrier", after its whiskery, almost human face. It was developed in the 17th century to catch rats in kitchens, stables and shops, and it later became a lapdog in its own right. It's thought to be an ancestor of the Brussels Griffon and the Miniature Schnauzer. In 2013 an Affenpinscher called Banana Joe won Best in Show at the Westminster Kennel Club show.

What an Affenpinscher's size means at home

The AKC standard calls for 9–11.5 in (23–29 cm) at the shoulder, and Affenpinschers weigh about 3–4.5 kg (7–10 lb). An Affen reaches around mid-shin on an adult. It's sturdy and square, with a rough, shaggy coat. It suits any home and usually needs a 24″ (61 cm) crate. Check with the crate size calculator. It easily fits the airline cabin limit. Affenpinscher vs Japanese Chin and Brussels Griffon

The Japanese Chin is about the same size but silky-coated and much more delicate. The Brussels Griffon looks similar, with a flat face and a beard, but it has a shorter muzzle and different coat colours.

Temperament

Affenpinschers are confident, curious and funny, with a big personality and no idea of their own size. They're affectionate with their family but can be territorial and bossy. They make alert watchdogs and can be vocal. They need daily walks and play, and short, fun, consistent training sessions work best. They're best with older children, who handle small dogs gently.

Coat and health

The rough, wiry coat sheds little. It needs brushing a couple of times a week and occasional trimming or hand-stripping to keep its tousled look. Known health problems include loose kneecaps, Legg-Calvé-Perthes disease (a hip condition), eye problems and breathing difficulties linked to the short muzzle. Affenpinschers often live 12–15 years.

Sources: AKC breed standard and breed history; FCI standard No. 186 (Affenpinscher); Westminster Kennel Club.

Affenpinscher size: common questions

How big does an Affenpinscher get?

An adult Affenpinscher stands 23–29 cm (9.1–11.4 in) tall at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 3.2–4.5 kg (7.1–9.9 lb).

Is an Affenpinscher a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 3.9 kg (8.5 lb), the Affenpinscher counts as a toy dog breed on this site (toy = under 5 kg (11 lb) on average). It is taller than 11% of the 267 breeds listed.

How long does an Affenpinscher live?

Typical life expectancy is 12–15 years, about average for toy breeds.

Can an Affenpinscher fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually yes. At 3.2–4.5 kg (7.1–9.9 lb), it fits even the strictest airline cabin rule, where dog and carrier together may weigh 8 kg (17.6 lb). Check your airline's rules and carrier size limits before booking.
