How big is a Podengo Galego?

The Podengo Galego is a medium-sized dog breed from Spain (Galicia), in the Hound group. Males stand 46–52 cm (18.1–20.5 in) at the shoulder and weigh 10–15 kg (22–33 lb). Females stand 42–46 cm (16.5–18.1 in) and weigh 10–15 kg (22–33 lb). On average it is taller than 45% of the 267 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 35%. At the shoulder, an average male Podengo Galego reaches about knee height on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 12–14 years, about average for medium breeds. It is too heavy for airlines that cap dog and carrier at 8 kg (17.6 lb), so it usually flies in the hold unless the airline only checks the carrier size.

About the Podengo Galego

The Podengo Galego is the podenco of Galicia, in north-west Spain. It's found mostly in the provinces of Pontevedra and Ourense, where hunters use it to flush rabbits, hares and foxes out of dense gorse and heather. Like all Iberian podencos it shares a common origin with the Podenco Ibicenco, Podenco Andaluz, Portuguese Podengo and Pharaoh Hound, and it's closest to the medium Portuguese Podengo from just across the border. The Galician government (Xunta de Galicia) recognises it as a native breed with an official standard. The FCI does not recognise it.

What a Podengo Galego's size means at home

The official standard gives a height of 46–52 cm at the shoulder for males and 42–46 cm for females, and a weight of 10–15 kg. That's a light, medium-sized dog that reaches about knee height on an adult. It's about the size of the medium Podenco Andaluz, but more lightly built. It's too heavy for most airline cabins.

Podengo Galego vs Podenco Andaluz and Portuguese Podengo

Next to a medium Podenco Andaluz, a Podengo Galego covers a similar height range but is usually lighter. The medium Portuguese Podengo is its closest relative and very similar in type, with a slightly wider height range.

Living with a Podengo Galego

Podengos Galegos are clever, lively and tireless, with a strong hunting instinct. They hunt by scent, sight and hearing, so recall can be unreliable near rabbits, cats or birds, and they need a secure garden. The standard describes them as wary of strangers and alert, so they often bark when someone comes to the house. They need daily walks and a chance to run.

Care and health

The coat is semi-short to medium length, thick and dense, usually cinnamon from sand to red, often with a white collar, chest and feet. It needs very little grooming. The standard gives no life span, and little breed-specific health data exists. Like other podencos, they're generally hardy and often live 12–14 years.

Sources: official breed standard of the Podengo Galego, Xunta de Galicia (Consellería do Medio Rural).

Podengo Galego size: common questions

How big does a Podengo Galego get?

A fully grown male Podengo Galego stands 46–52 cm (18.1–20.5 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 10–15 kg (22–33 lb). Females stand 42–46 cm (16.5–18.1 in) and weigh 10–15 kg (22–33 lb).

Is a Podengo Galego a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 13 kg (28 lb), the Podengo Galego counts as a medium dog breed on this site (medium = 10–25 kg (22–55 lb) on average). It is taller than 45% of the 267 breeds listed.

How long does a Podengo Galego live?

Typical life expectancy is 12–14 years, about average for medium breeds.

Can a Podengo Galego fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Podengo Galego weighs 10–15 kg (22–33 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).
