How big is a Podenco Andaluz (Large)?

The Podenco Andaluz (Large) is a large-sized dog breed from Spain, in the Hound group. Males stand 54–64 cm (21.3–25.2 in) at the shoulder and weigh 21–33 kg (46–73 lb). Females stand 53–61 cm (20.9–24 in) and weigh 21–33 kg (46–73 lb). On average it is taller than 65% of the 268 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 69%.

Podenco Andaluz (Large) compared to a human

Next to a 175 cm (5′9″) adult, an average male Podenco Andaluz (Large) stands 59 cm (23.2 in) at the shoulder: 34% of the person's height, reaching about mid-thigh. An average female, at 57 cm (22.4 in), reaches about knee height. In feet, males stand 1 ft 9 in to 2 ft 1 in tall at the shoulder and females 1 ft 9 in to 2 ft. Next to a 120 cm (3′11″) child, about seven years old, the shoulder comes to about hip height. The head sits higher than the shoulder, so the dog looks taller than these numbers suggest.

Typical life expectancy is 12–14 years, longer than the 12.1-year average for large breeds.

About the Podenco Andaluz (Large)

The large Podenco Andaluz (Podenco Andaluz grande) is the biggest of the three sizes of the Andalusian Hound. In southern Spain it was traditionally used on bigger game such as boar and deer, as well as rabbits, often in packs. Like all Podencos, it hunts by scent, sight and hearing at once, and it has the classic large upright ears and lean build of the Mediterranean warren hounds. For the breed's history and the three sizes and coats, see the medium Podenco Andaluz.

What a large Podenco's size means at home

The RSCE standard (No. 401) calls for 54–64 cm at the shoulder for males and 53–61 cm for females, and about 27 kg ± 6 kg. A large Podenco reaches roughly an adult's mid-thigh. It's strong, athletic and a great jumper, so garden fences need to be high, ideally 1.8 m (6 ft) or more. The crate size calculator suggests a 48″ (122 cm) crate for an average male.

Large Podenco vs Ibizan Hound and Podenco Valenciano

The Ibizan Hound is taller and lighter. The Podenco Valenciano is about the same height and lighter. A Treeing Walker Coonhound is a little taller and about the same weight.

Living with a large Podenco

Large Podencos are affectionate and gentle at home, and usually good with other dogs. Their hunting instinct is strong, so recall is often unreliable and cats may not be safe. They need daily exercise and a secure area to run. Many large Podencos come from Spanish rescue organisations, which rehome them across Europe.

Care and health

The coat can be smooth, wiry or long, and it's easy to care for. Podencos are generally hardy and often live 12–14 years.

Sources: RSCE breed standard No. 401 (Podenco Andaluz).

Podenco Andaluz (Large) size: common questions

How big does a Podenco Andaluz (Large) get?

A fully grown male Podenco Andaluz (Large) stands 54–64 cm (21.3–25.2 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 21–33 kg (46–73 lb). Females stand 53–61 cm (20.9–24 in) and weigh 21–33 kg (46–73 lb).

How tall is a Podenco Andaluz (Large) in feet?

A male Podenco Andaluz (Large) stands 1 ft 9 in to 2 ft 1 in tall at the shoulder (54–64 cm) and a female 1 ft 9 in to 2 ft (53–61 cm). Next to a 5′9″ adult, an average male reaches about mid-thigh.

Is a Podenco Andaluz (Large) a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 27 kg (60 lb), the Podenco Andaluz (Large) counts as a large dog breed on this site (large = 25–45 kg (55–99 lb) on average). It is taller than 65% of the 268 breeds listed.

How long does a Podenco Andaluz (Large) live?

Typical life expectancy is 12–14 years, longer than the 12.1-year average for large breeds.

Can a Podenco Andaluz (Large) fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Podenco Andaluz (Large) weighs 21–33 kg (46–73 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).
