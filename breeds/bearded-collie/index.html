How big is a Bearded Collie?

The Bearded Collie is a medium-sized dog breed from Scotland, in the Herding group. Males stand 54–56 cm (21.1–22 in) at the shoulder and weigh 21–25 kg (45–55 lb). Females stand 51–54 cm (20.1–21.1 in) and weigh 21–25 kg (45–55 lb). On average it is taller than 56% of the 256 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 55%. At the shoulder, an average male Bearded Collie reaches about knee height on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 12–14 years, about average for medium breeds. It is too heavy for airlines that cap dog and carrier at 8 kg (17.6 lb), so it usually flies in the hold unless the airline only checks the carrier size.

About the Bearded Collie

The Bearded Collie is a herding dog from the Scottish Highlands and Borders, used for centuries to work sheep and cattle in rough terrain. It is probably descended from Polish Lowland Sheepdogs brought to Scotland by traders, crossed with local herding dogs. As a working dog it was never standardised, and by the Second World War show "Beardies" had almost died out. In the 1940s Mrs G. Olive Willison began breeding from a pair of Beardies, and today's breed descends largely from her dogs. Known for its bouncing energy and sense of humour, the Beardie is a lively, affectionate companion.

What a Beardie's size means at home

The AKC standard calls for 21–22 in (53.5–56 cm) at the shoulder for males and 20–21 in (51–53.5 cm) for females. Beardies weigh about 20.5–25 kg (45–55 lb). A Beardie reaches roughly an adult's knee, and its long coat makes it look bigger. It needs a medium to large car boot, and the crate size calculator suggests a 42″ (107 cm) crate for an average adult. It's too heavy for an airline cabin.

Beardie vs Australian Shepherd and Old English Sheepdog

The Beardie is about the same height as an Australian Shepherd but usually lighter, with a much longer coat and a long tail. The Old English Sheepdog looks similar but is bigger and heavier, with a stockier build and a naturally short or docked tail.

Temperament and exercise

Beardies are cheerful, enthusiastic and full of energy, with a bouncy gait and a playful personality. They're affectionate with their family and usually good with children and other dogs. They're clever but can be independent and stubborn, so training should be fun and consistent. They need plenty of daily exercise and enjoy herding, agility and long walks.

Coat care

The Beardie's long, harsh double coat is beautiful but high-maintenance. It needs a thorough brush, down to the skin, at least once or twice a week, more during moults, to prevent mats. Puppies are born black, brown, blue or fawn, and many lighten as they grow, sometimes dramatically, before darkening again in adulthood.

Health

Beardies are generally healthy. Known problems include hip dysplasia, hypothyroidism, Addison's disease and autoimmune conditions. They often live 12–14 years.

Bearded Collie size: common questions

How big does a Bearded Collie get?

A fully grown male Bearded Collie stands 54–56 cm (21.1–22 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 21–25 kg (45–55 lb). Females stand 51–54 cm (20.1–21.1 in) and weigh 21–25 kg (45–55 lb).

Is a Bearded Collie a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 23 kg (50 lb), the Bearded Collie counts as a medium dog breed on this site (medium = 10–25 kg (22–55 lb) on average). It is taller than 56% of the 256 breeds listed.

How long does a Bearded Collie live?

Typical life expectancy is 12–14 years, about average for medium breeds.

Can a Bearded Collie fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Bearded Collie weighs 21–25 kg (45–55 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).

Sizes are breed-standard ranges for adult dogs; individual dogs, working lines and dogs from different countries can be bigger or smaller. Airline notes are a general guide only. Check with your airline and your vet. Disclaimer.
