How big is a Cardigan Welsh Corgi?

The Cardigan Welsh Corgi is a medium-sized dog breed from Wales, in the Herding group. Males stand 27–32 cm (10.4–12.6 in) at the shoulder and weigh 14–17 kg (30–37 lb). Females stand 27–32 cm (10.4–12.6 in) and weigh 12–16 kg (25–34 lb). On average it is taller than 17% of the 256 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 38%. At the shoulder, an average male Cardigan Welsh Corgi reaches about mid-shin on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 12–15 years, about average for medium breeds. It is too heavy for airlines that cap dog and carrier at 8 kg (17.6 lb), so it usually flies in the hold unless the airline only checks the carrier size.

About the Cardigan Welsh Corgi

The Cardigan Welsh Corgi is the older of the two Welsh corgi breeds, from Cardiganshire in west Wales. Its ancestors are thought to have arrived with Celtic peoples who settled in Wales centuries ago. Farmers used Cardigans to drive cattle by nipping at their heels and to guard the farm. The Cardigan and the Pembroke were shown as one breed until 1934, when The Kennel Club separated them. The Cardigan is less well known than its royal cousin, but it's a steady, loyal and clever dog.

What a Cardigan's size means at home

A Cardigan stands about 27–32 cm at the shoulder, around mid-shin on an adult, but it is long and solidly built: males weigh about 13.5–17 kg (30–38 lb) and females 11.5–15.5 kg (25–34 lb). That's heavy enough to put it in the medium size class on this site, even though it's short. It usually needs a 30″ (76 cm) crate because of its long body. See the crate size calculator. A Cardigan is too heavy for an airline cabin.

Cardigan vs Pembroke

The Pembroke Welsh Corgi is a little smaller and lighter. The easiest way to tell them apart:

  • Tail: the Cardigan has a long, fox-like tail. The Pembroke's is naturally short or traditionally docked.
  • Ears: the Cardigan's are larger and more rounded at the tips.
  • Colours: Cardigans come in more colours, including blue merle and brindle. Pembrokes are mostly red, sable, fawn or tricolour.
  • Build: the Cardigan is heavier-boned, with slightly bowed front legs.

Short legs, long back

Like the Pembroke and the Dachshund, the Cardigan's short legs come from a form of dwarfism (chondrodysplasia), and its long back can be prone to disc problems. Keep your Cardigan lean, use ramps instead of letting it jump off high furniture, and support its back when lifting.

Temperament and care

Cardigans are calmer and more reserved than many Pembrokes. They're loyal and affectionate with their family, often wary of strangers, and make good watchdogs. They're true herders, so they need daily walks, games and training, and they may nip at heels. The double coat sheds heavily, especially in spring and autumn. Known health problems include degenerative myelopathy, a spinal cord disease (a DNA test exists), and progressive retinal atrophy (PRA). Cardigans often live 12–15 years.

Cardigan Welsh Corgi size: common questions

How big does a Cardigan Welsh Corgi get?

A fully grown male Cardigan Welsh Corgi stands 27–32 cm (10.4–12.6 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 14–17 kg (30–37 lb). Females stand 27–32 cm (10.4–12.6 in) and weigh 12–16 kg (25–34 lb).

Is a Cardigan Welsh Corgi a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 14 kg (32 lb), the Cardigan Welsh Corgi counts as a medium dog breed on this site (medium = 10–25 kg (22–55 lb) on average). It is taller than 17% of the 256 breeds listed.

How long does a Cardigan Welsh Corgi live?

Typical life expectancy is 12–15 years, about average for medium breeds.

Can a Cardigan Welsh Corgi fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Cardigan Welsh Corgi weighs 12–17 kg (25–37 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).
