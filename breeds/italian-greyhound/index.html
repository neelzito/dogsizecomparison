How big is an Italian Greyhound?

The Italian Greyhound is a toy dog breed from Italy, in the Toy group. Adults stand 33–38 cm (13–15 in) at the shoulder and weigh 3.2–6.4 kg (7.1–14 lb); the breed standard gives the same range for males and females. On average it is taller than 27% of the 247 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 8%. At the shoulder, an average Italian Greyhound reaches just below the knee on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 14–15 years, longer than the 13.9-year average for toy breeds. At 3.2–6.4 kg (7.1–14 lb), it fits even the strictest airline cabin rule, where dog and carrier together may weigh 8 kg (17.6 lb).

Italian Greyhound size: common questions

How big does an Italian Greyhound get?

An adult Italian Greyhound stands 33–38 cm (13–15 in) tall at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 3.2–6.4 kg (7.1–14 lb).

Is an Italian Greyhound a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 4.8 kg (11 lb), the Italian Greyhound counts as a toy dog breed on this site (toy = under 5 kg (11 lb) on average). It is taller than 27% of the 247 breeds listed.

How long does an Italian Greyhound live?

Typical life expectancy is 14–15 years, longer than the 13.9-year average for toy breeds.

Can an Italian Greyhound fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually yes. At 3.2–6.4 kg (7.1–14 lb), it fits even the strictest airline cabin rule, where dog and carrier together may weigh 8 kg (17.6 lb). Check your airline's rules and carrier size limits before booking.
