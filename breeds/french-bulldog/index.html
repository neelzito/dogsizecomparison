How big is a French Bulldog?

The French Bulldog is a medium-sized dog breed from France, in the Non-Sporting group. Males stand 28–33 cm (11–13 in) at the shoulder and weigh 9–13 kg (20–28 lb). Females stand 28–33 cm (11–13 in) and weigh 8–13 kg (18–28 lb). On average it is taller than 18% of the 256 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 29%. At the shoulder, an average male French Bulldog reaches about mid-shin on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 10–12 years, shorter than the 13.3-year average for medium breeds. It is too heavy for airlines that cap dog and carrier at 8 kg (17.6 lb), so it usually flies in the hold unless the airline only checks the carrier size.

French Bulldog size: common questions

How big does a French Bulldog get?

A fully grown male French Bulldog stands 28–33 cm (11–13 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 9–13 kg (20–28 lb). Females stand 28–33 cm (11–13 in) and weigh 8–13 kg (18–28 lb).

Is a French Bulldog a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 11 kg (23 lb), the French Bulldog counts as a medium dog breed on this site (medium = 10–25 kg (22–55 lb) on average). It is taller than 18% of the 256 breeds listed.

How long does a French Bulldog live?

Typical life expectancy is 10–12 years, shorter than the 13.3-year average for medium breeds.

Can a French Bulldog fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A French Bulldog weighs 8–13 kg (18–28 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).

Sizes are breed-standard ranges for adult dogs; individual dogs, working lines and dogs from different countries can be bigger or smaller. Airline notes are a general guide only. Check with your airline and your vet. Disclaimer.
