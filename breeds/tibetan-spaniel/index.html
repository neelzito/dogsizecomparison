How big is a Tibetan Spaniel?

The Tibetan Spaniel is a small-sized dog breed from China (Tibet), in the Non-Sporting group. Adults stand 26 cm (10 in) at the shoulder and weigh 4.1–6.8 kg (9–15 lb); the breed standard gives the same range for males and females. On average it is taller than 9% of the 268 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 11%.

Tibetan Spaniel compared to a human

Next to a 175 cm (5′9″) adult, an average Tibetan Spaniel stands 26 cm (10 in) at the shoulder: 15% of the person's height, reaching about mid-shin. In feet, adults stand 10 in tall at the shoulder. Next to a 120 cm (3′11″) child, about seven years old, the shoulder comes to just below the knee. The head sits higher than the shoulder, so the dog looks taller than these numbers suggest.

Typical life expectancy is 12–15 years, about average for small breeds. Lighter individuals fit airline cabin limits of 8 kg (17.6 lb) for dog and carrier; heavier ones need an airline that weighs only the carrier size, or travel in the hold.

About the Tibetan Spaniel

The Tibetan Spaniel isn't really a spaniel. It's an ancient companion dog from the monasteries of Tibet, where it sat on the walls as a lookout and was known as a "little lion". It was given as a gift rather than sold. It reached Britain in the early 20th century. It's small, alert and confident, with a silky coat and a plumed tail curled over its back.

What a Tibbie's size means at home

The AKC standard calls for about 10 in (25.5 cm) at the shoulder, and Tibbies weigh about 4–7 kg (9–15 lb). A Tibbie reaches around mid-shin on an adult. It suits any home and usually needs a 24″ (61 cm) crate. Check with the crate size calculator. Lighter Tibbies may fit the cabin limit. Check with the airline cabin check.

Tibetan Spaniel vs Volpino and Pekingese

The Volpino Italiano is a little taller and heavier. The Pekingese has a much flatter face and a heavier coat.

Temperament and exercise

Tibbies are affectionate, clever and independent, devoted to their family and reserved with strangers. They like high places to watch from. They need daily walks and play.

Coat and health

The silky double coat needs brushing a couple of times a week. Known health problems include progressive retinal atrophy (PRA) and loose kneecaps. Tibbies often live 12–15 years.

Sources: AKC breed standard and breed history; FCI standard No. 231 (Tibetan Spaniel).

Tibetan Spaniel size: common questions

How big does a Tibetan Spaniel get?

An adult Tibetan Spaniel stands 26 cm (10 in) tall at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 4.1–6.8 kg (9–15 lb).

How tall is a Tibetan Spaniel in feet?

An adult Tibetan Spaniel stands 10 in tall at the shoulder (26 cm). Next to a 5′9″ adult, that reaches about mid-shin.

Is a Tibetan Spaniel a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 5.4 kg (12 lb), the Tibetan Spaniel counts as a small dog breed on this site (small = 5–10 kg (11–22 lb) on average). It is taller than 9% of the 268 breeds listed.

How long does a Tibetan Spaniel live?

Typical life expectancy is 12–15 years, about average for small breeds.

Can a Tibetan Spaniel fly in the airplane cabin?

Sometimes. Lighter individuals fit airline cabin limits of 8 kg (17.6 lb) for dog and carrier; heavier ones need an airline that weighs only the carrier size, or travel in the hold. Rules vary by airline, so check before booking.
