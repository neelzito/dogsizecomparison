How big is a Cavapoo?

The Cavapoo is a small-sized dog breed from Australia, in the designer & mixed category. Males stand 23–36 cm (9.1–14.2 in) at the shoulder and weigh 4.5–11 kg (9.9–24 lb). Females stand 23–34 cm (9.1–13.4 in) and weigh 4.5–9 kg (9.9–20 lb). On average it is taller than 17% of the 267 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 19%. At the shoulder, an average male Cavapoo reaches about mid-shin on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 12–15 years, about average for small breeds. Lighter individuals fit airline cabin limits of 8 kg (17.6 lb) for dog and carrier; heavier ones need an airline that weighs only the carrier size, or travel in the hold.

About the Cavapoo

The Cavapoo (also called the Cavoodle in Australia) is a cross between a Cavalier King Charles Spaniel and a Poodle, usually a Miniature or Toy Poodle. It was first bred in Australia in the 1990s and has since become one of the most popular small crossbreeds in Australia, the UK and beyond. It combines the Cavalier's sweet, affectionate nature with the Poodle's intelligence and often low-shedding coat. As a crossbreed, it has no official standard.

How big does a Cavapoo get?

A Cavapoo's size depends on the Poodle parent. Most are about 23–36 cm at the shoulder, around mid-shin on an adult, and weigh about 4.5–11 kg (10–24 lb). Cavapoos with a Toy Poodle parent are at the small end, and those with a Miniature Poodle parent at the larger end. Puppies in one litter can still differ quite a bit, so ask about both parents' size, and use the puppy weight calculator as a guide.

Next to a Cavalier, a Cavapoo is usually similar in size or a little smaller. A Cockapoo tends to be slightly bigger and more energetic, while a Maltipoo is usually smaller.

What a Cavapoo's size means at home

A Cavapoo is an easy size for flats, small cars and laps, and it usually needs a 24″ (61 cm) crate. Check with the crate size calculator. It enjoys daily walks and play but doesn't need hours of exercise. Cavapoos are very people-focused and often struggle when left alone for long.

Can a Cavapoo fly in the cabin?

Smaller Cavapoos often can. Many airlines limit dog and carrier together to 8 kg (17.6 lb), which leaves about 6.5 kg for the dog. A Cavapoo from a Toy Poodle parent may fit, but larger ones won't. Use the airline cabin check.

Coat and health

Coats range from tight curls to loose waves. They shed little but tangle easily, so brush several times a week and book a trim every 6–8 weeks. Floppy ears need regular cleaning. Cavapoos can inherit the Cavalier's heart disease (mitral valve disease), along with eye problems and loose kneecaps. Choose a breeder who heart-tests the Cavalier parent and screens both parents' eyes and joints.

Sources: Cavapoo Club UK; AKC breed standards for the Cavalier King Charles Spaniel and the Poodle.

Cavapoo size: common questions

How big does a Cavapoo get?

A fully grown male Cavapoo stands 23–36 cm (9.1–14.2 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 4.5–11 kg (9.9–24 lb). Females stand 23–34 cm (9.1–13.4 in) and weigh 4.5–9 kg (9.9–20 lb).

Is a Cavapoo a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 7.3 kg (16 lb), the Cavapoo counts as a small dog breed on this site (small = 5–10 kg (11–22 lb) on average). It is taller than 17% of the 267 breeds listed.

How long does a Cavapoo live?

Typical life expectancy is 12–15 years, about average for small breeds.

Can a Cavapoo fly in the airplane cabin?

Sometimes. Lighter individuals fit airline cabin limits of 8 kg (17.6 lb) for dog and carrier; heavier ones need an airline that weighs only the carrier size, or travel in the hold. Rules vary by airline, so check before booking.
