How big is a Redbone Coonhound?

The Redbone Coonhound is a large-sized dog breed from the United States, in the Hound group. Males stand 56–69 cm (22–27 in) at the shoulder and weigh 21–32 kg (45–71 lb). Females stand 54–66 cm (21.1–26 in) and weigh 21–32 kg (45–71 lb). On average it is taller than 71% of the 247 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 64%. At the shoulder, an average male Redbone Coonhound reaches about mid-thigh on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 12–15 years, longer than the 12.1-year average for large breeds.

Redbone Coonhound size: common questions

How big does a Redbone Coonhound get?

A fully grown male Redbone Coonhound stands 56–69 cm (22–27 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 21–32 kg (45–71 lb). Females stand 54–66 cm (21.1–26 in) and weigh 21–32 kg (45–71 lb).

Is a Redbone Coonhound a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 26 kg (58 lb), the Redbone Coonhound counts as a large dog breed on this site (large = 25–45 kg (55–99 lb) on average). It is taller than 71% of the 247 breeds listed.

How long does a Redbone Coonhound live?

Typical life expectancy is 12–15 years, longer than the 12.1-year average for large breeds.

Can a Redbone Coonhound fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Redbone Coonhound weighs 21–32 kg (45–71 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).
