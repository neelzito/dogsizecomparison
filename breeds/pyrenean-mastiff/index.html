How big is a Pyrenean Mastiff?

The Pyrenean Mastiff is a giant-sized dog breed from Spain, in the Working group. Males stand 76–79 cm (29.9–30.9 in) at the shoulder and weigh 55–86 kg (120–190 lb). Females stand 71–76 cm (28–29.9 in) and weigh 55–86 kg (120–190 lb). On average that makes it the 6th tallest of the 266 breeds on this site. At the shoulder, an average male Pyrenean Mastiff reaches about hip height on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 10–12 years, longer than the 10.1-year average for giant breeds.

About the Pyrenean Mastiff

The Pyrenean Mastiff (Mastín del Pirineo) is a giant livestock guardian from the Spanish side of the Pyrenees, in Aragon and Navarre. It protected flocks during their seasonal migrations between summer and winter pastures, guarding against wolves and bears. It's white with darker patches on the head and body. It's calm, gentle with its family and very protective.

What a Pyrenean Mastiff's size means at home

The breed standard sets minimum heights of about 77 cm for males and 72 cm for females. Most weigh about 54.5–86 kg (120–190 lb). A Pyrenean Mastiff reaches roughly an adult's hip, and its weight puts it in the giant size class on this site. It needs a large vehicle, a 54″ (137 cm) crate (see the crate size calculator) and a large, secure property.

Pyrenean Mastiff vs Great Dane and Spanish Mastiff

A Great Dane is about the same height and weight, but much leaner and short-coated. The Spanish Mastiff is a close relative, usually solid-coloured.

Temperament and care

Pyrenean Mastiffs are calm, noble and gentle with their family, but protective and wary of strangers. They need early socialisation and an experienced owner. The thick double coat sheds seasonally. Expect drool. Known health problems include hip dysplasia and bloat. They often live 10–12 years.

Sources: FCI standard No. 92 (Mastín del Pirineo); AKC breed information.

Pyrenean Mastiff size: common questions

How big does a Pyrenean Mastiff get?

A fully grown male Pyrenean Mastiff stands 76–79 cm (29.9–30.9 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 55–86 kg (120–190 lb). Females stand 71–76 cm (28–29.9 in) and weigh 55–86 kg (120–190 lb).

Is a Pyrenean Mastiff a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 70 kg (155 lb), the Pyrenean Mastiff counts as a giant dog breed on this site (giant = 45 kg (99 lb) and over on average). It is the 6th tallest of the 266 breeds listed.

How long does a Pyrenean Mastiff live?

Typical life expectancy is 10–12 years, longer than the 10.1-year average for giant breeds.

Can a Pyrenean Mastiff fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Pyrenean Mastiff weighs 55–86 kg (120–190 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).

Sizes are breed-standard ranges for adult dogs; individual dogs, working lines and dogs from different countries can be bigger or smaller. Airline notes are a general guide only. Check with your airline and your vet. Disclaimer.
