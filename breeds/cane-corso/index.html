How big is a Cane Corso?

The Cane Corso is a giant-sized dog breed from Italy, in the Working group. Males stand 64–70 cm (25–27.6 in) at the shoulder and weigh 45–50 kg (99–110 lb). Females stand 60–66 cm (23.4–26 in) and weigh 40–45 kg (88–99 lb). On average it is taller than 83% of the 247 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 89%. At the shoulder, an average male Cane Corso reaches about mid-thigh on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 9–12 years, about average for giant breeds.

Cane Corso size: common questions

How big does a Cane Corso get?

A fully grown male Cane Corso stands 64–70 cm (25–27.6 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 45–50 kg (99–110 lb). Females stand 60–66 cm (23.4–26 in) and weigh 40–45 kg (88–99 lb).

Is a Cane Corso a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 45 kg (99 lb), the Cane Corso counts as a giant dog breed on this site (giant = 45 kg (99 lb) and over on average). It is taller than 83% of the 247 breeds listed.

How long does a Cane Corso live?

Typical life expectancy is 9–12 years, about average for giant breeds.

Can a Cane Corso fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Cane Corso weighs 40–50 kg (88–110 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).

Sizes are breed-standard ranges for adult dogs; individual dogs, working lines and dogs from different countries can be bigger or smaller. Airline notes are a general guide only. Check with your airline and your vet. Disclaimer.
