How big is a Lakeland Terrier?

The Lakeland Terrier is a small-sized dog breed from England, in the Terrier group. Males stand 37–38 cm (14.6–15 in) at the shoulder and weigh 7.7 kg (17 lb). Females stand 33–36 cm (13–14.2 in) and weigh 6.5–7.5 kg (14–17 lb). On average it is taller than 29% of the 256 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 20%. At the shoulder, an average male Lakeland Terrier reaches just below the knee on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 12–15 years, about average for small breeds. Lighter individuals fit airline cabin limits of 8 kg (17.6 lb) for dog and carrier; heavier ones need an airline that weighs only the carrier size, or travel in the hold.

About the Lakeland Terrier

The Lakeland Terrier comes from the Lake District in north-west England, where farmers used it to hunt foxes that preyed on lambs in the rocky fells. It had to be tough enough to work in cold, wet weather, narrow enough to follow a fox into rocky dens, and leggy enough to keep up with the hounds. It's closely related to the Welsh and Fell terriers. A Lakeland won Best in Show at Crufts in 1963 and 1967, and at Westminster in 1968 and 1976.

What a Lakeland's size means at home

The AKC standard calls for about 14.5 in (37 cm) at the shoulder for males, with females a little smaller, and about 17 lb (7.7 kg) for males. A Lakeland reaches just below an adult's knee. It's square, narrow and agile. It usually needs a 30″ (76 cm) crate. Check with the crate size calculator. Lighter Lakelands in a light carrier may fit the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit. Check with the airline cabin check.

Lakeland vs Welsh and Rat Terrier

The Lakeland looks like a small Welsh Terrier or Airedale, but it comes in more colours, including black and tan, blue, red, liver and wheaten. The Rat Terrier is about the same size, with a smooth coat.

Temperament and exercise

Lakelands are bold, cheerful and confident, with lots of energy and a sense of humour. They need daily exercise and mental stimulation, and they love to dig and chase, so a secure garden is a must. They can be stubborn and may not tolerate other dogs.

Coat and health

The wiry coat sheds very little and is traditionally hand-stripped a few times a year. Lakelands are generally healthy. Known problems include eye conditions such as lens luxation, and Legg-Calvé-Perthes disease. They often live 12–15 years.

Lakeland Terrier size: common questions

How big does a Lakeland Terrier get?

A fully grown male Lakeland Terrier stands 37–38 cm (14.6–15 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 7.7 kg (17 lb). Females stand 33–36 cm (13–14.2 in) and weigh 6.5–7.5 kg (14–17 lb).

Is a Lakeland Terrier a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 7.4 kg (16 lb), the Lakeland Terrier counts as a small dog breed on this site (small = 5–10 kg (11–22 lb) on average). It is taller than 29% of the 256 breeds listed.

How long does a Lakeland Terrier live?

Typical life expectancy is 12–15 years, about average for small breeds.

Can a Lakeland Terrier fly in the airplane cabin?

Sometimes. Lighter individuals fit airline cabin limits of 8 kg (17.6 lb) for dog and carrier; heavier ones need an airline that weighs only the carrier size, or travel in the hold. Rules vary by airline, so check before booking.
