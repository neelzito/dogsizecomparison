How big is an Entlebucher Mountain Dog?

The Entlebucher Mountain Dog is a medium-sized dog breed from Switzerland, in the Herding group. Males stand 45–52 cm (17.5–20.5 in) at the shoulder and weigh 23–30 kg (50–65 lb). Females stand 42–50 cm (16.5–19.5 in) and weigh 18–25 kg (40–55 lb). On average it is taller than 46% of the 266 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 59%. At the shoulder, an average male Entlebucher Mountain Dog reaches about knee height on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 11–13 years, shorter than the 13.2-year average for medium breeds. It is too heavy for airlines that cap dog and carrier at 8 kg (17.6 lb), so it usually flies in the hold unless the airline only checks the carrier size.

About the Entlebucher Mountain Dog

The Entlebucher Mountain Dog (Entlebucher Sennenhund) is the smallest of the four Swiss mountain dog breeds. It's named after the Entlebuch valley in the canton of Lucerne, where farmers used it to drive cattle to the high pastures and guard the farm. The breed was nearly lost by the early 20th century, but Swiss enthusiasts revived it, and the first breed club was founded in 1926. It shares the tricolour coat of the Bernese and Greater Swiss Mountain Dogs, in a compact, energetic package.

What an Entlebucher's size means at home

The AKC standard calls for 17–21 in (44.5–52 cm) at the shoulder for males and 16–20 in (42–49.5 cm) for females. Entlebuchers weigh about 22.5–29.5 kg (50–65 lb) for males and 18–25 kg (40–55 lb) for females. An Entlebucher reaches roughly an adult's knee. It's compact, muscular and a little longer than tall. It usually needs a 36″ (91 cm) crate. Check with the crate size calculator. It's too heavy for an airline cabin.

The four Swiss mountain dogs

From smallest to largest: the Entlebucher, the Appenzeller, the Bernese Mountain Dog and the Greater Swiss Mountain Dog. The Entlebucher is much smaller than the Bernese and the Swissy and more of a herder. Next to an Australian Shepherd, it's a little shorter but about as heavy.

Temperament and exercise

Entlebuchers are lively, clever, confident and very loyal to their family, often wary of strangers. They're true herding dogs, with lots of energy and a tendency to nip at heels. They need plenty of daily exercise and mental work, such as herding, agility, obedience or long walks. They can be strong-willed, so consistent, positive training and early socialisation are important.

Coat and health

The short, dense, shiny double coat sheds seasonally and needs only weekly brushing. Known health problems include hip and elbow dysplasia, progressive retinal atrophy (PRA), for which a DNA test exists, and Entlebucher urinary syndrome, which involves abnormalities of the urinary tract. Entlebuchers often live 11–13 years.

Entlebucher Mountain Dog size: common questions

How big does an Entlebucher Mountain Dog get?

A fully grown male Entlebucher Mountain Dog stands 45–52 cm (17.5–20.5 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 23–30 kg (50–65 lb). Females stand 42–50 cm (16.5–19.5 in) and weigh 18–25 kg (40–55 lb).

Is an Entlebucher Mountain Dog a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 24 kg (52 lb), the Entlebucher Mountain Dog counts as a medium dog breed on this site (medium = 10–25 kg (22–55 lb) on average). It is taller than 46% of the 266 breeds listed.

How long does an Entlebucher Mountain Dog live?

Typical life expectancy is 11–13 years, shorter than the 13.2-year average for medium breeds.

Can an Entlebucher Mountain Dog fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Entlebucher Mountain Dog weighs 18–30 kg (40–65 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).
