How big is a Weimaraner?

The Weimaraner is a large-sized dog breed from Germany, in the Sporting group. Males stand 64–69 cm (25–27 in) at the shoulder and weigh 32–41 kg (71–90 lb). Females stand 59–64 cm (23–25 in) and weigh 25–34 kg (55–75 lb). On average it is taller than 79% of the 247 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 80%. At the shoulder, an average male Weimaraner reaches about mid-thigh on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 10–13 years, shorter than the 12.1-year average for large breeds.

Weimaraner size: common questions

How big does a Weimaraner get?

A fully grown male Weimaraner stands 64–69 cm (25–27 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 32–41 kg (71–90 lb). Females stand 59–64 cm (23–25 in) and weigh 25–34 kg (55–75 lb).

Is a Weimaraner a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 33 kg (73 lb), the Weimaraner counts as a large dog breed on this site (large = 25–45 kg (55–99 lb) on average). It is taller than 79% of the 247 breeds listed.

How long does a Weimaraner live?

Typical life expectancy is 10–13 years, shorter than the 12.1-year average for large breeds.

Can a Weimaraner fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Weimaraner weighs 25–41 kg (55–90 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).

Sizes are breed-standard ranges for adult dogs; individual dogs, working lines and dogs from different countries can be bigger or smaller. Airline notes are a general guide only. Check with your airline and your vet. Disclaimer.
