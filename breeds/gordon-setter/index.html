How big is a Gordon Setter?

The Gordon Setter is a large-sized dog breed from Scotland, in the Sporting group. Males stand 61–69 cm (24–27 in) at the shoulder and weigh 25–37 kg (55–80 lb). Females stand 59–66 cm (23–26 in) and weigh 21–32 kg (45–71 lb). On average it is taller than 80% of the 256 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 74%. At the shoulder, an average male Gordon Setter reaches about mid-thigh on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 12–13 years, about average for large breeds.

About the Gordon Setter

The Gordon Setter is Scotland's setter, named after the 4th Duke of Gordon, who bred black-and-tan setters at Gordon Castle in the 1820s. Black-and-tan setters were known in Scotland before then, but the Duke's kennel made them famous. Gordons were bred for stamina and a good nose rather than speed, to work the moors for grouse all day. It's the most heavily built of the setter breeds and a loyal, affectionate family dog for active homes.

What a Gordon's size means at home

The AKC standard calls for 24–27 in (61–68.5 cm) and 55–80 lb (25–36.5 kg) for males, and 23–26 in (58.5–66 cm) and 45–70 lb (20.5–32 kg) for females. A Gordon reaches roughly an adult's mid-thigh. It's sturdy, well-muscled and athletic. It needs a big boot and usually a 48″ (122 cm) crate. Check with the crate size calculator.

Gordon vs Irish and English Setter

The Irish Setter is a little taller and similar in weight, with a mahogany coat. The English Setter is a little taller and heavier on average, with a speckled belton coat. The Gordon is always black and tan, with a heavier build and head than the other setters.

Temperament and exercise

Gordons are loyal, affectionate and confident, devoted to their family and sometimes reserved with strangers. They make better watchdogs than other setters. They mature slowly and stay puppyish for a few years. They need at least one to two hours of exercise every day, ideally with free running in a safe area. They have a strong bird-hunting instinct, so recall needs a lot of work. They can be stubborn, so patient, consistent training works best.

Coat and health

The long, silky coat with feathering needs brushing a few times a week and regular trimming around the feet and ears. Known health problems include hip and elbow dysplasia, bloat, hypothyroidism, and progressive retinal atrophy (PRA) and cerebellar cortical degeneration (a nerve disease), both of which have DNA tests. Gordons often live 12–13 years.

Gordon Setter size: common questions

How big does a Gordon Setter get?

A fully grown male Gordon Setter stands 61–69 cm (24–27 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 25–37 kg (55–80 lb). Females stand 59–66 cm (23–26 in) and weigh 21–32 kg (45–71 lb).

Is a Gordon Setter a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 29 kg (63 lb), the Gordon Setter counts as a large dog breed on this site (large = 25–45 kg (55–99 lb) on average). It is taller than 80% of the 256 breeds listed.

How long does a Gordon Setter live?

Typical life expectancy is 12–13 years, about average for large breeds.

Can a Gordon Setter fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Gordon Setter weighs 21–37 kg (45–80 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).

Sizes are breed-standard ranges for adult dogs; individual dogs, working lines and dogs from different countries can be bigger or smaller. Airline notes are a general guide only. Check with your airline and your vet. Disclaimer.
