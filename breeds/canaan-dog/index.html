How big is a Canaan Dog?

The Canaan Dog is a medium-sized dog breed from Israel, in the Herding group. Males stand 51–61 cm (20.1–24 in) at the shoulder and weigh 21–25 kg (45–55 lb). Females stand 49–59 cm (19.1–23 in) and weigh 16–21 kg (35–45 lb). On average it is taller than 58% of the 256 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 52%. At the shoulder, an average male Canaan Dog reaches about knee height on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 12–15 years, about average for medium breeds. It is too heavy for airlines that cap dog and carrier at 8 kg (17.6 lb), so it usually flies in the hold unless the airline only checks the carrier size.

About the Canaan Dog

The Canaan Dog is the national dog of Israel. Its ancestors were semi-wild pariah dogs that lived in the deserts of the Middle East for thousands of years. In the 1930s, Dr Rudolphina Menzel, a dog expert working with the Haganah defence force, captured and bred some of these dogs to create a hardy, intelligent working dog. Canaan Dogs served as guard, messenger and mine-detection dogs. Today Bedouin people still keep similar dogs to guard their camps and herds.

What a Canaan Dog's size means at home

The AKC standard calls for 20–24 in (51–61 cm) at the shoulder and 45–55 lb (20.5–25 kg) for males, and 19–23 in (48.5–58.5 cm) and 35–45 lb (16–20.5 kg) for females. A Canaan Dog reaches roughly an adult's knee. It's lean, square and agile. The crate size calculator suggests a 42″ (107 cm) crate for an average male. It's too heavy for an airline cabin.

Canaan Dog vs Bearded Collie

The Bearded Collie is about the same size but has a long, shaggy coat. The Canaan Dog has a short-to-medium double coat, pricked ears and a bushy tail that curls over its back when it's alert.

Temperament and exercise

Canaan Dogs are alert, loyal and devoted to their family, but naturally wary of strangers and quick to bark at anything unusual. They're intelligent and independent, a legacy of surviving on their own, so they need patient, consistent training and early socialisation. They need daily exercise and mental stimulation, and some dig.

Care and health

The double coat sheds heavily once or twice a year but otherwise needs little care. Canaan Dogs are generally hardy and healthy. Known problems include hip dysplasia, hypothyroidism and epilepsy. They often live 12–15 years.

Sources: AKC breed standard and breed history; FCI standard No. 273 (Canaan Dog); Canaan Dog Club of America.

Canaan Dog size: common questions

How big does a Canaan Dog get?

A fully grown male Canaan Dog stands 51–61 cm (20.1–24 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 21–25 kg (45–55 lb). Females stand 49–59 cm (19.1–23 in) and weigh 16–21 kg (35–45 lb).

Is a Canaan Dog a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 21 kg (45 lb), the Canaan Dog counts as a medium dog breed on this site (medium = 10–25 kg (22–55 lb) on average). It is taller than 58% of the 256 breeds listed.

How long does a Canaan Dog live?

Typical life expectancy is 12–15 years, about average for medium breeds.

Can a Canaan Dog fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Canaan Dog weighs 16–25 kg (35–55 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).

Sizes are breed-standard ranges for adult dogs; individual dogs, working lines and dogs from different countries can be bigger or smaller. Airline notes are a general guide only. Check with your airline and your vet. Disclaimer.
