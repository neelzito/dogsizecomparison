How big is an Old English Sheepdog?

The Old English Sheepdog is a large-sized dog breed from England, in the Herding group. Males stand 56–61 cm (22–24 in) at the shoulder and weigh 27–46 kg (60–100 lb). Females stand 54–58 cm (21.1–22.8 in) and weigh 27–46 kg (60–100 lb). On average it is taller than 62% of the 256 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 85%. At the shoulder, an average male Old English Sheepdog reaches about mid-thigh on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 10–12 years, shorter than the 12.2-year average for large breeds.

About the Old English Sheepdog

The Old English Sheepdog was developed in the west of England in the 18th and 19th centuries as a drover's dog, used to drive cattle and sheep to market. Drovers' dogs were exempt from a tax on dogs if their tails were docked, which is said to be how the breed got its nickname, the "Bobtail". In the UK it is famous as the long-running mascot of Dulux paint adverts. Today it's a cheerful, clownish and affectionate family dog with a huge shaggy coat.

What an Old English Sheepdog's size means at home

The AKC standard sets minimum heights: 22 in (56 cm) and up for males, and 21 in (53.5 cm) and up for females. Most weigh about 27–45.5 kg (60–100 lb). An Old English Sheepdog reaches roughly an adult's mid-thigh, and its coat makes it look even bigger. It's a big, heavy-boned dog with a distinctive rolling, bear-like gait. It needs a large boot and usually a 42″ (107 cm) crate. Check with the crate size calculator.

OES, Bearded Collie and Sheepadoodle

The Bearded Collie looks similar but is smaller and leaner, with a longer tail and a lighter build. The Sheepadoodle is a cross between the Old English Sheepdog and the Poodle, often with a similar black-and-white look but a curlier coat.

The coat

The OES coat is its glory and its biggest commitment. The dense, shaggy double coat needs thorough brushing, down to the skin, several times a week, and it mats quickly if neglected. Many pet owners keep it in a shorter clip, which is much easier to manage. Hair over the eyes is often tied up or trimmed. The coat traps mud, leaves and water, so expect mess.

Temperament and exercise

Old English Sheepdogs are friendly, playful and gentle, usually good with children and other dogs, though their herding instinct may make them try to round up kids. They're clever but can be stubborn. They need daily exercise and mental stimulation. Puppies are boisterous, and adults stay playful for years.

Health

Known health problems include hip dysplasia, hypothyroidism, eye diseases (cataracts, progressive retinal atrophy), deafness, and sensitivity to some medicines due to the MDR1 gene variant. A DNA test for MDR1 is worth doing. OES often live 10–12 years.

Old English Sheepdog size: common questions

How big does an Old English Sheepdog get?

A fully grown male Old English Sheepdog stands 56–61 cm (22–24 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 27–46 kg (60–100 lb). Females stand 54–58 cm (21.1–22.8 in) and weigh 27–46 kg (60–100 lb).

Is an Old English Sheepdog a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 36 kg (80 lb), the Old English Sheepdog counts as a large dog breed on this site (large = 25–45 kg (55–99 lb) on average). It is taller than 62% of the 256 breeds listed.

How long does an Old English Sheepdog live?

Typical life expectancy is 10–12 years, shorter than the 12.2-year average for large breeds.

Can an Old English Sheepdog fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Old English Sheepdog weighs 27–46 kg (60–100 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).

Sizes are breed-standard ranges for adult dogs; individual dogs, working lines and dogs from different countries can be bigger or smaller. Airline notes are a general guide only. Check with your airline and your vet. Disclaimer.
