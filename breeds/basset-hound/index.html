How big is a Basset Hound?

The Basset Hound is a medium-sized dog breed from France, in the Hound group. Males stand 33–38 cm (13–15 in) at the shoulder and weigh 18–30 kg (40–65 lb). Females stand 28–38 cm (11–15 in) and weigh 18–30 kg (40–65 lb). On average it is taller than 24% of the 256 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 58%. At the shoulder, an average male Basset Hound reaches just below the knee on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 12–13 years, shorter than the 13.3-year average for medium breeds. It is too heavy for airlines that cap dog and carrier at 8 kg (17.6 lb), so it usually flies in the hold unless the airline only checks the carrier size.

About the Basset Hound

The Basset Hound comes from France, where the word bas means "low". Short-legged hounds were bred so that hunters on foot could keep up with them while they followed the scent of rabbits and hares. The breed is thought to descend from the St Hubert hound, the ancestor of the Bloodhound, which explains its superb nose. Bassets came to Britain in the 1860s and became popular across the English-speaking world, helped by the "Hush Puppies" shoe adverts. Today it is a gentle, easy-going and famously stubborn family dog.

What a Basset's size means at home

The AKC standard says a Basset should not be over 14 in (35.5 cm) at the shoulder. Most stand about 28–38 cm, just below an adult's knee, but they're very heavy for their height: about 18–29.5 kg (40–65 lb). That's more than a Border Collie, which stands almost 20 cm taller. Bassets are long, deep-chested and heavy-boned, so the crate size calculator suggests a 42″ (107 cm) crate because of their length. Lifting a Basset into a car takes effort, so a ramp helps, and it's too heavy for an airline cabin.

Basset vs Beagle

The Beagle is about the same height, but a Basset is much longer and roughly twice as heavy. Both are friendly, food-loving scent hounds that like to follow their nose.

Nose first

A Basset's nose rules its life. On a scent, it may ignore you completely, so many can't be let off the lead in open areas. They are famously slow to obey, more from stubbornness than lack of brains. Patient, food-based training works best. They bay loudly, which neighbours may not appreciate.

Ears, eyes and weight

Those long ears trail on the ground, so check and clean them weekly to prevent infections. The droopy lower eyelids can collect dirt and become inflamed. Bassets love food, and extra weight strains their long back and short legs, so keep yours lean. Use ramps instead of letting it jump, and support its back and chest when lifting.

Health

Known health problems include ear infections, eye problems (ectropion, glaucoma), back problems, elbow dysplasia and bloat, as in other deep-chested breeds. Bassets often live 12–13 years.

Basset Hound size: common questions

How big does a Basset Hound get?

A fully grown male Basset Hound stands 33–38 cm (13–15 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 18–30 kg (40–65 lb). Females stand 28–38 cm (11–15 in) and weigh 18–30 kg (40–65 lb).

Is a Basset Hound a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 24 kg (52 lb), the Basset Hound counts as a medium dog breed on this site (medium = 10–25 kg (22–55 lb) on average). It is taller than 24% of the 256 breeds listed.

How long does a Basset Hound live?

Typical life expectancy is 12–13 years, shorter than the 13.3-year average for medium breeds.

Can a Basset Hound fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Basset Hound weighs 18–30 kg (40–65 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).
