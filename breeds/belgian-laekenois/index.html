How big is a Belgian Laekenois?

The Belgian Laekenois is a large-sized dog breed from Belgium, in the Herding group. Males stand 61–66 cm (24–26 in) at the shoulder and weigh 25–30 kg (55–65 lb). Females stand 56–61 cm (22–24 in) and weigh 25–30 kg (55–65 lb). On average it is taller than 72% of the 268 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 70%.

Belgian Laekenois compared to a human

Next to a 175 cm (5′9″) adult, an average male Belgian Laekenois stands 64 cm (25 in) at the shoulder: 36% of the person's height, reaching about mid-thigh. An average female, at 59 cm (23 in), reaches about mid-thigh. In feet, males stand 2 ft to 2 ft 2 in tall at the shoulder and females 1 ft 10 in to 2 ft. Next to a 120 cm (3′11″) child, about seven years old, the shoulder comes to about hip height. The head sits higher than the shoulder, so the dog looks taller than these numbers suggest.

Typical life expectancy is 10–12 years, shorter than the 12.1-year average for large breeds.

Belgian Laekenois size: common questions

How big does a Belgian Laekenois get?

A fully grown male Belgian Laekenois stands 61–66 cm (24–26 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 25–30 kg (55–65 lb). Females stand 56–61 cm (22–24 in) and weigh 25–30 kg (55–65 lb).

How tall is a Belgian Laekenois in feet?

A male Belgian Laekenois stands 2 ft to 2 ft 2 in tall at the shoulder (61–66 cm) and a female 1 ft 10 in to 2 ft (56–61 cm). Next to a 5′9″ adult, an average male reaches about mid-thigh.

Is a Belgian Laekenois a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 27 kg (60 lb), the Belgian Laekenois counts as a large dog breed on this site (large = 25–45 kg (55–99 lb) on average). It is taller than 72% of the 268 breeds listed.

How long does a Belgian Laekenois live?

Typical life expectancy is 10–12 years, shorter than the 12.1-year average for large breeds.

Can a Belgian Laekenois fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Belgian Laekenois weighs 25–30 kg (55–65 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).
