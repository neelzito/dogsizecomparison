How big is an Estrela Mountain Dog?

The Estrela Mountain Dog is a giant-sized dog breed from Portugal, in the Working group. Adults stand 62–74 cm (24.4–28.9 in) at the shoulder and weigh 35–60 kg (77–132 lb); the breed standard gives the same range for males and females. On average it is taller than 92% of the 256 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 91%. At the shoulder, an average Estrela Mountain Dog reaches about mid-thigh on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 10–14 years, longer than the 10.1-year average for giant breeds.

Sizes follow the American Kennel Club (AKC) breed standard.

About the Estrela Mountain Dog

The Estrela Mountain Dog (Cão da Serra da Estrela) is one of Portugal's oldest breeds, from the Serra da Estrela, the country's highest mountain range. Shepherds used it for centuries to protect flocks from wolves on the high pastures. It's still used as a livestock guardian, and Portuguese police have used it too. It comes with a long or a short coat.

What an Estrela's size means at home

The FCI standard calls for 65–72 cm at the shoulder for males and 62–68 cm for females. Estrelas weigh about 35–60 kg (77–132 lb). An Estrela reaches roughly an adult's mid-thigh, and its weight puts it in the giant size class on this site. It needs a large vehicle, a 54″ (137 cm) crate (see the crate size calculator) and a large, secure property.

Estrela vs Tibetan Mastiff

The Tibetan Mastiff is a little taller and heavier, with a heavier mane. Estrelas are usually fawn, wolf-grey or brindle, with a dark mask.

Temperament

Estrelas are loyal, calm and gentle with their family, but protective and wary of strangers. They're independent guardians and can be stubborn, so they need early socialisation, consistent training and an experienced owner. They often bark at night.

Care and health

The coat sheds heavily once or twice a year and needs weekly brushing, more for long coats. Known health problems include hip and elbow dysplasia and bloat. Estrelas often live 10–14 years.

Sources: FCI standard No. 173 (Cão da Serra da Estrela); AKC breed information; Clube Português de Canicultura.

Estrela Mountain Dog size: common questions

How big does an Estrela Mountain Dog get?

An adult Estrela Mountain Dog stands 62–74 cm (24.4–28.9 in) tall at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 35–60 kg (77–132 lb).

Is an Estrela Mountain Dog a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 48 kg (105 lb), the Estrela Mountain Dog counts as a giant dog breed on this site (giant = 45 kg (99 lb) and over on average). It is taller than 92% of the 256 breeds listed.

How long does an Estrela Mountain Dog live?

Typical life expectancy is 10–14 years, longer than the 10.1-year average for giant breeds.

Can an Estrela Mountain Dog fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Estrela Mountain Dog weighs 35–60 kg (77–132 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).
