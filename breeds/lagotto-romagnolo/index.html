How big is a Lagotto Romagnolo?

The Lagotto Romagnolo is a medium-sized dog breed from Italy, in the Sporting group. Males stand 43–49 cm (16.9–19.1 in) at the shoulder and weigh 13–16 kg (29–35 lb). Females stand 41–46 cm (15.9–17.9 in) and weigh 11–14 kg (24–31 lb). On average it is taller than 42% of the 268 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 36%.

Lagotto Romagnolo compared to a human

Next to a 175 cm (5′9″) adult, an average male Lagotto Romagnolo stands 46 cm (18 in) at the shoulder: 26% of the person's height, reaching about knee height. An average female, at 43 cm (16.9 in), reaches just below the knee. In feet, males stand 1 ft 5 in to 1 ft 7 in tall at the shoulder and females 1 ft 4 in to 1 ft 6 in. Next to a 120 cm (3′11″) child, about seven years old, the shoulder comes to about mid-thigh. The head sits higher than the shoulder, so the dog looks taller than these numbers suggest.

Typical life expectancy is 15–17 years, longer than the 13.3-year average for medium breeds. It is too heavy for airlines that cap dog and carrier at 8 kg (17.6 lb), so it usually flies in the hold unless the airline only checks the carrier size.

About the Lagotto Romagnolo

The Lagotto Romagnolo comes from the Romagna region of north-eastern Italy, where it was used for centuries as a water retriever in the marshes. Its name means "water dog of Romagna". When the marshes were drained in the 19th century, the Lagotto found a new job: hunting truffles. Today it is the only breed recognised specifically as a truffle-hunting dog. With its curly, woolly coat and teddy-bear face, it's also becoming popular as a family companion.

What a Lagotto's size means at home

The AKC standard calls for 17–19 in (43–48.5 cm) at the shoulder and about 28–35 lb (13–16 kg) for males, and 16–18 in (40.5–45.5 cm) and about 24–31 lb (11–14 kg) for females. A Lagotto reaches roughly an adult's knee. It's compact, square and sturdy. It usually needs a 36″ (91 cm) crate. Check with the crate size calculator. It's too heavy for an airline cabin.

Lagotto vs English Cocker and doodles

The Lagotto is a little taller than an English Cocker Spaniel and similar in weight. With its curly coat, it's often mistaken for a doodle, but it's a purebred breed with a predictable size and temperament.

Nose and digging

Lagottos have a superb nose and love to use it. Scent work and nose games are great for them, and truffle hunting is the ultimate. They also love to dig, which is useful for truffles and less so for your garden. Give your Lagotto a digging spot and plenty of mental work.

Temperament and exercise

Lagottos are affectionate, lively and intelligent, devoted to their family and usually good with children. They need daily exercise and mental stimulation, and they're quick to learn with reward-based training. Without enough to do, they can become bored, noisy or destructive.

Coat and health

The dense, woolly, curly coat sheds very little but mats easily, so it needs regular brushing and a trim every 6–8 weeks. It should never be brushed out into a fluffy look, which spoils the natural curls. Known health problems include benign juvenile epilepsy and a storage disease (LSD), both with DNA tests, along with hip dysplasia and eye conditions. Lagottos are long-lived, often 15–17 years.

Lagotto Romagnolo size: common questions

How big does a Lagotto Romagnolo get?

A fully grown male Lagotto Romagnolo stands 43–49 cm (16.9–19.1 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 13–16 kg (29–35 lb). Females stand 41–46 cm (15.9–17.9 in) and weigh 11–14 kg (24–31 lb).

How tall is a Lagotto Romagnolo in feet?

A male Lagotto Romagnolo stands 1 ft 5 in to 1 ft 7 in tall at the shoulder (43–49 cm) and a female 1 ft 4 in to 1 ft 6 in (41–46 cm). Next to a 5′9″ adult, an average male reaches about knee height.

Is a Lagotto Romagnolo a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 14 kg (30 lb), the Lagotto Romagnolo counts as a medium dog breed on this site (medium = 10–25 kg (22–55 lb) on average). It is taller than 42% of the 268 breeds listed.

How long does a Lagotto Romagnolo live?

Typical life expectancy is 15–17 years, longer than the 13.3-year average for medium breeds.

Can a Lagotto Romagnolo fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Lagotto Romagnolo weighs 11–16 kg (24–35 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).
