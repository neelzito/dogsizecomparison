How big is a Kangal Shepherd Dog?

The Kangal Shepherd Dog is a giant-sized dog breed from Turkey, in the Working group. Males stand 72–78 cm (28.3–30.7 in) at the shoulder and weigh 48–60 kg (106–132 lb). Females stand 65–73 cm (25.6–28.7 in) and weigh 40–50 kg (88–110 lb). On average that makes it the 10th tallest of the 256 breeds on this site. At the shoulder, an average male Kangal Shepherd Dog reaches about mid-thigh on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 12–15 years, longer than the 10.1-year average for giant breeds.

About the Kangal Shepherd Dog

The Kangal Shepherd Dog is the national dog of Turkey, named after the town of Kangal in Sivas province in central Anatolia. Turkish shepherds have used it for centuries to protect flocks from wolves, bears and jackals. It typically wears a spiked collar for protection, and it patrols and guards with great independence. In Turkey the Kangal is a source of national pride: it has appeared on stamps and coins, and exports of pure Kangals have been restricted. Since the 1990s, Kangals have also been used in Namibia and Kenya to protect livestock from cheetahs.

What a Kangal's size means at home

The FCI standard calls for 72–78 cm at the shoulder and about 48–60 kg for males, and 65–73 cm and about 40–50 kg for females. A Kangal reaches roughly an adult's mid-thigh. It's tall, powerful and athletic, heavy enough to put it in the giant size class on this site. It needs a large vehicle, a 54″ (137 cm) crate (see the crate size calculator) and a large, securely fenced property.

Kangal vs Anatolian Shepherd and Great Pyrenees

The Anatolian Shepherd is very similar in size. The FCI renamed its Anatolian standard "Kangal Shepherd Dog" in 2018, but some clubs still keep the two apart. The Kangal is always a solid dun or fawn with a black mask. The Great Pyrenees is about the same height and a little lighter, with a long white coat.

A livestock guardian

Kangals are calm, loyal and gentle with their family and animals, and very protective. They're independent, bred to work without commands, so training takes patience, and recall is often unreliable. They need a large, secure property, early socialisation and an experienced owner. They're happiest with a job, ideally guarding livestock. In some countries and regions, large guardian breeds are subject to special rules, so check local laws.

Care and health

The short, dense double coat sheds heavily once or twice a year but otherwise needs little care. Kangals cope well with both heat and cold. They're generally hardy. Known problems include hip dysplasia, entropion and sensitivity to anaesthesia. Kangals often live 12–15 years.

Sources: FCI standard No. 331 (Kangal Shepherd Dog); United Kennel Club breed standard; Cheetah Conservation Fund.

Kangal Shepherd Dog size: common questions

How big does a Kangal Shepherd Dog get?

A fully grown male Kangal Shepherd Dog stands 72–78 cm (28.3–30.7 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 48–60 kg (106–132 lb). Females stand 65–73 cm (25.6–28.7 in) and weigh 40–50 kg (88–110 lb).

Is a Kangal Shepherd Dog a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 50 kg (109 lb), the Kangal Shepherd Dog counts as a giant dog breed on this site (giant = 45 kg (99 lb) and over on average). It is the 10th tallest of the 256 breeds listed.

How long does a Kangal Shepherd Dog live?

Typical life expectancy is 12–15 years, longer than the 10.1-year average for giant breeds.

Can a Kangal Shepherd Dog fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Kangal Shepherd Dog weighs 40–60 kg (88–132 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).

Sizes are breed-standard ranges for adult dogs; individual dogs, working lines and dogs from different countries can be bigger or smaller. Airline notes are a general guide only. Check with your airline and your vet. Disclaimer.
