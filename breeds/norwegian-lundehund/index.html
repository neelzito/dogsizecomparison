How big is a Norwegian Lundehund?

The Norwegian Lundehund is a small-sized dog breed from Norway, in the Hound group. Males stand 33–38 cm (13–15 in) at the shoulder and weigh 6.8–8.2 kg (15–18 lb). Females stand 31–36 cm (12–14 in) and weigh 5.4–6.8 kg (12–15 lb). On average it is taller than 25% of the 267 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 15%. At the shoulder, an average male Norwegian Lundehund reaches just below the knee on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 12–15 years, about average for small breeds. Lighter individuals fit airline cabin limits of 8 kg (17.6 lb) for dog and carrier; heavier ones need an airline that weighs only the carrier size, or travel in the hold.

About the Norwegian Lundehund

The Norwegian Lundehund is one of the most unusual dog breeds in the world. It was bred on the remote islands of northern Norway to hunt puffins (lunde) on steep cliffs and in narrow rock crevices. To do that, it developed six toes on each foot, extremely flexible shoulders that let its front legs stretch out to the side, a neck that bends backwards until the head touches the spine, and ears that fold shut. When puffin hunting was banned, the breed almost died out, and it was rebuilt from just a handful of dogs.

What a Lundehund's size means at home

The AKC standard calls for 13–15 in (33–38 cm) at the shoulder for males and 12–14 in (30.5–35.5 cm) for females, with weights of about 6.5–8 kg (15–18 lb) for males and 5.5–7 kg for females. A Lundehund reaches just below an adult's knee. It usually needs a 24″ (61 cm) crate. Check with the crate size calculator. A light Lundehund may fit the cabin limit. Lundehund vs Beagle and Podengo Pequeno

The Lundehund is about the same height as a Beagle but much lighter. The Portuguese Podengo Pequeno is smaller.

Temperament

Lundehunds are alert, playful and independent. They can be hard to house-train and are often reserved with strangers. They need daily exercise and like to climb and explore.

Health

The small founding population means the breed is prone to "Lundehund syndrome", a group of digestive disorders that affect how food is absorbed. A careful diet and regular vet checks are important. Lundehunds often live 12–15 years.

Norwegian Lundehund size: common questions

How big does a Norwegian Lundehund get?

A fully grown male Norwegian Lundehund stands 33–38 cm (13–15 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 6.8–8.2 kg (15–18 lb). Females stand 31–36 cm (12–14 in) and weigh 5.4–6.8 kg (12–15 lb).

Is a Norwegian Lundehund a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 6.8 kg (15 lb), the Norwegian Lundehund counts as a small dog breed on this site (small = 5–10 kg (11–22 lb) on average). It is taller than 25% of the 267 breeds listed.

How long does a Norwegian Lundehund live?

Typical life expectancy is 12–15 years, about average for small breeds.

Can a Norwegian Lundehund fly in the airplane cabin?

Sometimes. Lighter individuals fit airline cabin limits of 8 kg (17.6 lb) for dog and carrier; heavier ones need an airline that weighs only the carrier size, or travel in the hold. Rules vary by airline, so check before booking.
