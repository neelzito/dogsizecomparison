How big is a Chihuahua?

The Chihuahua is a toy dog breed from Mexico, in the Toy group. Adults stand 12.5–21 cm (4.9–8.1 in) at the shoulder and weigh 1.4–2.7 kg (3.1–6 lb); the breed standard gives the same range for males and females. On average that makes it the 3rd shortest of the 256 breeds on this site. At the shoulder, an average Chihuahua reaches about ankle height on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 14–16 years, longer than the 13.9-year average for toy breeds. At 1.4–2.7 kg (3.1–6 lb), it fits even the strictest airline cabin rule, where dog and carrier together may weigh 8 kg (17.6 lb).

Sizes follow the American Kennel Club (AKC) breed standard.

About the Chihuahua

The Chihuahua is the lightest dog breed in the world, named after the Mexican state of Chihuahua, where the breed was found in the 19th century. It is thought to descend from the Techichi, a small companion dog kept by the Toltecs and later the Aztecs, possibly crossed with small dogs brought by Europeans. American visitors brought Chihuahuas to the United States in the late 1800s, and the breed has been a favourite ever since. Its personality is the opposite of its size: alert, loyal and bold, often devoted to one person.

What a Chihuahua's size means at home

A Chihuahua stands about 13–20 cm at the shoulder, around an adult's ankle, and weighs about 1.4–2.7 kg (3–6 lb). The AKC standard sets 6 lb as the upper limit. It fits into a handbag-sized carrier, an 18″ (46 cm) crate (check with the crate size calculator) and almost any home. Next to the biggest breeds, the difference is huge: see how a Chihuahua compares with a Great Dane.

Flying with a Chihuahua

A Chihuahua is the ideal size for flying in the cabin. Even with its carrier, it weighs far less than the 8 kg (17.6 lb) limit many airlines set for dogs in the cabin. Check your airline's carrier sizes with the airline cabin check.

Keeping a tiny dog safe

Being so small brings risks: a Chihuahua can be hurt by a jump from a sofa, a fall from someone's arms, or a rough game with a bigger dog. Use pet steps, and teach children to sit on the floor to hold it. Chihuahuas feel the cold, so a coat helps on winter walks. Many puppies have a soft spot on the top of the skull (a molera), which often closes with age but should be handled gently. Very small "teacup" Chihuahuas are more prone to low blood sugar and other health problems.

Coat and temperament

Chihuahuas come in a smooth coat and a long coat, in almost any colour. Both are easy to care for. They can be wary of strangers and quick to bark, and they often get on best with other Chihuahuas. Train and socialise them like any other dog, not like an accessory: a well-trained Chihuahua is a confident, happy one. Chihuahuas are among the longest-lived breeds, often reaching 14–16 years or more.

Sources: AKC breed standard and breed history; The Kennel Club breed standard; FCI standard No. 218 (Chihuahueño).

Chihuahua size: common questions

How big does a Chihuahua get?

An adult Chihuahua stands 12.5–21 cm (4.9–8.1 in) tall at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 1.4–2.7 kg (3.1–6 lb).

Is a Chihuahua a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 2.1 kg (4.5 lb), the Chihuahua counts as a toy dog breed on this site (toy = under 5 kg (11 lb) on average). It is the 3rd shortest of the 256 breeds listed.

How long does a Chihuahua live?

Typical life expectancy is 14–16 years, longer than the 13.9-year average for toy breeds.

Can a Chihuahua fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually yes. At 1.4–2.7 kg (3.1–6 lb), it fits even the strictest airline cabin rule, where dog and carrier together may weigh 8 kg (17.6 lb). Check your airline's rules and carrier size limits before booking.
