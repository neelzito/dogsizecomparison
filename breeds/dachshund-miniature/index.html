How big is a Dachshund (Miniature)?

The Dachshund (Miniature) is a toy dog breed from Germany, in the Hound group. Males stand 12.5–15 cm (4.9–5.9 in) at the shoulder and weigh 3.5–5 kg (7.7–11 lb). Females stand 12.5–15 cm (4.9–5.9 in) and weigh 3.2–5 kg (7.1–11 lb). On average that makes it the shortest of the 266 breeds on this site. At the shoulder, an average male Dachshund (Miniature) reaches about ankle height on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 12–16 years, about average for toy breeds. At 3.2–5 kg (7.1–11 lb), it fits even the strictest airline cabin rule, where dog and carrier together may weigh 8 kg (17.6 lb).

About the Dachshund (Miniature)

The Miniature Dachshund is the small version of the German "badger dog". Smaller Dachshunds were bred from the 19th century onwards to go after smaller quarry, such as rabbits and stoats, in narrower burrows. Today the Miniature Dachshund is a hugely popular companion: brave, loyal, playful and full of character. Like the Standard Dachshund, it comes in three coats: smooth, wire-haired and long-haired.

How small is a Miniature Dachshund?

Sizing depends on the kennel club:

  • AKC (US): a Miniature weighs 11 lb (5 kg) or less at 12 months or older.
  • The Kennel Club (UK): an ideal weight of about 4.5 kg (10 lb).
  • FCI (Europe): sized by chest circumference instead. A Miniature (Zwergteckel) measures 30–35 cm around the chest, and the even smaller Rabbit Dachshund (Kaninchenteckel) up to 30 cm.

A Miniature typically stands only about 13–15 cm at the shoulder, around ankle height on an adult. Next to a Standard Dachshund, it is about 8 cm shorter and well under half the weight.

What a Miniature Dachshund's size means at home

A Miniature Dachshund fits any home and usually needs an 18″ (46 cm) crate. Check with the crate size calculator. Because it's long rather than tall, a carrier needs enough length for it to lie stretched out.

Flying with a Miniature Dachshund

Most Miniature Dachshunds fit in the cabin, staying under the 8 kg (17.6 lb) limit many airlines set even with a carrier. Check with the airline cabin check.

Protect the back

Miniature Dachshunds share the breed's high risk of intervertebral disc disease (IVDD), where a disc in the spine bulges or bursts. To lower the risk, keep your dog lean, use ramps or steps instead of letting it jump on and off furniture, carry it on stairs where possible, and support its back and bottom when you lift it. If it suddenly cries out, refuses to move, or wobbles or drags its back legs, see a vet straight away.

Temperament

Miniature Dachshunds are bold, curious and often stubborn, with a big bark for their size. They need patient, reward-based training and daily walks to sniff and explore. Some dig and chase small animals. They often live 12–16 years.

Dachshund (Miniature) size: common questions

How big does a Dachshund (Miniature) get?

A fully grown male Dachshund (Miniature) stands 12.5–15 cm (4.9–5.9 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 3.5–5 kg (7.7–11 lb). Females stand 12.5–15 cm (4.9–5.9 in) and weigh 3.2–5 kg (7.1–11 lb).

Is a Dachshund (Miniature) a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 4.2 kg (9.2 lb), the Dachshund (Miniature) counts as a toy dog breed on this site (toy = under 5 kg (11 lb) on average). It is the shortest of the 266 breeds listed.

How long does a Dachshund (Miniature) live?

Typical life expectancy is 12–16 years, about average for toy breeds.

Can a Dachshund (Miniature) fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually yes. At 3.2–5 kg (7.1–11 lb), it fits even the strictest airline cabin rule, where dog and carrier together may weigh 8 kg (17.6 lb). Check your airline's rules and carrier size limits before booking.
