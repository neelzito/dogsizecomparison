How big is a Pug?

The Pug is a small-sized dog breed from China, in the Toy group. Adults stand 26–33 cm (10–13 in) at the shoulder and weigh 6.4–8.2 kg (14–18 lb); the breed standard gives the same range for males and females. On average it is taller than 17% of the 256 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 19%. At the shoulder, an average Pug reaches about mid-shin on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 13–15 years, about average for small breeds. Lighter individuals fit airline cabin limits of 8 kg (17.6 lb) for dog and carrier; heavier ones need an airline that weighs only the carrier size, or travel in the hold.

Sizes follow the American Kennel Club (AKC) breed standard.

About the Pug

The Pug is an ancient Chinese breed, kept as a companion by emperors for centuries. Dutch traders brought Pugs to Europe in the 16th century, and the breed became linked with the Dutch royal House of Orange: according to legend, a Pug saved William the Silent by barking a warning of approaching assassins. Pugs later became favourites of European royalty, including Queen Victoria. The Latin motto multum in parvo, "a lot in a little", sums up the breed: a small, solid, charming dog with a big personality.

What a Pug's size means at home

A Pug stands about 25–33 cm at the shoulder, around mid-shin on an adult, but it is compact and heavy for its size, at about 6.5–8 kg (14–18 lb). It suits flats and city life and doesn't need much exercise. It usually needs a 24″ (61 cm) crate. Check with the crate size calculator. Pugs love food, and extra weight makes their breathing worse, so keep yours slim.

Can a Pug fly?

In the cabin, sometimes. A lighter Pug in a light carrier may fit under the 8 kg (17.6 lb) limit many airlines set for dog and carrier. Use the airline cabin check. In the hold, often not: because of their flat faces, many airlines don't accept Pugs and other snub-nosed breeds in the hold at all.

Pug vs French Bulldog and Boston Terrier

These three flat-faced companions are often compared. The French Bulldog is about the same height but heavier and more muscular, with bat ears. The Boston Terrier is taller and leggier, with a tuxedo-like coat and a slightly longer muzzle.

Breathing, eyes and heat

The Pug's very flat face shortens its airways. Many Pugs snore, struggle to breathe during exercise, and overheat quickly in warm weather. Walk them in the cool part of the day and never leave them in a warm car. Their large, prominent eyes are easily injured, so see a vet promptly if an eye looks red, cloudy or painful. Clean the deep facial wrinkle regularly to prevent infection. If you're buying a puppy, look for breeders who select for open nostrils and a less extreme face, and ask about breathing assessments for the parents.

Temperament

Pugs are affectionate, funny and sociable, and they are usually good with children and other pets. They want to be with their people and don't like being alone. They're not built for long runs, but they enjoy short walks and play. Pugs often live 13–15 years.

Pug size: common questions

How big does a Pug get?

An adult Pug stands 26–33 cm (10–13 in) tall at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 6.4–8.2 kg (14–18 lb).

Is a Pug a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 7.3 kg (16 lb), the Pug counts as a small dog breed on this site (small = 5–10 kg (11–22 lb) on average). It is taller than 17% of the 256 breeds listed.

How long does a Pug live?

Typical life expectancy is 13–15 years, about average for small breeds.

Can a Pug fly in the airplane cabin?

Sometimes. Lighter individuals fit airline cabin limits of 8 kg (17.6 lb) for dog and carrier; heavier ones need an airline that weighs only the carrier size, or travel in the hold. Rules vary by airline, so check before booking.
