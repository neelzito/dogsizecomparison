How big is a Kuvasz?

The Kuvasz is a large-sized dog breed from Hungary, in the Working group. Males stand 71–76 cm (28–29.9 in) at the shoulder and weigh 46–52 kg (100–115 lb). Females stand 66–71 cm (26–28 in) and weigh 32–41 kg (71–90 lb). On average it is taller than 93% of the 247 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 88%. At the shoulder, an average male Kuvasz reaches about mid-thigh on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 10–12 years, shorter than the 12.1-year average for large breeds.

Kuvasz size: common questions

How big does a Kuvasz get?

A fully grown male Kuvasz stands 71–76 cm (28–29.9 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 46–52 kg (100–115 lb). Females stand 66–71 cm (26–28 in) and weigh 32–41 kg (71–90 lb).

Is a Kuvasz a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 43 kg (94 lb), the Kuvasz counts as a large dog breed on this site (large = 25–45 kg (55–99 lb) on average). It is taller than 93% of the 247 breeds listed.

How long does a Kuvasz live?

Typical life expectancy is 10–12 years, shorter than the 12.1-year average for large breeds.

Can a Kuvasz fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Kuvasz weighs 32–52 kg (71–115 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).

Sizes are breed-standard ranges for adult dogs; individual dogs, working lines and dogs from different countries can be bigger or smaller. Airline notes are a general guide only. Check with your airline and your vet. Disclaimer.
