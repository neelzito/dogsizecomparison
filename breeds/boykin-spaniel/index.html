How big is a Boykin Spaniel?

The Boykin Spaniel is a medium-sized dog breed from the United States, in the Sporting group. Males stand 40–46 cm (15.6–17.9 in) at the shoulder and weigh 14–18 kg (30–40 lb). Females stand 36–42 cm (14–16.5 in) and weigh 12–16 kg (25–35 lb). On average it is taller than 36% of the 256 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 38%. At the shoulder, an average male Boykin Spaniel reaches just below the knee on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 10–15 years, shorter than the 13.3-year average for medium breeds. It is too heavy for airlines that cap dog and carrier at 8 kg (17.6 lb), so it usually flies in the hold unless the airline only checks the carrier size.

About the Boykin Spaniel

The Boykin Spaniel is a small American gundog from South Carolina, developed in the early 20th century to hunt wild turkeys and ducks along the Wateree River. According to tradition, the breed began with a small brown stray spaniel found near a church in Spartanburg, later bred by Whit Boykin. Hunters needed a dog small enough to fit in a narrow boat. It's the state dog of South Carolina, and the AKC recognised it in 2009.

What a Boykin's size means at home

The AKC standard calls for 15.5–18 in (39.5–45.5 cm) at the shoulder and 30–40 lb (13.5–18 kg) for males, and 14–16.5 in (35.5–42 cm) and 25–35 lb (11.5–16 kg) for females. A Boykin reaches just below an adult's knee. It usually needs a 36″ (91 cm) crate. Check with the crate size calculator. It's too heavy for an airline cabin.

Boykin vs American Water Spaniel and English Cocker

The American Water Spaniel is about the same size and a little heavier, with tighter curls. The English Cocker Spaniel is about the same height and a little lighter. Boykins are always solid liver or brown.

Temperament and exercise

Boykins are friendly, eager and energetic, great with children and other dogs. They need plenty of daily exercise and love swimming and retrieving. They're keen to please and easy to train.

Coat and health

The medium-length wavy coat needs weekly brushing, and the ears need regular checks. Known health problems include hip dysplasia, eye conditions, and exercise-induced collapse, for which a DNA test exists. Boykins often live 10–15 years.

Sources: AKC breed standard and breed history; Boykin Spaniel Society.

Boykin Spaniel size: common questions

How big does a Boykin Spaniel get?

A fully grown male Boykin Spaniel stands 40–46 cm (15.6–17.9 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 14–18 kg (30–40 lb). Females stand 36–42 cm (14–16.5 in) and weigh 12–16 kg (25–35 lb).

Is a Boykin Spaniel a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 15 kg (33 lb), the Boykin Spaniel counts as a medium dog breed on this site (medium = 10–25 kg (22–55 lb) on average). It is taller than 36% of the 256 breeds listed.

How long does a Boykin Spaniel live?

Typical life expectancy is 10–15 years, shorter than the 13.3-year average for medium breeds.

Can a Boykin Spaniel fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Boykin Spaniel weighs 12–18 kg (25–40 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).
