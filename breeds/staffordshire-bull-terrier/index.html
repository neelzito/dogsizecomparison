How big is a Staffordshire Bull Terrier?

The Staffordshire Bull Terrier is a medium-sized dog breed from England, in the Terrier group. Males stand 36–41 cm (14–15.9 in) at the shoulder and weigh 13–17 kg (28–37 lb). Females stand 36–41 cm (14–15.9 in) and weigh 11–16 kg (24–34 lb). On average it is taller than 32% of the 247 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 38%. At the shoulder, an average male Staffordshire Bull Terrier reaches just below the knee on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 12–15 years, about average for medium breeds. It is too heavy for airlines that cap dog and carrier at 8 kg (17.6 lb), so it usually flies in the hold unless the airline only checks the carrier size.

Staffordshire Bull Terrier size: common questions

How big does a Staffordshire Bull Terrier get?

A fully grown male Staffordshire Bull Terrier stands 36–41 cm (14–15.9 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 13–17 kg (28–37 lb). Females stand 36–41 cm (14–15.9 in) and weigh 11–16 kg (24–34 lb).

Is a Staffordshire Bull Terrier a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 14 kg (31 lb), the Staffordshire Bull Terrier counts as a medium dog breed on this site (medium = 10–25 kg (22–55 lb) on average). It is taller than 32% of the 247 breeds listed.

How long does a Staffordshire Bull Terrier live?

Typical life expectancy is 12–15 years, about average for medium breeds.

Can a Staffordshire Bull Terrier fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Staffordshire Bull Terrier weighs 11–17 kg (24–37 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).

Sizes are breed-standard ranges for adult dogs; individual dogs, working lines and dogs from different countries can be bigger or smaller. Airline notes are a general guide only. Check with your airline and your vet. Disclaimer.
