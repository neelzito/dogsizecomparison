How big is a Pyrenean Shepherd?

The Pyrenean Shepherd is a medium-sized dog breed from France, in the Herding group. Males stand 40–54 cm (15.6–21.1 in) at the shoulder and weigh 6.8–14 kg (15–30 lb). Females stand 38–52 cm (15–20.5 in) and weigh 6.8–14 kg (15–30 lb). On average it is taller than 45% of the 268 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 29%.

Pyrenean Shepherd compared to a human

Next to a 175 cm (5′9″) adult, an average male Pyrenean Shepherd stands 47 cm (18.3 in) at the shoulder: 27% of the person's height, reaching about knee height. An average female, at 45 cm (17.7 in), reaches just below the knee. In feet, males stand 1 ft 4 in to 1 ft 9 in tall at the shoulder and females 1 ft 3 in to 1 ft 8 in. Next to a 120 cm (3′11″) child, about seven years old, the shoulder comes to about mid-thigh. The head sits higher than the shoulder, so the dog looks taller than these numbers suggest.

Typical life expectancy is 17–19 years, longer than the 13.3-year average for medium breeds. It is too heavy for airlines that cap dog and carrier at 8 kg (17.6 lb), so it usually flies in the hold unless the airline only checks the carrier size.

About the Pyrenean Shepherd

The Pyrenean Shepherd (Berger des Pyrénées) is the smallest French herding dog, from the Pyrenees mountains. For centuries it herded sheep on the high pastures, often working alongside the much bigger Great Pyrenees, which guarded the flock. During the First World War, Pyrenean Shepherds served as messengers and search dogs. They come in two types: the rough-faced, with a shaggy coat, and the smooth-faced, with shorter hair on the face.

What a Pyrenean Shepherd's size means at home

The AKC standard calls for about 15.5–21 in (39.5–53.5 cm) at the shoulder for males and 15–20.5 in (38–52 cm) for females, with the smooth-faced type allowed a little taller. Pyr Sheps weigh about 7–13.5 kg (15–30 lb). One reaches roughly an adult's knee. It usually needs a 36″ (91 cm) crate. Check with the crate size calculator. It's too heavy for an airline cabin.

Pyrenean Shepherd vs Mudi

The Mudi is about the same size. The Pyrenean Shepherd is lean, light and very quick on its feet.

Temperament and exercise

Pyr Sheps are intense, energetic and very loyal, often bonding closely with one person and wary of strangers. They need a lot of exercise and mental work, and they're fast learners who excel at agility and herding. They can be vocal.

Coat and health

The rough coat needs occasional brushing and can form loose cords on the hindquarters. Pyr Sheps are generally healthy and among the longest-lived breeds, often well into their teens. Known problems include hip dysplasia and eye conditions.

Sources: AKC breed standard and breed history; FCI standard No. 141 (Berger des Pyrénées à poil long).

Pyrenean Shepherd size: common questions

How big does a Pyrenean Shepherd get?

A fully grown male Pyrenean Shepherd stands 40–54 cm (15.6–21.1 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 6.8–14 kg (15–30 lb). Females stand 38–52 cm (15–20.5 in) and weigh 6.8–14 kg (15–30 lb).

How tall is a Pyrenean Shepherd in feet?

A male Pyrenean Shepherd stands 1 ft 4 in to 1 ft 9 in tall at the shoulder (40–54 cm) and a female 1 ft 3 in to 1 ft 8 in (38–52 cm). Next to a 5′9″ adult, an average male reaches about knee height.

Is a Pyrenean Shepherd a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 10 kg (22 lb), the Pyrenean Shepherd counts as a medium dog breed on this site (medium = 10–25 kg (22–55 lb) on average). It is taller than 45% of the 268 breeds listed.

How long does a Pyrenean Shepherd live?

Typical life expectancy is 17–19 years, longer than the 13.3-year average for medium breeds.

Can a Pyrenean Shepherd fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Pyrenean Shepherd weighs 6.8–14 kg (15–30 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).
