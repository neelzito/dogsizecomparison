How big is an English Foxhound?

The English Foxhound is a large-sized dog breed from England, in the Hound group. Adults stand 61 cm (24 in) at the shoulder and weigh 27–34 kg (60–75 lb); the breed standard gives the same range for males and females. On average it is taller than 72% of the 268 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 79%.

English Foxhound compared to a human

Next to a 175 cm (5′9″) adult, an average English Foxhound stands 61 cm (24 in) at the shoulder: 35% of the person's height, reaching about mid-thigh. In feet, adults stand 2 ft tall at the shoulder. Next to a 120 cm (3′11″) child, about seven years old, the shoulder comes to about hip height. The head sits higher than the shoulder, so the dog looks taller than these numbers suggest.

Typical life expectancy is 10–13 years, shorter than the 12.1-year average for large breeds.

About the English Foxhound

The English Foxhound was developed in Britain from the 16th century onwards to hunt foxes in packs, followed by riders on horseback. Hunts kept careful stud books, so many packs can trace their pedigrees back to the 18th century. Hunting foxes with hounds was banned in England and Wales in 2005 and in Scotland in 2002, and today packs follow artificial trails. English Foxhounds are rarely kept as pets, but they're friendly, sociable dogs.

What an English Foxhound's size means at home

The AKC standard calls for about 24 in (61 cm) at the shoulder, and English Foxhounds weigh about 27–34 kg (60–75 lb). An English Foxhound reaches roughly an adult's mid-thigh. It's solid, heavy-boned and athletic. The crate size calculator suggests a 42″ (107 cm) crate for an average adult.

English vs American Foxhound and Greyhound

The American Foxhound is about the same height and weight but lighter-boned and leggier. The Greyhound is much taller and about the same weight.

Temperament and exercise

English Foxhounds are friendly, gentle and very sociable, bred to live and work in packs, so they get on well with other dogs. They need a lot of exercise, ideally long runs, and their nose leads them, so recall is often unreliable. They bay loudly and don't like being alone.

Care and health

The short, hard coat needs only weekly brushing. English Foxhounds are generally healthy. Known problems include hip dysplasia and kidney disease. They often live 10–13 years.

Sources: AKC breed standard and breed history; Masters of Foxhounds Association; Hunting Act 2004.

English Foxhound size: common questions

How big does an English Foxhound get?

An adult English Foxhound stands 61 cm (24 in) tall at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 27–34 kg (60–75 lb).

How tall is an English Foxhound in feet?

An adult English Foxhound stands 2 ft tall at the shoulder (61 cm). Next to a 5′9″ adult, that reaches about mid-thigh.

Is an English Foxhound a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 31 kg (67 lb), the English Foxhound counts as a large dog breed on this site (large = 25–45 kg (55–99 lb) on average). It is taller than 72% of the 268 breeds listed.

How long does an English Foxhound live?

Typical life expectancy is 10–13 years, shorter than the 12.1-year average for large breeds.

Can an English Foxhound fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A English Foxhound weighs 27–34 kg (60–75 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).

Sizes are breed-standard ranges for adult dogs; individual dogs, working lines and dogs from different countries can be bigger or smaller. Airline notes are a general guide only. Check with your airline and your vet. Disclaimer.
