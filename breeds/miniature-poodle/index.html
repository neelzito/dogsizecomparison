How big is a Miniature Poodle?

The Miniature Poodle is a small-sized dog breed from France/Germany, in the Non-Sporting group. Adults stand 26–38 cm (10–15 in) at the shoulder and weigh 4.5–6.8 kg (9.9–15 lb); the breed standard gives the same range for males and females. On average it is taller than 21% of the 256 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 13%. At the shoulder, an average Miniature Poodle reaches about mid-shin on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 10–18 years, about average for small breeds. Lighter individuals fit airline cabin limits of 8 kg (17.6 lb) for dog and carrier; heavier ones need an airline that weighs only the carrier size, or travel in the hold.

Sizes follow the American Kennel Club (AKC) breed standard.

About the Miniature Poodle

The Miniature Poodle is the middle size of the Poodle family in the US and UK. Poodles began as water retrievers, and smaller ones were bred down from the Standard Poodle by the 18th century. Miniature Poodles became favourites as companions, and they also worked as performers: their intelligence and trainability made them stars in circuses and as truffle hunters in France. Today the Miniature Poodle is a lively, clever and affectionate family dog, and the Poodle parent of many popular small crossbreeds.

What a Miniature Poodle's size means at home

The AKC defines a Miniature Poodle as over 10 in (25.5 cm) and up to 15 in (38 cm) at the shoulder. Most weigh about 4.5–7 kg (10–15 lb), and at about 32 cm a typical one reaches mid-shin on an adult. It suits flats and small cars well, and it usually needs a 24″ (61 cm) crate. Check with the crate size calculator.

Can a Miniature Poodle fly in the cabin?

Often. Most Miniature Poodles plus a light carrier stay within the 8 kg (17.6 lb) limit many airlines set, though bigger dogs may not. Check with the airline cabin check.

Three (or four) Poodle sizes

The Toy Poodle is the smallest, at 10 in (25.5 cm) or under. The Standard Poodle is over 15 in (38 cm), and usually much bigger. The FCI adds a fourth size, the Medium Poodle (35–45 cm), and defines the Miniature as 28–35 cm. So a dog sold as a "Miniature" in Europe may be smaller than one in the US.

Coat care

Miniature Poodles have the same curly, low-shedding coat as other Poodles. It keeps growing and mats easily, so it needs brushing several times a week, down to the skin, and a clip every 4–8 weeks. Many owners choose a short, easy "puppy" or "lamb" clip.

Temperament and health

Miniature Poodles are very bright, quick to learn and eager to please, and they excel at tricks, agility and obedience. They're lively and need daily walks and play. Without mental stimulation they can become barky. Known health problems include eye diseases such as progressive retinal atrophy (PRA), loose kneecaps, Legg-Calvé-Perthes disease (a hip condition) and epilepsy. Miniature Poodles are long-lived, often 14–18 years.

Sources: AKC breed standard; FCI standard No. 172 (Poodle); The Kennel Club breed standard.

Miniature Poodle size: common questions

How big does a Miniature Poodle get?

An adult Miniature Poodle stands 26–38 cm (10–15 in) tall at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 4.5–6.8 kg (9.9–15 lb).

Is a Miniature Poodle a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 5.7 kg (12 lb), the Miniature Poodle counts as a small dog breed on this site (small = 5–10 kg (11–22 lb) on average). It is taller than 21% of the 256 breeds listed.

How long does a Miniature Poodle live?

Typical life expectancy is 10–18 years, about average for small breeds.

Can a Miniature Poodle fly in the airplane cabin?

Sometimes. Lighter individuals fit airline cabin limits of 8 kg (17.6 lb) for dog and carrier; heavier ones need an airline that weighs only the carrier size, or travel in the hold. Rules vary by airline, so check before booking.
