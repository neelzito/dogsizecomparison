How big is a Welsh Terrier?

The Welsh Terrier is a small-sized dog breed from Wales, in the Terrier group. Males stand 38 cm (15 in) at the shoulder and weigh 9.1 kg (20 lb). Females stand 36–38 cm (14.2–15 in) and weigh 8–9.5 kg (18–21 lb). On average it is taller than 32% of the 267 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 25%. At the shoulder, an average male Welsh Terrier reaches just below the knee on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 12–15 years, about average for small breeds. It is too heavy for airlines that cap dog and carrier at 8 kg (17.6 lb), so it usually flies in the hold unless the airline only checks the carrier size.

About the Welsh Terrier

The Welsh Terrier is one of the oldest terrier breeds, from the hills of Wales, where it was used to hunt foxes, badgers and otters, often running with packs of hounds. It's black and tan, square and wiry, and looks like a small Airedale Terrier. President John F. Kennedy's family had a Welsh Terrier called Charlie at the White House.

What a Welsh Terrier's size means at home

The AKC standard calls for about 15 in (38 cm) at the shoulder and about 20 lb (9 kg) for males, with females a little smaller. A Welsh Terrier reaches just below an adult's knee. It's square, sturdy and athletic. It usually needs a 30″ (76 cm) crate. Check with the crate size calculator. It's too heavy for most airline cabins.

Welsh Terrier vs Airedale and Bedlington

The Airedale is much bigger, with the same colouring. The Lakeland Terrier is a little smaller and comes in more colours. The Bedlington Terrier is a little taller and about the same weight, with a lamb-like curly coat.

Temperament and exercise

Welsh Terriers are friendly, spirited and confident, usually less fiery than some terriers but still independent. They need daily exercise and mental stimulation, and they love to dig and chase. They're clever but can be stubborn.

Coat and health

The wiry coat sheds little and is traditionally hand-stripped a few times a year, or clipped for pets. Welsh Terriers are generally healthy. Known problems include eye conditions such as lens luxation and glaucoma, and allergies. They often live 12–15 years.

Sources: AKC breed standard and breed history; FCI standard No. 78 (Welsh Terrier); Welsh Terrier Club of America.

Welsh Terrier size: common questions

How big does a Welsh Terrier get?

A fully grown male Welsh Terrier stands 38 cm (15 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 9.1 kg (20 lb). Females stand 36–38 cm (14.2–15 in) and weigh 8–9.5 kg (18–21 lb).

Is a Welsh Terrier a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 8.9 kg (20 lb), the Welsh Terrier counts as a small dog breed on this site (small = 5–10 kg (11–22 lb) on average). It is taller than 32% of the 267 breeds listed.

How long does a Welsh Terrier live?

Typical life expectancy is 12–15 years, about average for small breeds.

Can a Welsh Terrier fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Welsh Terrier weighs 8–9.5 kg (18–21 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).

Sizes are breed-standard ranges for adult dogs; individual dogs, working lines and dogs from different countries can be bigger or smaller. Airline notes are a general guide only. Check with your airline and your vet. Disclaimer.
