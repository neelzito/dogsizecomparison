How big is a Norwich Terrier?

The Norwich Terrier is a small-sized dog breed from England, in the Terrier group. Adults stand 26 cm (10 in) at the shoulder and weigh 5.4 kg (12 lb); the breed standard gives the same range for males and females. On average it is taller than 10% of the 256 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 11%. At the shoulder, an average Norwich Terrier reaches about mid-shin on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 12–15 years, about average for small breeds. At 5.4 kg (12 lb), it fits even the strictest airline cabin rule, where dog and carrier together may weigh 8 kg (17.6 lb).

Sizes follow the American Kennel Club (AKC) breed standard.

About the Norwich Terrier

The Norwich Terrier is one of the smallest working terriers. It was developed in East Anglia, England, in the late 19th century, and was popular with Cambridge University students as a ratter, earning the early name "Cantab terrier". Norwich and Norfolk Terriers were shown as one breed until The Kennel Club separated them in 1964, with the AKC following in 1979. The easiest way to tell them apart: the Norwich has pricked ears, the Norfolk drop ears.

What a Norwich's size means at home

The AKC standard calls for a height not exceeding 10 in (25.5 cm) at the shoulder and a weight of about 12 lb (5.5 kg). A Norwich reaches around mid-shin on an adult. It's compact, sturdy and short-legged. It suits any home and usually needs a 24″ (61 cm) crate. Check with the crate size calculator.

Flying with a Norwich

A Norwich usually fits in the cabin, staying within the 8 kg (17.6 lb) limit many airlines set, even with a carrier. Check with the airline cabin check.

Norwich vs Norfolk and Cairn

The Norfolk Terrier is about the same size, with drop ears. The Cairn Terrier is about the same height and a little heavier, with a shaggier coat.

Temperament and exercise

Norwich Terriers are cheerful, fearless and affectionate, usually more sociable with other dogs than many terriers. They're curious and energetic, and they need daily walks and play. Like all terriers, they love to dig and chase, and their prey drive is strong, so a secure garden is essential and recall can be unreliable near small animals.

Coat and health

The harsh, wiry double coat sheds little. It's traditionally hand-stripped a couple of times a year to keep its texture. Known health problems include upper airway syndrome (narrowed airways), epilepsy, kneecap problems and hip dysplasia. Norwich Terriers often live 12–15 years.

Sources: AKC breed standard and breed history; The Kennel Club breed standard; Norwich Terrier Club of America.

Norwich Terrier size: common questions

How big does a Norwich Terrier get?

An adult Norwich Terrier stands 26 cm (10 in) tall at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 5.4 kg (12 lb).

Is a Norwich Terrier a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 5.4 kg (12 lb), the Norwich Terrier counts as a small dog breed on this site (small = 5–10 kg (11–22 lb) on average). It is taller than 10% of the 256 breeds listed.

How long does a Norwich Terrier live?

Typical life expectancy is 12–15 years, about average for small breeds.

Can a Norwich Terrier fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually yes. At 5.4 kg (12 lb), it fits even the strictest airline cabin rule, where dog and carrier together may weigh 8 kg (17.6 lb). Check your airline's rules and carrier size limits before booking.
