How big is a Catahoula Leopard Dog?

The Catahoula Leopard Dog is a large-sized dog breed from the United States, in the Herding group. Adults stand 56–61 cm (22–24 in) at the shoulder and weigh 23–43 kg (50–95 lb); the breed standard gives the same range for males and females. On average it is taller than 67% of the 268 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 81%.

Catahoula Leopard Dog compared to a human

Next to a 175 cm (5′9″) adult, an average Catahoula Leopard Dog stands 59 cm (23 in) at the shoulder: 33% of the person's height, reaching about mid-thigh. In feet, adults stand 1 ft 10 in to 2 ft tall at the shoulder. Next to a 120 cm (3′11″) child, about seven years old, the shoulder comes to about hip height. The head sits higher than the shoulder, so the dog looks taller than these numbers suggest.

Typical life expectancy is 10–14 years, about average for large breeds.

About the Catahoula Leopard Dog

The Catahoula Leopard Dog is named after Catahoula Parish in Louisiana, where it was developed to hunt wild hogs and round up semi-wild cattle in the swamps and woods. Its ancestors probably included dogs kept by Native Americans and dogs brought by Spanish and French settlers. It's the state dog of Louisiana. The breed is known for its striking merle "leopard" coat and its often pale or mismatched "glass" eyes.

What a Catahoula's size means at home

Catahoulas usually stand about 56–61 cm at the shoulder, roughly an adult's mid-thigh, and weigh about 22.5–43 kg (50–95 lb). Their size varies a lot, because they were bred for working ability rather than looks. A Catahoula is muscular and athletic. The crate size calculator suggests a 42″ (107 cm) crate for an average adult.

Catahoula vs Old English Sheepdog and Bergamasco

The Old English Sheepdog and the Bergamasco are similar in size, but both have heavy, long coats. The Catahoula has a short, flat coat in merle, solid or brindle colours.

Temperament and exercise

Catahoulas are intelligent, energetic and independent, loyal to their family and often wary of strangers. They were bred to work hard all day, so they need a lot of exercise and a job: herding, tracking, agility or long, active days outdoors. They can be strong-willed and need consistent, confident training and early socialisation.

Merle and health

As in other merle breeds, two merle dogs should never be bred together, because "double merle" puppies have a high risk of deafness and blindness. Other known problems include hip dysplasia and eye conditions. Catahoulas often live 10–14 years.

Catahoula Leopard Dog size: common questions

How big does a Catahoula Leopard Dog get?

An adult Catahoula Leopard Dog stands 56–61 cm (22–24 in) tall at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 23–43 kg (50–95 lb).

How tall is a Catahoula Leopard Dog in feet?

An adult Catahoula Leopard Dog stands 1 ft 10 in to 2 ft tall at the shoulder (56–61 cm). Next to a 5′9″ adult, that reaches about mid-thigh.

Is a Catahoula Leopard Dog a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 33 kg (72 lb), the Catahoula Leopard Dog counts as a large dog breed on this site (large = 25–45 kg (55–99 lb) on average). It is taller than 67% of the 268 breeds listed.

How long does a Catahoula Leopard Dog live?

Typical life expectancy is 10–14 years, about average for large breeds.

Can a Catahoula Leopard Dog fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Catahoula Leopard Dog weighs 23–43 kg (50–95 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).
