How big is a Whippet?

The Whippet is a medium-sized dog breed from England, in the Hound group. Males stand 49–56 cm (19.1–22 in) at the shoulder and weigh 12–18 kg (25–40 lb). Females stand 46–54 cm (17.9–21.1 in) and weigh 12–18 kg (25–40 lb). On average it is taller than 55% of the 267 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 39%.

Whippet compared to a human

Next to a 175 cm (5′9″) adult, an average male Whippet stands 52 cm (20.6 in) at the shoulder: 30% of the person's height, reaching about knee height. An average female, at 50 cm (19.5 in), reaches about knee height. In feet, males stand 1 ft 7 in to 1 ft 10 in tall at the shoulder and females 1 ft 6 in to 1 ft 9 in. Next to a 120 cm (3′11″) child, about seven years old, the shoulder comes to about hip height. The head sits higher than the shoulder, so the dog looks taller than these numbers suggest.

Typical life expectancy is 12–15 years, about average for medium breeds. It is too heavy for airlines that cap dog and carrier at 8 kg (17.6 lb), so it usually flies in the hold unless the airline only checks the carrier size.

About the Whippet

The Whippet was developed in the north of England in the 19th century, mainly by working-class people in mining and mill towns. It was used to catch rabbits and for racing, and it earned the nickname "the poor man's racehorse". It is essentially a smaller Greyhound, probably with some terrier blood added. Whippets are the fastest dogs of their size and can reach speeds of around 55 km/h (35 mph). At home, though, they're calm, gentle and famously fond of sofas.

What a Whippet's size means at home

A male Whippet stands about 48–56 cm at the shoulder, roughly an adult's knee, and both sexes weigh about 11–18 kg (25–40 lb). It is lean and long-legged, so it looks bigger than its weight suggests, and it curls up surprisingly small. The crate size calculator suggests a 42″ (107 cm) crate because of its long legs and height. A Whippet is too heavy for an airline cabin.

Whippet vs Greyhound and Italian Greyhound

The three are like a set of nesting dolls. The Greyhound is much taller and about twice as heavy. The Italian Greyhound is far smaller, a toy breed of about 3–6 kg. All three share the same build, gentle temperament and need to sprint.

Exercise and recall

Whippets need a daily chance to run flat-out in a safe, enclosed area. Short, fast sprints matter more to them than long, slow walks. Their instinct to chase small, fast-moving animals is strong, and at full speed they can't hear you calling, so be careful about letting them off the lead near roads, livestock or wildlife. Many owners use a well-fitted martingale or sighthound collar, because a Whippet's narrow head can slip out of a normal collar.

Thin coat, thin skin

The short coat sheds little and needs almost no grooming, but it offers little protection from cold and rain. Most Whippets wear a coat in winter and love soft beds and blankets. Their thin skin tears easily on barbed wire or brambles, and small cuts are common.

Temperament and health

Whippets are affectionate, quiet and gentle, and good with children and other dogs. They rarely bark. They are sensitive, so gentle training works best. They're generally healthy and long-lived, often 12–15 years. Known problems include heart conditions and eye diseases. Because sighthounds react differently to some anaesthetics, it's worth making sure your vet knows the breed.

Whippet size: common questions

How big does a Whippet get?

A fully grown male Whippet stands 49–56 cm (19.1–22 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 12–18 kg (25–40 lb). Females stand 46–54 cm (17.9–21.1 in) and weigh 12–18 kg (25–40 lb).

How tall is a Whippet in feet?

A male Whippet stands 1 ft 7 in to 1 ft 10 in tall at the shoulder (49–56 cm) and a female 1 ft 6 in to 1 ft 9 in (46–54 cm). Next to a 5′9″ adult, an average male reaches about knee height.

Is a Whippet a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 15 kg (33 lb), the Whippet counts as a medium dog breed on this site (medium = 10–25 kg (22–55 lb) on average). It is taller than 55% of the 267 breeds listed.

How long does a Whippet live?

Typical life expectancy is 12–15 years, about average for medium breeds.

Can a Whippet fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Whippet weighs 12–18 kg (25–40 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).

Sizes are breed-standard ranges for adult dogs; individual dogs, working lines and dogs from different countries can be bigger or smaller. Airline notes are a general guide only. Check with your airline and your vet. Disclaimer.
