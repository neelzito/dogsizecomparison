How big is a Podenco Andaluz (Medium)?

The Podenco Andaluz (Medium) is a medium-sized dog breed from Spain, in the Hound group. Males stand 43–53 cm (16.9–20.9 in) at the shoulder and weigh 10–22 kg (22–49 lb). Females stand 42–52 cm (16.5–20.5 in) and weigh 10–22 kg (22–49 lb). On average it is taller than 49% of the 256 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 42%. At the shoulder, an average male Podenco Andaluz (Medium) reaches about knee height on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 12–14 years, about average for medium breeds. It is too heavy for airlines that cap dog and carrier at 8 kg (17.6 lb), so it usually flies in the hold unless the airline only checks the carrier size.

About the Podenco Andaluz (Medium)

The Podenco Andaluz (Andalusian Hound) is a lean, quick hunting dog from southern Spain, with large upright ears and a keen nose. Hunters in Andalusia have used it for centuries, mainly to hunt rabbits in packs. It uses scent, sight and hearing at once, and it flushes rabbits from thick scrub and rocky ground. Like the Ibizan Hound and the Pharaoh Hound, it belongs to the old Mediterranean "warren hound" type.

Three sizes and three coats

The Spanish kennel club (RSCE) recognises the Podenco Andaluz in three sizes:

SizeHeight at the shoulderWeight
Large (grande)53–64 cmabout 21–33 kg
Medium (mediano)42–53 cmabout 10–22 kg
Small (chico)32–42 cmabout 5–11 kg

Each size comes in three coats: smooth and short (liso), wiry (duro) and long (sedeño). That makes nine varieties of the same breed. The sizes reflect different hunting terrain: the small ones work their way into dense brambles where rabbits hide, while the large ones were also used on bigger game. The breed is recognised by the RSCE but not by the FCI, so you won't see it at most international shows.

What a medium Podenco's size means at home

At around 48 cm at the shoulder, a medium Podenco reaches roughly an adult's knee. It is slim and fine-boned, so it weighs less than its height suggests. It curls up small on a sofa, but it is a strong runner and jumper: a garden fence often needs to be 1.8 m (6 ft) high, and many Podencos can open doors and climb.

Living with a Podenco

Podencos are gentle and affectionate with their people, often reserved with strangers, and usually get along well with other dogs, since they are bred to hunt in packs. Their hunting instinct is strong, so recall off the lead can be unreliable wherever there are rabbits, cats or birds. A long line and a secure area for running work well. They need daily exercise but are usually calm indoors once that need is met.

Adopting a Podenco

Many Podencos end up in Spanish shelters, often after the hunting season, and rescue groups rehome them all over Europe. If you're thinking about a Podenco, adoption is a common route. Ask the rescue about the dog's size, temperament and how it gets on with cats, because Podencos from shelters are often mixes of sizes and coats.

Podenco Andaluz (Medium) size: common questions

How big does a Podenco Andaluz (Medium) get?

A fully grown male Podenco Andaluz (Medium) stands 43–53 cm (16.9–20.9 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 10–22 kg (22–49 lb). Females stand 42–52 cm (16.5–20.5 in) and weigh 10–22 kg (22–49 lb).

Is a Podenco Andaluz (Medium) a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 16 kg (35 lb), the Podenco Andaluz (Medium) counts as a medium dog breed on this site (medium = 10–25 kg (22–55 lb) on average). It is taller than 49% of the 256 breeds listed.

How long does a Podenco Andaluz (Medium) live?

Typical life expectancy is 12–14 years, about average for medium breeds.

Can a Podenco Andaluz (Medium) fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Podenco Andaluz (Medium) weighs 10–22 kg (22–49 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).
