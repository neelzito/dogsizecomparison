How big is a Puggle?

The Puggle is a small-sized dog breed from the United States, in the designer & mixed category. Males stand 25–38 cm (9.8–15 in) at the shoulder and weigh 6.8–14 kg (15–30 lb). Females stand 25–36 cm (9.8–14.2 in) and weigh 6.5–12 kg (14–26 lb). On average it is taller than 20% of the 268 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 28%.

Puggle compared to a human

Next to a 175 cm (5′9″) adult, an average male Puggle stands 32 cm (12.4 in) at the shoulder: 18% of the person's height, reaching about mid-shin. An average female, at 31 cm (12 in), reaches about mid-shin. In feet, males stand 10 in to 1 ft 3 in tall at the shoulder and females 10 in to 1 ft 2 in. Next to a 120 cm (3′11″) child, about seven years old, the shoulder comes to about knee height. The head sits higher than the shoulder, so the dog looks taller than these numbers suggest.

Typical life expectancy is 10–15 years, shorter than the 13.7-year average for small breeds. Lighter individuals fit airline cabin limits of 8 kg (17.6 lb) for dog and carrier; heavier ones need an airline that weighs only the carrier size, or travel in the hold.

About the Puggle

The Puggle is a cross between a Pug and a Beagle. Breeders in the US began producing them in the 1980s, and they became one of the most fashionable designer crosses of the 2000s. The idea was a dog with a longer nose than a Pug, for easier breathing, combined with the Beagle's friendly nature. As a crossbreed, the Puggle has no official standard, and its looks and health vary.

How big does a Puggle get?

Most Puggles stand about 25–38 cm at the shoulder, around mid-shin on an adult, and weigh about 6.5–13.5 kg (15–30 lb). Next to a Pomsky, a Puggle is about the same height and a little lighter. Use the puppy weight calculator as a rough guide.

What a Puggle's size means at home

A Puggle suits flats and houses and usually needs a 24″ (61 cm) crate. Check with the crate size calculator. Lighter Puggles may fit within an 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit, but many won't. Check with the airline cabin check.

Temperament and exercise

Puggles are affectionate, playful and sociable, usually good with children and other dogs. They can inherit the Beagle's nose and stubbornness, and its bay. They love food and gain weight easily, so watch portions. They need daily walks and play.

Breathing and health

A Puggle's nose is usually longer than a Pug's, but some still have breathing problems, especially in heat. Other known problems include eye conditions, loose kneecaps, hip dysplasia and allergies. Choose a breeder who health-tests both parents. Puggles often live 10–15 years.

Sources: AKC breed standards for the Pug and the Beagle.

Puggle size: common questions

How big does a Puggle get?

A fully grown male Puggle stands 25–38 cm (9.8–15 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 6.8–14 kg (15–30 lb). Females stand 25–36 cm (9.8–14.2 in) and weigh 6.5–12 kg (14–26 lb).

How tall is a Puggle in feet?

A male Puggle stands 10 in to 1 ft 3 in tall at the shoulder (25–38 cm) and a female 10 in to 1 ft 2 in (25–36 cm). Next to a 5′9″ adult, an average male reaches about mid-shin.

Is a Puggle a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 9.7 kg (21 lb), the Puggle counts as a small dog breed on this site (small = 5–10 kg (11–22 lb) on average). It is taller than 20% of the 268 breeds listed.

How long does a Puggle live?

Typical life expectancy is 10–15 years, shorter than the 13.7-year average for small breeds.

Can a Puggle fly in the airplane cabin?

Sometimes. Lighter individuals fit airline cabin limits of 8 kg (17.6 lb) for dog and carrier; heavier ones need an airline that weighs only the carrier size, or travel in the hold. Rules vary by airline, so check before booking.
