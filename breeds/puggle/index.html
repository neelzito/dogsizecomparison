How big is a Puggle?

The Puggle is a small-sized dog breed from the United States, in the designer & mixed category. Males stand 25–38 cm (9.8–15 in) at the shoulder and weigh 6.8–14 kg (15–30 lb). Females stand 25–36 cm (9.8–14.2 in) and weigh 6.5–12 kg (14–26 lb). On average it is taller than 19% of the 256 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 27%. At the shoulder, an average male Puggle reaches about mid-shin on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 10–15 years, shorter than the 13.7-year average for small breeds. Lighter individuals fit airline cabin limits of 8 kg (17.6 lb) for dog and carrier; heavier ones need an airline that weighs only the carrier size, or travel in the hold.

Puggle size: common questions

How big does a Puggle get?

A fully grown male Puggle stands 25–38 cm (9.8–15 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 6.8–14 kg (15–30 lb). Females stand 25–36 cm (9.8–14.2 in) and weigh 6.5–12 kg (14–26 lb).

Is a Puggle a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 9.7 kg (21 lb), the Puggle counts as a small dog breed on this site (small = 5–10 kg (11–22 lb) on average). It is taller than 19% of the 256 breeds listed.

How long does a Puggle live?

Typical life expectancy is 10–15 years, shorter than the 13.7-year average for small breeds.

Can a Puggle fly in the airplane cabin?

Sometimes. Lighter individuals fit airline cabin limits of 8 kg (17.6 lb) for dog and carrier; heavier ones need an airline that weighs only the carrier size, or travel in the hold. Rules vary by airline, so check before booking.
