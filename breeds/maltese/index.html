How big is a Maltese?

The Maltese is a toy dog breed from Malta, in the Toy group. Adults stand 18–23 cm (7.1–9.1 in) at the shoulder and weigh 1.8–3.2 kg (4–7.1 lb); the breed standard gives the same range for males and females. On average that makes it the 7th shortest of the 266 breeds on this site. At the shoulder, an average Maltese reaches about ankle height on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 12–15 years, about average for toy breeds. At 1.8–3.2 kg (4–7.1 lb), it fits even the strictest airline cabin rule, where dog and carrier together may weigh 8 kg (17.6 lb).

Sizes follow the American Kennel Club (AKC) breed standard.

About the Maltese

The Maltese is one of the oldest toy breeds, a small white companion dog that has been kept around the Mediterranean for more than two thousand years. Ancient Greek and Roman writers described little white dogs from the region, and Maltese-type dogs appear on Greek pottery. The breed is named after Malta, although some historians think the name refers to the Croatian island of Mljet or a town in Sicily. Kept by noblewomen and royalty for centuries, the Maltese is gentle, playful and devoted to its people.

What a Maltese's size means at home

A Maltese stands about 18–23 cm at the shoulder, around ankle height on an adult. The AKC calls for under 7 lb (3.2 kg), and most weigh 2–3 kg. It fits into any home, including small flats, and usually needs an 18″ (46 cm) crate or carrier. Check with the crate size calculator. Its small size means it can be hurt by falls or rough handling, so teach children to sit down to hold it.

Flying with a Maltese

A Maltese is one of the easiest dogs to take in an airline cabin. Even with a carrier, it is far under the 8 kg (17.6 lb) limit many airlines set. Check your airline's carrier rules with the airline cabin check.

Maltese vs Bichon, Shih Tzu and Yorkie

The Maltese is often confused with other small white or long-coated dogs:

  • The Bichon Frise is taller and heavier, with a curly coat.
  • The Shih Tzu is sturdier, with a flatter face.
  • The Yorkshire Terrier is about the same size but has a blue-and-tan coat and a terrier's temperament.

Coat care

The Maltese has a single coat of long, straight, silky white hair and no undercoat. It sheds very little, but a full coat needs daily brushing to prevent mats. Most pet owners choose a short "puppy cut". Tear staining (reddish-brown marks under the eyes) is common, and gentle daily cleaning helps.

Health

Like many toy breeds, the Maltese is prone to dental disease because its teeth are crowded. Daily tooth brushing and regular dental checks make a big difference. Loose kneecaps (luxating patella) and a weak windpipe (tracheal collapse) also occur, so use a harness rather than a collar. Maltese are long-lived, typically 12–15 years.

Sources: AKC breed standard and breed history; The Kennel Club breed standard; FCI standard No. 65 (Maltese).

Maltese size: common questions

How big does a Maltese get?

An adult Maltese stands 18–23 cm (7.1–9.1 in) tall at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 1.8–3.2 kg (4–7.1 lb).

Is a Maltese a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 2.5 kg (5.5 lb), the Maltese counts as a toy dog breed on this site (toy = under 5 kg (11 lb) on average). It is the 7th shortest of the 266 breeds listed.

How long does a Maltese live?

Typical life expectancy is 12–15 years, about average for toy breeds.

Can a Maltese fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually yes. At 1.8–3.2 kg (4–7.1 lb), it fits even the strictest airline cabin rule, where dog and carrier together may weigh 8 kg (17.6 lb). Check your airline's rules and carrier size limits before booking.
