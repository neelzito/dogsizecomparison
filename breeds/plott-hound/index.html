How big is a Plott Hound?

The Plott Hound is a medium-sized dog breed from the United States, in the Hound group. Males stand 51–64 cm (20.1–25 in) at the shoulder and weigh 23–27 kg (50–60 lb). Females stand 51–59 cm (20.1–23 in) and weigh 18–25 kg (40–55 lb). On average it is taller than 60% of the 256 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 56%. At the shoulder, an average male Plott Hound reaches about knee height on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 12–14 years, about average for medium breeds. It is too heavy for airlines that cap dog and carrier at 8 kg (17.6 lb), so it usually flies in the hold unless the airline only checks the carrier size.

About the Plott Hound

The Plott Hound is named after Johannes Plott, a German immigrant who brought hunting dogs to North Carolina around 1750. The Plott family bred these dogs for generations to hunt bears and wild boar in the Great Smoky Mountains. Unlike most American coonhounds, which descend from English foxhounds, the Plott has German roots. It's the state dog of North Carolina, and it's known for its brindle coat and bold, determined nature.

What a Plott's size means at home

The AKC standard calls for 20–25 in (51–63.5 cm) at the shoulder and 50–60 lb (22.5–27 kg) for males, and 20–23 in (51–58.5 cm) and 40–55 lb (18–25 kg) for females. A Plott reaches roughly an adult's knee. The crate size calculator suggests a 42″ (107 cm) crate for an average male.

Plott vs Pharaoh Hound and Thai Ridgeback

The Pharaoh Hound is taller and a little lighter. The Thai Ridgeback is about the same size.

Temperament and exercise

Plotts are loyal, brave and intelligent, affectionate with their family. They need a lot of exercise, and their nose and hunting drive are strong. They have a loud, ringing bay.

Care and health

The short, glossy coat needs only weekly brushing. Known health problems include bloat and hip dysplasia. Plotts often live 12–14 years.

Sources: AKC breed standard and breed history; National Plott Hound Association.

Plott Hound size: common questions

How big does a Plott Hound get?

A fully grown male Plott Hound stands 51–64 cm (20.1–25 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 23–27 kg (50–60 lb). Females stand 51–59 cm (20.1–23 in) and weigh 18–25 kg (40–55 lb).

Is a Plott Hound a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 23 kg (51 lb), the Plott Hound counts as a medium dog breed on this site (medium = 10–25 kg (22–55 lb) on average). It is taller than 60% of the 256 breeds listed.

How long does a Plott Hound live?

Typical life expectancy is 12–14 years, about average for medium breeds.

Can a Plott Hound fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Plott Hound weighs 18–27 kg (40–60 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).
