How big is a Podenco Orito Español?

The Podenco Orito Español is a medium-sized dog breed from Spain, in the Hound group. Males stand 40–50 cm (15.7–19.7 in) at the shoulder and weigh 16–22 kg (35–49 lb). Females stand 38–48 cm (15–18.9 in) and weigh 14–20 kg (31–44 lb). On average it is taller than 40% of the 256 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 43%. At the shoulder, an average male Podenco Orito Español reaches just below the knee on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 12–14 years, about average for medium breeds. It is too heavy for airlines that cap dog and carrier at 8 kg (17.6 lb), so it usually flies in the hold unless the airline only checks the carrier size.

About the Podenco Orito Español

The Podenco Orito Español is a medium-sized Spanish hunting dog used to hunt rabbits, mainly in central and southern Spain. It's recognised by the Spanish kennel club (RSCE) as an ethnic group, but it has no definitive published standard yet, and it is not recognised by the FCI. It shares the classic Podenco build: lean, agile, with large upright ears and sharp senses.

What an Orito's size means at home

Because there is no final standard, published sizes vary. The figures on this page, about 40–50 cm at the shoulder and 14–22 kg (30–48 lb), are commonly cited ranges rather than official ones. An Orito reaches just below an adult's knee. The crate size calculator suggests a 36″ (91 cm) crate for an average dog.

Orito vs Portuguese Podengo and GBGV

The Portuguese Podengo (Medium) is about the same size. The Grand Basset Griffon Vendéen is a similar weight but longer and lower.

Living with an Orito

Oritos are affectionate and gentle with their family and usually good with other dogs. Their hunting instinct is strong, so recall can be unreliable, and they need a secure garden. Like other Podencos, many end up in Spanish shelters and are adopted abroad.

Care and health

The short coat is easy to care for. Little breed-specific health data exists. Oritos are generally hardy and often live 12–14 years.

Sources: Real Sociedad Canina de España (RSCE); Spanish Podenco breed associations.

Podenco Orito Español size: common questions

How big does a Podenco Orito Español get?

A fully grown male Podenco Orito Español stands 40–50 cm (15.7–19.7 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 16–22 kg (35–49 lb). Females stand 38–48 cm (15–18.9 in) and weigh 14–20 kg (31–44 lb).

Is a Podenco Orito Español a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 18 kg (40 lb), the Podenco Orito Español counts as a medium dog breed on this site (medium = 10–25 kg (22–55 lb) on average). It is taller than 40% of the 256 breeds listed.

How long does a Podenco Orito Español live?

Typical life expectancy is 12–14 years, about average for medium breeds.

Can a Podenco Orito Español fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Podenco Orito Español weighs 14–22 kg (31–49 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).

Sizes are breed-standard ranges for adult dogs; individual dogs, working lines and dogs from different countries can be bigger or smaller. Airline notes are a general guide only. Check with your airline and your vet. Disclaimer.
