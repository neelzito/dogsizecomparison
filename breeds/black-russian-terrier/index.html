How big is a Black Russian Terrier?

The Black Russian Terrier is a giant-sized dog breed from Russia, in the Working group. Males stand 69–76 cm (27–29.9 in) at the shoulder and weigh 37–59 kg (80–130 lb). Females stand 66–74 cm (26–28.9 in) and weigh 37–59 kg (80–130 lb). On average it is taller than 94% of the 266 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 92%. At the shoulder, an average male Black Russian Terrier reaches about mid-thigh on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 10–12 years, longer than the 10.1-year average for giant breeds.

About the Black Russian Terrier

The Black Russian Terrier was created by the Soviet army in the 1940s and 50s at the Red Star Kennel near Moscow. After the Second World War, the Soviet Union needed a large, tough, trainable guard dog that could cope with the Russian climate. Breeders crossed the Giant Schnauzer, Airedale Terrier, Rottweiler, Newfoundland and other breeds. Despite its name, it's not really a terrier, but a large working and guard dog. The FCI recognised it in 1984 and the AKC in 2004.

What a Black Russian's size means at home

The AKC standard calls for 27–30 in (68.5–76 cm) at the shoulder for males and 26–29 in (66–73.5 cm) for females. Black Russians weigh about 36.5–59 kg (80–130 lb), heavy enough to put them in the giant size class on this site. A Black Russian reaches roughly an adult's mid-thigh, and its thick black coat makes it look even bigger. It needs a large vehicle and, because of its height, the crate size calculator suggests a 54″ (137 cm) crate for an average male.

Black Russian vs Komondor and Kuvasz

The Hungarian Komondor is a little taller and heavier on average, with its unique white cords. The Kuvasz, another white Hungarian guardian, is about the same size. Compared with the Giant Schnauzer, one of its ancestors, the Black Russian is taller and more heavily built.

Temperament and training

Black Russian Terriers are calm, confident and very loyal, often gentle with their own family and children, but naturally protective and wary of strangers. They're intelligent but can be strong-willed, so they need early socialisation and consistent, confident training. They need daily exercise and mental work, and they want to be close to their people. Left alone in a garden, they can become bored and destructive.

Coat and health

The thick, wavy, weather-resistant coat sheds little but needs brushing at least twice a week and a trim every couple of months. The beard collects food and water and needs regular cleaning. Known health problems include hip and elbow dysplasia, eye conditions, and juvenile laryngeal paralysis with polyneuropathy, for which a DNA test exists. Black Russians often live 10–12 years.

Sources: AKC breed standard and breed history; FCI standard No. 327 (Russian Black Terrier); Black Russian Terrier Club of America.

Black Russian Terrier size: common questions

How big does a Black Russian Terrier get?

A fully grown male Black Russian Terrier stands 69–76 cm (27–29.9 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 37–59 kg (80–130 lb). Females stand 66–74 cm (26–28.9 in) and weigh 37–59 kg (80–130 lb).

Is a Black Russian Terrier a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 48 kg (105 lb), the Black Russian Terrier counts as a giant dog breed on this site (giant = 45 kg (99 lb) and over on average). It is taller than 94% of the 266 breeds listed.

How long does a Black Russian Terrier live?

Typical life expectancy is 10–12 years, longer than the 10.1-year average for giant breeds.

Can a Black Russian Terrier fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Black Russian Terrier weighs 37–59 kg (80–130 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).

Sizes are breed-standard ranges for adult dogs; individual dogs, working lines and dogs from different countries can be bigger or smaller. Airline notes are a general guide only. Check with your airline and your vet. Disclaimer.
