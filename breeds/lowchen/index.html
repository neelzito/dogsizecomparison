How big is a Löwchen?

The Löwchen is a small-sized dog breed from France/Germany, in the Non-Sporting group. Adults stand 31–36 cm (12–14 in) at the shoulder and weigh 6.8 kg (15 lb); the breed standard gives the same range for males and females. On average it is taller than 23% of the 256 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 15%. At the shoulder, an average Löwchen reaches about mid-shin on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 13–15 years, about average for small breeds. It is too heavy for airlines that cap dog and carrier at 8 kg (17.6 lb), so it usually flies in the hold unless the airline only checks the carrier size.

Sizes follow the American Kennel Club (AKC) breed standard.

About the Löwchen

The Löwchen ("little lion dog") is a small European companion dog, related to the Bichon family. It appears in European paintings from the Renaissance onwards. It's traditionally clipped in a "lion trim", with the hindquarters shaved and a full mane at the front. By the 1960s it was so rare that Guinness listed it as the world's rarest breed. Breeders have since built up its numbers. It's a lively, affectionate and confident little dog.

What a Löwchen's size means at home

The AKC standard calls for 12–14 in (30.5–35.5 cm) at the shoulder, and Löwchen weigh about 6.5–7 kg (15 lb). A Löwchen reaches around mid-shin on an adult. It suits any home and usually needs a 24″ (61 cm) crate. Check with the crate size calculator. With a carrier it's usually just over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit.

Löwchen vs Japanese Spitz and Bichon

The Japanese Spitz is about the same height and a little heavier. The Löwchen is related to the Bichon Frise but has a wavier, longer coat and comes in many colours.

Temperament and exercise

Löwchen are cheerful, playful and very attached to their people. They're alert watchdogs and can be vocal. They need daily walks and play, and they enjoy learning tricks.

Coat and health

The long, wavy coat sheds little but needs brushing several times a week. Löwchen are generally healthy. Known problems include loose kneecaps and eye conditions. They often live 13–15 years.

Sources: AKC breed standard and breed history; FCI standard No. 233 (Petit Chien Lion); The Kennel Club breed standard.

Löwchen size: common questions

How big does a Löwchen get?

An adult Löwchen stands 31–36 cm (12–14 in) tall at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 6.8 kg (15 lb).

Is a Löwchen a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 6.8 kg (15 lb), the Löwchen counts as a small dog breed on this site (small = 5–10 kg (11–22 lb) on average). It is taller than 23% of the 256 breeds listed.

How long does a Löwchen live?

Typical life expectancy is 13–15 years, about average for small breeds.

Can a Löwchen fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Löwchen weighs 6.8 kg (15 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).
