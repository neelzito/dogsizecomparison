How big is a Greyhound?

The Greyhound is a large-sized dog breed from England, in the Hound group. Males stand 71–76 cm (28–29.9 in) at the shoulder and weigh 30–32 kg (65–71 lb). Females stand 69–71 cm (27–28 in) and weigh 27–30 kg (60–65 lb). On average it is taller than 96% of the 268 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 75%.

Greyhound compared to a human

Next to a 175 cm (5′9″) adult, an average male Greyhound stands 74 cm (28.9 in) at the shoulder: 42% of the person's height, reaching about mid-thigh. An average female, at 70 cm (27.5 in), reaches about mid-thigh. In feet, males stand 2 ft 4 in to 2 ft 6 in tall at the shoulder and females 2 ft 3 in to 2 ft 4 in. Next to a 120 cm (3′11″) child, about seven years old, the shoulder comes to about waist height. The head sits higher than the shoulder, so the dog looks taller than these numbers suggest.

Typical life expectancy is 10–13 years, shorter than the 12.1-year average for large breeds.

About the Greyhound

The Greyhound is one of the oldest dog types. Greyhound-like sighthounds appear in art from ancient Egypt, Greece and Rome. In Britain, the breed was long a symbol of nobility, used for coursing hares and deer. In the 20th century, Greyhound track racing became hugely popular in the UK, Ireland, the US and Australia. The Greyhound is the fastest dog breed, reaching about 70 km/h (45 mph) over a short distance. At home, though, it's a famously calm and gentle "45 mph couch potato".

What a Greyhound's size means at home

A male Greyhound stands about 71–76 cm at the shoulder, roughly an adult's mid-thigh, and weighs about 30–32 kg (65–70 lb). Females are about 69–71 cm and 27–30 kg. Racing Greyhounds are often a little smaller than show Greyhounds. It's tall and long-legged but slim, and it folds up surprisingly small on a sofa. Because of its height, the crate size calculator suggests a 54″ (137 cm) crate for an average male.

Greyhound vs Whippet

The Whippet looks like a small Greyhound: it is about 20 cm shorter and half the weight. The English Foxhound is shorter but similar in weight. Among the sighthounds, the Irish Wolfhound, Scottish Deerhound and Borzoi are taller on average.

Retired racing Greyhounds

Thousands of Greyhounds retire from racing every year, often at only 2–5 years old, and rescue organisations rehome them as pets. Retired racers are usually calm, gentle and easy to live with. They often need help with things they've never seen, such as stairs, glass doors or the inside of a house. Many have a strong urge to chase small animals, so ask the rescue whether a dog has been tested with cats.

Living with a Greyhound

Greyhounds need less exercise than people expect. Two walks a day and a chance to sprint safely in an enclosed area are usually enough, and they sleep a lot. They shouldn't be let off the lead near roads or livestock, because at full speed they can't hear you. Their thin skin and short coat give little protection, so they need a coat in winter and a soft bed. Use a martingale collar, because a Greyhound's narrow head slips out of normal collars.

Health

Greyhounds are generally healthy, but bone cancer (osteosarcoma) is more common than in many breeds. They also react differently to some anaesthetics because they have so little body fat, so choose a vet who knows sighthounds. They often live 10–13 years.

Greyhound size: common questions

How big does a Greyhound get?

A fully grown male Greyhound stands 71–76 cm (28–29.9 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 30–32 kg (65–71 lb). Females stand 69–71 cm (27–28 in) and weigh 27–30 kg (60–65 lb).

How tall is a Greyhound in feet?

A male Greyhound stands 2 ft 4 in to 2 ft 6 in tall at the shoulder (71–76 cm) and a female 2 ft 3 in to 2 ft 4 in (69–71 cm). Next to a 5′9″ adult, an average male reaches about mid-thigh.

Is a Greyhound a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 30 kg (65 lb), the Greyhound counts as a large dog breed on this site (large = 25–45 kg (55–99 lb) on average). It is taller than 96% of the 268 breeds listed.

How long does a Greyhound live?

Typical life expectancy is 10–13 years, shorter than the 12.1-year average for large breeds.

Can a Greyhound fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Greyhound weighs 27–32 kg (60–71 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).

Sizes are breed-standard ranges for adult dogs; individual dogs, working lines and dogs from different countries can be bigger or smaller. Airline notes are a general guide only. Check with your airline and your vet. Disclaimer.
