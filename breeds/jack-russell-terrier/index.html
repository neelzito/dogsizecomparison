How big is a Jack Russell Terrier?

The Jack Russell Terrier is a small-sized dog breed from England, in the Terrier group. Adults stand 25–30 cm (9.8–11.8 in) at the shoulder and weigh 5–6 kg (11–13 lb); the breed standard gives the same range for males and females. On average it is taller than 14% of the 256 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 12%. At the shoulder, an average Jack Russell Terrier reaches about mid-shin on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 13–16 years, longer than the 13.7-year average for small breeds. At 5–6 kg (11–13 lb), it fits even the strictest airline cabin rule, where dog and carrier together may weigh 8 kg (17.6 lb).

Sizes follow the FCI breed standard.

About the Jack Russell Terrier

The Jack Russell Terrier is named after the Reverend John "Jack" Russell, a hunting parson in Devon in the 19th century, who bred small, white, tough terriers that could run with foxhounds and bolt foxes from their dens. White coats made the dogs easy to tell apart from the fox. Over time, Russell-type terriers split into several breeds, and today the name can mean slightly different dogs depending on the country.

Jack Russell, Parson Russell or Russell Terrier?

  • Jack Russell Terrier (FCI, The Kennel Club): the shorter-legged type, 25–30 cm at the shoulder and about 5–6 kg, slightly longer than tall. This page shows this type.
  • Parson Russell Terrier: the taller, square-built type, about 33–36 cm, closer to the original hunting dogs.
  • Russell Terrier (AKC): the American name for the short-legged type, about 25–30 cm.

In everyday use, "Jack Russell" is often used for any small, mostly white working terrier, so sizes of pet dogs vary a lot.

What a Jack Russell's size means at home

A Jack Russell stands around mid-shin on an adult and weighs about 5–6 kg (11–13 lb). It fits in a flat or small car and usually needs a 24″ (61 cm) crate. Check with the crate size calculator. But don't let the size fool you: this is a high-energy working terrier that needs a lot of exercise and mental stimulation every day.

Flying with a Jack Russell

A Jack Russell is one of the few active terrier breeds that usually fits the airline cabin. With a light carrier, it stays under the 8 kg (17.6 lb) limit many airlines set for dog and carrier. Check with the airline cabin check.

Living with a Jack Russell

Jack Russells are clever, fearless and full of energy. They love to dig, chase and explore, and they can be escape artists, so a secure garden is essential. Their prey drive is strong, so small pets may not be safe, and recall can be unreliable near squirrels or rabbits. A bored Jack Russell can be noisy and destructive. With enough exercise, training and games, they are bright, funny and affectionate companions.

Health

Jack Russells are generally hardy and long-lived, often 13–16 years. Known problems include loose kneecaps, lens luxation (a displaced lens in the eye), deafness, and late-onset ataxia, a nerve disease. DNA tests exist for some of these, so ask breeders for the results.

Sources: FCI standard No. 345 (Jack Russell Terrier); The Kennel Club breed standard; AKC breed standards (Russell Terrier, Parson Russell Terrier).

Jack Russell Terrier size: common questions

How big does a Jack Russell Terrier get?

An adult Jack Russell Terrier stands 25–30 cm (9.8–11.8 in) tall at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 5–6 kg (11–13 lb).

Is a Jack Russell Terrier a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 5.5 kg (12 lb), the Jack Russell Terrier counts as a small dog breed on this site (small = 5–10 kg (11–22 lb) on average). It is taller than 14% of the 256 breeds listed.

How long does a Jack Russell Terrier live?

Typical life expectancy is 13–16 years, longer than the 13.7-year average for small breeds.

Can a Jack Russell Terrier fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually yes. At 5–6 kg (11–13 lb), it fits even the strictest airline cabin rule, where dog and carrier together may weigh 8 kg (17.6 lb). Check your airline's rules and carrier size limits before booking.
