How big is a Dalmatian?

The Dalmatian is a large-sized dog breed from Croatia, in the Non-Sporting group. Adults stand 49–61 cm (19.1–24 in) at the shoulder and weigh 21–32 kg (45–71 lb); the breed standard gives the same range for males and females. On average it is taller than 58% of the 247 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 64%. At the shoulder, an average Dalmatian reaches about knee height on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 11–13 years, about average for large breeds.

Dalmatian size: common questions

How big does a Dalmatian get?

An adult Dalmatian stands 49–61 cm (19.1–24 in) tall at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 21–32 kg (45–71 lb).

Is a Dalmatian a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 26 kg (58 lb), the Dalmatian counts as a large dog breed on this site (large = 25–45 kg (55–99 lb) on average). It is taller than 58% of the 247 breeds listed.

How long does a Dalmatian live?

Typical life expectancy is 11–13 years, about average for large breeds.

Can a Dalmatian fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Dalmatian weighs 21–32 kg (45–71 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).

Sizes are breed-standard ranges for adult dogs; individual dogs, working lines and dogs from different countries can be bigger or smaller. Airline notes are a general guide only. Check with your airline and your vet. Disclaimer.
