How big is a Japanese Chin?

The Japanese Chin is a toy dog breed from Japan, in the Toy group. Adults stand 21–28 cm (8.1–11 in) at the shoulder and weigh 3.2–5 kg (7.1–11 lb); the breed standard gives the same range for males and females. On average it is taller than 4% of the 247 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 6%. At the shoulder, an average Japanese Chin reaches about mid-shin on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 10–12 years, shorter than the 13.9-year average for toy breeds. At 3.2–5 kg (7.1–11 lb), it fits even the strictest airline cabin rule, where dog and carrier together may weigh 8 kg (17.6 lb).

Japanese Chin size: common questions

How big does a Japanese Chin get?

An adult Japanese Chin stands 21–28 cm (8.1–11 in) tall at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 3.2–5 kg (7.1–11 lb).

Is a Japanese Chin a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 4.1 kg (9 lb), the Japanese Chin counts as a toy dog breed on this site (toy = under 5 kg (11 lb) on average). It is taller than 4% of the 247 breeds listed.

How long does a Japanese Chin live?

Typical life expectancy is 10–12 years, shorter than the 13.9-year average for toy breeds.

Can a Japanese Chin fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually yes. At 3.2–5 kg (7.1–11 lb), it fits even the strictest airline cabin rule, where dog and carrier together may weigh 8 kg (17.6 lb). Check your airline's rules and carrier size limits before booking.
