How big is a Thai Ridgeback?

The Thai Ridgeback is a large-sized dog breed from Thailand, in the Hound group. Adults stand 51–61 cm (20.1–24 in) at the shoulder and weigh 16–34 kg (35–75 lb); the breed standard gives the same range for males and females. On average it is taller than 61% of the 267 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 64%. At the shoulder, an average Thai Ridgeback reaches about knee height on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 14–15 years, longer than the 12.1-year average for large breeds.

Sizes follow the American Kennel Club (AKC) breed standard.

About the Thai Ridgeback

The Thai Ridgeback is an ancient breed from Thailand, especially the east of the country, where it was used for hunting, guarding and pulling carts. Like the Rhodesian Ridgeback, it has a ridge of hair growing in the opposite direction along its back. It developed in relative isolation, with little outside influence, and it remains rare outside Thailand.

What a Thai Ridgeback's size means at home

The breed standard calls for about 56–61 cm at the shoulder for males and 51–56 cm for females. The figures on this page show about 16–34 kg (35–75 lb). A Thai Ridgeback reaches roughly an adult's knee. It's muscular and agile, and its average weight puts it in the large size class on this site. The crate size calculator suggests a 42″ (107 cm) crate.

Thai vs Rhodesian Ridgeback and Plott

The Rhodesian Ridgeback is taller and heavier. The Plott Hound is about the same size.

Temperament and exercise

Thai Ridgebacks are independent, loyal and protective, devoted to their family but wary of strangers. They have a strong prey drive and can climb and jump. They need an experienced owner, early socialisation and daily exercise.

Care and health

The short coat needs little grooming. Like the Rhodesian, Thai Ridgebacks can be born with dermoid sinus, a defect under the skin along the spine. Thai Ridgebacks often live 14–15 years.

Sources: AKC breed information; FCI standard No. 338 (Thai Ridgeback Dog).

Thai Ridgeback size: common questions

How big does a Thai Ridgeback get?

An adult Thai Ridgeback stands 51–61 cm (20.1–24 in) tall at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 16–34 kg (35–75 lb).

Is a Thai Ridgeback a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 25 kg (55 lb), the Thai Ridgeback counts as a large dog breed on this site (large = 25–45 kg (55–99 lb) on average). It is taller than 61% of the 267 breeds listed.

How long does a Thai Ridgeback live?

Typical life expectancy is 14–15 years, longer than the 12.1-year average for large breeds.

Can a Thai Ridgeback fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Thai Ridgeback weighs 16–34 kg (35–75 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).
