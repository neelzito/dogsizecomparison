How big is a Bolognese?

The Bolognese is a toy dog breed from Italy, in the Toy group. Adults stand 26–31 cm (10–12 in) at the shoulder and weigh 2.7–4.5 kg (6–9.9 lb); the breed standard gives the same range for males and females. On average it is taller than 13% of the 268 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 4%.

Bolognese compared to a human

Next to a 175 cm (5′9″) adult, an average Bolognese stands 28 cm (11 in) at the shoulder: 16% of the person's height, reaching about mid-shin. In feet, adults stand 10 in to 1 ft tall at the shoulder. Next to a 120 cm (3′11″) child, about seven years old, the shoulder comes to just below the knee. The head sits higher than the shoulder, so the dog looks taller than these numbers suggest.

Typical life expectancy is 12–14 years, shorter than the 14.1-year average for toy breeds. At 2.7–4.5 kg (6–9.9 lb), it fits even the strictest airline cabin rule, where dog and carrier together may weigh 8 kg (17.6 lb).

About the Bolognese

The Bolognese is a small white Italian companion dog from the Bichon family, named after the city of Bologna. It was a favourite of Italian nobility during the Renaissance, and the Medici family gave Bolognese dogs as gifts to other royal courts. Unlike the Bichon Frise's curly coat, the Bolognese has a coat of loose, fluffy ringlets. It's a calm, devoted and quiet companion.

What a Bolognese's size means at home

The FCI standard calls for about 27–30 cm at the shoulder for males and 25–28 cm for females, and Bolognese weigh about 2.5–4.5 kg (6–10 lb). A Bolognese reaches around mid-shin on an adult. It suits any home and usually needs a 24″ (61 cm) crate. Check with the crate size calculator. It easily fits the airline cabin limit. Check with the airline cabin check.

Bolognese vs Bichon, Maltese and Havanese

The Bolognese is closely related to the Bichon Frise, the Maltese and the Havanese. It's usually a little smaller and calmer than the Bichon. Next to a Miniature Pinscher, it's about the same size, with a completely different coat and temperament.

Temperament

Bolognese are gentle, calm and very attached to their people, often more reserved than the Bichon. They need short daily walks and play. They can be shy with strangers, and many don't like being left alone.

Coat and health

The fluffy white coat sheds very little but mats easily, so it needs brushing several times a week. Bolognese are generally healthy. Known problems include loose kneecaps, dental problems and eye conditions. They often live 12–14 years.

Sources: FCI standard No. 196 (Bolognese); AKC breed information; The Kennel Club breed standard.

Bolognese size: common questions

How big does a Bolognese get?

An adult Bolognese stands 26–31 cm (10–12 in) tall at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 2.7–4.5 kg (6–9.9 lb).

How tall is a Bolognese in feet?

An adult Bolognese stands 10 in to 1 ft tall at the shoulder (26–31 cm). Next to a 5′9″ adult, that reaches about mid-shin.

Is a Bolognese a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 3.6 kg (7.9 lb), the Bolognese counts as a toy dog breed on this site (toy = under 5 kg (11 lb) on average). It is taller than 13% of the 268 breeds listed.

How long does a Bolognese live?

Typical life expectancy is 12–14 years, shorter than the 14.1-year average for toy breeds.

Can a Bolognese fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually yes. At 2.7–4.5 kg (6–9.9 lb), it fits even the strictest airline cabin rule, where dog and carrier together may weigh 8 kg (17.6 lb). Check your airline's rules and carrier size limits before booking.
