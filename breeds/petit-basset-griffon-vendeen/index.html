How big is a Petit Basset Griffon Vendéen?

The Petit Basset Griffon Vendéen is a medium-sized dog breed from France, in the Hound group. Adults stand 33–38 cm (13–15 in) at the shoulder and weigh 12–18 kg (25–40 lb); the breed standard gives the same range for males and females. On average it is taller than 27% of the 268 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 39%.

Petit Basset Griffon Vendéen compared to a human

Next to a 175 cm (5′9″) adult, an average Petit Basset Griffon Vendéen stands 36 cm (14 in) at the shoulder: 20% of the person's height, reaching just below the knee. In feet, adults stand 1 ft 1 in to 1 ft 3 in tall at the shoulder. Next to a 120 cm (3′11″) child, about seven years old, the shoulder comes to about knee height. The head sits higher than the shoulder, so the dog looks taller than these numbers suggest.

Typical life expectancy is 14–16 years, longer than the 13.3-year average for medium breeds. It is too heavy for airlines that cap dog and carrier at 8 kg (17.6 lb), so it usually flies in the hold unless the airline only checks the carrier size.

About the Petit Basset Griffon Vendéen

The Petit Basset Griffon Vendéen, or PBGV, is a small French scent hound from the Vendée region on the Atlantic coast. Its name describes it: petit (small), basset (low), griffon (rough-coated) and Vendéen (from the Vendée). It was bred to hunt rabbits and hares through thick brambles and rough country, often in packs. A PBGV named Buddy Holly won Best in Show at Westminster in 2023.

What a PBGV's size means at home

The AKC standard calls for 13–15 in (33–38 cm) at the shoulder, and PBGVs weigh about 11.5–18 kg (25–40 lb). A PBGV reaches just below an adult's knee. It's longer than tall, sturdy and rough-coated. It usually needs a 30″ (76 cm) crate. Check with the crate size calculator. It's too heavy for an airline cabin.

PBGV vs Grand Basset Griffon Vendéen and Basset Fauve

The Grand Basset Griffon Vendéen (GBGV) is its bigger cousin. The Basset Fauve de Bretagne is about the same size, with a short, fawn coat. The PBGV is longer-legged and less heavy than the Basset Hound.

Temperament and exercise

PBGVs are cheerful, curious and friendly, good with children and other dogs. They're scent hounds, so their nose leads them, and recall can be unreliable. They're also vocal. They need daily walks long enough to sniff and explore, and a secure garden.

Coat and health

The rough, tousled coat needs weekly brushing and occasional hand-stripping. Known health problems include eye diseases (primary lens luxation, persistent pupillary membranes), hip dysplasia and back problems. PBGVs often live 14–16 years.

Sources: AKC breed standard and breed history; FCI standard No. 67 (Petit Basset Griffon Vendéen); Westminster Kennel Club.

Petit Basset Griffon Vendéen size: common questions

How big does a Petit Basset Griffon Vendéen get?

An adult Petit Basset Griffon Vendéen stands 33–38 cm (13–15 in) tall at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 12–18 kg (25–40 lb).

How tall is a Petit Basset Griffon Vendéen in feet?

An adult Petit Basset Griffon Vendéen stands 1 ft 1 in to 1 ft 3 in tall at the shoulder (33–38 cm). Next to a 5′9″ adult, that reaches just below the knee.

Is a Petit Basset Griffon Vendéen a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 15 kg (33 lb), the Petit Basset Griffon Vendéen counts as a medium dog breed on this site (medium = 10–25 kg (22–55 lb) on average). It is taller than 27% of the 268 breeds listed.

How long does a Petit Basset Griffon Vendéen live?

Typical life expectancy is 14–16 years, longer than the 13.3-year average for medium breeds.

Can a Petit Basset Griffon Vendéen fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Petit Basset Griffon Vendéen weighs 12–18 kg (25–40 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).
