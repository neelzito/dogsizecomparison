How big is a Podenco Andaluz (Small)?

The Podenco Andaluz (Small) is a small-sized dog breed from Spain, in the Hound group. Males stand 35–42 cm (13.8–16.5 in) at the shoulder and weigh 5–11 kg (11–24 lb). Females stand 32–41 cm (12.6–16.1 in) and weigh 5–11 kg (11–24 lb). On average it is taller than 31% of the 256 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 22%. At the shoulder, an average male Podenco Andaluz (Small) reaches just below the knee on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 12–14 years, shorter than the 13.7-year average for small breeds. Lighter individuals fit airline cabin limits of 8 kg (17.6 lb) for dog and carrier; heavier ones need an airline that weighs only the carrier size, or travel in the hold.

About the Podenco Andaluz (Small)

The small Podenco Andaluz (Podenco Andaluz chico) is the smallest of the three sizes of the Andalusian Hound. In southern Spain it hunted rabbits in thick brambles and scrub, where bigger dogs couldn't follow. It has the same large upright ears, keen senses and lively personality as its bigger relatives. For the breed's history and its three sizes and coats, see the medium Podenco Andaluz.

What a small Podenco's size means at home

The RSCE standard (No. 401) calls for 35–42 cm at the shoulder for males and 32–41 cm for females, and about 8 kg ± 3 kg. A small Podenco reaches just below an adult's knee. Because of its legs and big ears, the crate size calculator suggests a 36″ (91 cm) crate for an average dog. Lighter dogs may fit the airline cabin limit. Check with the airline cabin check.

Small Podenco vs Lundehund and Maneto

The Norwegian Lundehund is about the same size. The Maneto, another Andalusian hunter, is shorter-legged.

Living with a small Podenco

Small Podencos are affectionate, playful and lively, good with other dogs. They're agile jumpers and climbers with a strong prey drive, so they need a secure garden and careful recall training. Many are adopted from Spanish shelters.

Care and health

The coat can be smooth, wiry or long and is easy to care for. Small Podencos are generally hardy and often live 12–14 years.

Sources: RSCE breed standard No. 401 (Podenco Andaluz).

Podenco Andaluz (Small) size: common questions

How big does a Podenco Andaluz (Small) get?

A fully grown male Podenco Andaluz (Small) stands 35–42 cm (13.8–16.5 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 5–11 kg (11–24 lb). Females stand 32–41 cm (12.6–16.1 in) and weigh 5–11 kg (11–24 lb).

Is a Podenco Andaluz (Small) a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 8 kg (18 lb), the Podenco Andaluz (Small) counts as a small dog breed on this site (small = 5–10 kg (11–22 lb) on average). It is taller than 31% of the 256 breeds listed.

How long does a Podenco Andaluz (Small) live?

Typical life expectancy is 12–14 years, shorter than the 13.7-year average for small breeds.

Can a Podenco Andaluz (Small) fly in the airplane cabin?

Sometimes. Lighter individuals fit airline cabin limits of 8 kg (17.6 lb) for dog and carrier; heavier ones need an airline that weighs only the carrier size, or travel in the hold. Rules vary by airline, so check before booking.
