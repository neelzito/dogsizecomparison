How big is a Cirneco dell'Etna?

The Cirneco dell'Etna is a medium-sized dog breed from Italy (Sicily), in the Hound group. Males stand 46–51 cm (17.9–20.1 in) at the shoulder and weigh 9.1–13 kg (20–28 lb). Females stand 43–49 cm (16.9–19.1 in) and weigh 8.6–12 kg (19–25 lb). On average it is taller than 46% of the 268 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 30%.

Cirneco dell'Etna compared to a human

Next to a 175 cm (5′9″) adult, an average male Cirneco dell'Etna stands 48 cm (19 in) at the shoulder: 28% of the person's height, reaching about knee height. An average female, at 46 cm (18 in), reaches about knee height. In feet, males stand 1 ft 6 in to 1 ft 8 in tall at the shoulder and females 1 ft 5 in to 1 ft 7 in. Next to a 120 cm (3′11″) child, about seven years old, the shoulder comes to about mid-thigh. The head sits higher than the shoulder, so the dog looks taller than these numbers suggest.

Typical life expectancy is 15–16 years, longer than the 13.3-year average for medium breeds. It is too heavy for airlines that cap dog and carrier at 8 kg (17.6 lb), so it usually flies in the hold unless the airline only checks the carrier size.

About the Cirneco dell'Etna

The Cirneco dell'Etna (say "cheer-NEH-ko") is an ancient hunting dog from Sicily, named after Mount Etna, the volcano on whose slopes it has hunted rabbits for centuries. It works on hard, sharp lava terrain, using scent, sight and hearing together. Dogs like it appear on Sicilian coins from the 5th century BC. It's a smaller cousin of the Pharaoh Hound and Ibizan Hound, with the same large upright ears and lean build. The AKC recognised it in 2015.

What a Cirneco's size means at home

The AKC standard calls for 18–20 in (46–50 cm) at the shoulder for males and 16.5–18 in (42–46 cm) for females. Cirnechi weigh about 9–12.5 kg (20–28 lb) for males and 8.5–11.5 kg (18–25 lb) for females. A Cirneco reaches roughly an adult's knee. It's lean, elegant and light. The crate size calculator suggests a 36″ (91 cm) crate because of its leg length and big ears. It's too heavy for most airline cabins.

Cirneco vs Basenji and Whippet

The Basenji is a little shorter and about the same weight. The Whippet is a little taller and heavier. The Cirneco is always tan, sometimes with white markings.

Temperament and exercise

Cirnechi are affectionate, gentle and playful with their family, and reserved with strangers. They're independent hunters, so recall can be unreliable near rabbits or cats, and they need a secure garden. They need daily exercise and a chance to run. They're sensitive and respond best to gentle training.

Care and health

The short coat needs very little grooming. Cirnechi feel the cold, so a coat helps in winter. They're generally healthy and long-lived, often 15–16 years. Like other sighthound-type dogs, they can be sensitive to some anaesthetics.

Sources: AKC breed standard and breed history; FCI standard No. 199 (Cirneco dell'Etna); Cirneco dell'Etna Club of America.

Cirneco dell'Etna size: common questions

How big does a Cirneco dell'Etna get?

A fully grown male Cirneco dell'Etna stands 46–51 cm (17.9–20.1 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 9.1–13 kg (20–28 lb). Females stand 43–49 cm (16.9–19.1 in) and weigh 8.6–12 kg (19–25 lb).

How tall is a Cirneco dell'Etna in feet?

A male Cirneco dell'Etna stands 1 ft 6 in to 1 ft 8 in tall at the shoulder (46–51 cm) and a female 1 ft 5 in to 1 ft 7 in (43–49 cm). Next to a 5′9″ adult, an average male reaches about knee height.

Is a Cirneco dell'Etna a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 10 kg (23 lb), the Cirneco dell'Etna counts as a medium dog breed on this site (medium = 10–25 kg (22–55 lb) on average). It is taller than 46% of the 268 breeds listed.

How long does a Cirneco dell'Etna live?

Typical life expectancy is 15–16 years, longer than the 13.3-year average for medium breeds.

Can a Cirneco dell'Etna fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Cirneco dell'Etna weighs 8.6–13 kg (19–28 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).
