How big is a Belgian Tervuren?

The Belgian Tervuren is a large-sized dog breed from Belgium, in the Herding group. Males stand 61–66 cm (24–26 in) at the shoulder and weigh 25–34 kg (55–75 lb). Females stand 56–61 cm (22–24 in) and weigh 21–27 kg (45–60 lb). On average it is taller than 72% of the 267 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 68%. At the shoulder, an average male Belgian Tervuren reaches about mid-thigh on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 12–14 years, longer than the 12.1-year average for large breeds.

About the Belgian Tervuren

The Belgian Tervuren takes its name from the village of Tervuren near Brussels, where an early breeder, M. F. Corbeel, kept some of the foundation dogs in the late 19th century. It is one of four varieties of Belgian Shepherd, along with the Malinois, the Groenendael and the Laekenois, which differ mainly in coat. The Tervuren has a long, rich coat in fawn to mahogany with a black overlay and a black mask. Like the other Belgian Shepherds, it's an intelligent, sensitive and very capable working dog.

One breed or four?

The FCI treats the Belgian Shepherd as one breed with four coat varieties. The AKC and some other clubs treat the Tervuren as a separate breed. Either way, the Tervuren is the long-haired fawn variety, the Groenendael the long-haired black one, and the Malinois the short-haired one. All are about the same size.

What a Tervuren's size means at home

The AKC standard calls for 24–26 in (61–66 cm) at the shoulder for males and 22–24 in (56–61 cm) for females. Tervurens weigh about 25–34 kg (55–75 lb) for males and 20.5–27 kg (45–60 lb) for females. A Tervuren reaches roughly an adult's mid-thigh. It's elegant, square and athletic, and its coat makes it look bigger. It needs a big boot and usually a 48″ (122 cm) crate. Check with the crate size calculator.

Temperament and exercise

Tervurens are intelligent, alert and devoted to their family, often bonding closely with one person. They're watchful with strangers. They're slightly less intense than many Malinois, but they still need a lot of daily exercise and mental work. They excel at herding, obedience, agility and tracking. They're sensitive, so they respond best to gentle, consistent, reward-based training. Without enough to do, they can become anxious or destructive.

Coat and health

The long double coat sheds heavily, especially seasonally, and needs brushing a few times a week. Tervurens are generally healthy. Known problems include hip and elbow dysplasia, epilepsy, eye diseases and hypothyroidism. They often live 12–14 years.

Sources: AKC breed standard and breed history; FCI standard No. 15 (Chien de Berger Belge); American Belgian Tervuren Club.

Belgian Tervuren size: common questions

How big does a Belgian Tervuren get?

A fully grown male Belgian Tervuren stands 61–66 cm (24–26 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 25–34 kg (55–75 lb). Females stand 56–61 cm (22–24 in) and weigh 21–27 kg (45–60 lb).

Is a Belgian Tervuren a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 27 kg (59 lb), the Belgian Tervuren counts as a large dog breed on this site (large = 25–45 kg (55–99 lb) on average). It is taller than 72% of the 267 breeds listed.

How long does a Belgian Tervuren live?

Typical life expectancy is 12–14 years, longer than the 12.1-year average for large breeds.

Can a Belgian Tervuren fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Belgian Tervuren weighs 21–34 kg (45–75 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).

Sizes are breed-standard ranges for adult dogs; individual dogs, working lines and dogs from different countries can be bigger or smaller. Airline notes are a general guide only. Check with your airline and your vet. Disclaimer.
