How big is a Norfolk Terrier?

The Norfolk Terrier is a small-sized dog breed from England, in the Terrier group. Adults stand 23–26 cm (9.1–10 in) at the shoulder and weigh 5–5.4 kg (11–12 lb); the breed standard gives the same range for males and females. On average it is taller than 4% of the 256 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 10%. At the shoulder, an average Norfolk Terrier reaches about mid-shin on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 12–16 years, about average for small breeds. At 5–5.4 kg (11–12 lb), it fits even the strictest airline cabin rule, where dog and carrier together may weigh 8 kg (17.6 lb).

Sizes follow the American Kennel Club (AKC) breed standard.

About the Norfolk Terrier

The Norfolk Terrier is one of the smallest working terriers, from East Anglia in England. It was shown together with the Norwich Terrier until 1964, when The Kennel Club separated them. The AKC followed in 1979. The main difference is the ears: the Norfolk's fold forward, while the Norwich's stand upright. Bred to hunt rats and bolt foxes, the Norfolk is a fearless, cheerful little dog.

What a Norfolk's size means at home

The AKC standard calls for 9–10 in (23–25.5 cm) at the shoulder and about 11–12 lb (5–5.5 kg). A Norfolk reaches around mid-shin on an adult. It's compact, short-legged and sturdy. It suits any home and usually needs a 24″ (61 cm) crate. Check with the crate size calculator. It usually fits the airline cabin limit. Check with the airline cabin check.

Norfolk vs Norwich

The Norwich Terrier is the same size and build, with pricked ears. Both come in red, wheaten, black and tan, or grizzle.

Temperament and exercise

Norfolks are affectionate, bold and full of energy, and generally more sociable with other dogs than many terriers. They need daily walks and play. They love to dig and chase small animals, so a secure garden is essential, and recall can be unreliable.

Coat and health

The harsh, wiry coat sheds little and is traditionally hand-stripped a couple of times a year. Known health problems include heart conditions (mitral valve disease), hip dysplasia and loose kneecaps. Norfolks often live 12–16 years.

Sources: AKC breed standard and breed history; The Kennel Club breed standard; Norfolk Terrier Club.

Norfolk Terrier size: common questions

How big does a Norfolk Terrier get?

An adult Norfolk Terrier stands 23–26 cm (9.1–10 in) tall at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 5–5.4 kg (11–12 lb).

Is a Norfolk Terrier a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 5.2 kg (11 lb), the Norfolk Terrier counts as a small dog breed on this site (small = 5–10 kg (11–22 lb) on average). It is taller than 4% of the 256 breeds listed.

How long does a Norfolk Terrier live?

Typical life expectancy is 12–16 years, about average for small breeds.

Can a Norfolk Terrier fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually yes. At 5–5.4 kg (11–12 lb), it fits even the strictest airline cabin rule, where dog and carrier together may weigh 8 kg (17.6 lb). Check your airline's rules and carrier size limits before booking.
