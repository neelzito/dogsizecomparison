How big is a Pembroke Welsh Corgi?

The Pembroke Welsh Corgi is a medium-sized dog breed from Wales, in the Herding group. Males stand 26–31 cm (10–12 in) at the shoulder and weigh 11–14 kg (24–30 lb). Females stand 26–31 cm (10–12 in) and weigh 10–13 kg (22–28 lb). On average it is taller than 14% of the 267 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 34%.

Pembroke Welsh Corgi compared to a human

Next to a 175 cm (5′9″) adult, an average male Pembroke Welsh Corgi stands 28 cm (11 in) at the shoulder: 16% of the person's height, reaching about mid-shin. An average female, at 28 cm (11 in), reaches about mid-shin. In feet, males stand 10 in to 1 ft tall at the shoulder and females 10 in to 1 ft. Next to a 120 cm (3′11″) child, about seven years old, the shoulder comes to just below the knee. The head sits higher than the shoulder, so the dog looks taller than these numbers suggest.

Typical life expectancy is 12–13 years, shorter than the 13.2-year average for medium breeds. It is too heavy for airlines that cap dog and carrier at 8 kg (17.6 lb), so it usually flies in the hold unless the airline only checks the carrier size.

About the Pembroke Welsh Corgi

The Pembroke Welsh Corgi is a small cattle dog from Pembrokeshire in south-west Wales. Farmers used corgis to drive cattle by nipping at their heels. The dog's low height let it dodge kicks, which rushed over its head. In the 20th century the Pembroke became famous as the favourite breed of Queen Elizabeth II, who owned more than 30 corgis during her life. Today it is a lively, clever and bold companion, a big dog in a small body.

What a corgi's size means at home

A Pembroke stands only about 25–30 cm at the shoulder, around mid-shin on an adult, but it is long and solid: males weigh up to about 14 kg (30 lb). It is small enough for a flat or a small car, and it usually needs a 30″ (76 cm) crate because of its long body. See the crate size calculator. Even small adults weigh well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier.

Pembroke vs Cardigan

Wales has two corgi breeds. The Cardigan Welsh Corgi is older, a little bigger and heavier, with rounder ears and a long, fox-like tail, and it comes in more colours, including blue merle. The Pembroke is more compact, has more pointed ears, and is traditionally born with a short or docked tail. Docking is banned in many countries, so you'll often see Pembrokes with a full, natural tail.

Short legs, long back

The corgi's short legs come from a form of dwarfism (chondrodysplasia), the same trait seen in Dachshunds and Basset Hounds. As in those breeds, the long back can be prone to disc problems. You can help by keeping your corgi lean, using ramps instead of letting it jump off high furniture, and carrying it on stairs when it's young. Corgis love food, and extra weight puts strain on the spine and joints.

Living with a corgi

Corgis are true herders: energetic, quick to learn and inclined to nip at heels, including children's. They need daily walks, games and training to keep them happy. They shed heavily, especially in spring and autumn, so expect regular brushing. Older corgis can develop degenerative myelopathy, a spinal cord disease, and a DNA test lets breeders avoid producing affected puppies.

Pembroke Welsh Corgi size: common questions

How big does a Pembroke Welsh Corgi get?

A fully grown male Pembroke Welsh Corgi stands 26–31 cm (10–12 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 11–14 kg (24–30 lb). Females stand 26–31 cm (10–12 in) and weigh 10–13 kg (22–28 lb).

How tall is a Pembroke Welsh Corgi in feet?

A male Pembroke Welsh Corgi stands 10 in to 1 ft tall at the shoulder (26–31 cm) and a female 10 in to 1 ft (26–31 cm). Next to a 5′9″ adult, an average male reaches about mid-shin.

Is a Pembroke Welsh Corgi a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 12 kg (26 lb), the Pembroke Welsh Corgi counts as a medium dog breed on this site (medium = 10–25 kg (22–55 lb) on average). It is taller than 14% of the 267 breeds listed.

How long does a Pembroke Welsh Corgi live?

Typical life expectancy is 12–13 years, shorter than the 13.2-year average for medium breeds.

Can a Pembroke Welsh Corgi fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Pembroke Welsh Corgi weighs 10–14 kg (22–30 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).
