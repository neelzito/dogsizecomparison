How big is an English Toy Spaniel?

The English Toy Spaniel is a small-sized dog breed from England, in the Toy group. Adults stand 23–26 cm (9.1–10 in) at the shoulder and weigh 3.6–6.4 kg (7.9–14 lb); the breed standard gives the same range for males and females. On average it is taller than 4% of the 268 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 9%.

English Toy Spaniel compared to a human

Next to a 175 cm (5′9″) adult, an average English Toy Spaniel stands 24 cm (9.5 in) at the shoulder: 14% of the person's height, reaching about mid-shin. In feet, adults stand 9 in to 10 in tall at the shoulder. Next to a 120 cm (3′11″) child, about seven years old, the shoulder comes to just below the knee. The head sits higher than the shoulder, so the dog looks taller than these numbers suggest.

Typical life expectancy is 10–12 years, shorter than the 13.7-year average for small breeds. At 3.6–6.4 kg (7.9–14 lb), it fits even the strictest airline cabin rule, where dog and carrier together may weigh 8 kg (17.6 lb).

About the English Toy Spaniel

The English Toy Spaniel is called the King Charles Spaniel in the UK. It descends from the small toy spaniels loved by English royalty, especially King Charles II in the 17th century. Over time, breeders gave it a flatter face and a domed head. In the 1920s, breeders who wanted to bring back the older, longer-nosed type created the separate Cavalier King Charles Spaniel. The English Toy Spaniel is a gentle, affectionate and calm companion.

What an English Toy Spaniel's size means at home

The AKC standard calls for about 8–14 lb (3.5–6.5 kg). English Toy Spaniels stand about 23–25.5 cm at the shoulder, around mid-shin on an adult. They're compact and cobby. They suit any home and usually need an 18″ (46 cm) crate. Check with the crate size calculator. They fit the airline cabin weight limit. Check with the airline cabin check.

English Toy vs Cavalier King Charles Spaniel

The Cavalier King Charles Spaniel is taller and heavier, with a longer muzzle and a flat head. The Coton de Tuléar and the Silky Terrier are about the same size.

Temperament

English Toy Spaniels are gentle, quiet and loving, devoted to their family and often reserved with strangers. They need short daily walks and play. They're sensitive and suit calm homes.

Coat and health

The long, silky coat needs brushing a couple of times a week. As a flat-faced breed, it's sensitive to heat and can have breathing problems, and many airlines won't carry it in the hold. Other known problems include heart disease (mitral valve disease), eye problems and loose kneecaps. English Toy Spaniels often live 10–12 years.

English Toy Spaniel size: common questions

How big does an English Toy Spaniel get?

An adult English Toy Spaniel stands 23–26 cm (9.1–10 in) tall at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 3.6–6.4 kg (7.9–14 lb).

How tall is an English Toy Spaniel in feet?

An adult English Toy Spaniel stands 9 in to 10 in tall at the shoulder (23–26 cm). Next to a 5′9″ adult, that reaches about mid-shin.

Is an English Toy Spaniel a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 5 kg (11 lb), the English Toy Spaniel counts as a small dog breed on this site (small = 5–10 kg (11–22 lb) on average). It is taller than 4% of the 268 breeds listed.

How long does an English Toy Spaniel live?

Typical life expectancy is 10–12 years, shorter than the 13.7-year average for small breeds.

Can an English Toy Spaniel fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually yes. At 3.6–6.4 kg (7.9–14 lb), it fits even the strictest airline cabin rule, where dog and carrier together may weigh 8 kg (17.6 lb). Check your airline's rules and carrier size limits before booking.
