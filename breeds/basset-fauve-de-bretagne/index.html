How big is a Basset Fauve de Bretagne?

The Basset Fauve de Bretagne is a medium-sized dog breed from France, in the Hound group. Adults stand 32–40 cm (12.6–15.6 in) at the shoulder and weigh 11–18 kg (23–39 lb); the breed standard gives the same range for males and females. On average it is taller than 29% of the 256 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 37%. At the shoulder, an average Basset Fauve de Bretagne reaches just below the knee on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 13–16 years, longer than the 13.3-year average for medium breeds. It is too heavy for airlines that cap dog and carrier at 8 kg (17.6 lb), so it usually flies in the hold unless the airline only checks the carrier size.

Sizes follow the American Kennel Club (AKC) breed standard.

About the Basset Fauve de Bretagne

The Basset Fauve de Bretagne is a small French scent hound from Brittany, descended from the larger, now-extinct Grand Fauve de Bretagne. Fauve means "fawn", after its wiry red-gold coat. Hunters in Brittany used it in packs to hunt rabbits and hares through dense undergrowth. It's lively, compact and less long-bodied than the Basset Hound.

What a Basset Fauve's size means at home

The breed standard calls for about 32–38 cm at the shoulder, and Basset Fauves weigh about 10.5–17.5 kg (23–38 lb). One reaches just below an adult's knee. Because of its long body, the crate size calculator suggests a 42″ (107 cm) crate for an average dog. It's too heavy for an airline cabin.

Basset Fauve vs Beagle and PBGV

A Beagle is about the same height and lighter. The Petit Basset Griffon Vendéen is about the same size, with a longer, shaggier coat.

Temperament and exercise

Basset Fauves are friendly, cheerful and energetic, good with children and other dogs. They need plenty of exercise, and their nose leads them, so recall is often unreliable.

Coat and health

The harsh, wiry coat needs weekly brushing and occasional hand-stripping. Basset Fauves are generally healthy and often live 13–16 years.

Sources: FCI standard No. 36 (Basset Fauve de Bretagne); AKC breed information; The Kennel Club breed standard.

Basset Fauve de Bretagne size: common questions

How big does a Basset Fauve de Bretagne get?

An adult Basset Fauve de Bretagne stands 32–40 cm (12.6–15.6 in) tall at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 11–18 kg (23–39 lb).

Is a Basset Fauve de Bretagne a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 14 kg (31 lb), the Basset Fauve de Bretagne counts as a medium dog breed on this site (medium = 10–25 kg (22–55 lb) on average). It is taller than 29% of the 256 breeds listed.

How long does a Basset Fauve de Bretagne live?

Typical life expectancy is 13–16 years, longer than the 13.3-year average for medium breeds.

Can a Basset Fauve de Bretagne fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Basset Fauve de Bretagne weighs 11–18 kg (23–39 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).
