How big is a Cavachon?

The Cavachon is a small-sized dog breed from the United States, in the designer & mixed category. Males stand 30–33 cm (11.8–13 in) at the shoulder and weigh 6–9 kg (13–20 lb). Females stand 28–33 cm (11–13 in) and weigh 5.5–8 kg (12–18 lb). On average it is taller than 19% of the 256 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 18%. At the shoulder, an average male Cavachon reaches about mid-shin on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 12–15 years, about average for small breeds. Lighter individuals fit airline cabin limits of 8 kg (17.6 lb) for dog and carrier; heavier ones need an airline that weighs only the carrier size, or travel in the hold.

About the Cavachon

The Cavachon is a cross between a Cavalier King Charles Spaniel and a Bichon Frise. It was first bred deliberately in the US in the 1990s and has become a popular small family dog in North America and the UK. It combines the Cavalier's gentle, affectionate nature with the Bichon's cheerfulness and often low-shedding coat. As a crossbreed, the Cavachon isn't recognised by the major kennel clubs and has no official standard.

How big does a Cavachon get?

Most Cavachons stand about 28–33 cm at the shoulder, around mid-shin on an adult, and weigh about 5.5–9 kg (12–20 lb). Because both parent breeds are small and fairly close in size, Cavachons vary less than doodle crosses, but puppies can still differ. Ask about both parents' size, and use the puppy weight calculator as a guide.

Next to a Cavapoo, a Cavachon is usually a little taller on average and similar in weight. A Cockapoo and a Miniature Schnoodle are similar in size.

What a Cavachon's size means at home

A Cavachon suits flats, small houses and small cars. It usually needs a 24″ (61 cm) crate. Check with the crate size calculator. It enjoys daily walks and play but doesn't need a lot of exercise, and it loves being close to its people.

Can a Cavachon fly in the cabin?

Sometimes. A lighter Cavachon in a light carrier may stay within the 8 kg (17.6 lb) limit many airlines set for dog and carrier. Heavier ones won't. Check with the airline cabin check.

Coat care

Cavachon coats are soft and usually wavy or curly, often white, cream, apricot or with Blenheim (chestnut and white) markings. They shed little but mat easily, so brush several times a week and book a trim every 6–8 weeks. Keep the ears clean, and wipe tear stains around the eyes. No dog is truly hypoallergenic.

Temperament and health

Cavachons are friendly, gentle and sociable, usually good with children, other dogs and cats. They're eager to please and fairly easy to train, but many struggle when left alone. They can inherit health problems from both parents, especially the Cavalier's mitral valve disease (a heart condition), along with eye problems, loose kneecaps and allergies. Choose a breeder who heart-tests the Cavalier parent. Cavachons often live 12–15 years.

Sources: AKC breed standards for the Cavalier King Charles Spaniel and the Bichon Frise.

Cavachon size: common questions

How big does a Cavachon get?

A fully grown male Cavachon stands 30–33 cm (11.8–13 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 6–9 kg (13–20 lb). Females stand 28–33 cm (11–13 in) and weigh 5.5–8 kg (12–18 lb).

Is a Cavachon a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 7.1 kg (16 lb), the Cavachon counts as a small dog breed on this site (small = 5–10 kg (11–22 lb) on average). It is taller than 19% of the 256 breeds listed.

How long does a Cavachon live?

Typical life expectancy is 12–15 years, about average for small breeds.

Can a Cavachon fly in the airplane cabin?

Sometimes. Lighter individuals fit airline cabin limits of 8 kg (17.6 lb) for dog and carrier; heavier ones need an airline that weighs only the carrier size, or travel in the hold. Rules vary by airline, so check before booking.
