How big is an American Hairless Terrier?

The American Hairless Terrier is a small-sized dog breed from the United States, in the Terrier group. Adults stand 31–41 cm (12–15.9 in) at the shoulder and weigh 5.4–13 kg (12–28 lb); the breed standard gives the same range for males and females. On average it is taller than 27% of the 268 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 25%.

American Hairless Terrier compared to a human

Next to a 175 cm (5′9″) adult, an average American Hairless Terrier stands 36 cm (14 in) at the shoulder: 20% of the person's height, reaching just below the knee. In feet, adults stand 1 ft to 1 ft 4 in tall at the shoulder. Next to a 120 cm (3′11″) child, about seven years old, the shoulder comes to about knee height. The head sits higher than the shoulder, so the dog looks taller than these numbers suggest.

Typical life expectancy is 14–16 years, longer than the 13.7-year average for small breeds. Lighter individuals fit airline cabin limits of 8 kg (17.6 lb) for dog and carrier; heavier ones need an airline that weighs only the carrier size, or travel in the hold.

American Hairless Terrier size: common questions

How big does an American Hairless Terrier get?

An adult American Hairless Terrier stands 31–41 cm (12–15.9 in) tall at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 5.4–13 kg (12–28 lb).

How tall is an American Hairless Terrier in feet?

An adult American Hairless Terrier stands 1 ft to 1 ft 4 in tall at the shoulder (31–41 cm). Next to a 5′9″ adult, that reaches just below the knee.

Is an American Hairless Terrier a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 9.1 kg (20 lb), the American Hairless Terrier counts as a small dog breed on this site (small = 5–10 kg (11–22 lb) on average). It is taller than 27% of the 268 breeds listed.

How long does an American Hairless Terrier live?

Typical life expectancy is 14–16 years, longer than the 13.7-year average for small breeds.

Can an American Hairless Terrier fly in the airplane cabin?

Sometimes. Lighter individuals fit airline cabin limits of 8 kg (17.6 lb) for dog and carrier; heavier ones need an airline that weighs only the carrier size, or travel in the hold. Rules vary by airline, so check before booking.
