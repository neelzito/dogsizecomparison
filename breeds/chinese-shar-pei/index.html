How big is a Chinese Shar-Pei?

The Chinese Shar-Pei is a medium-sized dog breed from China, in the Non-Sporting group. Adults stand 46–51 cm (17.9–20.1 in) at the shoulder and weigh 21–27 kg (45–60 lb); the breed standard gives the same range for males and females. On average it is taller than 50% of the 256 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 58%. At the shoulder, an average Chinese Shar-Pei reaches about knee height on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 8–12 years, shorter than the 13.3-year average for medium breeds. It is too heavy for airlines that cap dog and carrier at 8 kg (17.6 lb), so it usually flies in the hold unless the airline only checks the carrier size.

Sizes follow the American Kennel Club (AKC) breed standard.

Chinese Shar-Pei size: common questions

How big does a Chinese Shar-Pei get?

An adult Chinese Shar-Pei stands 46–51 cm (17.9–20.1 in) tall at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 21–27 kg (45–60 lb).

Is a Chinese Shar-Pei a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 24 kg (52 lb), the Chinese Shar-Pei counts as a medium dog breed on this site (medium = 10–25 kg (22–55 lb) on average). It is taller than 50% of the 256 breeds listed.

How long does a Chinese Shar-Pei live?

Typical life expectancy is 8–12 years, shorter than the 13.3-year average for medium breeds.

Can a Chinese Shar-Pei fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Chinese Shar-Pei weighs 21–27 kg (45–60 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).

Sizes are breed-standard ranges for adult dogs; individual dogs, working lines and dogs from different countries can be bigger or smaller. Airline notes are a general guide only. Check with your airline and your vet. Disclaimer.
