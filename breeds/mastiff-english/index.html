How big is a Mastiff (English)?

The Mastiff (English) is a giant-sized dog breed from England, in the Working group. Males stand 76–91 cm (29.9–35.8 in) at the shoulder and weigh 73–105 kg (160–230 lb). Females stand 70–84 cm (27.6–33.1 in) and weigh 55–77 kg (120–170 lb). On average that makes it the 2nd tallest of the 256 breeds on this site. At the shoulder, an average male Mastiff (English) reaches about hip height on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 6–10 years, shorter than the 10.1-year average for giant breeds.

About the Mastiff (English)

The Mastiff (often called the English Mastiff, or the Old English Mastiff) is one of the largest and heaviest dog breeds in the world. Massive mastiff-type dogs were kept in Britain for over two thousand years as guard dogs, war dogs and, sadly, for blood sports. By the end of the Second World War, the breed had almost died out in Britain and was rebuilt with dogs from North America. A Mastiff called Zorba held the Guinness record for the heaviest dog in 1989, at about 155 kg (343 lb). Today's Mastiff is a calm, dignified and gentle family guardian.

What a Mastiff's size means at home

The AKC standard sets minimum heights of 30 in (76 cm) for males and 27.5 in (70 cm) for females and gives no maximum. Most males stand about 76–91 cm at the shoulder, roughly an adult's hip, and weigh about 73–105 kg (160–230 lb). Females weigh about 55–77 kg (120–170 lb). That's as heavy as a grown man. An average male is too big for standard crates, as the crate size calculator shows. It needs a van or very large car, a ramp, and a home with plenty of space. Food, vet and medicine bills are giant-sized too.

Mastiff vs Great Dane, Saint Bernard and Cane Corso

The Great Dane is a little shorter on average and much lighter and leaner. The Saint Bernard is shorter and lighter. The Cane Corso is much smaller and more athletic. The Spanish Mastiff is similar in size, with a longer coat.

Temperament

Mastiffs are calm, good-natured and very affectionate with their families. They're naturally protective but not usually aggressive, and they tend to deter intruders by size alone. They're happy with moderate exercise and spend much of the day asleep. Early training and socialisation are essential, because an untrained 80 kg dog that pulls or jumps up is hard to manage. Expect drool and loud snoring.

Growth and health

Mastiff puppies grow very fast and need a giant-breed puppy food, a lean body and protection from hard exercise and stairs until about 18–24 months. Known health problems include hip and elbow dysplasia, bloat, heart disease, eyelid problems and bone cancer. Like many giant breeds, Mastiffs have a short life, usually 6–10 years.

Sources: AKC breed standard and breed history; FCI standard No. 264 (Mastiff); The Kennel Club breed standard; Guinness World Records.

Mastiff (English) size: common questions

How big does a Mastiff (English) get?

A fully grown male Mastiff (English) stands 76–91 cm (29.9–35.8 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 73–105 kg (160–230 lb). Females stand 70–84 cm (27.6–33.1 in) and weigh 55–77 kg (120–170 lb).

Is a Mastiff (English) a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 77 kg (170 lb), the Mastiff (English) counts as a giant dog breed on this site (giant = 45 kg (99 lb) and over on average). It is the 2nd tallest of the 256 breeds listed.

How long does a Mastiff (English) live?

Typical life expectancy is 6–10 years, shorter than the 10.1-year average for giant breeds.

Can a Mastiff (English) fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Mastiff (English) weighs 55–105 kg (120–230 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).

Sizes are breed-standard ranges for adult dogs; individual dogs, working lines and dogs from different countries can be bigger or smaller. Airline notes are a general guide only. Check with your airline and your vet. Disclaimer.
