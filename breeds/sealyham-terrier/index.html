How big is a Sealyham Terrier?

The Sealyham Terrier is a small-sized dog breed from Wales, in the Terrier group. Males stand 27 cm (10.4 in) at the shoulder and weigh 11 kg (23–24 lb). Females stand 27 cm (10.4 in) and weigh 8–10 kg (18–22 lb). On average it is taller than 12% of the 267 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 28%. At the shoulder, an average male Sealyham Terrier reaches about mid-shin on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 12–14 years, shorter than the 13.7-year average for small breeds. It is too heavy for airlines that cap dog and carrier at 8 kg (17.6 lb), so it usually flies in the hold unless the airline only checks the carrier size.

About the Sealyham Terrier

The Sealyham Terrier was developed in the mid-19th century by Captain John Edwardes at Sealyham House in Pembrokeshire, Wales. He wanted a white, short-legged, fearless terrier to hunt otters, badgers and foxes. The breed was very fashionable in the early 20th century, with owners including Alfred Hitchcock, who walked his two Sealyhams in his cameo in The Birds (1963). Today it's rare, and The Kennel Club lists it as a vulnerable native breed.

What a Sealyham's size means at home

The AKC standard calls for about 10.5 in (26.5 cm) at the shoulder and about 23–24 lb (10.5–11 kg) for males, with females slightly lighter. A Sealyham reaches around mid-shin on an adult. It's low, sturdy and heavy for its size. It usually needs a 24″ (61 cm) crate. Check with the crate size calculator. It's too heavy for the airline cabin.

Sealyham vs Scottish Terrier and Mini Bull Terrier

The Scottish Terrier is about the same height and a little lighter, usually black. The Miniature Bull Terrier is a little taller and about the same weight.

Temperament and exercise

Sealyhams are calmer than many terriers but still brave, independent and alert. They're devoted to their family and good watchdogs. They need daily walks and play, and they can be stubborn.

Coat and health

The wiry white coat needs regular brushing and hand-stripping or clipping. Known health problems include eye diseases (lens luxation, retinal dysplasia), deafness and back problems. Sealyhams often live 12–14 years.

Sealyham Terrier size: common questions

How big does a Sealyham Terrier get?

A fully grown male Sealyham Terrier stands 27 cm (10.4 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 11 kg (23–24 lb). Females stand 27 cm (10.4 in) and weigh 8–10 kg (18–22 lb).

Is a Sealyham Terrier a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 9.9 kg (22 lb), the Sealyham Terrier counts as a small dog breed on this site (small = 5–10 kg (11–22 lb) on average). It is taller than 12% of the 267 breeds listed.

How long does a Sealyham Terrier live?

Typical life expectancy is 12–14 years, shorter than the 13.7-year average for small breeds.

Can a Sealyham Terrier fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Sealyham Terrier weighs 8–11 kg (18–24 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).
