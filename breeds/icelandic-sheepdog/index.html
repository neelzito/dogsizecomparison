How big is an Icelandic Sheepdog?

The Icelandic Sheepdog is a medium-sized dog breed from Iceland, in the Herding group. Males stand 46 cm (17.9 in) at the shoulder and weigh 14 kg (30 lb). Females stand 42 cm (16.5 in) and weigh 12 kg (25 lb). On average it is taller than 40% of the 256 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 34%. At the shoulder, an average male Icelandic Sheepdog reaches about knee height on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 12–14 years, about average for medium breeds. It is too heavy for airlines that cap dog and carrier at 8 kg (17.6 lb), so it usually flies in the hold unless the airline only checks the carrier size.

About the Icelandic Sheepdog

The Icelandic Sheepdog is Iceland's only native dog breed. Its ancestors came to Iceland with the Vikings in the 9th and 10th centuries, and for more than a thousand years it herded sheep and horses and guarded farms. Disease and imports nearly wiped it out in the 20th century, but breeders in Iceland and England saved it. It's a cheerful, friendly spitz with a curled tail and a fox-like face.

What an Icelandic Sheepdog's size means at home

The AKC standard calls for about 18 in (45.5 cm) at the shoulder for males and 16.5 in (42 cm) for females, with typical weights around 13.5 kg (30 lb) for males and 11.5 kg (25 lb) for females. An Icelandic Sheepdog reaches roughly an adult's knee. It's a little longer than tall. It usually needs a 36″ (91 cm) crate. Check with the crate size calculator. It's too heavy for an airline cabin.

Icelandic Sheepdog vs Pumi and Buhund

The Hungarian Pumi is a little smaller and lighter, with a curly coat. The Norwegian Buhund, a close Nordic relative, is similar in size.

Temperament and exercise

Icelandic Sheepdogs are friendly, playful and curious, usually good with children, other dogs and visitors. They're vocal herders and alert watchdogs. They need daily exercise and like to be part of family life.

Coat and health

The thick double coat comes in short and long versions and sheds heavily once or twice a year. Icelandic Sheepdogs often have double dewclaws on their hind legs. Known health problems include hip dysplasia and eye conditions. They often live 12–14 years.

Sources: AKC breed standard and breed history; FCI standard No. 289 (Icelandic Sheepdog); Icelandic Sheepdog Association of America.

Icelandic Sheepdog size: common questions

How big does an Icelandic Sheepdog get?

A fully grown male Icelandic Sheepdog stands 46 cm (17.9 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 14 kg (30 lb). Females stand 42 cm (16.5 in) and weigh 12 kg (25 lb).

Is an Icelandic Sheepdog a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 13 kg (28 lb), the Icelandic Sheepdog counts as a medium dog breed on this site (medium = 10–25 kg (22–55 lb) on average). It is taller than 40% of the 256 breeds listed.

How long does an Icelandic Sheepdog live?

Typical life expectancy is 12–14 years, about average for medium breeds.

Can an Icelandic Sheepdog fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Icelandic Sheepdog weighs 12–14 kg (25–30 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).

Sizes are breed-standard ranges for adult dogs; individual dogs, working lines and dogs from different countries can be bigger or smaller. Airline notes are a general guide only. Check with your airline and your vet. Disclaimer.
