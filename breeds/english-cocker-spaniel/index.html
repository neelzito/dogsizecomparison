How big is an English Cocker Spaniel?

The English Cocker Spaniel is a medium-sized dog breed from England, in the Sporting group. Males stand 41–43 cm (15.9–16.9 in) at the shoulder and weigh 13–16 kg (28–34 lb). Females stand 38–41 cm (15–15.9 in) and weigh 12–15 kg (26–32 lb). On average it is taller than 36% of the 268 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 37%.

English Cocker Spaniel compared to a human

Next to a 175 cm (5′9″) adult, an average male English Cocker Spaniel stands 42 cm (16.4 in) at the shoulder: 24% of the person's height, reaching just below the knee. An average female, at 39 cm (15.5 in), reaches just below the knee. In feet, males stand 1 ft 4 in to 1 ft 5 in tall at the shoulder and females 1 ft 3 in to 1 ft 4 in. Next to a 120 cm (3′11″) child, about seven years old, the shoulder comes to about mid-thigh. The head sits higher than the shoulder, so the dog looks taller than these numbers suggest.

Typical life expectancy is 12–14 years, about average for medium breeds. It is too heavy for airlines that cap dog and carrier at 8 kg (17.6 lb), so it usually flies in the hold unless the airline only checks the carrier size.

About the English Cocker Spaniel

The English Cocker Spaniel is a British gundog bred to flush and retrieve game birds, especially woodcock, which gave the "cocker" its name. In the 19th century, land spaniels were sorted by size: the smaller ones were used on woodcock and became Cockers, and the larger ones became Springers. The Cocker was recognised as a separate breed in 1893. It is one of the most popular breeds in the UK, loved for its merry, wagging nature, and is still widely used as a working gundog.

What a Cocker's size means at home

An English Cocker stands about 38–43 cm at the shoulder, just below an adult's knee, and weighs about 12–15.5 kg (26–34 lb). Males are a little bigger than females. It is a compact, sturdy dog that suits most homes, but it has lots of energy. It needs daily walks and play, ideally off the lead somewhere safe where it can use its nose. The crate size calculator suggests a 36″ (91 cm) crate for an average adult. Cockers are too heavy for an airline cabin.

Working Cockers vs show Cockers

The two types are almost different dogs. Show Cockers have a longer, thicker coat, long ears, a more domed head and a calmer nature. Working Cockers are smaller and lighter, with less coat and shorter ears, and they have huge energy and drive. They're superb in the field and at dog sports but need much more exercise and mental work. Before you get a puppy, decide which type suits your life.

English vs American Cocker and Springer

The American Cocker Spaniel is a separate breed. It is smaller, with a shorter muzzle, a rounder head and a much fuller coat. The English Springer Spaniel is the Cocker's bigger, leggier cousin. Cocker Spaniels are also one parent of the popular Cockapoo.

Coat and ears

The silky, feathered coat needs brushing a few times a week and regular trimming, especially around the feet and ears. Those long, hanging ears trap warmth and moisture, so check and clean them weekly to prevent ear infections.

Health

Health issues include eye diseases such as progressive retinal atrophy (PRA), hip dysplasia and familial nephropathy, a kidney disease. DNA tests exist for several of these, so ask breeders for the parents' results. Some solid-coloured Cockers are prone to "rage syndrome" (sudden aggression), which is rare but well documented. English Cockers often live 12–14 years.

English Cocker Spaniel size: common questions

How big does an English Cocker Spaniel get?

A fully grown male English Cocker Spaniel stands 41–43 cm (15.9–16.9 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 13–16 kg (28–34 lb). Females stand 38–41 cm (15–15.9 in) and weigh 12–15 kg (26–32 lb).

How tall is an English Cocker Spaniel in feet?

A male English Cocker Spaniel stands 1 ft 4 in to 1 ft 5 in tall at the shoulder (41–43 cm) and a female 1 ft 3 in to 1 ft 4 in (38–41 cm). Next to a 5′9″ adult, an average male reaches just below the knee.

Is an English Cocker Spaniel a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 14 kg (30 lb), the English Cocker Spaniel counts as a medium dog breed on this site (medium = 10–25 kg (22–55 lb) on average). It is taller than 36% of the 268 breeds listed.

How long does an English Cocker Spaniel live?

Typical life expectancy is 12–14 years, about average for medium breeds.

Can an English Cocker Spaniel fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A English Cocker Spaniel weighs 12–16 kg (26–34 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).
