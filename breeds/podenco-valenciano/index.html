How big is a Podenco Valenciano?

The Podenco Valenciano is a medium-sized dog breed from Spain, in the Hound group. Males stand 55–61 cm (21.7–24 in) at the shoulder and weigh 16–24 kg (35–53 lb). Females stand 50–57 cm (19.7–22.4 in) and weigh 14–22 kg (31–49 lb). On average it is taller than 61% of the 267 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 47%. At the shoulder, an average male Podenco Valenciano reaches about mid-thigh on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 12–14 years, about average for medium breeds. It is too heavy for airlines that cap dog and carrier at 8 kg (17.6 lb), so it usually flies in the hold unless the airline only checks the carrier size.

About the Podenco Valenciano

The Podenco Valenciano, known locally as the Xarnego Valenciano, is a hunting dog from the Valencia region of eastern Spain. For generations, hunters there used it to hunt rabbits, often in packs, in the dry hills and scrub. Like the other Spanish Podencos, it uses scent, sight and hearing together, and it has the classic lean build and large upright ears of the Mediterranean warren hounds. The Spanish kennel club (RSCE) recognised it as an ethnic group in 2013. It is not recognised by the FCI.

What a Podenco Valenciano's size means at home

The RSCE standard gives heights of 55–61 cm at the shoulder for males and 50–57 cm for females, and weights of about 20 kg for males and 18 kg for females, varying by up to 4 kg with the hunting season. A Podenco Valenciano reaches roughly an adult's mid-thigh. It's lean and athletic, light for its height, so its weight puts it in the medium size class on this site. Because of its height and big ears, the crate size calculator suggests a 48″ (122 cm) crate for an average male.

Valenciano vs Podenco Canario and Podenco Andaluz

The Podenco Canario is about the same height and a little heavier. The Podenco Andaluz (Large) is a little taller and heavier. The Valenciano's coat is usually red or fawn, often with white, and can be smooth or wiry.

Living with a Podenco Valenciano

Podencos are affectionate, gentle and sensitive with their families, and they usually get on well with other dogs because they're bred to hunt in packs. Their hunting instinct is very strong, so recall is often unreliable wherever there are rabbits, cats or birds. A long line and a securely fenced area with high fences are essential. They need daily exercise and a chance to run, but are usually calm at home.

Adoption

Many Podencos, including Valencianos and Podenco mixes, end up in Spanish shelters, often after the hunting season. Rescue organisations rehome them across Europe. Ask the rescue about each dog's size, temperament and behaviour with cats.

Care and health

The coat needs little grooming. Podencos feel the cold, so a coat helps in winter. Little breed-specific health data exists. Podencos are generally hardy and often live 12–14 years.

Podenco Valenciano size: common questions

How big does a Podenco Valenciano get?

A fully grown male Podenco Valenciano stands 55–61 cm (21.7–24 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 16–24 kg (35–53 lb). Females stand 50–57 cm (19.7–22.4 in) and weigh 14–22 kg (31–49 lb).

Is a Podenco Valenciano a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 19 kg (42 lb), the Podenco Valenciano counts as a medium dog breed on this site (medium = 10–25 kg (22–55 lb) on average). It is taller than 61% of the 267 breeds listed.

How long does a Podenco Valenciano live?

Typical life expectancy is 12–14 years, about average for medium breeds.

Can a Podenco Valenciano fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Podenco Valenciano weighs 14–24 kg (31–53 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).
