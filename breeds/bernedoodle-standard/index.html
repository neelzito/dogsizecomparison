How big is a Bernedoodle (Standard)?

The Bernedoodle (Standard) is a large-sized dog breed from the United States/Canada, in the designer & mixed category. Males stand 58–74 cm (22.8–29.1 in) at the shoulder and weigh 32–41 kg (71–90 lb). Females stand 58–69 cm (22.8–27.2 in) and weigh 27–36 kg (60–79 lb). On average it is taller than 84% of the 268 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 83%.

Bernedoodle (Standard) compared to a human

Next to a 175 cm (5′9″) adult, an average male Bernedoodle (Standard) stands 66 cm (26 in) at the shoulder: 38% of the person's height, reaching about mid-thigh. An average female, at 64 cm (25 in), reaches about mid-thigh. In feet, males stand 1 ft 11 in to 2 ft 5 in tall at the shoulder and females 1 ft 11 in to 2 ft 3 in. Next to a 120 cm (3′11″) child, about seven years old, the shoulder comes to about waist height. The head sits higher than the shoulder, so the dog looks taller than these numbers suggest.

Typical life expectancy is 12–15 years, longer than the 12.1-year average for large breeds.

About the Bernedoodle (Standard)

The Bernedoodle is a cross between a Bernese Mountain Dog and a Poodle. The first intentional Bernedoodles are usually credited to a breeder in Ontario, Canada, in 2003. The aim was to combine the Bernese's gentle, affectionate nature and striking tricolour markings with the Poodle's intelligence and lower-shedding coat. The cross quickly became popular across North America and Europe. As a crossbreed, it isn't recognised by the major kennel clubs and has no official standard.

How big does a Bernedoodle get?

Size depends mainly on the Poodle parent:

  • Standard Bernedoodle (Standard Poodle parent): about 58–74 cm at the shoulder and 32–41 kg (70–90 lb) for males
  • Mini Bernedoodle: about 45–56 cm
  • Tiny or Toy Bernedoodle: about 30–43 cm

These are informal breeder terms. This page shows the Standard Bernedoodle, which reaches roughly mid-thigh on an adult. Next to a Bernese Mountain Dog, a Standard Bernedoodle is about as tall but usually lighter. The Sheepadoodle is a little smaller. Ask breeders about both parents' size, and use the puppy weight calculator as a rough guide.

What a Bernedoodle's size means at home

A Standard Bernedoodle is a big, bouncy dog that needs a large vehicle and plenty of space. The crate size calculator suggests a 54″ (137 cm) crate for an average adult. It needs daily exercise and is far too heavy for an airline cabin.

Coat and care

Bernedoodle coats range from curly to wavy to straight. Curly and wavy coats shed less but mat easily, so they need brushing several times a week and a professional groom every 6–8 weeks. Straight coats shed more, like a Bernese. No dog is truly hypoallergenic. The classic black, white and rust tricolour is popular, but colours vary.

Temperament and health

Bernedoodles are usually friendly, affectionate and playful, and good with children. Some are stubborn, especially as puppies. They can inherit problems from both parents, including hip and elbow dysplasia, eye diseases, and the Bernese's high risk of cancer. Crossbreeding doesn't guarantee better health. Choose a breeder who health-tests both parents. Standard Bernedoodles often live 12–15 years, longer on average than purebred Bernese.

Sources: AKC breed standards for the Bernese Mountain Dog and the Poodle; Bernedoodle breeder associations.

Bernedoodle (Standard) size: common questions

How big does a Bernedoodle (Standard) get?

A fully grown male Bernedoodle (Standard) stands 58–74 cm (22.8–29.1 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 32–41 kg (71–90 lb). Females stand 58–69 cm (22.8–27.2 in) and weigh 27–36 kg (60–79 lb).

How tall is a Bernedoodle (Standard) in feet?

A male Bernedoodle (Standard) stands 1 ft 11 in to 2 ft 5 in tall at the shoulder (58–74 cm) and a female 1 ft 11 in to 2 ft 3 in (58–69 cm). Next to a 5′9″ adult, an average male reaches about mid-thigh.

Is a Bernedoodle (Standard) a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 34 kg (75 lb), the Bernedoodle (Standard) counts as a large dog breed on this site (large = 25–45 kg (55–99 lb) on average). It is taller than 84% of the 268 breeds listed.

How long does a Bernedoodle (Standard) live?

Typical life expectancy is 12–15 years, longer than the 12.1-year average for large breeds.

Can a Bernedoodle (Standard) fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Bernedoodle (Standard) weighs 27–41 kg (60–90 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).

Sizes are breed-standard ranges for adult dogs; individual dogs, working lines and dogs from different countries can be bigger or smaller. Airline notes are a general guide only. Check with your airline and your vet. Disclaimer.
