How big is a German Pinscher?

The German Pinscher is a medium-sized dog breed from Germany, in the Working group. Adults stand 43–51 cm (16.9–20.1 in) at the shoulder and weigh 11–20 kg (25–45 lb); the breed standard gives the same range for males and females. On average it is taller than 46% of the 267 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 42%. At the shoulder, an average German Pinscher reaches about knee height on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 12–14 years, about average for medium breeds. It is too heavy for airlines that cap dog and carrier at 8 kg (17.6 lb), so it usually flies in the hold unless the airline only checks the carrier size.

Sizes follow the American Kennel Club (AKC) breed standard.

German Pinscher size: common questions

How big does a German Pinscher get?

An adult German Pinscher stands 43–51 cm (16.9–20.1 in) tall at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 11–20 kg (25–45 lb).

Is a German Pinscher a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 16 kg (35 lb), the German Pinscher counts as a medium dog breed on this site (medium = 10–25 kg (22–55 lb) on average). It is taller than 46% of the 267 breeds listed.

How long does a German Pinscher live?

Typical life expectancy is 12–14 years, about average for medium breeds.

Can a German Pinscher fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A German Pinscher weighs 11–20 kg (25–45 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).

Sizes are breed-standard ranges for adult dogs; individual dogs, working lines and dogs from different countries can be bigger or smaller. Airline notes are a general guide only. Check with your airline and your vet. Disclaimer.
