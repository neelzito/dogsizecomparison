How big is a Portuguese Podengo (Medium)?

The Portuguese Podengo (Medium) is a medium-sized dog breed from Portugal, in the Hound group. Adults stand 40–54 cm (15.7–21.3 in) at the shoulder and weigh 16–20 kg (35–44 lb); the breed standard gives the same range for males and females. On average it is taller than 45% of the 256 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 43%. At the shoulder, an average Portuguese Podengo (Medium) reaches about knee height on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 12–15 years, about average for medium breeds. It is too heavy for airlines that cap dog and carrier at 8 kg (17.6 lb), so it usually flies in the hold unless the airline only checks the carrier size.

Sizes follow the FCI breed standard.

About the Portuguese Podengo (Medium)

The Portuguese Podengo Médio is the medium size of the Portuguese warren hound, used in Portugal mainly to hunt rabbits, often in packs. It's the most common of the three sizes in Portugal. Like the Pequeno and the Grande, it hunts by sight, sound and scent and has large upright ears and a wedge-shaped head.

What a Podengo Médio's size means at home

The FCI standard calls for 40–54 cm at the shoulder and 16–20 kg. A Médio reaches roughly an adult's knee. The crate size calculator suggests a 42″ (107 cm) crate for an average dog. It's too heavy for an airline cabin.

Podengo Médio vs Podenco Andaluz, Orito and Basenji

The medium Podenco Andaluz and the Podenco Orito Español are about the same size. The Basenji is shorter and lighter.

Temperament and care

Podengos Médios are lively, alert and loyal, devoted to their family and wary of strangers. They have a strong prey drive and need a secure garden and daily exercise. The coat can be smooth or wire and is easy to care for. They're generally hardy and often live 12–15 years.

Sources: FCI standard No. 94 (Podengo Português); Clube Português de Canicultura.

Portuguese Podengo (Medium) size: common questions

How big does a Portuguese Podengo (Medium) get?

An adult Portuguese Podengo (Medium) stands 40–54 cm (15.7–21.3 in) tall at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 16–20 kg (35–44 lb).

Is a Portuguese Podengo (Medium) a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 18 kg (40 lb), the Portuguese Podengo (Medium) counts as a medium dog breed on this site (medium = 10–25 kg (22–55 lb) on average). It is taller than 45% of the 256 breeds listed.

How long does a Portuguese Podengo (Medium) live?

Typical life expectancy is 12–15 years, about average for medium breeds.

Can a Portuguese Podengo (Medium) fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Portuguese Podengo (Medium) weighs 16–20 kg (35–44 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).

Sizes are breed-standard ranges for adult dogs; individual dogs, working lines and dogs from different countries can be bigger or smaller. Airline notes are a general guide only. Check with your airline and your vet. Disclaimer.
