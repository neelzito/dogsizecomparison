How big is a Grand Basset Griffon Vendéen?

The Grand Basset Griffon Vendéen is a medium-sized dog breed from France, in the Hound group. Adults stand 40–46 cm (15.6–17.9 in) at the shoulder and weigh 18–21 kg (40–45 lb); the breed standard gives the same range for males and females. On average it is taller than 38% of the 256 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 47%. At the shoulder, an average Grand Basset Griffon Vendéen reaches just below the knee on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 13–15 years, longer than the 13.3-year average for medium breeds. It is too heavy for airlines that cap dog and carrier at 8 kg (17.6 lb), so it usually flies in the hold unless the airline only checks the carrier size.

Sizes follow the American Kennel Club (AKC) breed standard.

About the Grand Basset Griffon Vendéen

The Grand Basset Griffon Vendéen (GBGV) is a French scent hound from the Vendée region, the bigger cousin of the Petit Basset Griffon Vendéen. Its name means "large, low, rough-coated dog from the Vendée". It was bred to hunt hares and rabbits in packs through thick brambles. It's cheerful, sociable and full of energy.

What a GBGV's size means at home

The AKC standard calls for 15.5–18 in (39.5–45.5 cm) at the shoulder, and GBGVs weigh about 18–20.5 kg (40–45 lb). A GBGV reaches just below an adult's knee. It's longer than tall and sturdy. It usually needs a 36″ (91 cm) crate. Check with the crate size calculator. It's too heavy for an airline cabin.

GBGV vs PBGV and Bavarian

The Petit Basset Griffon Vendéen is smaller. The Bavarian Mountain Scent Hound is taller and heavier. The Podenco Orito Español is about the same size.

Temperament and exercise

GBGVs are friendly, outgoing and good with other dogs. They need plenty of exercise, and their nose leads them. They're vocal.

Coat and health

The rough coat needs weekly brushing. Known health problems include eye conditions and hip dysplasia. GBGVs often live 13–15 years.

Sources: AKC breed standard and breed history; FCI standard No. 33 (Grand Basset Griffon Vendéen).

Grand Basset Griffon Vendéen size: common questions

How big does a Grand Basset Griffon Vendéen get?

An adult Grand Basset Griffon Vendéen stands 40–46 cm (15.6–17.9 in) tall at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 18–21 kg (40–45 lb).

Is a Grand Basset Griffon Vendéen a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 19 kg (42 lb), the Grand Basset Griffon Vendéen counts as a medium dog breed on this site (medium = 10–25 kg (22–55 lb) on average). It is taller than 38% of the 256 breeds listed.

How long does a Grand Basset Griffon Vendéen live?

Typical life expectancy is 13–15 years, longer than the 13.3-year average for medium breeds.

Can a Grand Basset Griffon Vendéen fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Grand Basset Griffon Vendéen weighs 18–21 kg (40–45 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).
