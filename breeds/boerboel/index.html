How big is a Boerboel?

The Boerboel is a giant-sized dog breed from South Africa, in the Working group. Males stand 61–69 cm (24–27 in) at the shoulder and weigh 68–91 kg (150–200 lb). Females stand 56–64 cm (22–25 in) and weigh 68–91 kg (150–200 lb). On average it is taller than 77% of the 247 breeds on this site, and it is the heaviest breed. At the shoulder, an average male Boerboel reaches about mid-thigh on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 9–11 years, about average for giant breeds.

Boerboel size: common questions

How big does a Boerboel get?

A fully grown male Boerboel stands 61–69 cm (24–27 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 68–91 kg (150–200 lb). Females stand 56–64 cm (22–25 in) and weigh 68–91 kg (150–200 lb).

Is a Boerboel a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 79 kg (175 lb), the Boerboel counts as a giant dog breed on this site (giant = 45 kg (99 lb) and over on average). It is taller than 77% of the 247 breeds listed.

How long does a Boerboel live?

Typical life expectancy is 9–11 years, about average for giant breeds.

Can a Boerboel fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Boerboel weighs 68–91 kg (150–200 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).

Sizes are breed-standard ranges for adult dogs; individual dogs, working lines and dogs from different countries can be bigger or smaller. Airline notes are a general guide only. Check with your airline and your vet. Disclaimer.
