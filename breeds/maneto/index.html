How big is a Maneto?

The Maneto is a medium-sized dog breed from Spain, in the Hound group. Adults stand 30–35 cm (11.8–13.8 in) at the shoulder and weigh 8.5–12 kg (19–25 lb); the breed standard gives the same range for males and females. On average it is taller than 23% of the 267 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 29%.

Maneto compared to a human

Next to a 175 cm (5′9″) adult, an average Maneto stands 33 cm (12.8 in) at the shoulder: 19% of the person's height, reaching about mid-shin. In feet, adults stand 1 ft to 1 ft 2 in tall at the shoulder. Next to a 120 cm (3′11″) child, about seven years old, the shoulder comes to about knee height. The head sits higher than the shoulder, so the dog looks taller than these numbers suggest.

Typical life expectancy is 12–14 years, about average for medium breeds. It is too heavy for airlines that cap dog and carrier at 8 kg (17.6 lb), so it usually flies in the hold unless the airline only checks the carrier size.

About the Maneto

The Maneto is a rare short-legged Podenco from Andalusia in southern Spain, especially the provinces of Cádiz and Málaga. Hunters used it to hunt rabbits in thick scrub and bramble, where its low build let it push through dense undergrowth that taller dogs couldn't get into. It's closely related to the Podenco Andaluz, with the same large upright ears and keen senses, but on much shorter legs. The Spanish kennel club (RSCE) recognises it as a separate breed, standard No. 408. The FCI does not recognise it.

What a Maneto's size means at home

The RSCE standard gives a height of 30–35 cm at the shoulder for both sexes and a weight of about 10 kg (± 1.5 kg). A Maneto reaches around mid-shin on an adult, but it's long-bodied and solid. Its weight puts it in the medium size class on this site, even though it's short. Because of its long body and very large upright ears, the crate size calculator suggests a 42″ (107 cm) crate. Measure your own dog to be sure. A Maneto is too heavy for most airline cabins.

Maneto vs Dachshund, Beagle and Podenco Andaluz

Next to a Standard Dachshund, a Maneto is taller and similar in weight, with upright ears and a podenco head. A Beagle is about the same height and weight. The smallest size of the Podenco Andaluz is a little taller and leggier.

Living with a Maneto

Manetos are lively, affectionate and very attached to their family. Like all Podencos, they have a strong hunting instinct, so recall can be unreliable near rabbits, cats or birds, and they need a secure garden. They're energetic, curious and clever, and they need daily walks and play. Their short legs don't stop them running fast. Like other short-legged breeds, they should be kept lean to protect the back.

Rare and often rescued

The Maneto is rare, even in Spain, and few are seen outside Andalusia. Some end up in Spanish shelters, and rescue groups occasionally rehome Manetos and Maneto mixes abroad. If you adopt one, ask about its size, health and how it gets on with other animals.

Care and health

The short coat, usually fawn or cinnamon with white markings, needs very little grooming. Little breed-specific health data exists. Manetos are generally hardy and often live 12–14 years.

Maneto size: common questions

How big does a Maneto get?

An adult Maneto stands 30–35 cm (11.8–13.8 in) tall at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 8.5–12 kg (19–25 lb).

How tall is a Maneto in feet?

An adult Maneto stands 1 ft to 1 ft 2 in tall at the shoulder (30–35 cm). Next to a 5′9″ adult, that reaches about mid-shin.

Is a Maneto a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 10 kg (22 lb), the Maneto counts as a medium dog breed on this site (medium = 10–25 kg (22–55 lb) on average). It is taller than 23% of the 267 breeds listed.

How long does a Maneto live?

Typical life expectancy is 12–14 years, about average for medium breeds.

Can a Maneto fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Maneto weighs 8.5–12 kg (19–25 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).
