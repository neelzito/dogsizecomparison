How big is a Skye Terrier?

The Skye Terrier is a medium-sized dog breed from Scotland, in the Terrier group. Males stand 26 cm (10 in) at the shoulder and weigh 16–21 kg (35–45 lb). Females stand 24 cm (9.4 in) and weigh 11–14 kg (24–31 lb). On average it is taller than 7% of the 256 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 40%. At the shoulder, an average male Skye Terrier reaches about mid-shin on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 12–14 years, about average for medium breeds. It is too heavy for airlines that cap dog and carrier at 8 kg (17.6 lb), so it usually flies in the hold unless the airline only checks the carrier size.

About the Skye Terrier

The Skye Terrier comes from the Isle of Skye in the Scottish Hebrides, where it hunted foxes, otters and badgers among the rocks. Its long, low body and long, flowing coat make it one of the most distinctive terriers. It was very fashionable in Victorian times, when Queen Victoria kept Skyes. Greyfriars Bobby, the famous Edinburgh dog who is said to have guarded his master's grave for 14 years, is often described as a Skye Terrier. The breed is now rare, and The Kennel Club lists it as vulnerable.

What a Skye's size means at home

The AKC standard calls for about 10 in (25.5 cm) at the shoulder for males and 9.5 in (24 cm) for females, and the dog should be twice as long as it is tall. Skyes are heavy for their height: the figures on this page show about 16–20.5 kg (35–45 lb) for males and 11–14 kg for females, putting them in the medium size class on this site. A Skye reaches around mid-shin on an adult. Because of its length, the crate size calculator suggests a 30″ (76 cm) crate.

Skye vs Dandie Dinmont and Scottish Terrier

The Dandie Dinmont is about the same height but much lighter, with a topknot and a shorter coat. The Scottish Terrier is about the same height and much lighter.

Temperament and exercise

Skyes are loyal, brave and often devoted to one person, and reserved with strangers. They need daily walks and play. Like all long-backed dogs, they should be kept lean and discouraged from jumping.

Coat and health

The long, straight coat needs brushing several times a week. Known health problems include back problems and, in puppies, damage to the growth plates of the front legs from too much exercise or jumping. Skyes often live 12–14 years.

Skye Terrier size: common questions

How big does a Skye Terrier get?

A fully grown male Skye Terrier stands 26 cm (10 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 16–21 kg (35–45 lb). Females stand 24 cm (9.4 in) and weigh 11–14 kg (24–31 lb).

Is a Skye Terrier a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 15 kg (34 lb), the Skye Terrier counts as a medium dog breed on this site (medium = 10–25 kg (22–55 lb) on average). It is taller than 7% of the 256 breeds listed.

How long does a Skye Terrier live?

Typical life expectancy is 12–14 years, about average for medium breeds.

Can a Skye Terrier fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Skye Terrier weighs 11–21 kg (24–45 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).

Sizes are breed-standard ranges for adult dogs; individual dogs, working lines and dogs from different countries can be bigger or smaller. Airline notes are a general guide only. Check with your airline and your vet. Disclaimer.
