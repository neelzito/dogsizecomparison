How big is a Borzoi?

The Borzoi is a large-sized dog breed from Russia, in the Hound group. Males stand 71–85 cm (28–33.5 in) at the shoulder and weigh 34–48 kg (75–105 lb). Females stand 66–78 cm (26–30.7 in) and weigh 27–39 kg (60–85 lb). On average that makes it the 7th tallest of the 256 breeds on this site. At the shoulder, an average male Borzoi reaches about hip height on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 9–14 years, shorter than the 12.2-year average for large breeds.

About the Borzoi

The Borzoi, also known as the Russian Wolfhound, was the hunting dog of the Russian aristocracy. For centuries, nobles kept large packs of Borzois on their estates and held huge hunts, in which pairs or trios of Borzois chased down wolves, foxes and hares across open country. After the Russian Revolution of 1917, many estates and kennels were destroyed, and the breed survived largely thanks to dogs that had been exported to Western Europe and America. Today the Borzoi is an elegant, gentle and quiet companion.

What a Borzoi's size means at home

The AKC standard sets minimum heights only: 28 in (71 cm) for males and 26 in (66 cm) for females. Most males stand about 71–85 cm at the shoulder, roughly an adult's hip, and weigh about 34–47.5 kg (75–105 lb). Females weigh about 27–38.5 kg (60–85 lb). A Borzoi is tall and narrow, with a long, slim head, and it is surprisingly light for its height. Because of its height, the crate size calculator suggests a 54″ (137 cm) crate for an average male.

Borzoi vs Scottish Deerhound

The Scottish Deerhound is about the same height but a little heavier, with a wiry grey coat instead of the Borzoi's long, silky, often white-based coat. Both are gentle, quiet giants with a strong urge to chase.

Temperament and exercise

Borzois are calm, affectionate and quiet indoors, and often reserved with strangers. They're independent and sensitive, so gentle, patient training works best. Like all sighthounds, they have a strong prey drive and can reach high speeds. They need regular chances to run in a safe, enclosed area and shouldn't be let off the lead near roads or livestock.

Coat and health

The long, silky coat sheds seasonally and needs brushing once or twice a week, more during moults. Borzois are deep-chested, so they're at risk of bloat (gastric dilatation-volvulus). Feed smaller meals and avoid exercise right after eating. Like other sighthounds, they can be sensitive to some anaesthetics, so choose a vet who knows the breed. Heart problems and bone cancer also occur. Borzois often live 9–14 years.

Sources: AKC breed standard and breed history; FCI standard No. 193 (Russian Hunting Sighthound); The Kennel Club breed standard.

Borzoi size: common questions

How big does a Borzoi get?

A fully grown male Borzoi stands 71–85 cm (28–33.5 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 34–48 kg (75–105 lb). Females stand 66–78 cm (26–30.7 in) and weigh 27–39 kg (60–85 lb).

Is a Borzoi a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 37 kg (81 lb), the Borzoi counts as a large dog breed on this site (large = 25–45 kg (55–99 lb) on average). It is the 7th tallest of the 256 breeds listed.

How long does a Borzoi live?

Typical life expectancy is 9–14 years, shorter than the 12.2-year average for large breeds.

Can a Borzoi fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Borzoi weighs 27–48 kg (60–105 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).

Sizes are breed-standard ranges for adult dogs; individual dogs, working lines and dogs from different countries can be bigger or smaller. Airline notes are a general guide only. Check with your airline and your vet. Disclaimer.
