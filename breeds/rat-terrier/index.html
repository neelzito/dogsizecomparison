How big is a Rat Terrier?

The Rat Terrier is a small-sized dog breed from the United States, in the Terrier group. Adults stand 26–46 cm (10–17.9 in) at the shoulder and weigh 4.5–12 kg (9.9–25 lb); the breed standard gives the same range for males and females. On average it is taller than 27% of the 256 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 22%. At the shoulder, an average Rat Terrier reaches just below the knee on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 12–18 years, longer than the 13.7-year average for small breeds. Lighter individuals fit airline cabin limits of 8 kg (17.6 lb) for dog and carrier; heavier ones need an airline that weighs only the carrier size, or travel in the hold.

Sizes follow the American Kennel Club (AKC) breed standard.

About the Rat Terrier

The Rat Terrier is an American farm dog, developed in the early 20th century from crosses of fox terriers, Manchester Terriers, Whippets, Beagles and other breeds. Farmers used it to kill rats and other vermin and to hunt small game. Legend has it that President Theodore Roosevelt named the breed after his terriers cleared the White House of rats. The AKC recognised the Rat Terrier in 2013.

Two sizes

The AKC recognises two sizes, measured at the shoulder:

  • Miniature: 10–13 in (25.5–33 cm)
  • Standard: over 13 in up to 18 in (33–45.5 cm)

Rat Terriers weigh about 4.5–11.5 kg (10–25 lb). A typical one reaches just below an adult's knee. The crate size calculator suggests a 30″ (76 cm) crate for an average dog. Miniatures may fit the cabin limit. Check with the airline cabin check.

Rat Terrier vs Lakeland and Toy Fox Terrier

The Lakeland Terrier is about the same size, with a wiry coat. The Toy Fox Terrier, a close relative, is smaller.

Temperament and exercise

Rat Terriers are clever, lively and affectionate, devoted to their family and sometimes reserved with strangers. They need plenty of daily exercise and love to dig and chase. They're quick learners and do well at agility and earthdog trials.

Care and health

The short, smooth coat needs very little grooming. Rat Terriers are generally hardy. Known problems include loose kneecaps, allergies and dental problems. They're long-lived, often 12–18 years.

Sources: AKC breed standard and breed history; Rat Terrier Club of America.

Rat Terrier size: common questions

How big does a Rat Terrier get?

An adult Rat Terrier stands 26–46 cm (10–17.9 in) tall at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 4.5–12 kg (9.9–25 lb).

Is a Rat Terrier a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 8 kg (18 lb), the Rat Terrier counts as a small dog breed on this site (small = 5–10 kg (11–22 lb) on average). It is taller than 27% of the 256 breeds listed.

How long does a Rat Terrier live?

Typical life expectancy is 12–18 years, longer than the 13.7-year average for small breeds.

Can a Rat Terrier fly in the airplane cabin?

Sometimes. Lighter individuals fit airline cabin limits of 8 kg (17.6 lb) for dog and carrier; heavier ones need an airline that weighs only the carrier size, or travel in the hold. Rules vary by airline, so check before booking.
