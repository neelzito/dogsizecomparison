How big is a Sussex Spaniel?

The Sussex Spaniel is a medium-sized dog breed from England, in the Sporting group. Adults stand 33–38 cm (13–15 in) at the shoulder and weigh 16–21 kg (35–45 lb); the breed standard gives the same range for males and females. On average it is taller than 27% of the 267 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 47%. At the shoulder, an average Sussex Spaniel reaches just below the knee on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 13–15 years, longer than the 13.2-year average for medium breeds. It is too heavy for airlines that cap dog and carrier at 8 kg (17.6 lb), so it usually flies in the hold unless the airline only checks the carrier size.

Sizes follow the American Kennel Club (AKC) breed standard.

About the Sussex Spaniel

The Sussex Spaniel was developed in Sussex, England, in the late 18th century, notably by Augustus Fuller at Rosehill Park. It was bred to work slowly and steadily through thick cover, giving voice as it hunted. With its long, low body, golden liver coat and frowning expression, it's very distinctive. It nearly died out after the Second World War, and it's still rare: The Kennel Club lists it as a vulnerable native breed. A Sussex called Stump won Best in Show at Westminster in 2009, aged ten.

What a Sussex's size means at home

The AKC standard calls for 13–15 in (33–38 cm) at the shoulder, and Sussex Spaniels weigh about 16–20.5 kg (35–45 lb). A Sussex reaches just below an adult's knee, but it's long and heavy for its height. It usually needs a 30″ (76 cm) crate. Check with the crate size calculator. It's too heavy for an airline cabin.

Sussex vs American Water Spaniel

The American Water Spaniel is taller and a little lighter, with a curly coat.

Temperament and exercise

Sussex Spaniels are calm, affectionate and easy-going, slower than other spaniels. They need daily walks and enjoy sniffing. They can be vocal and stubborn.

Coat and health

The flat, feathered coat needs brushing a couple of times a week. Known health problems include back problems, ear infections, hip dysplasia and heart conditions. Keep them lean. Sussex Spaniels often live 13–15 years.

Sources: AKC breed standard and breed history; The Kennel Club vulnerable native breeds list; Westminster Kennel Club.

Sussex Spaniel size: common questions

How big does a Sussex Spaniel get?

An adult Sussex Spaniel stands 33–38 cm (13–15 in) tall at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 16–21 kg (35–45 lb).

Is a Sussex Spaniel a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 18 kg (40 lb), the Sussex Spaniel counts as a medium dog breed on this site (medium = 10–25 kg (22–55 lb) on average). It is taller than 27% of the 267 breeds listed.

How long does a Sussex Spaniel live?

Typical life expectancy is 13–15 years, longer than the 13.2-year average for medium breeds.

Can a Sussex Spaniel fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Sussex Spaniel weighs 16–21 kg (35–45 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).
