How big is a Samoyed?

The Samoyed is a medium-sized dog breed from Russia (Siberia), in the Working group. Males stand 54–60 cm (21.1–23.4 in) at the shoulder and weigh 21–30 kg (45–65 lb). Females stand 49–54 cm (19.1–21.1 in) and weigh 16–23 kg (35–50 lb). On average it is taller than 57% of the 256 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 55%. At the shoulder, an average male Samoyed reaches about knee height on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 12–14 years, about average for medium breeds. It is too heavy for airlines that cap dog and carrier at 8 kg (17.6 lb), so it usually flies in the hold unless the airline only checks the carrier size.

About the Samoyed

The Samoyed is named after the Samoyedic peoples of north-western Siberia, especially the Nenets, who used these dogs to herd reindeer, pull sleds and keep them warm at night in their tents. At the end of the 19th century, explorers brought Samoyeds to Europe, and some went on polar expeditions with Fridtjof Nansen and Roald Amundsen. The breed is famous for its sparkling white coat and the upturned corners of its mouth, the "Sammy smile", which helps stop drool from freezing on its face.

What a Samoyed's size means at home

A male Samoyed stands about 53–60 cm at the shoulder, roughly an adult's knee, and weighs about 20–30 kg (45–65 lb). Females are about 48–53 cm and 16–23 kg. Its huge coat makes it look much bigger than it is. It needs a medium-sized car and usually a 42″ (107 cm) crate. Check with the crate size calculator. Samoyeds are too heavy for an airline cabin.

Samoyed vs Siberian Husky

The two are very similar in size, and both are Siberian working dogs. The Samoyed is always white, cream or biscuit, with a fuller, fluffier coat and a smiling face. The Husky has a shorter coat, comes in many colours and is built more for speed. Both are friendly, vocal and energetic.

The coat

A Samoyed's double coat is its glory and its main chore. It sheds heavily all year and massively once or twice a year, so expect white hair everywhere. It needs brushing several times a week, and daily during moulting, right down to the skin. The coat is surprisingly good at shedding dirt, but bathing and drying a Samoyed is a big job. Don't shave it: the coat protects against heat and sunburn as well as cold.

Temperament and exercise

Samoyeds are sociable, gentle and friendly, and usually love everyone, including strangers, so they make poor guard dogs. They're clever but independent, and they can be stubborn. They need daily exercise and enjoy pulling sports, hiking and games. A bored or lonely Samoyed may bark, howl, dig or chew.

Health

Known health problems include hip dysplasia, eye diseases such as progressive retinal atrophy (PRA), diabetes, and Samoyed hereditary glomerulopathy, a kidney disease. Samoyeds often live 12–14 years.

Samoyed size: common questions

How big does a Samoyed get?

A fully grown male Samoyed stands 54–60 cm (21.1–23.4 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 21–30 kg (45–65 lb). Females stand 49–54 cm (19.1–21.1 in) and weigh 16–23 kg (35–50 lb).

Is a Samoyed a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 22 kg (49 lb), the Samoyed counts as a medium dog breed on this site (medium = 10–25 kg (22–55 lb) on average). It is taller than 57% of the 256 breeds listed.

How long does a Samoyed live?

Typical life expectancy is 12–14 years, about average for medium breeds.

Can a Samoyed fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Samoyed weighs 16–30 kg (35–65 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).

Sizes are breed-standard ranges for adult dogs; individual dogs, working lines and dogs from different countries can be bigger or smaller. Airline notes are a general guide only. Check with your airline and your vet. Disclaimer.
