How big is an English Springer Spaniel?

The English Springer Spaniel is a medium-sized dog breed from England, in the Sporting group. Males stand 51 cm (20.1 in) at the shoulder and weigh 23 kg (50 lb). Females stand 49 cm (19.1 in) and weigh 18 kg (40 lb). On average it is taller than 52% of the 247 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 50%. At the shoulder, an average male English Springer Spaniel reaches about knee height on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 12–14 years, about average for medium breeds. It is too heavy for airlines that cap dog and carrier at 8 kg (17.6 lb), so it usually flies in the hold unless the airline only checks the carrier size.

English Springer Spaniel size: common questions

How big does an English Springer Spaniel get?

A fully grown male English Springer Spaniel stands 51 cm (20.1 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 23 kg (50 lb). Females stand 49 cm (19.1 in) and weigh 18 kg (40 lb).

Is an English Springer Spaniel a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 20 kg (45 lb), the English Springer Spaniel counts as a medium dog breed on this site (medium = 10–25 kg (22–55 lb) on average). It is taller than 52% of the 247 breeds listed.

How long does an English Springer Spaniel live?

Typical life expectancy is 12–14 years, about average for medium breeds.

Can an English Springer Spaniel fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A English Springer Spaniel weighs 18–23 kg (40–50 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).

Sizes are breed-standard ranges for adult dogs; individual dogs, working lines and dogs from different countries can be bigger or smaller. Airline notes are a general guide only. Check with your airline and your vet. Disclaimer.
