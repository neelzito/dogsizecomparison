How big is a Bluetick Coonhound?

The Bluetick Coonhound is a large-sized dog breed from the United States, in the Hound group. Males stand 56–69 cm (22–27 in) at the shoulder and weigh 25–37 kg (55–80 lb). Females stand 54–64 cm (21.1–25 in) and weigh 21–30 kg (45–65 lb). On average it is taller than 71% of the 256 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 72%. At the shoulder, an average male Bluetick Coonhound reaches about mid-thigh on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 11–12 years, shorter than the 12.2-year average for large breeds.

About the Bluetick Coonhound

The Bluetick Coonhound was developed in the southern United States, especially Louisiana and Tennessee, from crosses of English Foxhounds and French hounds such as the Grand Bleu de Gascogne. It's a coldtrailing scent hound, able to follow an old trail long after the raccoon has passed, and it trees its quarry with a loud, bawling bay. Its name comes from its dark blue, ticked (speckled) coat. A Bluetick named Smokey is the mascot of the University of Tennessee.

What a Bluetick's size means at home

The AKC standard calls for 22–27 in (56–68.5 cm) at the shoulder for males and 21–25 in (53.5–63.5 cm) for females. Males weigh about 25–36.5 kg (55–80 lb), females 20.5–29.5 kg (45–65 lb). A Bluetick reaches roughly an adult's mid-thigh. It's athletic and muscular. It needs a big boot and usually a 48″ (122 cm) crate. Check with the crate size calculator.

Bluetick vs Treeing Walker

The Treeing Walker Coonhound is about the same height but a little lighter and faster, usually tricoloured. The Bluetick has a heavier build and a slower, more methodical hunting style.

Temperament and exercise

Blueticks are friendly, loyal and laid-back at home, and very determined on a trail. They need plenty of daily exercise, ideally long walks or runs where they can use their nose. Their prey drive is strong, so recall is often unreliable. They're vocal, with a loud, ringing bay.

Care and health

The short, glossy coat needs only weekly brushing, but the long ears need regular cleaning. Known health problems include hip dysplasia, eye problems and bloat. Blueticks often live 11–12 years.

Bluetick Coonhound size: common questions

How big does a Bluetick Coonhound get?

A fully grown male Bluetick Coonhound stands 56–69 cm (22–27 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 25–37 kg (55–80 lb). Females stand 54–64 cm (21.1–25 in) and weigh 21–30 kg (45–65 lb).

Is a Bluetick Coonhound a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 28 kg (61 lb), the Bluetick Coonhound counts as a large dog breed on this site (large = 25–45 kg (55–99 lb) on average). It is taller than 71% of the 256 breeds listed.

How long does a Bluetick Coonhound live?

Typical life expectancy is 11–12 years, shorter than the 12.2-year average for large breeds.

Can a Bluetick Coonhound fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Bluetick Coonhound weighs 21–37 kg (45–80 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).
