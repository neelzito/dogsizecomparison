How big is a Shih Tzu?

The Shih Tzu is a small-sized dog breed from China (Tibet), in the Toy group. Adults stand 23–27 cm (9.1–10.4 in) at the shoulder and weigh 4.1–7.3 kg (9–16 lb); the breed standard gives the same range for males and females. On average it is taller than 7% of the 267 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 13%. At the shoulder, an average Shih Tzu reaches about mid-shin on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 10–18 years, about average for small breeds. Lighter individuals fit airline cabin limits of 8 kg (17.6 lb) for dog and carrier; heavier ones need an airline that weighs only the carrier size, or travel in the hold.

Sizes follow the American Kennel Club (AKC) breed standard.

About the Shih Tzu

The Shih Tzu is an ancient companion breed from Tibet and China, where it was bred and kept for centuries by the Chinese imperial court. Its name means "lion dog", after the lions of Buddhist art. After the Chinese revolution of 1949, the breed nearly died out in China. It was rebuilt in Europe from a small number of dogs, which had been brought to England and Norway in the 1930s. Today the Shih Tzu is one of the most popular small companion dogs worldwide: affectionate, outgoing and happiest with its people.

What a Shih Tzu's size means at home

A Shih Tzu stands about 23–27 cm at the shoulder, around mid-shin on an adult, but it is sturdy for its size: adults weigh about 4–7 kg (9–16 lb). It suits flats and small homes and doesn't need much exercise, though it enjoys daily walks and play. It usually needs a 24″ (61 cm) crate. Check with the crate size calculator.

Can a Shih Tzu fly in the cabin?

Often. Lighter Shih Tzus in a light carrier usually stay under the 8 kg (17.6 lb) limit many airlines set for dog and carrier. Heavier dogs may not fit. Check with the airline cabin check. Because of its short nose, many airlines don't accept the Shih Tzu in the hold, so the cabin is usually the only option.

Shih Tzu vs Lhasa Apso and Maltese

The Lhasa Apso, also from Tibet, looks similar but is a little taller, with a longer nose and a more independent, watchful nature. The Maltese is smaller and lighter, with a pure white coat and a longer muzzle.

Coat care

A Shih Tzu's coat grows long and doesn't shed much, but it tangles easily. A full-length coat needs daily brushing. Most pet owners keep it in a short "puppy cut" and visit a groomer every 4–6 weeks. The hair on the head is often tied in a topknot so it stays out of the eyes.

Health

The flat face can cause breathing problems, overheating in hot weather, and eye problems because the eyes are large and exposed. Check them daily and see a vet promptly if an eye looks red, cloudy or painful. Dental problems are common because the teeth are crowded in the short jaw. Shih Tzus are long-lived: the range shown on this page is 10–18 years, and many live well into their teens.

Sources: AKC breed standard and breed history; The Kennel Club breed standard; FCI standard No. 208 (Shih Tzu).

Shih Tzu size: common questions

How big does a Shih Tzu get?

An adult Shih Tzu stands 23–27 cm (9.1–10.4 in) tall at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 4.1–7.3 kg (9–16 lb).

Is a Shih Tzu a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 5.7 kg (13 lb), the Shih Tzu counts as a small dog breed on this site (small = 5–10 kg (11–22 lb) on average). It is taller than 7% of the 267 breeds listed.

How long does a Shih Tzu live?

Typical life expectancy is 10–18 years, about average for small breeds.

Can a Shih Tzu fly in the airplane cabin?

Sometimes. Lighter individuals fit airline cabin limits of 8 kg (17.6 lb) for dog and carrier; heavier ones need an airline that weighs only the carrier size, or travel in the hold. Rules vary by airline, so check before booking.
