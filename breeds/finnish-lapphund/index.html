How big is a Finnish Lapphund?

The Finnish Lapphund is a medium-sized dog breed from Finland, in the Herding group. Males stand 46–54 cm (17.9–21.1 in) at the shoulder and weigh 15–24 kg (33–53 lb). Females stand 41–49 cm (15.9–19.1 in) and weigh 15–24 kg (33–53 lb). On average it is taller than 45% of the 256 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 49%. At the shoulder, an average male Finnish Lapphund reaches about knee height on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 12–15 years, about average for medium breeds. It is too heavy for airlines that cap dog and carrier at 8 kg (17.6 lb), so it usually flies in the hold unless the airline only checks the carrier size.

Finnish Lapphund size: common questions

How big does a Finnish Lapphund get?

A fully grown male Finnish Lapphund stands 46–54 cm (17.9–21.1 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 15–24 kg (33–53 lb). Females stand 41–49 cm (15.9–19.1 in) and weigh 15–24 kg (33–53 lb).

Is a Finnish Lapphund a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 20 kg (43 lb), the Finnish Lapphund counts as a medium dog breed on this site (medium = 10–25 kg (22–55 lb) on average). It is taller than 45% of the 256 breeds listed.

How long does a Finnish Lapphund live?

Typical life expectancy is 12–15 years, about average for medium breeds.

Can a Finnish Lapphund fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Finnish Lapphund weighs 15–24 kg (33–53 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).
