How big is a Smooth Fox Terrier?

The Smooth Fox Terrier is a small-sized dog breed from England, in the Terrier group. Males stand 40 cm (15.6 in) at the shoulder and weigh 8.2 kg (18 lb). Females stand 36–38 cm (14.2–15 in) and weigh 6.8–7.7 kg (15–17 lb). On average it is taller than 33% of the 268 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 21%.

Smooth Fox Terrier compared to a human

Next to a 175 cm (5′9″) adult, an average male Smooth Fox Terrier stands 40 cm (15.6 in) at the shoulder: 23% of the person's height, reaching just below the knee. An average female, at 37 cm (14.6 in), reaches just below the knee. In feet, males stand 1 ft 4 in tall at the shoulder and females 1 ft 2 in to 1 ft 3 in. Next to a 120 cm (3′11″) child, about seven years old, the shoulder comes to about knee height. The head sits higher than the shoulder, so the dog looks taller than these numbers suggest.

Typical life expectancy is 12–15 years, about average for small breeds. It is too heavy for airlines that cap dog and carrier at 8 kg (17.6 lb), so it usually flies in the hold unless the airline only checks the carrier size.

About the Smooth Fox Terrier

The Smooth Fox Terrier is one of the oldest fox terrier breeds, developed in England in the 18th and 19th centuries to bolt foxes from their dens for mounted hunts. It's mostly white so hunters wouldn't mistake it for the fox. The Smooth and Wire Fox Terriers were shown as one breed until they were separated. The Smooth was very popular in the late 19th century and appeared as Nipper, the dog listening to the gramophone in the "His Master's Voice" logo, though Nipper was probably a mix.

What a Smooth Fox Terrier's size means at home

The AKC standard says males should not exceed 15.5 in (39.5 cm) at the shoulder and calls for about 18 lb (8 kg), with females a little smaller and lighter. A Smooth Fox Terrier reaches just below an adult's knee. It's square, leggy and athletic. It usually needs a 30″ (76 cm) crate. Check with the crate size calculator. Most adults are over the cabin limit for dog and carrier.

Smooth vs Wire Fox Terrier and Manchester

The Wire Fox Terrier is the same size, with a wiry coat. The Manchester Terrier is about the same size, black and tan.

Temperament and exercise

Smooth Fox Terriers are lively, bold and playful, with lots of energy. They need daily exercise and mental stimulation, and they love to dig and chase. They may not tolerate other dogs, and they're vocal.

Care and health

The short, flat coat sheds and needs weekly brushing. Known health problems include lens luxation, deafness and loose kneecaps. Smooth Fox Terriers often live 12–15 years.

Smooth Fox Terrier size: common questions

How big does a Smooth Fox Terrier get?

A fully grown male Smooth Fox Terrier stands 40 cm (15.6 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 8.2 kg (18 lb). Females stand 36–38 cm (14.2–15 in) and weigh 6.8–7.7 kg (15–17 lb).

How tall is a Smooth Fox Terrier in feet?

A male Smooth Fox Terrier stands 1 ft 4 in tall at the shoulder (40 cm) and a female 1 ft 2 in to 1 ft 3 in (36–38 cm). Next to a 5′9″ adult, an average male reaches just below the knee.

Is a Smooth Fox Terrier a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 7.7 kg (17 lb), the Smooth Fox Terrier counts as a small dog breed on this site (small = 5–10 kg (11–22 lb) on average). It is taller than 33% of the 268 breeds listed.

How long does a Smooth Fox Terrier live?

Typical life expectancy is 12–15 years, about average for small breeds.

Can a Smooth Fox Terrier fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Smooth Fox Terrier weighs 6.8–8.2 kg (15–18 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).
