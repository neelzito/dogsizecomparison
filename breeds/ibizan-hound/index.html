How big is an Ibizan Hound?

The Ibizan Hound is a medium-sized dog breed from Spain (Ibiza), in the Hound group. Males stand 60–70 cm (23.4–27.6 in) at the shoulder and weigh 23 kg (50 lb). Females stand 57–66 cm (22.4–26 in) and weigh 21 kg (45 lb). On average it is taller than 80% of the 256 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 54%. At the shoulder, an average male Ibizan Hound reaches about mid-thigh on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 11–14 years, shorter than the 13.3-year average for medium breeds. It is too heavy for airlines that cap dog and carrier at 8 kg (17.6 lb), so it usually flies in the hold unless the airline only checks the carrier size.

About the Ibizan Hound

The Ibizan Hound (Podenco Ibicenco) comes from the Balearic Islands of Spain, especially Ibiza, where it has been used for centuries to hunt rabbits, often in packs. It is one of the Mediterranean "warren hounds", along with the Pharaoh Hound and the Podencos, with a lean body, large upright ears and a keen nose, sight and hearing. Ibizan Hounds are famous for their jumping: they can leap straight up from a standstill, often well over 1.5 m, to see over scrub or spot prey.

What an Ibizan's size means at home

The AKC standard calls for 23.5–27.5 in (59.5–70 cm) at the shoulder and about 50 lb (22.5 kg) for males, and 22.5–26 in (57–66 cm) and about 45 lb (20.5 kg) for females. An Ibizan reaches roughly an adult's mid-thigh. It's tall and very light for its height, so its weight puts it in the medium size class on this site. Because of its height and big ears, the crate size calculator suggests a 54″ (137 cm) crate for an average male.

Ibizan vs Pharaoh, Podenco Canario and Saluki

  • The Pharaoh Hound is a little shorter and similar in weight, always red or tan.
  • The Podenco Canario is shorter, and the Podenco Andaluz (Large) is a little shorter but heavier.
  • The Saluki is about the same height and weight, but a classic sighthound with feathered ears.

Ibizans can be smooth-coated or wire-coated, in red, white or red-and-white.

Living with an Ibizan Hound

Ibizans are gentle, affectionate and often clownish with their family, and reserved with strangers. They need daily exercise, including a chance to run in a large, secure area, and a high fence, ideally 1.8 m (6 ft) or more, because of their jumping. Their hunting instinct is strong, so recall is often unreliable and small pets may not be safe. They're sensitive and independent, so gentle, reward-based training works best.

Care and health

The coat needs little grooming. Ibizans feel the cold and appreciate a coat in winter. Like other sighthounds, they can be sensitive to some anaesthetics. Known health problems include deafness, eye diseases, allergies and seizures. Ibizans often live 11–14 years.

Ibizan Hound size: common questions

How big does an Ibizan Hound get?

A fully grown male Ibizan Hound stands 60–70 cm (23.4–27.6 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 23 kg (50 lb). Females stand 57–66 cm (22.4–26 in) and weigh 21 kg (45 lb).

Is an Ibizan Hound a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 22 kg (47 lb), the Ibizan Hound counts as a medium dog breed on this site (medium = 10–25 kg (22–55 lb) on average). It is taller than 80% of the 256 breeds listed.

How long does an Ibizan Hound live?

Typical life expectancy is 11–14 years, shorter than the 13.3-year average for medium breeds.

Can an Ibizan Hound fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Ibizan Hound weighs 21–23 kg (45–50 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).
