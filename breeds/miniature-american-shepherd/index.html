How big is a Miniature American Shepherd?

The Miniature American Shepherd is a medium-sized dog breed from the United States, in the Herding group. Males stand 36–46 cm (14–17.9 in) at the shoulder and weigh 9.1–18 kg (20–40 lb). Females stand 33–43 cm (13–16.9 in) and weigh 9.1–18 kg (20–40 lb). On average it is taller than 34% of the 268 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 37%.

Miniature American Shepherd compared to a human

Next to a 175 cm (5′9″) adult, an average male Miniature American Shepherd stands 41 cm (15.9 in) at the shoulder: 23% of the person's height, reaching just below the knee. An average female, at 38 cm (15 in), reaches just below the knee. In feet, males stand 1 ft 2 in to 1 ft 6 in tall at the shoulder and females 1 ft 1 in to 1 ft 5 in. Next to a 120 cm (3′11″) child, about seven years old, the shoulder comes to about mid-thigh. The head sits higher than the shoulder, so the dog looks taller than these numbers suggest.

Typical life expectancy is 12–13 years, shorter than the 13.3-year average for medium breeds. It is too heavy for airlines that cap dog and carrier at 8 kg (17.6 lb), so it usually flies in the hold unless the airline only checks the carrier size.

About the Miniature American Shepherd

The Miniature American Shepherd was developed in California in the late 1960s by breeding small Australian Shepherds together. The aim was a smaller dog with the same looks, intelligence and herding instinct. For years these dogs were called "Miniature Australian Shepherds" or "Mini Aussies". In 2015 the AKC recognised them as a separate breed under the name Miniature American Shepherd. Popular with horse people, it's a lively, loyal and clever companion.

What a Mini American Shepherd's size means at home

The AKC standard calls for 14–18 in (35.5–45.5 cm) at the shoulder for males and 13–17 in (33–43 cm) for females. Weights range from about 9 to 18 kg (20–40 lb). A typical Mini American reaches just below an adult's knee. It's compact and agile, a good size for most homes and cars. It usually needs a 36″ (91 cm) crate. Check with the crate size calculator. It's too heavy for an airline cabin.

Mini American Shepherd vs Australian Shepherd

Next to an Australian Shepherd, a Mini American is about 14 cm shorter and roughly half the weight. Otherwise they look very similar, with the same merle colours, bobtails and striking eyes. The Hungarian Puli is about the same size but looks completely different, with its corded coat.

Energy and herding

Mini Americans are smaller, but they're still true herding dogs. They need plenty of daily exercise and mental work, such as herding, agility, obedience or trick training. Bored Mini Americans may herd children, cats or bicycles, and nip at heels. They're devoted to their family, often reserved with strangers, and very trainable.

Coat and colours

The medium-length double coat sheds a lot, especially seasonally, and needs weekly brushing. Like Aussies, Mini Americans come in blue merle, red merle, black and red, often with white and copper markings. Two merle dogs should never be bred together, because "double merle" puppies are at high risk of deafness and blindness.

Health

Many Mini Americans carry the MDR1 gene variant, which makes them sensitive to some common medicines. A DNA test is worth doing, and your vet should know the result. Eye diseases, hip dysplasia and epilepsy are also known. Mini Americans often live 12–13 years or more.

Miniature American Shepherd size: common questions

How big does a Miniature American Shepherd get?

A fully grown male Miniature American Shepherd stands 36–46 cm (14–17.9 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 9.1–18 kg (20–40 lb). Females stand 33–43 cm (13–16.9 in) and weigh 9.1–18 kg (20–40 lb).

How tall is a Miniature American Shepherd in feet?

A male Miniature American Shepherd stands 1 ft 2 in to 1 ft 6 in tall at the shoulder (36–46 cm) and a female 1 ft 1 in to 1 ft 5 in (33–43 cm). Next to a 5′9″ adult, an average male reaches just below the knee.

Is a Miniature American Shepherd a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 14 kg (30 lb), the Miniature American Shepherd counts as a medium dog breed on this site (medium = 10–25 kg (22–55 lb) on average). It is taller than 34% of the 268 breeds listed.

How long does a Miniature American Shepherd live?

Typical life expectancy is 12–13 years, shorter than the 13.3-year average for medium breeds.

Can a Miniature American Shepherd fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Miniature American Shepherd weighs 9.1–18 kg (20–40 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).
