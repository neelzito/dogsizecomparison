How big is a Wirehaired Vizsla?

The Wirehaired Vizsla is a large-sized dog breed from Hungary, in the Sporting group. Males stand 59–64 cm (23–25 in) at the shoulder and weigh 25–30 kg (55–65 lb). Females stand 55–59 cm (21.5–23 in) and weigh 21–25 kg (45–55 lb). On average it is taller than 68% of the 267 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 64%. At the shoulder, an average male Wirehaired Vizsla reaches about mid-thigh on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 12–14 years, longer than the 12.1-year average for large breeds.

About the Wirehaired Vizsla

The Wirehaired Vizsla (Drótszőrű Magyar Vizsla) was developed in Hungary in the 1930s by hunters who wanted a Vizsla with a thicker coat to cope with cold weather, icy water and thick cover. They crossed Vizslas with German Wirehaired Pointers and possibly other breeds. The result has the Vizsla's golden-rust colour and gentle nature, with a wiry coat, a beard and bushy eyebrows. The FCI recognised it in 1986 and the AKC in 2014. It's a versatile hunting dog and an affectionate companion.

What a Wirehaired Vizsla's size means at home

The AKC standard calls for 23–25 in (58.5–63.5 cm) at the shoulder for males and 21.5–23 in (54.5–58.5 cm) for females. Wirehaired Vizslas weigh about 25–29.5 kg (55–65 lb) for males and 20.5–25 kg (45–55 lb) for females. A Wirehaired Vizsla reaches roughly an adult's mid-thigh. It's lean, muscular and athletic. It needs a big boot and usually a 48″ (122 cm) crate. Check with the crate size calculator.

Wirehaired vs smooth Vizsla and GSP

The Vizsla is a separate breed with the same colour, a little smaller and lighter, with a short, smooth coat. The German Shorthaired Pointer is about the same size, usually liver or liver and white.

Temperament and exercise

Wirehaired Vizslas are gentle, affectionate and loyal, and they want to be close to their people. They're less of a "Velcro dog" than the smooth Vizsla but still don't like being left alone for long. They need a lot of daily exercise, such as running, swimming, retrieving or hunting, plus mental work. They're sensitive, so gentle, reward-based training works best.

Coat and health

The dense, wiry coat sheds moderately and protects well against cold, water and brambles. It needs weekly brushing and occasional hand-stripping. The beard needs cleaning after meals. Known health problems include hip and elbow dysplasia, allergies and some eye conditions. Wirehaired Vizslas often live 12–14 years.

Sources: AKC breed standard and breed history; FCI standard No. 239 (Drótszőrű Magyar Vizsla); Wirehaired Vizsla Club of America.

Wirehaired Vizsla size: common questions

How big does a Wirehaired Vizsla get?

A fully grown male Wirehaired Vizsla stands 59–64 cm (23–25 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 25–30 kg (55–65 lb). Females stand 55–59 cm (21.5–23 in) and weigh 21–25 kg (45–55 lb).

Is a Wirehaired Vizsla a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 25 kg (55 lb), the Wirehaired Vizsla counts as a large dog breed on this site (large = 25–45 kg (55–99 lb) on average). It is taller than 68% of the 267 breeds listed.

How long does a Wirehaired Vizsla live?

Typical life expectancy is 12–14 years, longer than the 12.1-year average for large breeds.

Can a Wirehaired Vizsla fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Wirehaired Vizsla weighs 21–30 kg (45–65 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).
