How big is a Clumber Spaniel?

The Clumber Spaniel is a large-sized dog breed from England, in the Sporting group. Males stand 46–51 cm (17.9–20.1 in) at the shoulder and weigh 32–39 kg (71–85 lb). Females stand 43–49 cm (16.9–19.1 in) and weigh 25–32 kg (55–71 lb). On average it is taller than 46% of the 268 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 80%.

About the Clumber Spaniel

The Clumber Spaniel is the heaviest of the spaniels, named after Clumber Park, the Nottinghamshire estate of the Dukes of Newcastle, where it was developed in the 18th and 19th centuries. It was bred to work slowly and quietly through thick cover, flushing game birds for hunters on foot. Prince Albert and King Edward VII were among its royal fans. It's a calm, dignified and affectionate dog, and it's now rare.

What a Clumber's size means at home

The AKC standard calls for 18–20 in (45.5–51 cm) at the shoulder and 70–85 lb (32–38.5 kg) for males, and 17–19 in (43–48.5 cm) and 55–70 lb (25–32 kg) for females. A Clumber reaches roughly an adult's knee, but it's long, low and very heavily built, which puts it in the large size class on this site. The crate size calculator suggests a 42″ (107 cm) crate for an average male.

Clumber vs Labrador

A Labrador Retriever is much taller but about the same weight. The Clumber is long and low, with a massive head and heavy bone, and it's always white with lemon or orange markings.

Temperament and exercise

Clumbers are gentle, easy-going and affectionate, usually good with children and other dogs. They need daily walks and enjoy retrieving and sniffing, but they're less energetic than most spaniels. They can be stubborn. Expect drool and shedding.

Care and health

The dense, straight coat sheds a lot and needs brushing a few times a week. Known health problems include hip dysplasia, eyelid problems (entropion and ectropion), back problems, and pyruvate dehydrogenase phosphatase deficiency (PDP1), for which a DNA test exists. Keep your Clumber lean. Clumbers often live 10–12 years.

Clumber Spaniel size: common questions

How big does a Clumber Spaniel get?

A fully grown male Clumber Spaniel stands 46–51 cm (17.9–20.1 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 32–39 kg (71–85 lb). Females stand 43–49 cm (16.9–19.1 in) and weigh 25–32 kg (55–71 lb).

How tall is a Clumber Spaniel in feet?

A male Clumber Spaniel stands 1 ft 6 in to 1 ft 8 in tall at the shoulder (46–51 cm) and a female 1 ft 5 in to 1 ft 7 in (43–49 cm). Next to a 5′9″ adult, an average male reaches about knee height.

Is a Clumber Spaniel a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 32 kg (70 lb), the Clumber Spaniel counts as a large dog breed on this site (large = 25–45 kg (55–99 lb) on average). It is taller than 46% of the 268 breeds listed.

How long does a Clumber Spaniel live?

Typical life expectancy is 10–12 years, shorter than the 12.1-year average for large breeds.

Can a Clumber Spaniel fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Clumber Spaniel weighs 25–39 kg (55–85 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).

Sizes are breed-standard ranges for adult dogs; individual dogs, working lines and dogs from different countries can be bigger or smaller. Airline notes are a general guide only. Check with your airline and your vet. Disclaimer.
