How big is a Kromfohrländer?

The Kromfohrländer is a medium-sized dog breed from Germany, in the Terrier group. Adults stand 38–46 cm (15–17.9 in) at the shoulder and weigh 9.1–16 kg (20–35 lb); the breed standard gives the same range for males and females. On average it is taller than 37% of the 268 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 36%.

Kromfohrländer compared to a human

Next to a 175 cm (5′9″) adult, an average Kromfohrländer stands 42 cm (16.4 in) at the shoulder: 24% of the person's height, reaching just below the knee. In feet, adults stand 1 ft 3 in to 1 ft 6 in tall at the shoulder. Next to a 120 cm (3′11″) child, about seven years old, the shoulder comes to about mid-thigh. The head sits higher than the shoulder, so the dog looks taller than these numbers suggest.

Typical life expectancy is 13–15 years, longer than the 13.3-year average for medium breeds. It is too heavy for airlines that cap dog and carrier at 8 kg (17.6 lb), so it usually flies in the hold unless the airline only checks the carrier size.

About the Kromfohrländer

The Kromfohrländer is a young German breed. It began after the Second World War with a scruffy stray called Peter, which American soldiers brought to the town of Siegen. Ilse Schleifenbaum bred Peter with a local terrier-type female, and the puppies became the foundation of the breed, recognised by the FCI in 1955. It's a friendly, adaptable family dog with a rough or smooth coat.

What a Kromfohrländer's size means at home

The FCI standard calls for 38–46 cm at the shoulder, and Kromfohrländers weigh about 9–16 kg (20–35 lb). One reaches just below an adult's knee. It usually needs a 36″ (91 cm) crate. Check with the crate size calculator. It's too heavy for an airline cabin.

Kromfohrländer vs Irish Terrier and Staffie

The Irish Terrier is a little taller and about the same weight. The Staffordshire Bull Terrier is shorter and heavier.

Temperament and exercise

Kromfohrländers are affectionate, cheerful and loyal, often reserved with strangers, and less driven than many terriers. They need daily exercise and like to be with their family.

Health

The small founding population means some inherited problems occur, including epilepsy, von Willebrand's disease and hyperkeratosis (thickened paw pads). Breed clubs work with outcrossing programmes. Kromfohrländers often live 13–15 years.

Sources: FCI standard No. 192 (Kromfohrländer); Rassezuchtverein der Kromfohrländer.

Kromfohrländer size: common questions

How big does a Kromfohrländer get?

An adult Kromfohrländer stands 38–46 cm (15–17.9 in) tall at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 9.1–16 kg (20–35 lb).

How tall is a Kromfohrländer in feet?

An adult Kromfohrländer stands 1 ft 3 in to 1 ft 6 in tall at the shoulder (38–46 cm). Next to a 5′9″ adult, that reaches just below the knee.

Is a Kromfohrländer a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 13 kg (28 lb), the Kromfohrländer counts as a medium dog breed on this site (medium = 10–25 kg (22–55 lb) on average). It is taller than 37% of the 268 breeds listed.

How long does a Kromfohrländer live?

Typical life expectancy is 13–15 years, longer than the 13.3-year average for medium breeds.

Can a Kromfohrländer fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Kromfohrländer weighs 9.1–16 kg (20–35 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).
