How big is a Dachshund (Standard)?

The Dachshund (Standard) is a medium-sized dog breed from Germany, in the Hound group. Adults stand 21–23 cm (8.1–9.1 in) at the shoulder and weigh 7.3–15 kg (16–32 lb); the breed standard gives the same range for males and females. On average that makes it the 8th shortest of the 268 breeds on this site.

Dachshund (Standard) compared to a human

Next to a 175 cm (5′9″) adult, an average Dachshund (Standard) stands 22 cm (8.6 in) at the shoulder: 12% of the person's height, reaching about mid-shin. In feet, adults stand 8 in to 9 in tall at the shoulder. Next to a 120 cm (3′11″) child, about seven years old, the shoulder comes to about mid-shin. The head sits higher than the shoulder, so the dog looks taller than these numbers suggest.

Typical life expectancy is 12–16 years, longer than the 13.3-year average for medium breeds. It is too heavy for airlines that cap dog and carrier at 8 kg (17.6 lb), so it usually flies in the hold unless the airline only checks the carrier size.

About the Dachshund (Standard)

The Dachshund was bred in Germany to hunt badgers. Its German name means "badger dog". Its long body, short legs, deep chest and loud bark all have a purpose: to follow a badger or fox underground, hold it in its den and let the hunter know where it is. The breed comes in three coats (smooth, wire-haired and long-haired) and several sizes, and in Germany Dachshunds are still used for tracking wounded game.

Standard, miniature or rabbit?

How a Dachshund is sized depends on the kennel club:

  • AKC (US): two sizes by weight. Standard Dachshunds weigh 16–32 lb (7.3–14.5 kg); miniatures 11 lb (5 kg) or less.
  • FCI (Europe): three sizes by chest circumference, measured once the dog is at least 15 months old. Standard Dachshunds measure over 35 cm around the chest, miniatures 30–35 cm, and the smallest, the rabbit Dachshund (Kaninchenteckel), up to 30 cm. The idea is that a dog's chest size decides which burrow it can fit into.

So a "standard" Dachshund in the US and in Germany isn't measured the same way. This page shows the AKC standard size; the Miniature Dachshund has its own page.

What a Dachshund's size means at home

A standard Dachshund is only about 20–23 cm tall at the shoulder, reaching about mid-shin on an adult, but it is much longer than it is tall. Allow for that length when choosing a bed or crate: a 30″ (76 cm) crate usually fits, as the crate size calculator shows. It's a good size for a flat, but a standard Dachshund is too heavy for most airline cabins. A miniature often fits the cabin.

Protect the back

That long back comes with a known risk: intervertebral disc disease (IVDD), where a disc in the spine bulges or bursts and presses on the spinal cord. Dachshunds are the breed most often affected, and about one in four shows signs of back problems during its life. You can lower the risk:

  • Keep your dog lean, because extra weight strains the spine.
  • Use ramps or steps instead of letting it jump on and off the sofa or bed, and carry it on stairs where you can.
  • Give it regular, moderate exercise to keep the back muscles strong.

If your Dachshund suddenly cries out, refuses to move, wobbles on its back legs or drags them, see a vet straight away.

Dachshund (Standard) size: common questions

How big does a Dachshund (Standard) get?

An adult Dachshund (Standard) stands 21–23 cm (8.1–9.1 in) tall at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 7.3–15 kg (16–32 lb).

How tall is a Dachshund (Standard) in feet?

An adult Dachshund (Standard) stands 8 in to 9 in tall at the shoulder (21–23 cm). Next to a 5′9″ adult, that reaches about mid-shin.

Is a Dachshund (Standard) a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 11 kg (24 lb), the Dachshund (Standard) counts as a medium dog breed on this site (medium = 10–25 kg (22–55 lb) on average). It is the 8th shortest of the 268 breeds listed.

How long does a Dachshund (Standard) live?

Typical life expectancy is 12–16 years, longer than the 13.3-year average for medium breeds.

Can a Dachshund (Standard) fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Dachshund (Standard) weighs 7.3–15 kg (16–32 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).

Sizes are breed-standard ranges for adult dogs; individual dogs, working lines and dogs from different countries can be bigger or smaller. Airline notes are a general guide only. Check with your airline and your vet. Disclaimer.
