How big is a Spinone Italiano?

The Spinone Italiano is a large-sized dog breed from Italy, in the Sporting group. Males stand 59–69 cm (23–27 in) at the shoulder and weigh 34–39 kg (75–86 lb). Females stand 56–64 cm (22–25 in) and weigh 28–32 kg (62–71 lb). On average it is taller than 77% of the 267 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 82%. At the shoulder, an average male Spinone Italiano reaches about mid-thigh on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 10–12 years, shorter than the 12.1-year average for large breeds.

About the Spinone Italiano

The Spinone Italiano is an ancient Italian pointing breed, probably from the Piedmont region. It was bred as an all-round gundog to point, flush and retrieve game in thick cover and water. Its wiry coat protected it from thorns, and its name may come from the pino, a thorny bush. Unlike faster pointing breeds, the Spinone hunts at a steady, efficient trot. It's known for its gentle, soulful expression and its calm, patient nature.

What a Spinone's size means at home

The AKC standard calls for 23–27 in (58.5–68.5 cm) at the shoulder for males and 22–25 in (56–63.5 cm) for females. Males weigh about 34–39 kg (75–86 lb), females about 28–32 kg (62–71 lb). A Spinone reaches roughly an adult's mid-thigh. It's square, muscular and solid. It needs a big boot and usually a 48″ (122 cm) crate. Check with the crate size calculator.

Spinone vs Bracco and Weimaraner

The Bracco Italiano, the other Italian pointer, is a little shorter and lighter on average, with a short coat. The Weimaraner is a little taller and about the same weight.

Temperament and exercise

Spinoni are gentle, patient and affectionate, very good with children and other dogs. They're less frantic than many gundogs but still need daily exercise. They're sensitive and respond best to gentle, positive training.

Coat and health

The wiry coat needs weekly brushing and occasional hand-stripping. The beard collects food and water. Known health problems include hip dysplasia, bloat, and cerebellar ataxia, a nerve disease for which a DNA test exists. Spinoni often live 10–12 years.

Sources: AKC breed standard and breed history; FCI standard No. 165 (Spinone Italiano); Spinone Club of America.

Spinone Italiano size: common questions

How big does a Spinone Italiano get?

A fully grown male Spinone Italiano stands 59–69 cm (23–27 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 34–39 kg (75–86 lb). Females stand 56–64 cm (22–25 in) and weigh 28–32 kg (62–71 lb).

Is a Spinone Italiano a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 33 kg (73 lb), the Spinone Italiano counts as a large dog breed on this site (large = 25–45 kg (55–99 lb) on average). It is taller than 77% of the 267 breeds listed.

How long does a Spinone Italiano live?

Typical life expectancy is 10–12 years, shorter than the 12.1-year average for large breeds.

Can a Spinone Italiano fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Spinone Italiano weighs 28–39 kg (62–86 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).

Sizes are breed-standard ranges for adult dogs; individual dogs, working lines and dogs from different countries can be bigger or smaller. Airline notes are a general guide only. Check with your airline and your vet. Disclaimer.
