How big is a Sloughi?

The Sloughi is a medium-sized dog breed from North Africa, in the Hound group. Males stand 66–74 cm (26–28.9 in) at the shoulder and weigh 18–30 kg (40–65 lb). Females stand 61–69 cm (24–27 in) and weigh 18–30 kg (40–65 lb). On average it is taller than 90% of the 247 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 57%. At the shoulder, an average male Sloughi reaches about mid-thigh on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 10–15 years, shorter than the 13.3-year average for medium breeds. It is too heavy for airlines that cap dog and carrier at 8 kg (17.6 lb), so it usually flies in the hold unless the airline only checks the carrier size.

Sloughi size: common questions

How big does a Sloughi get?

A fully grown male Sloughi stands 66–74 cm (26–28.9 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 18–30 kg (40–65 lb). Females stand 61–69 cm (24–27 in) and weigh 18–30 kg (40–65 lb).

Is a Sloughi a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 24 kg (52 lb), the Sloughi counts as a medium dog breed on this site (medium = 10–25 kg (22–55 lb) on average). It is taller than 90% of the 247 breeds listed.

How long does a Sloughi live?

Typical life expectancy is 10–15 years, shorter than the 13.3-year average for medium breeds.

Can a Sloughi fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Sloughi weighs 18–30 kg (40–65 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).
