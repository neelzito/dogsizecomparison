How big is a Standard Poodle?

The Standard Poodle is a medium-sized dog breed from France/Germany, in the Non-Sporting group. Males stand 53–62 cm (20.9–24.4 in) at the shoulder and weigh 27–32 kg (60–71 lb). Females stand 50–58 cm (19.7–22.8 in) and weigh 18–23 kg (40–50 lb). On average it is taller than 60% of the 256 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 62%. At the shoulder, an average male Standard Poodle reaches about knee height on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 10–18 years, longer than the 13.3-year average for medium breeds. It is too heavy for airlines that cap dog and carrier at 8 kg (17.6 lb), so it usually flies in the hold unless the airline only checks the carrier size.

About the Standard Poodle

The Poodle is often thought of as a French lapdog, but the Standard Poodle started out as a working water retriever. The name comes from the German Pudel, linked to splashing in water, and the breed was used across Europe to fetch ducks. The famous show clip began as a practical cut: the hair was shaved from the legs and body so the dog could swim more easily, while tufts were left over the joints and chest to keep them warm. The FCI lists France as the breed's home country, and the Poodle became so associated with France that it is often called the national dog there.

Four sizes, one breed

Poodles come in several sizes, and kennel clubs divide them differently:

  • AKC and The Kennel Club: three sizes. In the US, a Standard is over 15 in (38 cm) at the shoulder, a Miniature 10–15 in (25–38 cm) and a Toy 10 in (25 cm) or under.
  • FCI: four sizes. Standard 45–60 cm, Medium 35–45 cm, Miniature 28–35 cm and Toy 24–28 cm.

What a Standard Poodle's size means at home

A Standard Poodle reaches roughly an adult's knee height at the shoulder, and its long neck and head carriage make it look taller than that. It is lighter than it looks: males weigh about 27–32 kg (60–70 lb), and many are lean. Under the coat it is an athletic, elegant dog that likes to run and swim. It usually needs a 42″ (107 cm) crate. Coat care

Poodles don't shed in the usual way, which is why they are popular with people who dislike dog hair and why they are the "oodle" in so many crossbreeds. The flip side is that the curly coat keeps growing. It needs brushing several times a week down to the skin, and a clip every 4–8 weeks, otherwise it mats. Many pet owners choose a short, easy "puppy" or "lamb" clip rather than a show trim.

Poodles and doodles

The Standard Poodle is the parent of the large doodles, such as the Labradoodle, Goldendoodle and Bernedoodle. Their size and coat depend heavily on which Poodle size was used. If you want a predictable size and coat, a purebred Poodle is the more reliable choice.

Standard Poodle size: common questions

How big does a Standard Poodle get?

A fully grown male Standard Poodle stands 53–62 cm (20.9–24.4 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 27–32 kg (60–71 lb). Females stand 50–58 cm (19.7–22.8 in) and weigh 18–23 kg (40–50 lb).

Is a Standard Poodle a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 25 kg (55 lb), the Standard Poodle counts as a medium dog breed on this site (medium = 10–25 kg (22–55 lb) on average). It is taller than 60% of the 256 breeds listed.

How long does a Standard Poodle live?

Typical life expectancy is 10–18 years, longer than the 13.3-year average for medium breeds.

Can a Standard Poodle fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Standard Poodle weighs 18–32 kg (40–71 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).
