How big is a Nederlandse Kooikerhondje?

The Nederlandse Kooikerhondje is a medium-sized dog breed from the Netherlands, in the Sporting group. Males stand 41 cm (15.9 in) at the shoulder and weigh 9.1–14 kg (20–30 lb). Females stand 38 cm (15 in) and weigh 9.1–14 kg (20–30 lb). On average it is taller than 34% of the 267 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 32%. At the shoulder, an average male Nederlandse Kooikerhondje reaches just below the knee on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 12–15 years, about average for medium breeds. It is too heavy for airlines that cap dog and carrier at 8 kg (17.6 lb), so it usually flies in the hold unless the airline only checks the carrier size.

About the Nederlandse Kooikerhondje

The Nederlandse Kooikerhondje (say "KOY-ker-hond-yuh") is a small Dutch spaniel bred to help with duck decoys, called eendenkooien. The dog would trot along the edge of a pond, waving its white-tipped tail, and curious ducks would follow it into a funnel-shaped trap. Kooikerhondjes appear in Dutch paintings from the 17th century. The breed nearly died out by the Second World War, but Baroness van Hardenbroek van Ammerstol revived it from a few dogs, starting with a female called Tommy.

What a Kooikerhondje's size means at home

The AKC standard calls for about 16 in (40.5 cm) at the shoulder for males and 15 in (38 cm) for females. Kooikers weigh about 9–13.5 kg (20–30 lb). A Kooiker reaches just below an adult's knee. It's compact, athletic and elegant. It usually needs a 30″ (76 cm) crate. Check with the crate size calculator. It's too heavy for most airline cabins.

Kooikerhondje vs Cocker Spaniel and Toller

The Kooiker is about the same size as an American Cocker Spaniel, but longer-legged, with a red-and-white coat and black-tipped ears ("earrings"). It's sometimes confused with the Nova Scotia Duck Tolling Retriever, which also lures ducks, but the Toller is bigger and red with white markings.

Temperament and exercise

Kooikers are lively, alert and affectionate, devoted to their family but often shy or reserved with strangers, so early socialisation is important. They're intelligent and quick to learn, and they excel at agility, obedience and trick training. They need daily exercise and mental stimulation. They're sensitive, so gentle, reward-based training works best.

Coat and health

The medium-length, slightly wavy coat sheds moderately and needs brushing once or twice a week. Known health problems include hereditary necrotising myelopathy (a nerve disease), von Willebrand's disease (a bleeding disorder) and epilepsy. DNA tests exist for the first two. Kooikers often live 12–15 years.

Nederlandse Kooikerhondje size: common questions

How big does a Nederlandse Kooikerhondje get?

A fully grown male Nederlandse Kooikerhondje stands 41 cm (15.9 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 9.1–14 kg (20–30 lb). Females stand 38 cm (15 in) and weigh 9.1–14 kg (20–30 lb).

Is a Nederlandse Kooikerhondje a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 11 kg (25 lb), the Nederlandse Kooikerhondje counts as a medium dog breed on this site (medium = 10–25 kg (22–55 lb) on average). It is taller than 34% of the 267 breeds listed.

How long does a Nederlandse Kooikerhondje live?

Typical life expectancy is 12–15 years, about average for medium breeds.

Can a Nederlandse Kooikerhondje fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Nederlandse Kooikerhondje weighs 9.1–14 kg (20–30 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).
