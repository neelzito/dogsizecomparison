How big is a Standard Schnauzer?

The Standard Schnauzer is a medium-sized dog breed from Germany, in the Working group. Males stand 47–50 cm (18.5–19.5 in) at the shoulder and weigh 16–23 kg (35–50 lb). Females stand 45–47 cm (17.5–18.5 in) and weigh 14–21 kg (30–45 lb). On average it is taller than 46% of the 268 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 46%.

Standard Schnauzer compared to a human

Next to a 175 cm (5′9″) adult, an average male Standard Schnauzer stands 48 cm (19 in) at the shoulder: 28% of the person's height, reaching about knee height. An average female, at 46 cm (18 in), reaches about knee height. In feet, males stand 1 ft 7 in tall at the shoulder and females 1 ft 6 in to 1 ft 7 in. Next to a 120 cm (3′11″) child, about seven years old, the shoulder comes to about mid-thigh. The head sits higher than the shoulder, so the dog looks taller than these numbers suggest.

Typical life expectancy is 13–16 years, longer than the 13.3-year average for medium breeds. It is too heavy for airlines that cap dog and carrier at 8 kg (17.6 lb), so it usually flies in the hold unless the airline only checks the carrier size.

About the Standard Schnauzer

The Standard Schnauzer is the original Schnauzer, from which the Miniature and Giant breeds were later developed. It comes from southern Germany, where farmers used it for centuries as an all-round farm dog: catching rats, guarding the yard and travelling with carts to market. Dogs of this type appear in paintings by Albrecht Dürer and Rembrandt. The name comes from the German Schnauze, "snout", after its famous bearded muzzle. Today the Standard Schnauzer is a clever, lively and loyal companion.

What a Standard Schnauzer's size means at home

The AKC standard calls for 18.5–19.5 in (47–49.5 cm) at the shoulder for males and 17.5–18.5 in (44.5–47 cm) for females. Standards weigh about 16–22.5 kg (35–50 lb) for males and 13.5–20.5 kg (30–45 lb) for females. A Standard reaches roughly an adult's knee. It's square, robust and medium-sized. It usually needs a 36″ (91 cm) crate. Check with the crate size calculator. It's too heavy for an airline cabin.

Three Schnauzer sizes

The Miniature Schnauzer is much smaller, about 15 cm shorter and less than half the weight. The Giant Schnauzer is much bigger, about 19 cm taller and twice the weight. All three share the wiry coat, beard and eyebrows, but they are separate breeds, and the Standard sits in the middle in size and energy.

Temperament and exercise

Standard Schnauzers are intelligent, spirited and devoted to their family. They make good watchdogs, alert and wary of strangers without being aggressive. They need daily exercise and mental work, and they excel at obedience, agility and tracking. They can be headstrong, so consistent training and early socialisation are important. A bored Standard Schnauzer may find its own jobs.

Coat and health

The harsh, wiry double coat sheds little. Show dogs are hand-stripped, and pets are often clipped every 6–8 weeks. The beard needs regular cleaning. Standard Schnauzers are generally healthy. Known problems include hip dysplasia, eye conditions and hypothyroidism. They're long-lived, often 13–16 years.

Standard Schnauzer size: common questions

How big does a Standard Schnauzer get?

A fully grown male Standard Schnauzer stands 47–50 cm (18.5–19.5 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 16–23 kg (35–50 lb). Females stand 45–47 cm (17.5–18.5 in) and weigh 14–21 kg (30–45 lb).

How tall is a Standard Schnauzer in feet?

A male Standard Schnauzer stands 1 ft 7 in tall at the shoulder (47–50 cm) and a female 1 ft 6 in to 1 ft 7 in (45–47 cm). Next to a 5′9″ adult, an average male reaches about knee height.

Is a Standard Schnauzer a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 18 kg (40 lb), the Standard Schnauzer counts as a medium dog breed on this site (medium = 10–25 kg (22–55 lb) on average). It is taller than 46% of the 268 breeds listed.

How long does a Standard Schnauzer live?

Typical life expectancy is 13–16 years, longer than the 13.3-year average for medium breeds.

Can a Standard Schnauzer fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Standard Schnauzer weighs 14–23 kg (30–50 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).
