How big is a Chesapeake Bay Retriever?

The Chesapeake Bay Retriever is a large-sized dog breed from the United States, in the Sporting group. Males stand 59–66 cm (23–26 in) at the shoulder and weigh 30–37 kg (65–80 lb). Females stand 54–61 cm (21.1–24 in) and weigh 25–32 kg (55–71 lb). On average it is taller than 70% of the 267 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 79%. At the shoulder, an average male Chesapeake Bay Retriever reaches about mid-thigh on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 10–13 years, shorter than the 12.1-year average for large breeds.

About the Chesapeake Bay Retriever

The Chesapeake Bay Retriever is an American breed, from the shores of Chesapeake Bay in Maryland. According to the breed's history, it began in 1807 with two Newfoundland-type puppies, Sailor and Canton, rescued from a sinking English ship. They were bred with local retrievers and hounds to create a tough dog that could retrieve ducks from icy, rough water all day. It is the state dog of Maryland. With its oily, wavy coat and strong will, the "Chessie" is one of the hardiest retrievers.

What a Chessie's size means at home

The AKC standard calls for 23–26 in (58.5–66 cm) and 65–80 lb (29.5–36.5 kg) for males, and 21–24 in (53.5–61 cm) and 55–70 lb (25–32 kg) for females. A Chessie reaches roughly an adult's mid-thigh. It's powerful and solidly built. It needs a big boot and usually a 48″ (122 cm) crate. Check with the crate size calculator.

Chessie vs Labrador

The Chessie is similar in size to a Labrador, a little taller on average and about the same weight. But it looks and behaves differently: its coat is wavy and oily, in shades of brown, sedge or "deadgrass" (straw to tan), and its eyes are often yellow or amber. It's also more independent and protective than the typically easy-going Labrador.

The coat

The Chessie's double coat has a dense, woolly undercoat and a short, harsh, wavy outer coat with natural oils that make it nearly waterproof. It needs only weekly brushing and occasional baths, as too much washing strips the oils. It sheds seasonally and has a distinctive doggy smell.

Temperament and exercise

Chessies are intelligent, loyal and hard-working, but more strong-willed and reserved with strangers than other retrievers. They're often protective of their family and property. They need plenty of daily exercise and love swimming and retrieving, even in cold water. Early socialisation and consistent, confident training are important.

Health

Known health problems include hip and elbow dysplasia, eye diseases such as progressive retinal atrophy (PRA), degenerative myelopathy and exercise-induced collapse. DNA tests exist for several of these. Chessies often live 10–13 years.

Chesapeake Bay Retriever size: common questions

How big does a Chesapeake Bay Retriever get?

A fully grown male Chesapeake Bay Retriever stands 59–66 cm (23–26 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 30–37 kg (65–80 lb). Females stand 54–61 cm (21.1–24 in) and weigh 25–32 kg (55–71 lb).

Is a Chesapeake Bay Retriever a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 31 kg (68 lb), the Chesapeake Bay Retriever counts as a large dog breed on this site (large = 25–45 kg (55–99 lb) on average). It is taller than 70% of the 267 breeds listed.

How long does a Chesapeake Bay Retriever live?

Typical life expectancy is 10–13 years, shorter than the 12.1-year average for large breeds.

Can a Chesapeake Bay Retriever fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Chesapeake Bay Retriever weighs 25–37 kg (55–80 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).

Sizes are breed-standard ranges for adult dogs; individual dogs, working lines and dogs from different countries can be bigger or smaller. Airline notes are a general guide only. Check with your airline and your vet. Disclaimer.
