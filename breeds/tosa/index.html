How big is a Tosa?

The Tosa is a giant-sized dog breed from Japan, in the Working group. Males stand 62–82 cm (24.4–32.3 in) at the shoulder and weigh 46–91 kg (100–200 lb). Females stand 55–65 cm (21.7–25.6 in) and weigh 46–91 kg (100–200 lb). On average it is taller than 87% of the 256 breeds on this site, and it is the 5th heaviest breed. At the shoulder, an average male Tosa reaches about mid-thigh on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 10–12 years, longer than the 10.1-year average for giant breeds.

About the Tosa

The Tosa (or Tosa Inu) is a large Japanese mastiff from the former Tosa province, now Kōchi Prefecture, on the island of Shikoku. It was developed in the late 19th century by crossing native Japanese dogs with Western breeds such as the Mastiff, Bulldog, Great Dane and Saint Bernard, specifically for Japanese dog fighting. Tosas are still used in this way in parts of Japan. Outside Japan, the breed is rare and widely restricted.

Laws and restrictions

The Tosa is banned or restricted in many countries. In the UK, it's one of the four types banned under the Dangerous Dogs Act 1991. Many other countries, regions and cities ban it or require special permits, insurance or muzzles. Check the current laws where you live, and where you plan to travel or move, before considering one.

What a Tosa's size means at home

Tosas vary a lot in size. Japanese fighting lines can be much smaller than Western show lines. The figures on this page cover about 55–82 cm at the shoulder and 45.5–90.5 kg (100–200 lb). A Tosa reaches roughly an adult's mid-thigh. It's massive and powerful, and its weight puts it in the giant size class on this site. It needs a large vehicle and a 54″ (137 cm) crate. Check with the crate size calculator.

Tosa vs Boerboel and Caucasian Shepherd

The Boerboel is shorter on average but usually heavier. The Caucasian Shepherd is a little shorter and lighter on average, with a thick coat.

Temperament

A well-bred, well-raised Tosa can be calm and quiet with its family. But it was bred for fighting and guarding, so it needs a very experienced owner, extensive socialisation and secure handling. Many are intolerant of other dogs. It's not a breed for first-time owners.

Care and health

The short coat needs little grooming. Known health problems include hip and elbow dysplasia, bloat and heart problems. Tosas often live 10–12 years.

Tosa size: common questions

How big does a Tosa get?

A fully grown male Tosa stands 62–82 cm (24.4–32.3 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 46–91 kg (100–200 lb). Females stand 55–65 cm (21.7–25.6 in) and weigh 46–91 kg (100–200 lb).

Is a Tosa a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 68 kg (150 lb), the Tosa counts as a giant dog breed on this site (giant = 45 kg (99 lb) and over on average). It is taller than 87% of the 256 breeds listed.

How long does a Tosa live?

Typical life expectancy is 10–12 years, longer than the 10.1-year average for giant breeds.

Can a Tosa fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Tosa weighs 46–91 kg (100–200 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).

Sizes are breed-standard ranges for adult dogs; individual dogs, working lines and dogs from different countries can be bigger or smaller. Airline notes are a general guide only. Check with your airline and your vet. Disclaimer.
