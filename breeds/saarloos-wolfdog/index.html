How big is a Saarloos Wolfdog?

The Saarloos Wolfdog is a large-sized dog breed from the Netherlands, in the Herding group. Males stand 65–75 cm (25.6–29.5 in) at the shoulder and weigh 36–41 kg (79–90 lb). Females stand 60–70 cm (23.6–27.6 in) and weigh 30–35 kg (66–77 lb). On average it is taller than 91% of the 256 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 84%. At the shoulder, an average male Saarloos Wolfdog reaches about mid-thigh on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 10–12 years, shorter than the 12.2-year average for large breeds.

About the Saarloos Wolfdog

The Saarloos Wolfdog was created in the Netherlands in the 1930s by Leendert Saarloos, who crossed a German Shepherd with a European wolf. He hoped to produce a hardier, healthier working dog. The dogs turned out too shy and independent for police work, but breeders continued the line, and the FCI recognised it in 1981, named after its founder. It's wolf-like in looks and behaviour, and it's very demanding to live with.

What a Saarloos's size means at home

The FCI standard calls for 65–75 cm at the shoulder for males and 60–70 cm for females. Typical weights are about 36–41 kg for males and 30–35 kg for females. A Saarloos reaches roughly an adult's mid-thigh. It's lean, long-legged and athletic. Because of its height, the crate size calculator suggests a 54″ (137 cm) crate for an average male.

Saarloos vs Briard and Czechoslovakian Wolfdog

The Briard is shorter and lighter. The Czechoslovakian Wolfdog, another wolf-German Shepherd cross, is about the same height and lighter.

Laws and restrictions

Some countries, regions and cities treat wolfdogs as wolf hybrids, with permits, restrictions or bans. Check the current rules before getting one.

Not a dog for most homes

Saarloos Wolfdogs are reserved, cautious and independent, very attached to their family but often fearful of strangers and new situations. They need extensive socialisation, a calm, experienced owner, a secure property and a lot of exercise. Many howl rather than bark.

Care and health

The thick coat sheds heavily seasonally. Known health problems include hip dysplasia and degenerative myelopathy. Saarloos often live 10–12 years.

Sources: FCI standard No. 311 (Saarloos Wolfhond); Nederlandse Vereniging van Saarlooswolfhonden.

Saarloos Wolfdog size: common questions

How big does a Saarloos Wolfdog get?

A fully grown male Saarloos Wolfdog stands 65–75 cm (25.6–29.5 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 36–41 kg (79–90 lb). Females stand 60–70 cm (23.6–27.6 in) and weigh 30–35 kg (66–77 lb).

Is a Saarloos Wolfdog a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 36 kg (78 lb), the Saarloos Wolfdog counts as a large dog breed on this site (large = 25–45 kg (55–99 lb) on average). It is taller than 91% of the 256 breeds listed.

How long does a Saarloos Wolfdog live?

Typical life expectancy is 10–12 years, shorter than the 12.2-year average for large breeds.

Can a Saarloos Wolfdog fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Saarloos Wolfdog weighs 30–41 kg (66–90 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).

Sizes are breed-standard ranges for adult dogs; individual dogs, working lines and dogs from different countries can be bigger or smaller. Airline notes are a general guide only. Check with your airline and your vet. Disclaimer.
