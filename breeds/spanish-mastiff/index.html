How big is a Spanish Mastiff?

The Spanish Mastiff is a giant-sized dog breed from Spain, in the Working group. Males stand 77–88 cm (30.3–34.6 in) at the shoulder and weigh 64–91 kg (140–200 lb). Females stand 72–80 cm (28.3–31.5 in) and weigh 64–91 kg (140–200 lb). On average that makes it the 3rd tallest of the 247 breeds on this site. At the shoulder, an average male Spanish Mastiff reaches about hip height on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 10–12 years, longer than the 10.1-year average for giant breeds.

Spanish Mastiff size: common questions

How big does a Spanish Mastiff get?

A fully grown male Spanish Mastiff stands 77–88 cm (30.3–34.6 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 64–91 kg (140–200 lb). Females stand 72–80 cm (28.3–31.5 in) and weigh 64–91 kg (140–200 lb).

Is a Spanish Mastiff a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 77 kg (170 lb), the Spanish Mastiff counts as a giant dog breed on this site (giant = 45 kg (99 lb) and over on average). It is the 3rd tallest of the 247 breeds listed.

How long does a Spanish Mastiff live?

Typical life expectancy is 10–12 years, longer than the 10.1-year average for giant breeds.

Can a Spanish Mastiff fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Spanish Mastiff weighs 64–91 kg (140–200 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).

Sizes are breed-standard ranges for adult dogs; individual dogs, working lines and dogs from different countries can be bigger or smaller. Airline notes are a general guide only. Check with your airline and your vet. Disclaimer.
