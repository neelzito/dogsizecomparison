How big is a Black and Tan Coonhound?

The Black and Tan Coonhound is a large-sized dog breed from the United States, in the Hound group. Males stand 64–69 cm (25–27 in) at the shoulder and weigh 30–50 kg (65–110 lb). Females stand 59–64 cm (23–25 in) and weigh 30–50 kg (65–110 lb). On average it is taller than 81% of the 267 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 87%. At the shoulder, an average male Black and Tan Coonhound reaches about mid-thigh on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 10–12 years, shorter than the 12.1-year average for large breeds.

About the Black and Tan Coonhound

The Black and Tan Coonhound is an American scent hound developed in the Appalachian and Blue Ridge mountains to hunt raccoons at night. It follows the trail with its nose and then "trees" the raccoon, baying at the foot of the tree until the hunter arrives. Its ancestors include the Bloodhound and the black-and-tan foxhounds of colonial America. In 1945 it became the first coonhound breed recognised by the AKC.

What a Coonhound's size means at home

The AKC standard calls for 25–27 in (63.5–68.5 cm) at the shoulder for males and 23–25 in (58.5–63.5 cm) for females. Weights range widely, from about 29.5 to 50 kg (65–110 lb). A Black and Tan reaches roughly an adult's mid-thigh. It's tall, powerful and long-eared. It needs a big boot and usually a 48″ (122 cm) crate. Check with the crate size calculator.

Black and Tan vs Bloodhound and Ridgeback

The Bloodhound is about the same height but heavier, with much looser skin and deeper wrinkles. The Rhodesian Ridgeback is about the same height and weight, but a very different dog in coat and temperament.

Temperament and exercise

Black and Tans are easy-going, friendly and gentle at home, and usually good with children and other dogs. Outdoors, their nose takes over. They need long daily walks, and many can't be trusted off the lead near scents. They have a loud, deep bay, which neighbours may notice.

Care and health

The short, dense coat needs weekly brushing. The long, low-hanging ears need regular cleaning to prevent infections. Known health problems include hip dysplasia, ear infections and eye problems. Black and Tans often live 10–12 years.

Sources: AKC breed standard and breed history; United Kennel Club breed standard; American Black and Tan Coonhound Club.

Black and Tan Coonhound size: common questions

How big does a Black and Tan Coonhound get?

A fully grown male Black and Tan Coonhound stands 64–69 cm (25–27 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 30–50 kg (65–110 lb). Females stand 59–64 cm (23–25 in) and weigh 30–50 kg (65–110 lb).

Is a Black and Tan Coonhound a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 40 kg (88 lb), the Black and Tan Coonhound counts as a large dog breed on this site (large = 25–45 kg (55–99 lb) on average). It is taller than 81% of the 267 breeds listed.

How long does a Black and Tan Coonhound live?

Typical life expectancy is 10–12 years, shorter than the 12.1-year average for large breeds.

Can a Black and Tan Coonhound fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Black and Tan Coonhound weighs 30–50 kg (65–110 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).

Sizes are breed-standard ranges for adult dogs; individual dogs, working lines and dogs from different countries can be bigger or smaller. Airline notes are a general guide only. Check with your airline and your vet. Disclaimer.
