How big is a Spanish Water Dog?

The Spanish Water Dog is a medium-sized dog breed from Spain, in the Herding group. Males stand 45–50 cm (17.5–19.7 in) at the shoulder and weigh 18–22 kg (40–49 lb). Females stand 40–46 cm (15.7–17.9 in) and weigh 14–18 kg (31–40 lb). On average it is taller than 43% of the 266 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 44%. At the shoulder, an average male Spanish Water Dog reaches about knee height on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 12–14 years, about average for medium breeds. It is too heavy for airlines that cap dog and carrier at 8 kg (17.6 lb), so it usually flies in the hold unless the airline only checks the carrier size.

About the Spanish Water Dog

The Spanish Water Dog (Perro de Agua Español) is a versatile working dog from Spain, especially Andalusia. It herded sheep and goats inland, and on the coast it helped fishermen by retrieving nets and objects from the water. It's known for its woolly, curly coat, which forms cords as it grows. The breed was recognised by the FCI in 1985 and the AKC in 2015. Today it's still used for herding and water work in Spain, and it's increasingly popular as an active family dog.

What a Spanish Water Dog's size means at home

The AKC standard calls for 17.5–19.75 in (44.5–50 cm) at the shoulder and 40–49 lb (18–22 kg) for males, and 15.75–18 in (40–45.5 cm) and 31–40 lb (14–18 kg) for females. A Spanish Water Dog reaches roughly an adult's knee. It's athletic, rustic and sturdy, a little longer than tall. It usually needs a 36″ (91 cm) crate. Check with the crate size calculator. It's too heavy for an airline cabin.

Spanish Water Dog vs Portuguese Water Dog and PON

The Portuguese Water Dog is a little taller and heavier, and it's clipped rather than corded. The Polish Lowland Sheepdog is about the same size, with a long, shaggy coat.

The coat: never brushed

The Spanish Water Dog's single coat is woolly and curly, and it's traditionally never brushed. As it grows, it forms natural cords. Most owners shear the whole coat once or twice a year, as Spanish shepherds did, rather than trimming it into a shape. The coat sheds very little.

Temperament and exercise

Spanish Water Dogs are intelligent, loyal and hard-working, devoted to their family and often reserved with strangers, so early socialisation is important. They're energetic and need plenty of daily exercise and mental work, and they excel at herding, agility, scent work and water sports. They can be protective and vocal.

Health

Known health problems include hip dysplasia, eye diseases such as progressive retinal atrophy (PRA), hypothyroidism, and congenital hypothyroidism with goitre and neuroaxonal dystrophy, both of which have DNA tests. Spanish Water Dogs often live 12–14 years.

Sources: AKC breed standard and breed history; FCI standard No. 336 (Perro de Agua Español); Spanish Water Dog Club of America.

Spanish Water Dog size: common questions

How big does a Spanish Water Dog get?

A fully grown male Spanish Water Dog stands 45–50 cm (17.5–19.7 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 18–22 kg (40–49 lb). Females stand 40–46 cm (15.7–17.9 in) and weigh 14–18 kg (31–40 lb).

Is a Spanish Water Dog a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 18 kg (40 lb), the Spanish Water Dog counts as a medium dog breed on this site (medium = 10–25 kg (22–55 lb) on average). It is taller than 43% of the 266 breeds listed.

How long does a Spanish Water Dog live?

Typical life expectancy is 12–14 years, about average for medium breeds.

Can a Spanish Water Dog fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Spanish Water Dog weighs 14–22 kg (31–49 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).

Sizes are breed-standard ranges for adult dogs; individual dogs, working lines and dogs from different countries can be bigger or smaller. Airline notes are a general guide only. Check with your airline and your vet. Disclaimer.
