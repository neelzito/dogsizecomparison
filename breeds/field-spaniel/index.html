How big is a Field Spaniel?

The Field Spaniel is a medium-sized dog breed from England, in the Sporting group. Males stand 46 cm (17.9 in) at the shoulder and weigh 16–23 kg (35–50 lb). Females stand 43 cm (16.9 in) and weigh 16–23 kg (35–50 lb). On average it is taller than 41% of the 256 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 47%. At the shoulder, an average male Field Spaniel reaches about knee height on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 12–13 years, shorter than the 13.3-year average for medium breeds. It is too heavy for airlines that cap dog and carrier at 8 kg (17.6 lb), so it usually flies in the hold unless the airline only checks the carrier size.

About the Field Spaniel

The Field Spaniel was developed in England in the 19th century from the same stock as the Cocker and the Sussex Spaniel. Breeders sorted spaniels by weight, and dogs over 25 lb became Field Spaniels. Show breeders then exaggerated its long, low body, and the breed nearly died out. In the 20th century, breeders restored a more balanced, practical gundog. It's still rare: The Kennel Club lists it as a vulnerable native breed.

What a Field Spaniel's size means at home

The AKC standard calls for about 18 in (45.5 cm) at the shoulder for males and 17 in (43 cm) for females. Field Spaniels weigh about 16–22.5 kg (35–50 lb). A Field reaches roughly an adult's knee. It's a little longer than tall, solid and athletic. It usually needs a 36″ (91 cm) crate. Check with the crate size calculator. It's too heavy for an airline cabin.

Field vs Welsh Springer and Cocker

The Welsh Springer Spaniel is about the same height and a little heavier, always red and white. The English Cocker is smaller. Field Spaniels are usually solid black, liver or roan.

Temperament and exercise

Field Spaniels are gentle, affectionate and sensitive, calmer than many spaniels. They need daily exercise and love retrieving and swimming. They respond best to gentle, reward-based training.

Coat and health

The silky, flat coat with feathering needs brushing a couple of times a week, and the ears need regular checks. Known health problems include hip dysplasia, hypothyroidism and ear infections. Field Spaniels often live 12–13 years.

Field Spaniel size: common questions

How big does a Field Spaniel get?

A fully grown male Field Spaniel stands 46 cm (17.9 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 16–23 kg (35–50 lb). Females stand 43 cm (16.9 in) and weigh 16–23 kg (35–50 lb).

Is a Field Spaniel a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 19 kg (42 lb), the Field Spaniel counts as a medium dog breed on this site (medium = 10–25 kg (22–55 lb) on average). It is taller than 41% of the 256 breeds listed.

How long does a Field Spaniel live?

Typical life expectancy is 12–13 years, shorter than the 13.3-year average for medium breeds.

Can a Field Spaniel fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Field Spaniel weighs 16–23 kg (35–50 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).

Sizes are breed-standard ranges for adult dogs; individual dogs, working lines and dogs from different countries can be bigger or smaller. Airline notes are a general guide only. Check with your airline and your vet. Disclaimer.
