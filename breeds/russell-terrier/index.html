How big is a Russell Terrier?

The Russell Terrier is a small-sized dog breed from England, in the Terrier group. Adults stand 26–31 cm (10–12 in) at the shoulder and weigh 4.1–6.8 kg (9–15 lb); the breed standard gives the same range for males and females. On average it is taller than 14% of the 256 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 11%. At the shoulder, an average Russell Terrier reaches about mid-shin on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 12–14 years, shorter than the 13.7-year average for small breeds. Lighter individuals fit airline cabin limits of 8 kg (17.6 lb) for dog and carrier; heavier ones need an airline that weighs only the carrier size, or travel in the hold.

Sizes follow the American Kennel Club (AKC) breed standard.

About the Russell Terrier

The Russell Terrier is the AKC's name for the short-legged Russell-type terrier, which much of the world, including the FCI and The Kennel Club, calls the Jack Russell Terrier. It descends from the fox-hunting terriers of the Reverend John Russell in 19th-century Devon, and was refined in Australia as a smaller, slightly longer-bodied type. The AKC recognised it in 2012.

What a Russell Terrier's size means at home

The AKC standard calls for 10–12 in (25.5–30.5 cm) at the shoulder, and Russell Terriers weigh about 4–7 kg (9–15 lb). One reaches around mid-shin on an adult. It usually needs a 24″ (61 cm) crate. Check with the crate size calculator. Lighter dogs fit the cabin limit. Check with the airline cabin check.

Russell Terrier vs Jack Russell and Parson Russell

The Jack Russell Terrier (FCI) is essentially the same dog. The Parson Russell Terrier is the taller, square type.

Temperament and exercise

Russell Terriers are bold, energetic and clever, real working terriers. They need a lot of exercise, love to dig and chase, and need a secure garden. Bored Russells can be noisy and destructive.

Coat and health

The smooth, broken or rough coat needs weekly brushing. Known health problems include lens luxation, deafness and loose kneecaps. Russell Terriers often live 12–14 years.

Sources: AKC breed standard and breed history; FCI standard No. 345 (Jack Russell Terrier).

Russell Terrier size: common questions

How big does a Russell Terrier get?

An adult Russell Terrier stands 26–31 cm (10–12 in) tall at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 4.1–6.8 kg (9–15 lb).

Is a Russell Terrier a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 5.4 kg (12 lb), the Russell Terrier counts as a small dog breed on this site (small = 5–10 kg (11–22 lb) on average). It is taller than 14% of the 256 breeds listed.

How long does a Russell Terrier live?

Typical life expectancy is 12–14 years, shorter than the 13.7-year average for small breeds.

Can a Russell Terrier fly in the airplane cabin?

Sometimes. Lighter individuals fit airline cabin limits of 8 kg (17.6 lb) for dog and carrier; heavier ones need an airline that weighs only the carrier size, or travel in the hold. Rules vary by airline, so check before booking.
