How big is a Landseer (European)?

The Landseer (European) is a giant-sized dog breed from Germany/Switzerland, in the Working group. Males stand 72–80 cm (28.3–31.5 in) at the shoulder and weigh 50–70 kg (110–154 lb). Females stand 67–72 cm (26.4–28.3 in) and weigh 45–60 kg (99–132 lb). On average that makes it the 8th tallest of the 247 breeds on this site. At the shoulder, an average male Landseer (European) reaches about hip height on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 10–12 years, longer than the 10.1-year average for giant breeds.

Landseer (European) size: common questions

How big does a Landseer (European) get?

A fully grown male Landseer (European) stands 72–80 cm (28.3–31.5 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 50–70 kg (110–154 lb). Females stand 67–72 cm (26.4–28.3 in) and weigh 45–60 kg (99–132 lb).

Is a Landseer (European) a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 56 kg (124 lb), the Landseer (European) counts as a giant dog breed on this site (giant = 45 kg (99 lb) and over on average). It is the 8th tallest of the 247 breeds listed.

How long does a Landseer (European) live?

Typical life expectancy is 10–12 years, longer than the 10.1-year average for giant breeds.

Can a Landseer (European) fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Landseer (European) weighs 45–70 kg (99–154 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).

Sizes are breed-standard ranges for adult dogs; individual dogs, working lines and dogs from different countries can be bigger or smaller. Airline notes are a general guide only. Check with your airline and your vet. Disclaimer.
