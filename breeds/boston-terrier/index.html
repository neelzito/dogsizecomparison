How big is a Boston Terrier?

The Boston Terrier is a small-sized dog breed from the United States, in the Non-Sporting group. Adults stand 38–43 cm (15–16.9 in) at the shoulder and weigh 5.4–12 kg (12–25 lb); the breed standard gives the same range for males and females. On average it is taller than 35% of the 256 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 24%. At the shoulder, an average Boston Terrier reaches just below the knee on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 11–13 years, shorter than the 13.7-year average for small breeds. Lighter individuals fit airline cabin limits of 8 kg (17.6 lb) for dog and carrier; heavier ones need an airline that weighs only the carrier size, or travel in the hold.

Sizes follow the American Kennel Club (AKC) breed standard. AKC lists 10–12 in, which conflicts with the breed’s usual 15–17 in; height kept as typical, not verified.

Boston Terrier size: common questions

How big does a Boston Terrier get?

An adult Boston Terrier stands 38–43 cm (15–16.9 in) tall at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 5.4–12 kg (12–25 lb).

Is a Boston Terrier a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 8.5 kg (19 lb), the Boston Terrier counts as a small dog breed on this site (small = 5–10 kg (11–22 lb) on average). It is taller than 35% of the 256 breeds listed.

How long does a Boston Terrier live?

Typical life expectancy is 11–13 years, shorter than the 13.7-year average for small breeds.

Can a Boston Terrier fly in the airplane cabin?

Sometimes. Lighter individuals fit airline cabin limits of 8 kg (17.6 lb) for dog and carrier; heavier ones need an airline that weighs only the carrier size, or travel in the hold. Rules vary by airline, so check before booking.

Sizes are breed-standard ranges for adult dogs; individual dogs, working lines and dogs from different countries can be bigger or smaller. Airline notes are a general guide only. Check with your airline and your vet. Disclaimer.
