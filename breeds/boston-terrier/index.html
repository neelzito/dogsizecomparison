How big is a Boston Terrier?

The Boston Terrier is a small-sized dog breed from the United States, in the Non-Sporting group. Adults stand 38–43 cm (15–16.9 in) at the shoulder and weigh 5.4–12 kg (12–25 lb); the breed standard gives the same range for males and females. On average it is taller than 36% of the 268 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 24%.

Boston Terrier compared to a human

Next to a 175 cm (5′9″) adult, an average Boston Terrier stands 41 cm (15.9 in) at the shoulder: 23% of the person's height, reaching just below the knee. In feet, adults stand 1 ft 3 in to 1 ft 5 in tall at the shoulder. Next to a 120 cm (3′11″) child, about seven years old, the shoulder comes to about mid-thigh. The head sits higher than the shoulder, so the dog looks taller than these numbers suggest.

Typical life expectancy is 11–13 years, shorter than the 13.7-year average for small breeds. Lighter individuals fit airline cabin limits of 8 kg (17.6 lb) for dog and carrier; heavier ones need an airline that weighs only the carrier size, or travel in the hold.

About the Boston Terrier

The Boston Terrier was developed in Boston, Massachusetts, in the 1870s. It descends from a dog called Hooper's Judge, a cross between an English Bulldog and the now-extinct white English Terrier. Breeders gradually made the dogs smaller and gentler, and the Boston Terrier became one of the first breeds developed in the United States, recognised by the AKC in 1893. Its neat black-and-white coat earned it the nickname "the American Gentleman". Today it is a friendly, lively companion that is equally happy in a flat or a house with a garden.

Three weight classes

The AKC standard sorts Boston Terriers by weight rather than height:

  • Under 15 lb (under 6.8 kg)
  • 15 to under 20 lb (6.8–9 kg)
  • 20 to 25 lb (9–11.3 kg)

So Bostons range from about 5.5 to 11.5 kg (12–25 lb). The AKC gives no exact height. The heights on this page (about 38–43 cm at the shoulder) are typical values, and smaller Bostons are often shorter. At that height a Boston reaches just below an adult's knee. Because of its height, the crate size calculator suggests a 36″ (91 cm) crate for an average dog, and a smaller Boston may fit a 30″ crate.

Can a Boston Terrier fly in the cabin?

Smaller Bostons sometimes can. A dog under about 6.5 kg in a light carrier may stay within the 8 kg (17.6 lb) limit many airlines set for dog and carrier. Larger Bostons won't. Use the airline cabin check. Like other short-nosed breeds, Bostons are refused in the hold by many airlines.

Boston vs French Bulldog and Pug

The Boston is taller and leggier than both. The French Bulldog is shorter but heavier and more muscular. The Pug is smaller and rounder, with a flatter face. Bostons also have a slightly longer muzzle, so many breathe more easily than Pugs or Frenchies, though they're still sensitive to heat.

Temperament and care

Boston Terriers are friendly, clever and playful, usually good with children, other dogs and visitors. They have more energy than their size suggests and enjoy walks, games and training. The short coat sheds little and needs very little grooming. Watch for eye injuries, because their large, round eyes stick out a bit. Bostons often live 11–13 years.

Sources: AKC breed standard and breed history; The Kennel Club breed standard; Boston Terrier Club of America.

Boston Terrier size: common questions

How big does a Boston Terrier get?

An adult Boston Terrier stands 38–43 cm (15–16.9 in) tall at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 5.4–12 kg (12–25 lb).

How tall is a Boston Terrier in feet?

An adult Boston Terrier stands 1 ft 3 in to 1 ft 5 in tall at the shoulder (38–43 cm). Next to a 5′9″ adult, that reaches just below the knee.

Is a Boston Terrier a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 8.5 kg (19 lb), the Boston Terrier counts as a small dog breed on this site (small = 5–10 kg (11–22 lb) on average). It is taller than 36% of the 268 breeds listed.

How long does a Boston Terrier live?

Typical life expectancy is 11–13 years, shorter than the 13.7-year average for small breeds.

Can a Boston Terrier fly in the airplane cabin?

Sometimes. Lighter individuals fit airline cabin limits of 8 kg (17.6 lb) for dog and carrier; heavier ones need an airline that weighs only the carrier size, or travel in the hold. Rules vary by airline, so check before booking.
