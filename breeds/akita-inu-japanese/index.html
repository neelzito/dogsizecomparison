How big is an Akita Inu (Japanese)?

The Akita Inu (Japanese) is a large-sized dog breed from Japan, in the Working group. Males stand 64–70 cm (25–27.6 in) at the shoulder and weigh 30–34 kg (65–75 lb). Females stand 57–64 cm (22.4–25 in) and weigh 25–30 kg (55–65 lb). On average it is taller than 80% of the 256 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 74%. At the shoulder, an average male Akita Inu (Japanese) reaches about mid-thigh on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 10–12 years, shorter than the 12.2-year average for large breeds.

Akita Inu (Japanese) size: common questions

How big does an Akita Inu (Japanese) get?

A fully grown male Akita Inu (Japanese) stands 64–70 cm (25–27.6 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 30–34 kg (65–75 lb). Females stand 57–64 cm (22.4–25 in) and weigh 25–30 kg (55–65 lb).

Is an Akita Inu (Japanese) a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 30 kg (65 lb), the Akita Inu (Japanese) counts as a large dog breed on this site (large = 25–45 kg (55–99 lb) on average). It is taller than 80% of the 256 breeds listed.

How long does an Akita Inu (Japanese) live?

Typical life expectancy is 10–12 years, shorter than the 12.2-year average for large breeds.

Can an Akita Inu (Japanese) fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Akita Inu (Japanese) weighs 25–34 kg (55–75 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).

Sizes are breed-standard ranges for adult dogs; individual dogs, working lines and dogs from different countries can be bigger or smaller. Airline notes are a general guide only. Check with your airline and your vet. Disclaimer.
