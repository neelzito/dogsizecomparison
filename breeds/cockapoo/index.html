How big is a Cockapoo?

The Cockapoo is a small-sized dog breed from the United States, in the designer & mixed category. Males stand 25–38 cm (9.8–15 in) at the shoulder and weigh 5.4–11 kg (12–24 lb). Females stand 25–36 cm (9.8–14.2 in) and weigh 5–10 kg (11–22 lb). On average it is taller than 19% of the 256 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 21%. At the shoulder, an average male Cockapoo reaches about mid-shin on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 13–18 years, longer than the 13.7-year average for small breeds. Lighter individuals fit airline cabin limits of 8 kg (17.6 lb) for dog and carrier; heavier ones need an airline that weighs only the carrier size, or travel in the hold.

About the Cockapoo

The Cockapoo is a cross between a Cocker Spaniel and a Poodle, usually a Miniature or Toy Poodle. It is one of the oldest "designer" crosses: Cockapoos were bred in the US from around the 1950s and 60s. They became hugely popular in the UK in the 2000s and are now one of the most common crossbreeds there. People love their friendly, cheerful nature and their curly or wavy coat, which often sheds little. As a crossbreed, the Cockapoo isn't recognised by the major kennel clubs and has no official breed standard.

How big does a Cockapoo get?

Because there is no standard, a Cockapoo's size depends mainly on the size of its Poodle parent:

  • Toy Cockapoo (Toy Poodle parent): usually under about 25 cm and under 5 kg
  • Miniature Cockapoo (Miniature Poodle parent): usually about 28–38 cm and 6–10 kg
  • Maxi or Standard Cockapoo (larger Poodle parent): bigger still, up to around 15 kg or more

These are informal terms used by breeders, not official sizes. The figures on this page show the typical range for the common Miniature type: about 25–38 cm at the shoulder, around mid-shin on an adult, and 5–11 kg. Puppies from the same litter can end up quite different in size, so ask the breeder about both parents' size, and use the puppy weight calculator as a rough guide.

English or American Cocker?

Both Cocker Spaniel breeds are used. UK Cockapoos are usually bred from the English Cocker Spaniel, which is a little taller and more athletic. American Cockapoos often come from the smaller American Cocker Spaniel. That's another reason Cockapoos vary.

What a Cockapoo's size means at home

A typical Cockapoo suits flats as well as houses and fits easily in a small car, usually in a 24″ (61 cm) crate. See the crate size calculator. Smaller Cockapoos may fit an airline cabin carrier, but most are over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) limit many airlines set for dog and carrier. Cockapoos are lively, sociable dogs that need daily walks and play, and they don't like being left alone for long.

Coat care

The Cockapoo coat can be tightly curled like a Poodle's, loosely wavy, or straighter like a spaniel's. "Low-shedding" isn't the same as "hypoallergenic": no dog is completely allergen-free. Curly and wavy coats mat easily, so they need brushing several times a week and a trim every 6–8 weeks. Check and clean the floppy ears regularly to prevent infections. Cockapoos often live 13–16 years or more.

Sources: The Cockapoo Club of Great Britain; AKC breed standards for the Poodle and the Cocker Spaniels.

Cockapoo size: common questions

How big does a Cockapoo get?

A fully grown male Cockapoo stands 25–38 cm (9.8–15 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 5.4–11 kg (12–24 lb). Females stand 25–36 cm (9.8–14.2 in) and weigh 5–10 kg (11–22 lb).

Is a Cockapoo a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 7.9 kg (17 lb), the Cockapoo counts as a small dog breed on this site (small = 5–10 kg (11–22 lb) on average). It is taller than 19% of the 256 breeds listed.

How long does a Cockapoo live?

Typical life expectancy is 13–18 years, longer than the 13.7-year average for small breeds.

Can a Cockapoo fly in the airplane cabin?

Sometimes. Lighter individuals fit airline cabin limits of 8 kg (17.6 lb) for dog and carrier; heavier ones need an airline that weighs only the carrier size, or travel in the hold. Rules vary by airline, so check before booking.

Sizes are breed-standard ranges for adult dogs; individual dogs, working lines and dogs from different countries can be bigger or smaller. Airline notes are a general guide only. Check with your airline and your vet. Disclaimer.
