How big is a Norwegian Buhund?

The Norwegian Buhund is a medium-sized dog breed from Norway, in the Herding group. Males stand 43–47 cm (16.9–18.5 in) at the shoulder and weigh 14–18 kg (31–40 lb). Females stand 41–45 cm (15.9–17.5 in) and weigh 12–16 kg (26–35 lb). On average it is taller than 40% of the 247 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 41%. At the shoulder, an average male Norwegian Buhund reaches just below the knee on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 12–15 years, about average for medium breeds. It is too heavy for airlines that cap dog and carrier at 8 kg (17.6 lb), so it usually flies in the hold unless the airline only checks the carrier size.

Norwegian Buhund size: common questions

How big does a Norwegian Buhund get?

A fully grown male Norwegian Buhund stands 43–47 cm (16.9–18.5 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 14–18 kg (31–40 lb). Females stand 41–45 cm (15.9–17.5 in) and weigh 12–16 kg (26–35 lb).

Is a Norwegian Buhund a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 15 kg (33 lb), the Norwegian Buhund counts as a medium dog breed on this site (medium = 10–25 kg (22–55 lb) on average). It is taller than 40% of the 247 breeds listed.

How long does a Norwegian Buhund live?

Typical life expectancy is 12–15 years, about average for medium breeds.

Can a Norwegian Buhund fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Norwegian Buhund weighs 12–18 kg (26–40 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).
