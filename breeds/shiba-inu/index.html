How big is a Shiba Inu?

The Shiba Inu is a small-sized dog breed from Japan, in the Non-Sporting group. Males stand 37–42 cm (14.6–16.5 in) at the shoulder and weigh 11 kg (23 lb). Females stand 35–40 cm (13.6–15.6 in) and weigh 7.7 kg (17 lb). On average it is taller than 33% of the 267 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 26%.

Shiba Inu compared to a human

Next to a 175 cm (5′9″) adult, an average male Shiba Inu stands 40 cm (15.6 in) at the shoulder: 23% of the person's height, reaching just below the knee. An average female, at 37 cm (14.6 in), reaches just below the knee. In feet, males stand 1 ft 3 in to 1 ft 5 in tall at the shoulder and females 1 ft 2 in to 1 ft 4 in. Next to a 120 cm (3′11″) child, about seven years old, the shoulder comes to about knee height. The head sits higher than the shoulder, so the dog looks taller than these numbers suggest.

Typical life expectancy is 13–16 years, longer than the 13.7-year average for small breeds. It is too heavy for airlines that cap dog and carrier at 8 kg (17.6 lb), so it usually flies in the hold unless the airline only checks the carrier size.

About the Shiba Inu

The Shiba Inu is the smallest of Japan's six native spitz breeds, an ancient hunting dog used to flush birds and small game in the mountains. It was declared a natural monument of Japan in 1936. During and after the Second World War, bombing, food shortages and a distemper epidemic nearly wiped it out. The modern breed was rebuilt from the few surviving bloodlines. Today it is the most popular dog breed in Japan and well known worldwide, partly thanks to internet fame as the "Doge".

What a Shiba's size means at home

A male Shiba stands about 37–42 cm at the shoulder, just below an adult's knee, and weighs around 10.5 kg (23 lb). Females are about 35–40 cm and 7.5–8 kg (17 lb). It is compact, muscular and fox-like, a good size for flats and city life as long as it gets daily exercise. It usually needs a 30″ (76 cm) crate. Check with the crate size calculator. A Shiba is too heavy for most airline cabins.

Shiba vs Akita

The Akita Inu looks like a much larger Shiba: it is about 25 cm taller and roughly three times as heavy. Both share the curled tail, pricked ears and independent nature of the Japanese spitz breeds. The Shiba is about the same height as a Boston Terrier, but a very different kind of dog.

Temperament: independent and proud

Shibas are clean, quiet indoors and loyal, but they're famously independent, more like a cat than a Labrador. They don't always come when called, and their strong prey drive means many can't be trusted off the lead. They're also escape artists. Shibas can be reserved with strangers and possessive of food or toys, so early socialisation and patient, reward-based training matter. When they're unhappy, some produce a loud, high-pitched "Shiba scream".

Coat and care

The Shiba's double coat sheds heavily, especially when it "blows" its undercoat once or twice a year. Expect clouds of fur, and brush daily during those weeks. The coat comes in red, black and tan, and sesame, all with cream markings (urajiro).

Health

Shibas are generally healthy and long-lived, often 13–16 years. Known problems include allergies, loose kneecaps, eye diseases such as glaucoma, and GM1 gangliosidosis, a rare inherited nerve disease for which a DNA test exists.

Shiba Inu size: common questions

How big does a Shiba Inu get?

A fully grown male Shiba Inu stands 37–42 cm (14.6–16.5 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 11 kg (23 lb). Females stand 35–40 cm (13.6–15.6 in) and weigh 7.7 kg (17 lb).

How tall is a Shiba Inu in feet?

A male Shiba Inu stands 1 ft 3 in to 1 ft 5 in tall at the shoulder (37–42 cm) and a female 1 ft 2 in to 1 ft 4 in (35–40 cm). Next to a 5′9″ adult, an average male reaches just below the knee.

Is a Shiba Inu a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 9.1 kg (20 lb), the Shiba Inu counts as a small dog breed on this site (small = 5–10 kg (11–22 lb) on average). It is taller than 33% of the 267 breeds listed.

How long does a Shiba Inu live?

Typical life expectancy is 13–16 years, longer than the 13.7-year average for small breeds.

Can a Shiba Inu fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Shiba Inu weighs 7.7–11 kg (17–23 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).
