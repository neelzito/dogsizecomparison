How big is a Patterdale Terrier?

The Patterdale Terrier is a small-sized dog breed from England, in the Terrier group. Males stand 26–38 cm (10–15 in) at the shoulder and weigh 5–6.5 kg (11–14 lb). Females stand 26–38 cm (10–15 in) and weigh 5–6 kg (11–13 lb). On average it is taller than 21% of the 268 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 12%.

About the Patterdale Terrier

The Patterdale Terrier is a working terrier from the Lake District in north-west England, named after the village of Patterdale near Ullswater. Farmers and huntsmen bred it in the 20th century to tackle foxes in rocky fell country, where it had to work underground and stand up to tough quarry. It's bred for working ability rather than looks, and The Kennel Club doesn't recognise it. Breed clubs such as the Patterdale Terrier Club of America and the UKC keep standards.

What a Patterdale's size means at home

Patterdales typically stand about 25.5–38 cm at the shoulder, around mid-shin on an adult, and weigh about 5–6.5 kg (11–14 lb). Their narrow chest lets them squeeze into fox earths. A Patterdale usually needs a 24″ (61 cm) crate. Check with the crate size calculator. It fits the airline cabin weight limit. Check with the airline cabin check.

Patterdale vs Border Terrier

The Border Terrier is a little taller and heavier and usually calmer. Patterdales are often black, red or chocolate, with a smooth, broken or rough coat.

A working terrier

Patterdales are intense, energetic and fearless, with a very strong prey drive. They need a lot of exercise and mental work, and a very secure garden. They can be scrappy with other dogs and aren't usually safe with small pets. With an active, experienced owner, they're loyal and affectionate.

Care and health

The coat is easy to care for. Patterdales are generally hardy. Known problems include eye conditions and skin allergies. They often live 13–15 years.

Patterdale Terrier size: common questions

How big does a Patterdale Terrier get?

A fully grown male Patterdale Terrier stands 26–38 cm (10–15 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 5–6.5 kg (11–14 lb). Females stand 26–38 cm (10–15 in) and weigh 5–6 kg (11–13 lb).

How tall is a Patterdale Terrier in feet?

A male Patterdale Terrier stands 10 in to 1 ft 3 in tall at the shoulder (26–38 cm) and a female 10 in to 1 ft 3 in (26–38 cm). Next to a 5′9″ adult, an average male reaches about mid-shin.

Is a Patterdale Terrier a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 5.6 kg (12 lb), the Patterdale Terrier counts as a small dog breed on this site (small = 5–10 kg (11–22 lb) on average). It is taller than 21% of the 268 breeds listed.

How long does a Patterdale Terrier live?

Typical life expectancy is 13–15 years, about average for small breeds.

Can a Patterdale Terrier fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually yes. At 5–6.5 kg (11–14 lb), it fits even the strictest airline cabin rule, where dog and carrier together may weigh 8 kg (17.6 lb). Check your airline's rules and carrier size limits before booking.
