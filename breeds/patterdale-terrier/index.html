How big is a Patterdale Terrier?

The Patterdale Terrier is a small-sized dog breed from England, in the Terrier group. Males stand 26–38 cm (10–15 in) at the shoulder and weigh 5–6.5 kg (11–14 lb). Females stand 26–38 cm (10–15 in) and weigh 5–6 kg (11–13 lb). On average it is taller than 22% of the 247 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 13%. At the shoulder, an average male Patterdale Terrier reaches about mid-shin on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 13–15 years, about average for small breeds. At 5–6.5 kg (11–14 lb), it fits even the strictest airline cabin rule, where dog and carrier together may weigh 8 kg (17.6 lb).

Patterdale Terrier size: common questions

How big does a Patterdale Terrier get?

A fully grown male Patterdale Terrier stands 26–38 cm (10–15 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 5–6.5 kg (11–14 lb). Females stand 26–38 cm (10–15 in) and weigh 5–6 kg (11–13 lb).

Is a Patterdale Terrier a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 5.6 kg (12 lb), the Patterdale Terrier counts as a small dog breed on this site (small = 5–10 kg (11–22 lb) on average). It is taller than 22% of the 247 breeds listed.

How long does a Patterdale Terrier live?

Typical life expectancy is 13–15 years, about average for small breeds.

Can a Patterdale Terrier fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually yes. At 5–6.5 kg (11–14 lb), it fits even the strictest airline cabin rule, where dog and carrier together may weigh 8 kg (17.6 lb). Check your airline's rules and carrier size limits before booking.
