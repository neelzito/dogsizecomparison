How big is an American Water Spaniel?

The American Water Spaniel is a medium-sized dog breed from the United States, in the Sporting group. Males stand 38–46 cm (15–17.9 in) at the shoulder and weigh 14–21 kg (30–45 lb). Females stand 38–46 cm (15–17.9 in) and weigh 12–18 kg (25–40 lb). On average it is taller than 36% of the 256 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 42%. At the shoulder, an average male American Water Spaniel reaches just below the knee on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 10–14 years, shorter than the 13.3-year average for medium breeds. It is too heavy for airlines that cap dog and carrier at 8 kg (17.6 lb), so it usually flies in the hold unless the airline only checks the carrier size.

About the American Water Spaniel

The American Water Spaniel was developed in the 19th century around the Great Lakes, especially in Wisconsin, where hunters needed a compact retriever that could work from small boats, flush birds and retrieve ducks from cold water. It's the state dog of Wisconsin. With its dense, curly liver-brown coat, it looks like a small Irish Water Spaniel, its likely ancestor.

What an AWS's size means at home

The AKC standard calls for 15–18 in (38–45.5 cm) at the shoulder, with weights of about 30–45 lb (13.5–20.5 kg) for males and 25–40 lb (11.5–18 kg) for females. An AWS reaches just below an adult's knee. It usually needs a 36″ (91 cm) crate. Check with the crate size calculator. It's too heavy for an airline cabin.

AWS vs Boykin and Sussex Spaniel

The Boykin Spaniel is about the same size and a little lighter. The Sussex Spaniel is shorter but heavier and longer-bodied.

Temperament and exercise

American Water Spaniels are eager, friendly and energetic, devoted to their family and sometimes reserved with strangers. They need plenty of daily exercise and love swimming and retrieving.

Coat and health

The curly, oily coat sheds moderately and needs weekly brushing. Known health problems include hip dysplasia and eye conditions. They often live 10–14 years.

Sources: AKC breed standard and breed history; American Water Spaniel Club.

American Water Spaniel size: common questions

How big does an American Water Spaniel get?

A fully grown male American Water Spaniel stands 38–46 cm (15–17.9 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 14–21 kg (30–45 lb). Females stand 38–46 cm (15–17.9 in) and weigh 12–18 kg (25–40 lb).

Is an American Water Spaniel a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 16 kg (35 lb), the American Water Spaniel counts as a medium dog breed on this site (medium = 10–25 kg (22–55 lb) on average). It is taller than 36% of the 256 breeds listed.

How long does an American Water Spaniel live?

Typical life expectancy is 10–14 years, shorter than the 13.3-year average for medium breeds.

Can an American Water Spaniel fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A American Water Spaniel weighs 12–21 kg (25–45 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).
