How big is a Portuguese Water Dog?

The Portuguese Water Dog is a medium-sized dog breed from Portugal, in the Working group. Males stand 51–59 cm (20.1–23 in) at the shoulder and weigh 19–27 kg (42–60 lb). Females stand 43–54 cm (16.9–21.1 in) and weigh 16–23 kg (35–50 lb). On average it is taller than 55% of the 247 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 52%. At the shoulder, an average male Portuguese Water Dog reaches about knee height on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 11–13 years, shorter than the 13.3-year average for medium breeds. It is too heavy for airlines that cap dog and carrier at 8 kg (17.6 lb), so it usually flies in the hold unless the airline only checks the carrier size.

Portuguese Water Dog size: common questions

How big does a Portuguese Water Dog get?

A fully grown male Portuguese Water Dog stands 51–59 cm (20.1–23 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 19–27 kg (42–60 lb). Females stand 43–54 cm (16.9–21.1 in) and weigh 16–23 kg (35–50 lb).

Is a Portuguese Water Dog a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 21 kg (47 lb), the Portuguese Water Dog counts as a medium dog breed on this site (medium = 10–25 kg (22–55 lb) on average). It is taller than 55% of the 247 breeds listed.

How long does a Portuguese Water Dog live?

Typical life expectancy is 11–13 years, shorter than the 13.3-year average for medium breeds.

Can a Portuguese Water Dog fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Portuguese Water Dog weighs 16–27 kg (35–60 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).

Sizes are breed-standard ranges for adult dogs; individual dogs, working lines and dogs from different countries can be bigger or smaller. Airline notes are a general guide only. Check with your airline and your vet. Disclaimer.
