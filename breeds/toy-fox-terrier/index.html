How big is a Toy Fox Terrier?

The Toy Fox Terrier is a toy dog breed from the United States, in the Toy group. Adults stand 22–29 cm (8.5–11.4 in) at the shoulder and weigh 1.8–4.1 kg (4–9 lb); the breed standard gives the same range for males and females. On average it is taller than 8% of the 267 breeds on this site, and it is the 8th lightest breed. At the shoulder, an average Toy Fox Terrier reaches about mid-shin on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 13–15 years, about average for toy breeds. At 1.8–4.1 kg (4–9 lb), it fits even the strictest airline cabin rule, where dog and carrier together may weigh 8 kg (17.6 lb).

Sizes follow the American Kennel Club (AKC) breed standard.

About the Toy Fox Terrier

The Toy Fox Terrier was developed in the US in the early 20th century by crossing small Smooth Fox Terriers with toy breeds such as the Chihuahua, the Italian Greyhound and the Manchester Terrier. The result was a tiny terrier that kept its ratting instinct and energy. It was a favourite on farms and later in circuses. The AKC recognised it in 2003.

What a Toy Fox Terrier's size means at home

The AKC standard calls for 8.5–11.5 in (21.5–29 cm) at the shoulder, and Toy Fox Terriers weigh about 2–4 kg (3.5–9 lb). One reaches around mid-shin on an adult. It suits any home and usually needs a 24″ (61 cm) crate. Check with the crate size calculator. It's ideal for cabin travel. Check with the airline cabin check.

Toy Fox Terrier vs Papillon and Rat Terrier

The Papillon is about the same size, but long-coated. The Rat Terrier is a bigger relative.

Temperament and exercise

Toy Fox Terriers are lively, clever and affectionate, with big terrier spirit. They need daily play and walks, and they learn tricks quickly. They feel the cold.

Care and health

The short, smooth coat needs almost no grooming. Known health problems include loose kneecaps and Legg-Calvé-Perthes disease. Toy Fox Terriers often live 13–15 years.

Sources: AKC breed standard and breed history; United Kennel Club breed standard.

Toy Fox Terrier size: common questions

How big does a Toy Fox Terrier get?

An adult Toy Fox Terrier stands 22–29 cm (8.5–11.4 in) tall at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 1.8–4.1 kg (4–9 lb).

Is a Toy Fox Terrier a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 2.9 kg (6.5 lb), the Toy Fox Terrier counts as a toy dog breed on this site (toy = under 5 kg (11 lb) on average). It is taller than 8% of the 267 breeds listed.

How long does a Toy Fox Terrier live?

Typical life expectancy is 13–15 years, about average for toy breeds.

Can a Toy Fox Terrier fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually yes. At 1.8–4.1 kg (4–9 lb), it fits even the strictest airline cabin rule, where dog and carrier together may weigh 8 kg (17.6 lb). Check your airline's rules and carrier size limits before booking.
