How big is an Anatolian Shepherd Dog?

The Anatolian Shepherd Dog is a giant-sized dog breed from Turkey, in the Working group. Males stand 74 cm (28.9 in) at the shoulder and weigh 50–68 kg (110–150 lb). Females stand 69 cm (27 in) and weigh 37–55 kg (80–120 lb). On average it is taller than 94% of the 266 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 94%. At the shoulder, an average male Anatolian Shepherd Dog reaches about mid-thigh on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 11–13 years, longer than the 10.1-year average for giant breeds.

About the Anatolian Shepherd Dog

The Anatolian Shepherd Dog is a livestock guardian from the Anatolian plateau of central Turkey, where shepherds have used large guardian dogs for thousands of years to protect sheep and goats from wolves, bears and jackals. It's bred to live with the flock, patrol large areas and make its own decisions. Since the 1990s, Anatolians and Kangals have been used in Namibia and other African countries to protect livestock from cheetahs, which reduces farmers' need to shoot the endangered cats.

Anatolian or Kangal?

The relationship between the Anatolian Shepherd and the Kangal Shepherd Dog is debated. In Turkey, the Kangal is seen as a distinct, pure breed, and the FCI renamed its Anatolian Shepherd standard "Kangal Shepherd Dog" in 2018. The AKC and some other clubs still recognise the Anatolian Shepherd as a breed, with a wider range of colours. In size, the two are very similar.

What an Anatolian's size means at home

The AKC standard sets minimum heights of 29 in (73.5 cm) for males and 27 in (68.5 cm) for females, with weights of about 50–68 kg (110–150 lb) for males and 36.5–54.5 kg (80–120 lb) for females. An Anatolian reaches roughly an adult's mid-thigh. It's tall, powerful and athletic, heavy enough to put it in the giant size class on this site. It needs a large vehicle, a 54″ (137 cm) crate (see the crate size calculator) and a large, securely fenced property.

Anatolian vs Great Pyrenees

The Great Pyrenees is a similar size, a little taller on average but usually lighter, with a long white coat. The Anatolian is short-coated, often fawn with a black mask.

A guardian, not a pet for everyone

Anatolians are calm, loyal and devoted to their family and animals, but suspicious of strangers and strongly protective. They're independent, bred to think for themselves, so training takes patience, and recall is often unreliable. They need early socialisation, an experienced owner, a secure fence and preferably a job, such as guarding livestock. They're often more active at night and may bark at perceived threats.

Care and health

The short double coat sheds heavily once or twice a year. Anatolians are generally hardy. Known problems include hip and elbow dysplasia, entropion, hypothyroidism and sensitivity to anaesthesia. They often live 11–13 years.

Anatolian Shepherd Dog size: common questions

How big does an Anatolian Shepherd Dog get?

A fully grown male Anatolian Shepherd Dog stands 74 cm (28.9 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 50–68 kg (110–150 lb). Females stand 69 cm (27 in) and weigh 37–55 kg (80–120 lb).

Is an Anatolian Shepherd Dog a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 52 kg (115 lb), the Anatolian Shepherd Dog counts as a giant dog breed on this site (giant = 45 kg (99 lb) and over on average). It is taller than 94% of the 266 breeds listed.

How long does an Anatolian Shepherd Dog live?

Typical life expectancy is 11–13 years, longer than the 10.1-year average for giant breeds.

Can an Anatolian Shepherd Dog fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Anatolian Shepherd Dog weighs 37–68 kg (80–150 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).
