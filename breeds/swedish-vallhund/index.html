How big is a Swedish Vallhund?

The Swedish Vallhund is a medium-sized dog breed from Sweden, in the Herding group. Males stand 32–35 cm (12.6–13.8 in) at the shoulder and weigh 9.1–16 kg (20–35 lb). Females stand 29–33 cm (11.4–12.8 in) and weigh 9.1–16 kg (20–35 lb). On average it is taller than 23% of the 267 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 36%. At the shoulder, an average male Swedish Vallhund reaches just below the knee on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 12–15 years, about average for medium breeds. It is too heavy for airlines that cap dog and carrier at 8 kg (17.6 lb), so it usually flies in the hold unless the airline only checks the carrier size.

About the Swedish Vallhund

The Swedish Vallhund is an ancient Swedish cattle dog, often called the "Viking dog", whose ancestors herded cattle on farms for over a thousand years. It's low, sturdy and agile, and it drives cattle by nipping at their heels. In the 1940s, Count Björn von Rosen helped save it from extinction. It looks a little like a Corgi with a wolf-grey coat, and some Vallhunds are born with a natural bobtail.

What a Vallhund's size means at home

The AKC standard calls for 12.5–13.5 in (32–34.5 cm) at the shoulder for males and 11.5–12.5 in (29–32 cm) for females, and Vallhunds weigh about 9–16 kg (20–35 lb). A Vallhund reaches just below an adult's knee. Because of its long body, it usually needs a 30″ (76 cm) crate. Check with the crate size calculator. It's too heavy for an airline cabin.

Vallhund vs Pembroke Corgi

The Pembroke Welsh Corgi is a little shorter and about the same weight. The two may share ancestry, but nobody knows which came first.

Temperament and exercise

Vallhunds are friendly, energetic and clever, alert watchdogs and vocal. They need daily exercise and mental work, and they excel at herding and agility.

Coat and health

The double coat sheds heavily once or twice a year. Known health problems include hip dysplasia and retinopathy. Vallhunds often live 12–15 years.

Swedish Vallhund size: common questions

How big does a Swedish Vallhund get?

A fully grown male Swedish Vallhund stands 32–35 cm (12.6–13.8 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 9.1–16 kg (20–35 lb). Females stand 29–33 cm (11.4–12.8 in) and weigh 9.1–16 kg (20–35 lb).

Is a Swedish Vallhund a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 13 kg (28 lb), the Swedish Vallhund counts as a medium dog breed on this site (medium = 10–25 kg (22–55 lb) on average). It is taller than 23% of the 267 breeds listed.

How long does a Swedish Vallhund live?

Typical life expectancy is 12–15 years, about average for medium breeds.

Can a Swedish Vallhund fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Swedish Vallhund weighs 9.1–16 kg (20–35 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).

Sizes are breed-standard ranges for adult dogs; individual dogs, working lines and dogs from different countries can be bigger or smaller. Airline notes are a general guide only. Check with your airline and your vet. Disclaimer.
