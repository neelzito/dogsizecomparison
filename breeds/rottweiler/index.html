How big is a Rottweiler?

The Rottweiler is a giant-sized dog breed from Germany, in the Working group. Males stand 61–69 cm (24–27 in) at the shoulder and weigh 43–61 kg (95–134 lb). Females stand 56–64 cm (22–25 in) and weigh 37–46 kg (80–100 lb). On average it is taller than 78% of the 266 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 91%. At the shoulder, an average male Rottweiler reaches about mid-thigh on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 9–10 years, about average for giant breeds.

About the Rottweiler

The Rottweiler takes its name from Rottweil, a market town in south-west Germany. There, for centuries, butchers and cattle dealers used powerful dogs to drive cattle to market and to guard their money on the way home. The dogs became known as Rottweiler Metzgerhunde (Rottweil butcher's dogs). The breed is often said to go back to the drover dogs of Roman legions that crossed the Alps, but that story is hard to prove. When cattle droving ended with the railways, the breed nearly disappeared. It was saved in the early 20th century as a police and guard dog.

What a Rottweiler's size means at home

A male Rottweiler stands about 61–69 cm at the shoulder, roughly an adult's mid-thigh, and weighs 43–61 kg (95–135 lb). That's heavy enough to put it in the giant size class on this site, even though it isn't especially tall. Females are smaller and much lighter, at about 36–46 kg (80–100 lb). A Rottweiler needs a big boot, a 48″ (122 cm) crate (see the crate size calculator) and a strong owner on the lead: at full weight, it can easily pull an adult off balance.

Rottweiler vs Doberman

These two German guard breeds are often compared. The Doberman is a little taller but much leaner and lighter. A Rottweiler is broad-chested, heavy-boned and muscular, built for strength rather than speed.

Temperament and training

A well-bred Rottweiler is calm, confident and loyal, often affectionate and playful with its family and naturally watchful with strangers. Because of its size and strength, training and socialisation matter more than in most breeds. Start early, use consistent, reward-based methods, and introduce it to many people, dogs and places while it's young. In some countries and regions, Rottweilers are subject to special rules, such as a licence, liability insurance or muzzle requirements. Check local laws before you get one.

Growth and health

Rottweilers grow fast but mature slowly. They reach adult height at around 12–18 months, but often don't fully fill out until 2–3 years. Feed a large-breed puppy food, keep puppies lean, and avoid hard exercise on young joints. Hip and elbow dysplasia, heart problems and bone cancer are known in the breed, and life expectancy is fairly short, around 9–10 years. Ask breeders for hip, elbow and heart test results for both parents.

Rottweiler size: common questions

How big does a Rottweiler get?

A fully grown male Rottweiler stands 61–69 cm (24–27 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 43–61 kg (95–134 lb). Females stand 56–64 cm (22–25 in) and weigh 37–46 kg (80–100 lb).

Is a Rottweiler a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 47 kg (103 lb), the Rottweiler counts as a giant dog breed on this site (giant = 45 kg (99 lb) and over on average). It is taller than 78% of the 266 breeds listed.

How long does a Rottweiler live?

Typical life expectancy is 9–10 years, about average for giant breeds.

Can a Rottweiler fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Rottweiler weighs 37–61 kg (80–134 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).

Sizes are breed-standard ranges for adult dogs; individual dogs, working lines and dogs from different countries can be bigger or smaller. Airline notes are a general guide only. Check with your airline and your vet. Disclaimer.
