How big is a Miniature Schnauzer?

The Miniature Schnauzer is a small-sized dog breed from Germany, in the Terrier group. Adults stand 31–36 cm (12–14 in) at the shoulder and weigh 5–9.1 kg (11–20 lb); the breed standard gives the same range for males and females. On average it is taller than 23% of the 247 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 18%. At the shoulder, an average Miniature Schnauzer reaches about mid-shin on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 12–15 years, about average for small breeds. Lighter individuals fit airline cabin limits of 8 kg (17.6 lb) for dog and carrier; heavier ones need an airline that weighs only the carrier size, or travel in the hold.

Miniature Schnauzer size: common questions

How big does a Miniature Schnauzer get?

An adult Miniature Schnauzer stands 31–36 cm (12–14 in) tall at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 5–9.1 kg (11–20 lb).

Is a Miniature Schnauzer a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 7.1 kg (16 lb), the Miniature Schnauzer counts as a small dog breed on this site (small = 5–10 kg (11–22 lb) on average). It is taller than 23% of the 247 breeds listed.

How long does a Miniature Schnauzer live?

Typical life expectancy is 12–15 years, about average for small breeds.

Can a Miniature Schnauzer fly in the airplane cabin?

Sometimes. Lighter individuals fit airline cabin limits of 8 kg (17.6 lb) for dog and carrier; heavier ones need an airline that weighs only the carrier size, or travel in the hold. Rules vary by airline, so check before booking.
