How big is a Large Münsterländer?

The Large Münsterländer is a large-sized dog breed from Germany, in the Sporting group. Adults stand 59–68 cm (23–26.6 in) at the shoulder and weigh 23–32 kg (50–71 lb); the breed standard gives the same range for males and females. On average it is taller than 78% of the 247 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 68%. At the shoulder, an average Large Münsterländer reaches about mid-thigh on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 12–14 years, longer than the 12.1-year average for large breeds.

Large Münsterländer size: common questions

How big does a Large Münsterländer get?

An adult Large Münsterländer stands 59–68 cm (23–26.6 in) tall at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 23–32 kg (50–71 lb).

Is a Large Münsterländer a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 27 kg (60 lb), the Large Münsterländer counts as a large dog breed on this site (large = 25–45 kg (55–99 lb) on average). It is taller than 78% of the 247 breeds listed.

How long does a Large Münsterländer live?

Typical life expectancy is 12–14 years, longer than the 12.1-year average for large breeds.

Can a Large Münsterländer fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Large Münsterländer weighs 23–32 kg (50–71 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).

Sizes are breed-standard ranges for adult dogs; individual dogs, working lines and dogs from different countries can be bigger or smaller. Airline notes are a general guide only. Check with your airline and your vet. Disclaimer.
