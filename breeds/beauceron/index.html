How big is a Beauceron?

The Beauceron is a large-sized dog breed from France, in the Herding group. Males stand 65–70 cm (25.6–27.6 in) at the shoulder and weigh 32–50 kg (71–110 lb). Females stand 61–68 cm (24–26.6 in) and weigh 32–50 kg (71–110 lb). On average it is taller than 87% of the 256 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 87%. At the shoulder, an average male Beauceron reaches about mid-thigh on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult.

Typical life expectancy is 10–12 years, shorter than the 12.2-year average for large breeds.

About the Beauceron

The Beauceron (Berger de Beauce) is France's largest herding dog, from the plains around Paris. For centuries it herded and guarded flocks of sheep and cattle, and it later served the French army as a messenger and guard dog in both World Wars. In France it's nicknamed "bas-rouge" (red stockings) after the tan markings on its legs. One of its unusual features is double dewclaws on the hind legs, which the breed standard requires.

What a Beauceron's size means at home

The AKC standard calls for 25.5–27.5 in (65–70 cm) at the shoulder for males and 24–26.5 in (61–67.5 cm) for females. Beaucerons typically weigh about 32–50 kg (70–110 lb). A Beauceron reaches roughly an adult's mid-thigh. It's powerful, muscular and athletic. It needs a large vehicle and, because of its height, the crate size calculator suggests a 54″ (137 cm) crate for an average male.

Beauceron vs Bouvier and Doberman

The Bouvier des Flandres is about the same size but shaggy-coated. With its black-and-tan coat, a Beauceron is often mistaken for a Doberman or a Rottweiler mix, but it's an older breed with a heavier, longer coat and a herding background.

Temperament and training

Beaucerons are intelligent, confident and loyal, devoted to their family and protective of their home. They're wary of strangers and can be strong-willed, especially as adolescents, so they need early socialisation and consistent, confident training. They mature slowly, often not until 3 years old. They need a lot of daily exercise and mental work, and they excel at herding, obedience, tracking and protection sports. They're best with experienced, active owners.

Coat and health

The short, dense double coat sheds seasonally and needs only weekly brushing. Beaucerons come in black and tan, or harlequin (grey, black and tan). Known health problems include hip dysplasia, bloat (as in other deep-chested breeds) and eye conditions. Beaucerons often live 10–12 years.

Sources: AKC breed standard and breed history; FCI standard No. 44 (Berger de Beauce); American Beauceron Club.

Beauceron size: common questions

How big does a Beauceron get?

A fully grown male Beauceron stands 65–70 cm (25.6–27.6 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 32–50 kg (71–110 lb). Females stand 61–68 cm (24–26.6 in) and weigh 32–50 kg (71–110 lb).

Is a Beauceron a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 41 kg (90 lb), the Beauceron counts as a large dog breed on this site (large = 25–45 kg (55–99 lb) on average). It is taller than 87% of the 256 breeds listed.

How long does a Beauceron live?

Typical life expectancy is 10–12 years, shorter than the 12.2-year average for large breeds.

Can a Beauceron fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Beauceron weighs 32–50 kg (71–110 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).

Sizes are breed-standard ranges for adult dogs; individual dogs, working lines and dogs from different countries can be bigger or smaller. Airline notes are a general guide only. Check with your airline and your vet. Disclaimer.
