How big is a Finnish Spitz?

The Finnish Spitz is a medium-sized dog breed from Finland, in the Non-Sporting group. Males stand 45–51 cm (17.5–20.1 in) at the shoulder and weigh 12–15 kg (25–33 lb). Females stand 40–46 cm (15.6–17.9 in) and weigh 9.1–13 kg (20–28 lb). On average it is taller than 43% of the 268 breeds on this site, and it is heavier than 34%.

Finnish Spitz compared to a human

Next to a 175 cm (5′9″) adult, an average male Finnish Spitz stands 48 cm (18.8 in) at the shoulder: 27% of the person's height, reaching about knee height. An average female, at 43 cm (16.7 in), reaches just below the knee. In feet, males stand 1 ft 6 in to 1 ft 8 in tall at the shoulder and females 1 ft 4 in to 1 ft 6 in. Next to a 120 cm (3′11″) child, about seven years old, the shoulder comes to about mid-thigh. The head sits higher than the shoulder, so the dog looks taller than these numbers suggest.

Typical life expectancy is 13–15 years, longer than the 13.3-year average for medium breeds. It is too heavy for airlines that cap dog and carrier at 8 kg (17.6 lb), so it usually flies in the hold unless the airline only checks the carrier size.

About the Finnish Spitz

The Finnish Spitz is the national dog of Finland, an ancient hunting breed used to find game birds such as capercaillie and black grouse. When it spots a bird in a tree, it barks continuously, in a rhythmic yodelling voice, to hold the bird's attention while the hunter approaches. In Finland, Finnish Spitzes compete in barking contests, and the winner is crowned "King of Barkers". With its fox-red coat, pointed ears and curled tail, it looks very fox-like.

What a Finnish Spitz's size means at home

The AKC standard calls for 17.5–20 in (44.5–51 cm) at the shoulder for males and 15.5–18 in (39.5–45.5 cm) for females. Males weigh about 11.5–15 kg (25–33 lb), females about 9–12.5 kg (20–28 lb). A Finnish Spitz reaches roughly an adult's knee. It's square, lean and agile. It usually needs a 36″ (91 cm) crate. Check with the crate size calculator. It's too heavy for an airline cabin.

Finnish Spitz vs American Eskimo and Shiba

The American Eskimo Dog is a little shorter and about the same weight, and always white. The Shiba Inu looks similar but is smaller.

Temperament and exercise

Finnish Spitzes are lively, friendly and independent, good with children and usually other dogs. They are very vocal, which may not suit flats or close neighbours. They need plenty of daily exercise. They're clever but easily bored, so training should be varied and fun.

Coat and health

The double coat sheds heavily once or twice a year and needs weekly brushing. Finnish Spitzes are generally healthy. Known problems include hip dysplasia, epilepsy and diabetes. They often live 13–15 years.

Sources: AKC breed standard and breed history; FCI standard No. 49 (Suomenpystykorva); Finnish Spitz Club of America.

Finnish Spitz size: common questions

How big does a Finnish Spitz get?

A fully grown male Finnish Spitz stands 45–51 cm (17.5–20.1 in) at the shoulder (withers) and weighs 12–15 kg (25–33 lb). Females stand 40–46 cm (15.6–17.9 in) and weigh 9.1–13 kg (20–28 lb).

How tall is a Finnish Spitz in feet?

A male Finnish Spitz stands 1 ft 6 in to 1 ft 8 in tall at the shoulder (45–51 cm) and a female 1 ft 4 in to 1 ft 6 in (40–46 cm). Next to a 5′9″ adult, an average male reaches about knee height.

Is a Finnish Spitz a small, medium or large dog?

With an average weight of about 12 kg (27 lb), the Finnish Spitz counts as a medium dog breed on this site (medium = 10–25 kg (22–55 lb) on average). It is taller than 43% of the 268 breeds listed.

How long does a Finnish Spitz live?

Typical life expectancy is 13–15 years, longer than the 13.3-year average for medium breeds.

Can a Finnish Spitz fly in the airplane cabin?

Usually not. A Finnish Spitz weighs 9.1–15 kg (20–33 lb), well over the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit many airlines set for dog and carrier, and is too big for an under-seat carrier. Most airlines fly dogs this size in the hold (cargo).
