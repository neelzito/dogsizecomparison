      Breed library

      Figures come from the American Kennel Club breed pages (226 breeds), with FCI, UKC and breed-club standards for breeds the AKC doesn’t list. Where a standard gives a single value (e.g. “27 inches”) the range shows that one value; where it gives none (e.g. “females slightly smaller”) a typical figure is shown and marked ⚠ in the table. Designer/mixed breeds have no standard and their ranges are typical. Individual dogs can fall outside every range shown here. Last checked November 2026.
