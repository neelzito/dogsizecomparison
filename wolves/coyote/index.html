How big is a coyote?

Males stand 58–66 cm (22.8–26 in) at the shoulder and weigh 8–20 kg (18–44 lb). Females stand 55–63 cm (21.7–24.8 in) and weigh 7–18 kg (15–40 lb). An average male is taller than 71% of the 268 dog breeds on this site and heavier than 37% of them.

A smaller, lighter cousin of the wolf that has spread across almost all of North America and now lives in many cities. Size varies a lot by region; eastern coyotes are the largest.

Coyote compared to a human

Next to a 175 cm (5′9″) adult, an average male coyote stands 62 cm (24.4 in) at the shoulder: 35% of the person's height, reaching about mid-thigh. An average female, at 59 cm (23.2 in), reaches about mid-thigh. In feet, males stand 1 ft 11 in to 2 ft 2 in tall at the shoulder and females 1 ft 10 in to 2 ft 1 in. Next to a 120 cm (3′11″) child, about seven years old, the shoulder comes to about hip height.

Coyote vs dogs

An average male coyote, at 62 cm (24.4 in) and 14 kg (31 lb), is 9.8 cm (3.8 in) taller than an average male Whippet (52 cm, 15 kg) and weighs about the same. An average male coyote, at 62 cm (24.4 in) and 14 kg (31 lb), is about the same height as an average male German Shepherd Dog (64 cm, 35 kg) and is less than half as heavy.

Wild animals have no breed standard: these are typical adult ranges, and size varies a lot between regions and individuals. Coyote shoulder heights are typical published ranges. Source: Wikipedia, “Coyote”, which summarises published field studies.

Coyote size: common questions

How big is a coyote?

A male coyote stands 58–66 cm (22.8–26 in) at the shoulder and weighs 8–20 kg (18–44 lb); a female stands 55–63 cm (21.7–24.8 in) and weighs 7–18 kg (15–40 lb).

How big is a coyote compared to a human?

At 1 ft 11 in to 2 ft 2 in at the shoulder, an average male coyote reaches about mid-thigh on a 5′9″ (175 cm) adult. Its head sits higher than its shoulder, so it looks taller than this figure.

Which dog breed is the same size as a coyote?

By height and weight, the closest breeds are the Whippet, the Podengo Galego, the Silken Windhound. An average male coyote, at 62 cm (24.4 in) and 14 kg (31 lb), is 9.8 cm (3.8 in) taller than an average male Whippet (52 cm, 15 kg) and weighs about the same.

Is a coyote bigger than a German Shepherd?

An average male coyote, at 62 cm (24.4 in) and 14 kg (31 lb), is about the same height as an average male German Shepherd Dog (64 cm, 35 kg) and is less than half as heavy.
