How big is a wolf compared to a dog?

A gray wolf is bigger than most dogs. An average male Eurasian wolf stands 78 cm (30.5 in) at the shoulder and weighs 47 kg (103 lb), taller than 98% of the 268 dog breeds on this site. The Northwestern wolf of Canada and Alaska is bigger still, at 84 cm (32.9 in) and 55 kg (121 lb) for an average male.

Only the giant breeds are bigger. Only one breed is taller on average than a male Northwestern wolf: the Irish Wolfhound. 17 breeds are heavier, among them the Boerboel, the Mastiff (English), the Spanish Mastiff and the Pyrenean Mastiff. Wolves often look bigger than dogs of the same height because they have longer legs, a narrower chest and large feet.

Wolf size depends on where they live: wolves in the far north are the biggest, and wolves in deserts and hot grasslands, such as the Arabian and Indian wolf, are much smaller. The list below also includes other wolf species and wild relatives like the coyote, dingo and dhole.

Wolf vs dog: common questions

Is a wolf bigger than a German Shepherd?

Yes. An average male Eurasian wolf, at 78 cm (30.5 in) and 47 kg (103 lb), is 14 cm (5.5 in) taller than an average male German Shepherd Dog (64 cm, 35 kg) and is about 32% heavier.

Which dog breeds are bigger than a wolf?

Only giant breeds. Only the Irish Wolfhound is taller on average than a male Northwestern wolf. By weight, 17 breeds are heavier, among them the Boerboel, the Mastiff (English), the Spanish Mastiff and the Pyrenean Mastiff.

What is the biggest wolf?

Among living wolves, northern gray wolves such as the Northwestern wolf are the biggest: males stand 76–91 cm (29.9–35.8 in) and weigh 45–65 kg (99–143 lb). The maned wolf of South America has the tallest shoulders of any wild canid, at 85–95 cm (33.5–37.4 in), thanks to its long legs, but weighs only 20–30 kg (44–66 lb) and is not a true wolf.

What is the smallest wolf?

The Arabian wolf is the smallest gray wolf, at 64–66 cm (25.2–26 in) and about 20 kg. The African golden wolf is smaller still, at about 40 cm and 7–15 kg (15–33 lb).

How big is a wolf compared to a human?

An average male Eurasian wolf reaches about hip height on a 175 cm (5′9″) adult, at 2 ft 4 in to 2 ft 9 in tall at the shoulder. A Northwestern wolf, at 84 cm (32.9 in), also reaches about hip height.
