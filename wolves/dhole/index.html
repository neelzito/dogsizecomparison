How big is a dhole?

Males stand 44–50 cm (17.3–19.7 in) at the shoulder and weigh 15–21 kg (33–46 lb). Females stand 42–48 cm (16.5–18.9 in) and weigh 10–17 kg (22–37 lb). An average male is taller than 43% of the 268 dog breeds on this site and heavier than 44% of them.

An endangered pack hunter that communicates with whistles. Dholes are compact and short-legged, a little taller than a Beagle and about as heavy as a Border Collie.

Dhole compared to a human

Next to a 175 cm (5′9″) adult, an average male dhole stands 47 cm (18.5 in) at the shoulder: 27% of the person's height, reaching about knee height. An average female, at 45 cm (17.7 in), reaches just below the knee. In feet, males stand 1 ft 5 in to 1 ft 8 in tall at the shoulder and females 1 ft 5 in to 1 ft 7 in. Next to a 120 cm (3′11″) child, about seven years old, the shoulder comes to about mid-thigh.

Dhole vs dogs

An average male dhole, at 47 cm (18.5 in) and 18 kg (40 lb), is about the same height as an average male Soft Coated Wheaten Terrier (47 cm, 17 kg) and weighs about the same. An average male dhole, at 47 cm (18.5 in) and 18 kg (40 lb), is 16.5 cm (6.5 in) shorter than an average male German Shepherd Dog (64 cm, 35 kg) and is about 49% lighter.

Wild animals have no breed standard: these are typical adult ranges, and size varies a lot between regions and individuals. Source: Wikipedia, “Dhole”, which summarises published field studies.

Dhole size: common questions

How big is a dhole?

A male dhole stands 44–50 cm (17.3–19.7 in) at the shoulder and weighs 15–21 kg (33–46 lb); a female stands 42–48 cm (16.5–18.9 in) and weighs 10–17 kg (22–37 lb).

How big is a dhole compared to a human?

At 1 ft 5 in to 1 ft 8 in at the shoulder, an average male dhole reaches about knee height on a 5′9″ (175 cm) adult. Its head sits higher than its shoulder, so it looks taller than this figure.

Which dog breed is the same size as a dhole?

By height and weight, the closest breeds are the Soft Coated Wheaten Terrier, the Kerry Blue Terrier, the German Pinscher. An average male dhole, at 47 cm (18.5 in) and 18 kg (40 lb), is about the same height as an average male Soft Coated Wheaten Terrier (47 cm, 17 kg) and weighs about the same.

Is a dhole bigger than a German Shepherd?

An average male dhole, at 47 cm (18.5 in) and 18 kg (40 lb), is 16.5 cm (6.5 in) shorter than an average male German Shepherd Dog (64 cm, 35 kg) and is about 49% lighter.
