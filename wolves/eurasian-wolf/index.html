How big is a Eurasian wolf?

Males stand 70–85 cm (27.6–33.5 in) at the shoulder and weigh 38–55 kg (84–121 lb). Females stand 65–80 cm (25.6–31.5 in) and weigh 30–45 kg (66–99 lb). An average male is taller than 98% of the 268 dog breeds on this site and heavier than 89% of them.

The most widespread gray wolf and the largest in the Old World, averaging about 39 kg in Europe. It is the wolf that has returned to Germany, Poland, France and the Alps.

Eurasian Wolf compared to a human

Next to a 175 cm (5′9″) adult, an average male Eurasian wolf stands 78 cm (30.5 in) at the shoulder: 44% of the person's height, reaching about hip height. An average female, at 73 cm (28.5 in), reaches about mid-thigh. In feet, males stand 2 ft 4 in to 2 ft 9 in tall at the shoulder and females 2 ft 2 in to 2 ft 7 in. Next to a 120 cm (3′11″) child, about seven years old, the shoulder comes to about waist height.

Eurasian Wolf vs dogs

An average male Eurasian wolf, at 78 cm (30.5 in) and 47 kg (103 lb), is about the same height as an average male Scottish Deerhound (79 cm, 44 kg) and weighs about the same. An average male Eurasian wolf, at 78 cm (30.5 in) and 47 kg (103 lb), is 14 cm (5.5 in) taller than an average male German Shepherd Dog (64 cm, 35 kg) and is about 32% heavier.

Wild animals have no breed standard: these are typical adult ranges, and size varies a lot between regions and individuals. Source: Wikipedia, “Eurasian wolf”, which summarises published field studies.

Eurasian Wolf size: common questions

How big is a Eurasian wolf?

A male Eurasian wolf stands 70–85 cm (27.6–33.5 in) at the shoulder and weighs 38–55 kg (84–121 lb); a female stands 65–80 cm (25.6–31.5 in) and weighs 30–45 kg (66–99 lb).

How big is a Eurasian wolf compared to a human?

At 2 ft 4 in to 2 ft 9 in at the shoulder, an average male Eurasian wolf reaches about hip height on a 5′9″ (175 cm) adult. Its head sits higher than its shoulder, so it looks taller than this figure.

Which dog breed is the same size as a Eurasian wolf?

By height and weight, the closest breeds are the Scottish Deerhound, the Kuvasz, the Borzoi. An average male Eurasian wolf, at 78 cm (30.5 in) and 47 kg (103 lb), is about the same height as an average male Scottish Deerhound (79 cm, 44 kg) and weighs about the same.

Is a Eurasian wolf bigger than a German Shepherd?

An average male Eurasian wolf, at 78 cm (30.5 in) and 47 kg (103 lb), is 14 cm (5.5 in) taller than an average male German Shepherd Dog (64 cm, 35 kg) and is about 32% heavier.
