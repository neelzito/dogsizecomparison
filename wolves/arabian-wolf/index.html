How big is an Arabian wolf?

Males stand 64–66 cm (25.2–26 in) at the shoulder and weigh 18–22 kg (40–49 lb). Females stand 62–64 cm (24.4–25.2 in) and weigh 16–20 kg (35–44 lb). An average male is taller than 82% of the 268 dog breeds on this site and heavier than 49% of them.

The smallest gray wolf, at about 20 kg. Its large ears and thin coat help it shed heat in the desert.

Arabian Wolf compared to a human

Next to a 175 cm (5′9″) adult, an average male Arabian wolf stands 65 cm (25.6 in) at the shoulder: 37% of the person's height, reaching about mid-thigh. An average female, at 63 cm (24.8 in), reaches about mid-thigh. In feet, males stand 2 ft 1 in to 2 ft 2 in tall at the shoulder and females 2 ft to 2 ft 1 in. Next to a 120 cm (3′11″) child, about seven years old, the shoulder comes to about hip height.

Arabian Wolf vs dogs

An average male Arabian wolf, at 65 cm (25.6 in) and 20 kg (44 lb), is 3.5 cm (1.4 in) shorter than an average male Azawakh (69 cm, 23 kg) and is about 11% lighter. An average male Arabian wolf, at 65 cm (25.6 in) and 20 kg (44 lb), is about the same height as an average male German Shepherd Dog (64 cm, 35 kg) and is about 43% lighter.

Wild animals have no breed standard: these are typical adult ranges, and size varies a lot between regions and individuals. Source: Wikipedia, “Arabian wolf”, which summarises published field studies.

Arabian Wolf size: common questions

How big is an Arabian wolf?

A male Arabian wolf stands 64–66 cm (25.2–26 in) at the shoulder and weighs 18–22 kg (40–49 lb); a female stands 62–64 cm (24.4–25.2 in) and weighs 16–20 kg (35–44 lb).

How big is an Arabian wolf compared to a human?

At 2 ft 1 in to 2 ft 2 in at the shoulder, an average male Arabian wolf reaches about mid-thigh on a 5′9″ (175 cm) adult. Its head sits higher than its shoulder, so it looks taller than this figure.

Which dog breed is the same size as an Arabian wolf?

By height and weight, the closest breeds are the Azawakh, the Ibizan Hound, the Irish Red and White Setter. An average male Arabian wolf, at 65 cm (25.6 in) and 20 kg (44 lb), is 3.5 cm (1.4 in) shorter than an average male Azawakh (69 cm, 23 kg) and is about 11% lighter.

Is an Arabian wolf bigger than a German Shepherd?

An average male Arabian wolf, at 65 cm (25.6 in) and 20 kg (44 lb), is about the same height as an average male German Shepherd Dog (64 cm, 35 kg) and is about 43% lighter.
