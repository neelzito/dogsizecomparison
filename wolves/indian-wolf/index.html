How big is an Indian wolf?

Males stand 62–72 cm (24.4–28.3 in) at the shoulder and weigh 19–25 kg (42–55 lb). Females stand 57–67 cm (22.4–26.4 in) and weigh 17–22 kg (37–49 lb). An average male is taller than 87% of the 268 dog breeds on this site and heavier than 52% of them.

A small, slender wolf with a short coat suited to hot grassland and scrub. Males weigh about 19–25 kg, about as much as a male Siberian Husky.

Indian Wolf compared to a human

Next to a 175 cm (5′9″) adult, an average male Indian wolf stands 67 cm (26.4 in) at the shoulder: 38% of the person's height, reaching about mid-thigh. An average female, at 62 cm (24.4 in), reaches about mid-thigh. In feet, males stand 2 ft to 2 ft 4 in tall at the shoulder and females 1 ft 10 in to 2 ft 2 in. Next to a 120 cm (3′11″) child, about seven years old, the shoulder comes to about waist height.

Indian Wolf vs dogs

An average male Indian wolf, at 67 cm (26.4 in) and 22 kg (49 lb), is 2.3 cm (0.9 in) taller than an average male Ibizan Hound (65 cm, 23 kg) and weighs about the same. An average male Indian wolf, at 67 cm (26.4 in) and 22 kg (49 lb), is 3.5 cm (1.4 in) taller than an average male German Shepherd Dog (64 cm, 35 kg) and is about 38% lighter.

Wild animals have no breed standard: these are typical adult ranges, and size varies a lot between regions and individuals. Source: Wikipedia, “Indian wolf”, which summarises published field studies.

Indian Wolf size: common questions

How big is an Indian wolf?

A male Indian wolf stands 62–72 cm (24.4–28.3 in) at the shoulder and weighs 19–25 kg (42–55 lb); a female stands 57–67 cm (22.4–26.4 in) and weighs 17–22 kg (37–49 lb).

How big is an Indian wolf compared to a human?

At 2 ft to 2 ft 4 in at the shoulder, an average male Indian wolf reaches about mid-thigh on a 5′9″ (175 cm) adult. Its head sits higher than its shoulder, so it looks taller than this figure.

Which dog breed is the same size as an Indian wolf?

By height and weight, the closest breeds are the Ibizan Hound, the Azawakh, the Irish Red and White Setter. An average male Indian wolf, at 67 cm (26.4 in) and 22 kg (49 lb), is 2.3 cm (0.9 in) taller than an average male Ibizan Hound (65 cm, 23 kg) and weighs about the same.

Is an Indian wolf bigger than a German Shepherd?

An average male Indian wolf, at 67 cm (26.4 in) and 22 kg (49 lb), is 3.5 cm (1.4 in) taller than an average male German Shepherd Dog (64 cm, 35 kg) and is about 38% lighter.
