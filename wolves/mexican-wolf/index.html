How big is a Mexican wolf?

Males stand 72–81 cm (28.3–31.9 in) at the shoulder and weigh 27–36 kg (60–79 lb). Females stand 70–77 cm (27.6–30.3 in) and weigh 23–32 kg (51–71 lb). An average male is taller than 97% of the 268 dog breeds on this site and heavier than 76% of them.

The smallest gray wolf in North America. It was almost wiped out in the wild by the 1970s and has been released again from captive breeding since 1998.

Mexican Wolf compared to a human

Next to a 175 cm (5′9″) adult, an average male Mexican wolf stands 77 cm (30.1 in) at the shoulder: 44% of the person's height, reaching about hip height. An average female, at 74 cm (28.9 in), reaches about mid-thigh. In feet, males stand 2 ft 4 in to 2 ft 8 in tall at the shoulder and females 2 ft 4 in to 2 ft 6 in. Next to a 120 cm (3′11″) child, about seven years old, the shoulder comes to about waist height.

Mexican Wolf vs dogs

An average male Mexican wolf, at 77 cm (30.1 in) and 32 kg (69 lb), is 3 cm (1.2 in) taller than an average male Greyhound (74 cm, 31 kg) and weighs about the same. An average male Mexican wolf, at 77 cm (30.1 in) and 32 kg (69 lb), is 13 cm (5.1 in) taller than an average male German Shepherd Dog (64 cm, 35 kg) and is about 11% lighter.

Wild animals have no breed standard: these are typical adult ranges, and size varies a lot between regions and individuals. Source: Wikipedia, “Mexican wolf”, which summarises published field studies.

Mexican Wolf size: common questions

How big is a Mexican wolf?

A male Mexican wolf stands 72–81 cm (28.3–31.9 in) at the shoulder and weighs 27–36 kg (60–79 lb); a female stands 70–77 cm (27.6–30.3 in) and weighs 23–32 kg (51–71 lb).

How big is a Mexican wolf compared to a human?

At 2 ft 4 in to 2 ft 8 in at the shoulder, an average male Mexican wolf reaches about hip height on a 5′9″ (175 cm) adult. Its head sits higher than its shoulder, so it looks taller than this figure.

Which dog breed is the same size as a Mexican wolf?

By height and weight, the closest breeds are the Greyhound, the Irish Setter, the Borzoi. An average male Mexican wolf, at 77 cm (30.1 in) and 32 kg (69 lb), is 3 cm (1.2 in) taller than an average male Greyhound (74 cm, 31 kg) and weighs about the same.

Is a Mexican wolf bigger than a German Shepherd?

An average male Mexican wolf, at 77 cm (30.1 in) and 32 kg (69 lb), is 13 cm (5.1 in) taller than an average male German Shepherd Dog (64 cm, 35 kg) and is about 11% lighter.
