How big is a dingo?

Males stand 55–62 cm (21.7–24.4 in) at the shoulder and weigh 13–20 kg (29–44 lb). Females stand 52–59 cm (20.5–23.2 in) and weigh 12–17 kg (26–37 lb). An average male is taller than 63% of the 268 dog breeds on this site and heavier than 43% of them.

Australia's wild dog, descended from domestic dogs that arrived at least 3,500 years ago. Wild males average about 59 cm and 16 kg; captive dingoes are often heavier.

Dingo compared to a human

Next to a 175 cm (5′9″) adult, an average male dingo stands 59 cm (23 in) at the shoulder: 33% of the person's height, reaching about mid-thigh. An average female, at 56 cm (21.9 in), reaches about knee height. In feet, males stand 1 ft 10 in to 2 ft tall at the shoulder and females 1 ft 8 in to 1 ft 11 in. Next to a 120 cm (3′11″) child, about seven years old, the shoulder comes to about hip height.

Dingo vs dogs

An average male dingo, at 59 cm (23 in) and 17 kg (36 lb), is 6.3 cm (2.5 in) taller than an average male Whippet (52 cm, 15 kg) and is about 12% heavier. An average male dingo, at 59 cm (23 in) and 17 kg (36 lb), is 5 cm (2 in) shorter than an average male German Shepherd Dog (64 cm, 35 kg) and is less than half as heavy.

Wild animals have no breed standard: these are typical adult ranges, and size varies a lot between regions and individuals. Source: Wikipedia, “Dingo”, which summarises published field studies.

Dingo size: common questions

How big is a dingo?

A male dingo stands 55–62 cm (21.7–24.4 in) at the shoulder and weighs 13–20 kg (29–44 lb); a female stands 52–59 cm (20.5–23.2 in) and weighs 12–17 kg (26–37 lb).

How big is a dingo compared to a human?

At 1 ft 10 in to 2 ft at the shoulder, an average male dingo reaches about mid-thigh on a 5′9″ (175 cm) adult. Its head sits higher than its shoulder, so it looks taller than this figure.

Which dog breed is the same size as a dingo?

By height and weight, the closest breeds are the Whippet, the Silken Windhound, the Podenco Valenciano. An average male dingo, at 59 cm (23 in) and 17 kg (36 lb), is 6.3 cm (2.5 in) taller than an average male Whippet (52 cm, 15 kg) and is about 12% heavier.

Is a dingo bigger than a German Shepherd?

An average male dingo, at 59 cm (23 in) and 17 kg (36 lb), is 5 cm (2 in) shorter than an average male German Shepherd Dog (64 cm, 35 kg) and is less than half as heavy.
