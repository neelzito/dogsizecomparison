Maned Wolf size compared to dogs and humans

The maned wolf (Chrysocyon brachyurus) is a wild relative of the wolf from grasslands of Brazil, Paraguay, Bolivia and Argentina. Height, weight and how it measures up to dog breeds, drawn to scale.

How big is a maned wolf?

Adults stand 85–95 cm (33.5–37.4 in) at the shoulder and weigh 20–30 kg (44–66 lb). An average male is taller than 100% of the 268 dog breeds on this site and heavier than 60% of them.

Neither a wolf nor a fox but the only member of its own genus. Its very long legs make it the tallest wild canid, yet it weighs only about 23 kg and eats a lot of fruit.

Maned Wolf compared to a human

Next to a 175 cm (5′9″) adult, an average maned wolf stands 90 cm (35.4 in) at the shoulder: 51% of the person's height, reaching about hip height. In feet, adults stand 2 ft 9 in to 3 ft 1 in tall at the shoulder. Next to a 120 cm (3′11″) child, about seven years old, the shoulder comes to about waist height.

Maned Wolf vs dogs

An average male maned wolf, at 90 cm (35.4 in) and 25 kg (55 lb), is 24 cm (9.4 in) taller than an average male Afghan Hound (66 cm, 25 kg) and weighs about the same. An average male maned wolf, at 90 cm (35.4 in) and 25 kg (55 lb), is 26.5 cm (10.4 in) taller than an average male German Shepherd Dog (64 cm, 35 kg) and is about 29% lighter.

Wild animals have no breed standard: these are typical adult ranges, and size varies a lot between regions and individuals. Maned wolf shoulder heights are given as about 90 cm, with some sources citing up to 110 cm. Source: Wikipedia, “Maned wolf”, which summarises published field studies.

At 2 ft 9 in to 3 ft 1 in at the shoulder, an average maned wolf reaches about hip height on a 5′9″ (175 cm) adult. Its head sits higher than its shoulder, so it looks taller than this figure.
