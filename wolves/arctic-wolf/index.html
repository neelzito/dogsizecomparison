How big is an Arctic wolf?

Males stand 66–79 cm (26–31.1 in) at the shoulder and weigh 38–55 kg (84–121 lb). Females stand 63–74 cm (24.8–29.1 in) and weigh 32–45 kg (71–99 lb). An average male is taller than 94% of the 268 dog breeds on this site and heavier than 89% of them.

A white subspecies of the far north, a little shorter and stockier than wolves further south, with smaller ears and a shorter muzzle that lose less heat.

Arctic Wolf compared to a human

Next to a 175 cm (5′9″) adult, an average male Arctic wolf stands 73 cm (28.5 in) at the shoulder: 41% of the person's height, reaching about mid-thigh. An average female, at 69 cm (27 in), reaches about mid-thigh. In feet, males stand 2 ft 2 in to 2 ft 7 in tall at the shoulder and females 2 ft 1 in to 2 ft 5 in. Next to a 120 cm (3′11″) child, about seven years old, the shoulder comes to about waist height.

Arctic Wolf vs dogs

An average male Arctic wolf, at 73 cm (28.5 in) and 47 kg (103 lb), is about the same height as an average male Kuvasz (74 cm, 49 kg) and weighs about the same. An average male Arctic wolf, at 73 cm (28.5 in) and 47 kg (103 lb), is 9 cm (3.5 in) taller than an average male German Shepherd Dog (64 cm, 35 kg) and is about 32% heavier.

Wild animals have no breed standard: these are typical adult ranges, and size varies a lot between regions and individuals. Shoulder heights for the Arctic wolf are typical published ranges; few measured studies exist. Source: Wikipedia, “Arctic wolf”, which summarises published field studies.

Arctic Wolf size: common questions

How big is an Arctic wolf?

A male Arctic wolf stands 66–79 cm (26–31.1 in) at the shoulder and weighs 38–55 kg (84–121 lb); a female stands 63–74 cm (24.8–29.1 in) and weighs 32–45 kg (71–99 lb).

How big is an Arctic wolf compared to a human?

At 2 ft 2 in to 2 ft 7 in at the shoulder, an average male Arctic wolf reaches about mid-thigh on a 5′9″ (175 cm) adult. Its head sits higher than its shoulder, so it looks taller than this figure.

Which dog breed is the same size as an Arctic wolf?

By height and weight, the closest breeds are the Kuvasz, the Black Russian Terrier, the Komondor. An average male Arctic wolf, at 73 cm (28.5 in) and 47 kg (103 lb), is about the same height as an average male Kuvasz (74 cm, 49 kg) and weighs about the same.

Is an Arctic wolf bigger than a German Shepherd?

An average male Arctic wolf, at 73 cm (28.5 in) and 47 kg (103 lb), is 9 cm (3.5 in) taller than an average male German Shepherd Dog (64 cm, 35 kg) and is about 32% heavier.
