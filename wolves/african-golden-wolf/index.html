How big is an African golden wolf?

Adults stand 40–45 cm (15.7–17.7 in) at the shoulder and weigh 7–15 kg (15–33 lb). An average male is taller than 37% of the 268 dog breeds on this site and heavier than 31% of them.

Long thought to be a golden jackal until DNA studies in 2015 showed it is a wolf-like species of its own. It is the smallest animal on this page that is called a wolf.

African Golden Wolf compared to a human

Next to a 175 cm (5′9″) adult, an average African golden wolf stands 43 cm (16.7 in) at the shoulder: 24% of the person's height, reaching just below the knee. In feet, adults stand 1 ft 4 in to 1 ft 6 in tall at the shoulder. Next to a 120 cm (3′11″) child, about seven years old, the shoulder comes to about mid-thigh.

African Golden Wolf vs dogs

An average male African golden wolf, at 43 cm (16.7 in) and 11 kg (24 lb), is about the same height as an average male Mudi (43 cm, 11 kg) and weighs about the same. An average male African golden wolf, at 43 cm (16.7 in) and 11 kg (24 lb), is 21 cm (8.3 in) shorter than an average male German Shepherd Dog (64 cm, 35 kg) and is less than half as heavy.

Wild animals have no breed standard: these are typical adult ranges, and size varies a lot between regions and individuals. Published shoulder heights for the African golden wolf are given as about 40 cm. Source: Wikipedia, “African wolf”, which summarises published field studies.

African Golden Wolf size: common questions

How big is an African golden wolf?

An adult African golden wolf stands 40–45 cm (15.7–17.7 in) at the shoulder and weighs 7–15 kg (15–33 lb).

How big is an African golden wolf compared to a human?

At 1 ft 4 in to 1 ft 6 in at the shoulder, an average African golden wolf reaches just below the knee on a 5′9″ (175 cm) adult. Its head sits higher than its shoulder, so it looks taller than this figure.

Which dog breed is the same size as an African golden wolf?

By height and weight, the closest breeds are the Mudi, the Pumi, the Basenji. An average male African golden wolf, at 43 cm (16.7 in) and 11 kg (24 lb), is about the same height as an average male Mudi (43 cm, 11 kg) and weighs about the same.

Is an African golden wolf bigger than a German Shepherd?

An average male African golden wolf, at 43 cm (16.7 in) and 11 kg (24 lb), is 21 cm (8.3 in) shorter than an average male German Shepherd Dog (64 cm, 35 kg) and is less than half as heavy.
