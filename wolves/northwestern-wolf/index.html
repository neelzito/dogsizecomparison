Northwestern Wolf size compared to dogs and humans

How big is a Northwestern wolf?

Males stand 76–91 cm (29.9–35.8 in) at the shoulder and weigh 45–65 kg (99–143 lb). Females stand 70–86 cm (27.6–33.9 in) and weigh 36–50 kg (79–110 lb). An average male is taller than 99% of the 268 dog breeds on this site and heavier than 94% of them.

One of the largest gray wolf subspecies. Wolves of this subspecies were brought from Canada to Yellowstone National Park in 1995, where adult males average about 50 kg and females about 40 kg.

Northwestern Wolf compared to a human

Next to a 175 cm (5′9″) adult, an average male Northwestern wolf stands 84 cm (32.9 in) at the shoulder: 48% of the person's height, reaching about hip height. An average female, at 78 cm (30.7 in), reaches about hip height. In feet, males stand 2 ft 6 in to 3 ft tall at the shoulder and females 2 ft 4 in to 2 ft 10 in. Next to a 120 cm (3′11″) child, about seven years old, the shoulder comes to about waist height.

Northwestern Wolf vs dogs

An average male Northwestern wolf, at 84 cm (32.9 in) and 55 kg (121 lb), is 8.5 cm (3.3 in) taller than an average male Kangal Shepherd Dog (75 cm, 54 kg) and weighs about the same. An average male Northwestern wolf, at 84 cm (32.9 in) and 55 kg (121 lb), is 20 cm (7.9 in) taller than an average male German Shepherd Dog (64 cm, 35 kg) and is about 56% heavier.

Wild animals have no breed standard: these are typical adult ranges, and size varies a lot between regions and individuals. Source: Wikipedia, “Northwestern wolf”, which summarises published field studies.

Northwestern Wolf size: common questions

How big is a Northwestern wolf?

A male Northwestern wolf stands 76–91 cm (29.9–35.8 in) at the shoulder and weighs 45–65 kg (99–143 lb); a female stands 70–86 cm (27.6–33.9 in) and weighs 36–50 kg (79–110 lb).

How big is a Northwestern wolf compared to a human?

At 2 ft 6 in to 3 ft at the shoulder, an average male Northwestern wolf reaches about hip height on a 5′9″ (175 cm) adult. Its head sits higher than its shoulder, so it looks taller than this figure.

Which dog breed is the same size as a Northwestern wolf?

By height and weight, the closest breeds are the Kangal Shepherd Dog, the Irish Wolfhound, the Great Pyrenees. An average male Northwestern wolf, at 84 cm (32.9 in) and 55 kg (121 lb), is 8.5 cm (3.3 in) taller than an average male Kangal Shepherd Dog (75 cm, 54 kg) and weighs about the same.

Is a Northwestern wolf bigger than a German Shepherd?

An average male Northwestern wolf, at 84 cm (32.9 in) and 55 kg (121 lb), is 20 cm (7.9 in) taller than an average male German Shepherd Dog (64 cm, 35 kg) and is about 56% heavier.
