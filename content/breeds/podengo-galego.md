The Podengo Galego is the podenco of Galicia, in north-west Spain. It's found mostly in the provinces of Pontevedra and Ourense, where hunters use it to flush rabbits, hares and foxes out of dense gorse and heather. Like all Iberian podencos it shares a common origin with the Podenco Ibicenco, Podenco Andaluz, Portuguese Podengo and Pharaoh Hound, and it's closest to the medium Portuguese Podengo from just across the border. The Galician government (Xunta de Galicia) recognises it as a native breed with an official standard. The FCI does not recognise it.

### What a Podengo Galego's size means at home

The official standard gives a height of 46–52 cm at the shoulder for males and 42–46 cm for females, and a weight of 10–15 kg. That's a light, medium-sized dog that reaches about knee height on an adult. It's about the size of the medium [Podenco Andaluz](/breeds/podenco-andaluz-medium/), but more lightly built. It's too heavy for most airline cabins.

### Podengo Galego vs Podenco Andaluz and Portuguese Podengo

Next to a medium [Podenco Andaluz](/breeds/podenco-andaluz-medium/), a Podengo Galego covers a similar height range but is usually lighter. The medium [Portuguese Podengo](/breeds/portuguese-podengo-medium/) is its closest relative and very similar in type, with a slightly wider height range.

### Living with a Podengo Galego

Podengos Galegos are clever, lively and tireless, with a strong hunting instinct. They hunt by scent, sight and hearing, so recall can be unreliable near rabbits, cats or birds, and they need a secure garden. The standard describes them as wary of strangers and alert, so they often bark when someone comes to the house. They need daily walks and a chance to run.

### Care and health

The coat is semi-short to medium length, thick and dense, usually cinnamon from sand to red, often with a white collar, chest and feet. It needs very little grooming. The standard gives no life span, and little breed-specific health data exists. Like other podencos, they're generally hardy and often live 12–14 years.

*Sources: official breed standard of the Podengo Galego, Xunta de Galicia (Consellería do Medio Rural).*
