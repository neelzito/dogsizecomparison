The large Podenco Andaluz (*Podenco Andaluz grande*) is the biggest of the three sizes of the Andalusian Hound. In southern Spain it was traditionally used on bigger game such as boar and deer, as well as rabbits, often in packs. Like all Podencos, it hunts by scent, sight and hearing at once, and it has the classic large upright ears and lean build of the Mediterranean warren hounds. For the breed's history and the three sizes and coats, see the [medium Podenco Andaluz](/breeds/podenco-andaluz-medium/).

### What a large Podenco's size means at home

The RSCE standard (No. 401) calls for 54–64 cm at the shoulder for males and 53–61 cm for females, and about 27 kg ± 6 kg. A large Podenco reaches roughly an adult's mid-thigh. It's strong, athletic and a great jumper, so garden fences need to be high, ideally 1.8 m (6 ft) or more. The [crate size calculator](/calculators/crate-size/?breed=podenco-andaluz-large) suggests a 48″ (122 cm) crate for an average male.

### Large Podenco vs Ibizan Hound and Podenco Valenciano

The [Ibizan Hound](/compare/podenco-andaluz-large-vs-ibizan-hound/) is taller and lighter. The [Podenco Valenciano](/compare/podenco-valenciano-vs-podenco-andaluz-large/) is about the same height and lighter. A [Treeing Walker Coonhound](/compare/podenco-andaluz-large-vs-treeing-walker-coonhound/) is a little taller and about the same weight.

### Living with a large Podenco

Large Podencos are affectionate and gentle at home, and usually good with other dogs. Their hunting instinct is strong, so recall is often unreliable and cats may not be safe. They need daily exercise and a secure area to run. Many large Podencos come from Spanish rescue organisations, which rehome them across Europe.

### Care and health

The coat can be smooth, wiry or long, and it's easy to care for. Podencos are generally hardy and often live 12–14 years.

*Sources: RSCE breed standard No. 401 (Podenco Andaluz).*
