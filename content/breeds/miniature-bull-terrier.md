The Miniature Bull Terrier is the small version of the Bull Terrier. Small Bull Terriers were bred alongside the standard size from the 19th century, but they were only recognised as a separate breed later: by The Kennel Club in 1939 and the AKC in 1991. It has the same egg-shaped head, muscular build and clownish personality as its bigger cousin, in a body that fits more easily into modern homes.

### What a Mini Bull Terrier's size means at home

The AKC standard calls for 10–14 in (25.5–35.5 cm) at the shoulder, with weight in proportion to height. Most weigh about 8–12.5 kg (18–28 lb). A Mini Bull reaches around mid-shin on an adult, but it's muscular and solid, heavy enough to put it in the medium size class on this site. It usually needs a 24″ (61 cm) crate. Check with the [crate size calculator](/calculators/crate-size/?breed=miniature-bull-terrier). Most Mini Bulls are too heavy for the 8 kg (17.6 lb) cabin limit once you add a carrier.

### Miniature vs standard Bull Terrier

The [Bull Terrier](/breeds/bull-terrier/) has no height limits and is much bigger, typically about 53–56 cm and twice the weight or more. The Mini is the same in shape and temperament, just smaller. Next to a [Sealyham Terrier](/compare/miniature-bull-terrier-vs-sealyham-terrier/), a Mini Bull is a little taller and similar in weight.

### Temperament and exercise

Mini Bull Terriers are playful, funny and affectionate, very attached to their people. They have a lot of energy and need daily exercise and play. They can be stubborn and may not get on with other dogs, so early socialisation and consistent, reward-based training matter. Some develop compulsive behaviours such as tail chasing. They don't like being left alone for long.

### Coat and health

The short, flat coat needs very little grooming. White Mini Bulls are prone to sunburn and are more often deaf, so ask breeders whether puppies have been BAER hearing tested. Primary lens luxation (a displaced lens in the eye) is common in the breed, and a DNA test exists for it. Kidney disease and heart conditions are also known. Mini Bulls often live 11–13 years.
