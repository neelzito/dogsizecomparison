The Podenco Orito Español is a medium-sized Spanish hunting dog used to hunt rabbits, mainly in central and southern Spain. It's recognised by the Spanish kennel club (RSCE) as an ethnic group, but it has no definitive published standard yet, and it is not recognised by the FCI. It shares the classic Podenco build: lean, agile, with large upright ears and sharp senses.

### What an Orito's size means at home

Because there is no final standard, published sizes vary. The figures on this page, about 40–50 cm at the shoulder and 14–22 kg (30–48 lb), are commonly cited ranges rather than official ones. An Orito reaches just below an adult's knee. The [crate size calculator](/calculators/crate-size/?breed=podenco-orito-espanol) suggests a 36″ (91 cm) crate for an average dog.

### Orito vs Portuguese Podengo and GBGV

The [Portuguese Podengo (Medium)](/compare/podenco-orito-espanol-vs-portuguese-podengo-medium/) is about the same size. The [Grand Basset Griffon Vendéen](/compare/grand-basset-griffon-vendeen-vs-podenco-orito-espanol/) is a similar weight but longer and lower.

### Living with an Orito

Oritos are affectionate and gentle with their family and usually good with other dogs. Their hunting instinct is strong, so recall can be unreliable, and they need a secure garden. Like other Podencos, many end up in Spanish shelters and are adopted abroad.

### Care and health

The short coat is easy to care for. Little breed-specific health data exists. Oritos are generally hardy and often live 12–14 years.

*Sources: Real Sociedad Canina de España (RSCE); Spanish Podenco breed associations.*
