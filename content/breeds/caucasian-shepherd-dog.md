The Caucasian Shepherd Dog (*Kavkazskaya Ovcharka*) is a large livestock guardian from the Caucasus Mountains, in today's Georgia, Armenia, Azerbaijan and southern Russia. For centuries, shepherds used it to protect flocks from wolves, bears and thieves. In the Soviet era it was bred as a guard dog for military sites, prisons and borders, including the Berlin Wall. It's a massive, powerful and fiercely protective dog, and it needs a very experienced owner.

### What a Caucasian Shepherd's size means at home

The AKC standard gives typical heights of about 23–30 in (58.5–76 cm) at the shoulder and weights of about 45–77 kg (100–170 lb), and many males are heavier. A Caucasian Shepherd reaches roughly an adult's mid-thigh. With its thick coat and broad head, it looks enormous, and its weight puts it in the giant size class on this site. It needs a large vehicle, a very secure property and a 54″ (137 cm) crate. Check with the [crate size calculator](/calculators/crate-size/?breed=caucasian-shepherd-dog).

### Caucasian vs Tibetan Mastiff, Neapolitan Mastiff and Tosa

The [Tibetan Mastiff](/compare/tibetan-mastiff-vs-caucasian-shepherd-dog/) is a little taller on average but usually lighter. The [Neapolitan Mastiff](/compare/caucasian-shepherd-dog-vs-neapolitan-mastiff/) and the [Tosa](/compare/caucasian-shepherd-dog-vs-tosa/) are taller and a little heavier on average, but short-coated.

### Laws and restrictions

The Caucasian Shepherd is banned or restricted in a number of countries, regions and cities, and some insurers and landlords have rules for it. Check the current laws where you live, and where you plan to travel or move, before getting one.

### A serious guardian

Caucasian Shepherds are devoted to their family but deeply suspicious of strangers, and they will defend their territory without hesitation. They're independent and strong-willed, bred to make their own decisions. They need extensive socialisation from puppyhood, a very experienced and confident owner, and a large, securely fenced property. They aren't suitable for first-time owners, busy households or homes with frequent visitors.

### Coat and health

The thick double coat comes in short, medium and long varieties. It sheds heavily once or twice a year and needs regular brushing. Caucasian Shepherds cope well with cold but struggle with heat. Known health problems include hip and elbow dysplasia, obesity and heart problems. They often live 10–12 years.

*Sources: AKC breed information; FCI standard No. 328 (Caucasian Shepherd Dog); Russian Kennel Club (RKF).*
