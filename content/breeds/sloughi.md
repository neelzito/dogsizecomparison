The Sloughi is an ancient sighthound from North Africa, especially Morocco, Algeria, Tunisia and Libya. Berber and Bedouin peoples have used it for centuries to hunt gazelles, hares and jackals, and to guard their camps. It's short-coated and elegant, with a melancholy expression. Unlike the Saluki, it has no feathering, and it has a strong, quiet loyalty to its family.

### What a Sloughi's size means at home

The AKC standard calls for 26–29 in (66–73.5 cm) at the shoulder for males and 24–27 in (61–68.5 cm) for females. Sloughis weigh about 18–29.5 kg (40–65 lb). A Sloughi reaches roughly an adult's mid-thigh. It's very lean for its height, so its weight puts it in the medium size class on this site. Because of its height, the [crate size calculator](/calculators/crate-size/?breed=sloughi) suggests a 54″ (137 cm) crate for an average male.

### Sloughi vs Saluki and Afghan Hound

The [Saluki](/compare/saluki-vs-sloughi/) is a little shorter and about the same weight, often with feathered ears and tail. The [Afghan Hound](/compare/afghan-hound-vs-sloughi/) is about the same size, with a long, silky coat.

### Temperament and exercise

Sloughis are gentle, sensitive and loyal, very attached to their family and reserved with strangers. They need regular chances to run in a large, secure area, and they have a strong chase instinct. They're quiet and calm indoors and dislike cold.

### Care and health

The short coat needs almost no grooming. Like other sighthounds, Sloughis can be sensitive to some anaesthetics. Progressive retinal atrophy (PRA) is known, and a DNA test exists. Sloughis often live 10–15 years.
