The Portuguese Podengo Médio is the medium size of the Portuguese warren hound, used in Portugal mainly to hunt rabbits, often in packs. It's the most common of the three sizes in Portugal. Like the [Pequeno](/breeds/portuguese-podengo-pequeno/) and the [Grande](/breeds/portuguese-podengo-large/), it hunts by sight, sound and scent and has large upright ears and a wedge-shaped head.

### What a Podengo Médio's size means at home

The FCI standard calls for 40–54 cm at the shoulder and 16–20 kg. A Médio reaches roughly an adult's knee. The [crate size calculator](/calculators/crate-size/?breed=portuguese-podengo-medium) suggests a 42″ (107 cm) crate for an average dog. It's too heavy for an airline cabin.

### Podengo Médio vs Podenco Andaluz, Orito and Basenji

The [medium Podenco Andaluz](/compare/podenco-andaluz-medium-vs-portuguese-podengo-medium/) and the [Podenco Orito Español](/compare/podenco-orito-espanol-vs-portuguese-podengo-medium/) are about the same size. The [Basenji](/compare/portuguese-podengo-medium-vs-basenji/) is shorter and lighter.

### Temperament and care

Podengos Médios are lively, alert and loyal, devoted to their family and wary of strangers. They have a strong prey drive and need a secure garden and daily exercise. The coat can be smooth or wire and is easy to care for. They're generally hardy and often live 12–15 years.

*Sources: FCI standard No. 94 (Podengo Português); Clube Português de Canicultura.*
