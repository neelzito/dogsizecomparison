The Maltipoo is a cross between a Maltese and a Toy or Miniature Poodle. It first became popular in the US in the 1990s and 2000s, when small "designer" crosses were in fashion, and it has stayed one of the most popular small crossbreeds. People love its affectionate, playful nature and its soft, often low-shedding coat. As a crossbreed, the Maltipoo isn't recognised by the major kennel clubs and has no official standard, so size and coat vary more than in a purebred.

### How big does a Maltipoo get?

Most Maltipoos stand about 20–30 cm at the shoulder, around mid-shin on an adult, and weigh about 2.5–7 kg (5–15 lb). Size depends mainly on the Poodle parent: Maltipoos from a Toy Poodle are smaller, those from a Miniature Poodle bigger. Breeders sometimes advertise "teacup", "toy" or "mini" Maltipoos. These are informal terms, and very small dogs tend to have more health problems. Ask about both parents' size, and use the [puppy weight calculator](/calculators/puppy-weight/?breed=maltipoo) as a rough guide.

Next to a [Cavapoo](/compare/cavapoo-vs-maltipoo/), a Maltipoo is usually smaller and lighter.

### What a Maltipoo's size means at home

A Maltipoo suits small flats and city life, fits on a lap and usually needs a 24″ (61 cm) crate. Check with the [crate size calculator](/calculators/crate-size/?breed=maltipoo). Smaller Maltipoos can be hurt by falls from furniture or arms, so teach children to sit down when holding one.

### Can a Maltipoo fly in the cabin?

Usually yes. Most Maltipoos plus a carrier stay under the 8 kg (17.6 lb) limit many airlines set, though the largest may not. Check with the [airline cabin check](/calculators/airline-cabin/?breed=maltipoo).

### Coat care

Maltipoo coats range from soft and wavy to curly like a Poodle's, usually white, cream or apricot. They shed little but mat easily, so brush several times a week and book a trim every 4–8 weeks. Tear staining around the eyes is common, and gentle daily cleaning helps. No dog is truly hypoallergenic, so people with allergies should meet the puppy's parents first.

### Temperament and health

Maltipoos are affectionate, people-focused and playful. They often struggle when left alone, and some bark a lot without training. They need daily walks and play but not long hikes. Like both parent breeds, they're prone to dental disease, loose kneecaps and eye problems, so choose a breeder who health-tests both parents. Maltipoos often live 10–15 years.

*Sources: AKC breed standards for the Maltese and the Poodle; North American Maltipoo Club and Registry.*
