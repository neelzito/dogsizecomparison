The Central Asian Shepherd Dog is an ancient livestock guardian from the steppes and mountains of Central Asia, in today's Turkmenistan, Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan and neighbouring countries. For thousands of years, nomadic herders used it to protect flocks and camps from wolves and other predators. In Turkmenistan the breed, called the Alabai, is a national symbol, with a large gold statue in the capital. It's a massive, independent and fiercely protective dog.

### What a Central Asian Shepherd's size means at home

The AKC standard sets minimums of about 27.5 in (70 cm) for males and 25.5 in (65 cm) for females, and many dogs are taller. Males typically weigh about 50–79 kg (110–175 lb) and females 40–65 kg (88–143 lb). A Central Asian Shepherd reaches roughly an adult's mid-thigh, and its weight puts it in the giant size class on this site. It needs a large vehicle, a 54″ (137 cm) crate (see the [crate size calculator](/calculators/crate-size/?breed=central-asian-shepherd-dog)) and a large, secure property.

### Central Asian vs Saint Bernard and Neapolitan Mastiff

The [Saint Bernard](/compare/central-asian-shepherd-dog-vs-saint-bernard/) is about the same height and usually heavier. The [Neapolitan Mastiff](/compare/central-asian-shepherd-dog-vs-neapolitan-mastiff/) is about the same size, but short-coated and very wrinkled.

### Laws and restrictions

Central Asian Shepherds are banned or restricted in some countries, regions and cities. Check the current laws where you live, and where you plan to travel or move, before getting one.

### A serious guardian

These dogs are calm and loyal with their family but highly protective and suspicious of strangers. They're independent, bred to make their own decisions. They need extensive socialisation, a very experienced owner and a securely fenced property. They aren't suitable for first-time owners.

### Care and health

The short or long double coat sheds heavily once or twice a year. Known health problems include hip and elbow dysplasia. Central Asian Shepherds often live 12–14 years.
