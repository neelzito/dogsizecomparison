The Tibetan Spaniel isn't really a spaniel. It's an ancient companion dog from the monasteries of Tibet, where it sat on the walls as a lookout and was known as a "little lion". It was given as a gift rather than sold. It reached Britain in the early 20th century. It's small, alert and confident, with a silky coat and a plumed tail curled over its back.

### What a Tibbie's size means at home

The AKC standard calls for about 10 in (25.5 cm) at the shoulder, and Tibbies weigh about 4–7 kg (9–15 lb). A Tibbie reaches around mid-shin on an adult. It suits any home and usually needs a 24″ (61 cm) crate. Check with the [crate size calculator](/calculators/crate-size/?breed=tibetan-spaniel). Lighter Tibbies may fit the cabin limit. Check with the [airline cabin check](/calculators/airline-cabin/?breed=tibetan-spaniel).

### Tibetan Spaniel vs Volpino and Pekingese

The [Volpino Italiano](/compare/tibetan-spaniel-vs-volpino-italiano/) is a little taller and heavier. The Pekingese has a much flatter face and a heavier coat.

### Temperament and exercise

Tibbies are affectionate, clever and independent, devoted to their family and reserved with strangers. They like high places to watch from. They need daily walks and play.

### Coat and health

The silky double coat needs brushing a couple of times a week. Known health problems include progressive retinal atrophy (PRA) and loose kneecaps. Tibbies often live 12–15 years.
