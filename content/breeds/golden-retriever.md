The Golden Retriever was developed in the Scottish Highlands in the second half of the 19th century. Lord Tweedmouth wanted a gundog that could retrieve shot birds from water and rough ground on his estate, Guisachan, near Inverness. He crossed a yellow retriever with the now-extinct Tweed Water Spaniel, and later added other retrievers, a little Irish Setter and possibly Bloodhound. The result was a soft-mouthed, eager and very trainable dog. That same nature makes the Golden one of the most popular family dogs and a common guide and assistance dog today.

### What a Golden's size means at home

A Golden Retriever stands about 55–61 cm at the shoulder, roughly an adult's mid-thigh, and weighs 25–34 kg (55–75 lb). It is about as tall as a Labrador but looks bigger because of its coat. It needs an estate car or SUV boot and usually a 42″ (107 cm) crate. Check with the [crate size calculator](/calculators/crate-size/?breed=golden-retriever). That waving, feathered tail sits at coffee-table height, so clear anything breakable. Goldens are far too heavy for an airline cabin and fly in the hold.

### Golden vs Labrador

The two are often confused. They're [very close in size](/compare/labrador-retriever-vs-golden-retriever/), but the Golden has a longer double coat with feathering on the legs, chest and tail, a slightly finer head, and a plume of a tail rather than the Labrador's thick otter tail. Goldens also come in a wider range of shades, from pale cream to deep red. The cream-coloured "English" type is common in Europe, while American breeders tend to prefer darker gold.

### Coat and care

That coat sheds a lot, especially twice a year when the undercoat comes out. Brush a few times a week (daily during the shedding season) to keep the house manageable and the coat free of mats behind the ears and on the trousers. Goldens love water and mud, so a towel by the door is part of the deal.

### Puppies and health

Goldens grow quickly and usually reach their adult height at about 12 months, then fill out until about 2 years. The [puppy weight calculator](/calculators/puppy-weight/?breed=golden-retriever) gives an estimate for your puppy. Keep your dog lean, as extra weight is hard on the hips and elbows. Cancer is the breed's biggest health concern: in the Golden Retriever Club of America's health survey, it caused about 60% of deaths. Regular vet checks and attention to any new lumps, weight loss or tiredness are especially worthwhile in this breed.
