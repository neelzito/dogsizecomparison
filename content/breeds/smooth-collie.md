The Smooth Collie is the short-coated version of the Collie, developed from the same Scottish herding dogs as the Rough Collie. Smooth Collies were often used as drovers' dogs, driving livestock to market. In the US, the AKC treats the Rough and Smooth as two varieties of one breed. The Kennel Club and the FCI treat them as separate breeds. Apart from the coat, they're the same dog.

### What a Smooth Collie's size means at home

The AKC standard calls for 24–26 in (61–66 cm) at the shoulder and 60–75 lb (27–34 kg) for males, and 22–24 in (56–61 cm) and 50–65 lb (22.5–29.5 kg) for females. A Smooth Collie reaches roughly an adult's mid-thigh. The UK and FCI standards are a little smaller. It needs a big boot and usually a 48″ (122 cm) crate. Check with the [crate size calculator](/calculators/crate-size/?breed=smooth-collie).

### Smooth vs Rough Collie

The [Rough Collie](/compare/rough-collie-vs-smooth-collie/) is the same size and build, with a long, flowing coat. The Smooth's short, dense coat is much easier to care for.

### Temperament and exercise

Smooth Collies are gentle, loyal and intelligent, usually good with children. They're sensitive and easy to train with kindness. They need daily exercise and mental work, and they enjoy herding, agility and obedience.

### Care and health

The short double coat sheds seasonally and needs weekly brushing. Like Rough Collies, many carry the MDR1 gene variant, which makes them sensitive to some medicines, so a DNA test is worth doing. Collie eye anomaly and progressive retinal atrophy are also known. Smooth Collies often live 12–14 years.

*Sources: AKC breed standard (Collie); The Kennel Club breed standard (Collie Smooth); FCI standard No. 296.*
