The small Podenco Andaluz (*Podenco Andaluz chico*) is the smallest of the three sizes of the Andalusian Hound. In southern Spain it hunted rabbits in thick brambles and scrub, where bigger dogs couldn't follow. It has the same large upright ears, keen senses and lively personality as its bigger relatives. For the breed's history and its three sizes and coats, see the [medium Podenco Andaluz](/breeds/podenco-andaluz-medium/).

### What a small Podenco's size means at home

The RSCE standard (No. 401) calls for 35–42 cm at the shoulder for males and 32–41 cm for females, and about 8 kg ± 3 kg. A small Podenco reaches just below an adult's knee. Because of its legs and big ears, the [crate size calculator](/calculators/crate-size/?breed=podenco-andaluz-small) suggests a 36″ (91 cm) crate for an average dog. Lighter dogs may fit the airline cabin limit. Check with the [airline cabin check](/calculators/airline-cabin/?breed=podenco-andaluz-small).

### Small Podenco vs Lundehund and Maneto

The [Norwegian Lundehund](/compare/norwegian-lundehund-vs-podenco-andaluz-small/) is about the same size. The [Maneto](/breeds/maneto/), another Andalusian hunter, is shorter-legged.

### Living with a small Podenco

Small Podencos are affectionate, playful and lively, good with other dogs. They're agile jumpers and climbers with a strong prey drive, so they need a secure garden and careful recall training. Many are adopted from Spanish shelters.

### Care and health

The coat can be smooth, wiry or long and is easy to care for. Small Podencos are generally hardy and often live 12–14 years.

*Sources: RSCE breed standard No. 401 (Podenco Andaluz).*
