The Grand Basset Griffon Vendéen (GBGV) is a French scent hound from the Vendée region, the bigger cousin of the Petit Basset Griffon Vendéen. Its name means "large, low, rough-coated dog from the Vendée". It was bred to hunt hares and rabbits in packs through thick brambles. It's cheerful, sociable and full of energy.

### What a GBGV's size means at home

The AKC standard calls for 15.5–18 in (39.5–45.5 cm) at the shoulder, and GBGVs weigh about 18–20.5 kg (40–45 lb). A GBGV reaches just below an adult's knee. It's longer than tall and sturdy. It usually needs a 36″ (91 cm) crate. Check with the [crate size calculator](/calculators/crate-size/?breed=grand-basset-griffon-vendeen). It's too heavy for an airline cabin.

### GBGV vs PBGV and Bavarian

The [Petit Basset Griffon Vendéen](/breeds/petit-basset-griffon-vendeen/) is smaller. The [Bavarian Mountain Scent Hound](/compare/bavarian-mountain-scent-hound-vs-grand-basset-griffon-vendeen/) is taller and heavier. The [Podenco Orito Español](/compare/grand-basset-griffon-vendeen-vs-podenco-orito-espanol/) is about the same size.

### Temperament and exercise

GBGVs are friendly, outgoing and good with other dogs. They need plenty of exercise, and their nose leads them. They're vocal.

### Coat and health

The rough coat needs weekly brushing. Known health problems include eye conditions and hip dysplasia. GBGVs often live 13–15 years.
