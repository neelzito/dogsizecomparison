The Sheepadoodle is a cross between an Old English Sheepdog and a Poodle. Crosses like this have existed for decades, but the Sheepadoodle became popular in the 2010s, especially in North America, as part of the wider "doodle" trend. People are drawn to its fluffy black-and-white look, friendly temperament and often low-shedding coat. As a crossbreed, it has no official standard, so size and coat vary.

### How big does a Sheepadoodle get?

Size depends mainly on the Poodle parent:

- **Standard Sheepadoodle** (Standard Poodle parent): about 53–69 cm at the shoulder and 27–36 kg (60–80 lb) for males
- **Mini Sheepadoodle:** about 38–50 cm
- **Toy or Micro Sheepadoodle:** smaller still

These are informal terms used by breeders. This page shows the Standard Sheepadoodle, which reaches roughly mid-thigh on an adult. It's a little smaller and lighter than a [Bernedoodle](/compare/bernedoodle-standard-vs-sheepadoodle-standard/) on average, and a little taller and heavier than a [Goldendoodle](/compare/goldendoodle-standard-vs-sheepadoodle-standard/). Ask breeders about both parents' size, and use the [puppy weight calculator](/calculators/puppy-weight/?breed=sheepadoodle-standard) as a rough guide.

### What a Sheepadoodle's size means at home

A Standard Sheepadoodle is a big, fluffy dog that needs a large boot and space indoors. The [crate size calculator](/calculators/crate-size/?breed=sheepadoodle-standard) suggests a 48″ (122 cm) crate for an average adult. It needs daily exercise and is too heavy for an airline cabin.

### Coat care

Sheepadoodle coats are usually wavy or curly and shed little, but they mat easily, especially behind the ears, under the legs and on the chest. Plan on brushing down to the skin several times a week and a professional groom every 6–8 weeks. Puppies that are born black and white often fade to grey and white as adults. No dog is truly hypoallergenic.

### Temperament and health

Sheepadoodles are usually friendly, playful and clever, and good with children, though the herding instinct of the Old English Sheepdog may show up as gentle nudging. They need daily walks and play, and they don't like being left alone for long. They can inherit problems from both parents, including hip dysplasia, eye diseases and the MDR1 drug sensitivity found in herding breeds, so choose a breeder who health-tests both parents. Sheepadoodles often live 12–15 years.

*Sources: AKC breed standards for the Old English Sheepdog and the Poodle.*
