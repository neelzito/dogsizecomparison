The Bullmastiff was developed in England in the 19th century as the "gamekeeper's night dog". Gamekeepers on large estates needed a powerful, silent dog to track and hold poachers without mauling them. They crossed Mastiffs with Bulldogs, roughly 60% Mastiff and 40% Bulldog, to get a dog faster than a Mastiff and bigger than a Bulldog. The Kennel Club recognised the Bullmastiff in 1924. Today it is a calm, loyal and affectionate family guardian.

### What a Bullmastiff's size means at home

The AKC standard calls for 25–27 in (63.5–68.5 cm) and 110–130 lb (50–59 kg) for males, and 24–26 in (61–66 cm) and 100–120 lb (45.5–54.5 kg) for females. A Bullmastiff reaches roughly an adult's mid-thigh. Its weight puts it in the giant size class on this site. It's broad, muscular and very strong. It needs a large vehicle, a ramp to save lifting, and a 48″ (122 cm) crate. Check with the [crate size calculator](/calculators/crate-size/?breed=bullmastiff).

### Bullmastiff vs Dogue de Bordeaux and Mastiff

The [Dogue de Bordeaux](/compare/bullmastiff-vs-dogue-de-bordeaux/) is a little shorter on average and a little heavier, with a huge head and a red coat. The [Greater Swiss Mountain Dog](/compare/bullmastiff-vs-greater-swiss-mountain-dog/) is a little taller and similar in weight. The English Mastiff is much bigger and heavier than both.

### Temperament

Bullmastiffs are calm, gentle and devoted to their family, usually good with children they know. They're naturally protective and wary of strangers, and they tend to deter intruders by presence rather than barking. Because of their size and strength, early socialisation and consistent training are essential. They need only moderate exercise and are often happy to doze at home. Expect drool and snoring.

### Care and health

The short coat needs little grooming. Bullmastiffs are sensitive to heat because of their short muzzle and bulk. Known health problems include hip and elbow dysplasia, bloat, eyelid problems (entropion), hypothyroidism and cancer, especially lymphoma. Like many giant breeds, Bullmastiffs have a short life, usually 7–9 years.
