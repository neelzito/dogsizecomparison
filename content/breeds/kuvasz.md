The Kuvasz is a large white guardian breed from Hungary, used for centuries to protect livestock and estates against wolves, bears and intruders. In the 15th century, King Matthias Corvinus of Hungary famously kept Kuvaszok as guards and hunting dogs. The breed nearly died out during the Second World War, when many were killed, but it was rebuilt from a small number of survivors. Today the Kuvasz is still used as a working guardian, and as a loyal, protective family dog for experienced owners.

### What a Kuvasz's size means at home

The AKC standard calls for 28–30 in (71–76 cm) at the shoulder and about 100–115 lb (45.5–52 kg) for males, and 26–28 in (66–71 cm) and about 70–90 lb (32–41 kg) for females. A Kuvasz reaches roughly an adult's mid-thigh. It's tall, muscular and athletic. It needs a large vehicle and, because of its height, the [crate size calculator](/calculators/crate-size/?breed=kuvasz) suggests a 54″ (137 cm) crate for an average male.

### Kuvasz vs Komondor, Great Pyrenees and Black Russian

The Komondor, the other big Hungarian guardian, is a little taller and heavier on average, with a corded coat. The Great Pyrenees is similar in size, with a heavier coat. The [Black Russian Terrier](/compare/black-russian-terrier-vs-kuvasz/) is about the same height and weight, but black.

### Temperament and training

Kuvaszok are loyal, brave and very protective of their family and territory, and naturally wary of strangers. They're independent and intelligent, bred to make their own decisions, so training takes patience and consistency. They need early socialisation, an experienced owner and a large, securely fenced property. They need daily exercise and do best with a job, such as guarding livestock.

### Coat and health

The thick, white double coat ranges from wavy to straight. It sheds heavily, especially seasonally, and needs brushing at least once or twice a week. Surprisingly, it tends to shed dirt well. Known health problems include hip and elbow dysplasia, hypothyroidism and osteochondrosis (a joint condition). Kuvaszok often live 10–12 years.
