The Saluki is one of the oldest dog breeds, a sighthound from the Middle East that has hunted gazelles, hares and foxes across the deserts for thousands of years, often together with falcons. Bedouin tribes prized Salukis so highly that they were treated as members of the family and given as gifts rather than sold. Saluki-like dogs appear in ancient Egyptian and Mesopotamian art. The breed came to Britain in the late 19th century, and it remains an elegant, gentle and independent dog.

### What a Saluki's size means at home

The AKC standard calls for 23–28 in (58.5–71 cm) at the shoulder for males, with females considerably smaller. Salukis weigh about 18–29.5 kg (40–65 lb). A Saluki reaches roughly an adult's mid-thigh. It's tall, slim and very light for its height, so its weight puts it in the medium size class on this site. Because of its height and long legs, the [crate size calculator](/calculators/crate-size/?breed=saluki) suggests a 48″ (122 cm) crate for an average male.

### Feathered or smooth

Salukis come in two coat varieties. The **feathered** Saluki has long, silky hair on the ears, legs and tail. The **smooth** Saluki has a short coat all over. Both shed little and need only weekly brushing, with extra care for feathering. Salukis come in many colours, including cream, fawn, red, black and tan, and tricolour.

### Saluki vs Sloughi and Ibizan

The [Sloughi](/compare/saluki-vs-sloughi/), a North African sighthound, is a little taller on average, similar in weight, and always smooth-coated. The [Ibizan Hound](/compare/ibizan-hound-vs-saluki/) is about the same height and weight, with large upright ears.

### Living with a Saluki

Salukis are gentle, dignified and affectionate with their family, often reserved with strangers. They're independent and sensitive, so gentle, patient training works best. They're among the fastest dogs over long distances and have a very strong urge to chase, so they need regular chances to run flat-out in a large, secure area, and should never be let off the lead near roads. At home, they're quiet and love soft furniture. They feel the cold and need a coat in winter.

### Health

Salukis are generally healthy. Known problems include heart conditions (cardiomyopathy), cancer, hypothyroidism, and sensitivity to some anaesthetics, as in other sighthounds. They often live 10–17 years.
