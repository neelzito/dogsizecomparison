The Podenco Valenciano, known locally as the Xarnego Valenciano, is a hunting dog from the Valencia region of eastern Spain. For generations, hunters there used it to hunt rabbits, often in packs, in the dry hills and scrub. Like the other Spanish Podencos, it uses scent, sight and hearing together, and it has the classic lean build and large upright ears of the Mediterranean warren hounds. The Spanish kennel club (RSCE) recognised it as an ethnic group in 2013. It is not recognised by the FCI.

### What a Podenco Valenciano's size means at home

The RSCE standard gives heights of 55–61 cm at the shoulder for males and 50–57 cm for females, and weights of about 20 kg for males and 18 kg for females, varying by up to 4 kg with the hunting season. A Podenco Valenciano reaches roughly an adult's mid-thigh. It's lean and athletic, light for its height, so its weight puts it in the medium size class on this site. Because of its height and big ears, the [crate size calculator](/calculators/crate-size/?breed=podenco-valenciano) suggests a 48″ (122 cm) crate for an average male.

### Valenciano vs Podenco Canario and Podenco Andaluz

The [Podenco Canario](/compare/podenco-canario-vs-podenco-valenciano/) is about the same height and a little heavier. The [Podenco Andaluz (Large)](/compare/podenco-valenciano-vs-podenco-andaluz-large/) is a little taller and heavier. The Valenciano's coat is usually red or fawn, often with white, and can be smooth or wiry.

### Living with a Podenco Valenciano

Podencos are affectionate, gentle and sensitive with their families, and they usually get on well with other dogs because they're bred to hunt in packs. Their hunting instinct is very strong, so recall is often unreliable wherever there are rabbits, cats or birds. A long line and a securely fenced area with high fences are essential. They need daily exercise and a chance to run, but are usually calm at home.

### Adoption

Many Podencos, including Valencianos and Podenco mixes, end up in Spanish shelters, often after the hunting season. Rescue organisations rehome them across Europe. Ask the rescue about each dog's size, temperament and behaviour with cats.

### Care and health

The coat needs little grooming. Podencos feel the cold, so a coat helps in winter. Little breed-specific health data exists. Podencos are generally hardy and often live 12–14 years.

*Sources: RSCE breed standard (Xarnego Valenciano); Real Sociedad Canina de España.*
