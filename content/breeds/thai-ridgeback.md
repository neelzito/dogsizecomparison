The Thai Ridgeback is an ancient breed from Thailand, especially the east of the country, where it was used for hunting, guarding and pulling carts. Like the Rhodesian Ridgeback, it has a ridge of hair growing in the opposite direction along its back. It developed in relative isolation, with little outside influence, and it remains rare outside Thailand.

### What a Thai Ridgeback's size means at home

The breed standard calls for about 56–61 cm at the shoulder for males and 51–56 cm for females. The figures on this page show about 16–34 kg (35–75 lb). A Thai Ridgeback reaches roughly an adult's knee. It's muscular and agile, and its average weight puts it in the large size class on this site. The [crate size calculator](/calculators/crate-size/?breed=thai-ridgeback) suggests a 42″ (107 cm) crate.

### Thai vs Rhodesian Ridgeback and Plott

The [Rhodesian Ridgeback](/breeds/rhodesian-ridgeback/) is taller and heavier. The [Plott Hound](/compare/plott-hound-vs-thai-ridgeback/) is about the same size.

### Temperament and exercise

Thai Ridgebacks are independent, loyal and protective, devoted to their family but wary of strangers. They have a strong prey drive and can climb and jump. They need an experienced owner, early socialisation and daily exercise.

### Care and health

The short coat needs little grooming. Like the Rhodesian, Thai Ridgebacks can be born with dermoid sinus, a defect under the skin along the spine. Thai Ridgebacks often live 14–15 years.

*Sources: AKC breed information; FCI standard No. 338 (Thai Ridgeback Dog).*
