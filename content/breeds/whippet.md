The Whippet was developed in the north of England in the 19th century, mainly by working-class people in mining and mill towns. It was used to catch rabbits and for racing, and it earned the nickname "the poor man's racehorse". It is essentially a smaller Greyhound, probably with some terrier blood added. Whippets are the fastest dogs of their size and can reach speeds of around 55 km/h (35 mph). At home, though, they're calm, gentle and famously fond of sofas.

### What a Whippet's size means at home

A male Whippet stands about 48–56 cm at the shoulder, roughly an adult's knee, and both sexes weigh about 11–18 kg (25–40 lb). It is lean and long-legged, so it looks bigger than its weight suggests, and it curls up surprisingly small. The [crate size calculator](/calculators/crate-size/?breed=whippet) suggests a 42″ (107 cm) crate because of its long legs and height. A Whippet is too heavy for an airline cabin.

### Whippet vs Greyhound and Italian Greyhound

The three are like a set of nesting dolls. The [Greyhound](/compare/greyhound-vs-whippet/) is much taller and about twice as heavy. The [Italian Greyhound](/compare/whippet-vs-italian-greyhound/) is far smaller, a toy breed of about 3–6 kg. All three share the same build, gentle temperament and need to sprint.

### Exercise and recall

Whippets need a daily chance to run flat-out in a safe, enclosed area. Short, fast sprints matter more to them than long, slow walks. Their instinct to chase small, fast-moving animals is strong, and at full speed they can't hear you calling, so be careful about letting them off the lead near roads, livestock or wildlife. Many owners use a well-fitted martingale or sighthound collar, because a Whippet's narrow head can slip out of a normal collar.

### Thin coat, thin skin

The short coat sheds little and needs almost no grooming, but it offers little protection from cold and rain. Most Whippets wear a coat in winter and love soft beds and blankets. Their thin skin tears easily on barbed wire or brambles, and small cuts are common.

### Temperament and health

Whippets are affectionate, quiet and gentle, and good with children and other dogs. They rarely bark. They are sensitive, so gentle training works best. They're generally healthy and long-lived, often 12–15 years. Known problems include heart conditions and eye diseases. Because sighthounds react differently to some anaesthetics, it's worth making sure your vet knows the breed.
