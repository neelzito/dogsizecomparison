The Cavapoo (also called the Cavoodle in Australia) is a cross between a Cavalier King Charles Spaniel and a Poodle, usually a Miniature or Toy Poodle. It was first bred in Australia in the 1990s and has since become one of the most popular small crossbreeds in Australia, the UK and beyond. It combines the Cavalier's sweet, affectionate nature with the Poodle's intelligence and often low-shedding coat. As a crossbreed, it has no official standard.

### How big does a Cavapoo get?

A Cavapoo's size depends on the Poodle parent. Most are about 23–36 cm at the shoulder, around mid-shin on an adult, and weigh about 4.5–11 kg (10–24 lb). Cavapoos with a Toy Poodle parent are at the small end, and those with a Miniature Poodle parent at the larger end. Puppies in one litter can still differ quite a bit, so ask about both parents' size, and use the [puppy weight calculator](/calculators/puppy-weight/?breed=cavapoo) as a guide.

Next to a [Cavalier](/compare/cavalier-king-charles-spaniel-vs-cavapoo/), a Cavapoo is usually similar in size or a little smaller. A [Cockapoo](/compare/cockapoo-vs-cavapoo/) tends to be slightly bigger and more energetic, while a [Maltipoo](/compare/cavapoo-vs-maltipoo/) is usually smaller.

### What a Cavapoo's size means at home

A Cavapoo is an easy size for flats, small cars and laps, and it usually needs a 24″ (61 cm) crate. Check with the [crate size calculator](/calculators/crate-size/?breed=cavapoo). It enjoys daily walks and play but doesn't need hours of exercise. Cavapoos are very people-focused and often struggle when left alone for long.

### Can a Cavapoo fly in the cabin?

Smaller Cavapoos often can. Many airlines limit dog and carrier together to 8 kg (17.6 lb), which leaves about 6.5 kg for the dog. A Cavapoo from a Toy Poodle parent may fit, but larger ones won't. Use the [airline cabin check](/calculators/airline-cabin/?breed=cavapoo).

### Coat and health

Coats range from tight curls to loose waves. They shed little but tangle easily, so brush several times a week and book a trim every 6–8 weeks. Floppy ears need regular cleaning. Cavapoos can inherit the Cavalier's heart disease (mitral valve disease), along with eye problems and loose kneecaps. Choose a breeder who heart-tests the Cavalier parent and screens both parents' eyes and joints.

*Sources: Cavapoo Club UK; AKC breed standards for the Cavalier King Charles Spaniel and the Poodle.*
