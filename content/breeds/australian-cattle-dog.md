The Australian Cattle Dog was developed in the 19th century by Australian cattle farmers who needed a tough dog to drive half-wild cattle over huge distances in heat and rough country. Breeders crossed working collies with the dingo, Australia's wild dog, and later added a little Dalmatian and Black and Tan Kelpie. The result is one of the hardiest herding breeds in the world. Cattle Dogs are also called Blue Heelers or Red Heelers, after their coat colour and their habit of nipping at cattle's heels. A Cattle Dog called Bluey holds the Guinness record for the oldest dog: he lived to 29 years and 5 months.

### What a Cattle Dog's size means at home

A male Cattle Dog stands about 46–51 cm at the shoulder, roughly an adult's knee, and females about 43–48 cm. Both weigh about 16–22.5 kg (35–50 lb). It's compact, muscular and very agile. It usually needs a 36″ (91 cm) crate. Check with the [crate size calculator](/calculators/crate-size/?breed=australian-cattle-dog). It's too heavy for an airline cabin.

### Cattle Dog vs Kelpie and Border Collie

The [Australian Kelpie](/compare/australian-cattle-dog-vs-australian-kelpie/), another Australian herder, is very similar in size, usually with a leaner, lighter-boned look. The [Border Collie](/compare/australian-cattle-dog-vs-border-collie/) is about as heavy but slightly taller, with a longer coat and a softer, more eye-based herding style. The Cattle Dog works by nipping at heels.

### A working dog first

Cattle Dogs are intelligent, alert and tireless. They need a lot of daily exercise and a real job for their brain: herding, agility, obedience, canicross or long, varied hikes. A bored Cattle Dog can become destructive, noisy or obsessive, and may herd children, cats or cyclists by nipping their heels. They're very loyal and often bond closely with one person. They can be wary of strangers and make good watchdogs.

### Coat and care

The short, weather-resistant double coat sheds heavily once or twice a year but otherwise needs little grooming. Puppies are born white and develop their blue or red speckled coat as they grow, an inheritance from the Dalmatian.

### Health

Cattle Dogs are generally hardy and long-lived, often 12–16 years. Known health problems include deafness, so ask about BAER hearing tests, progressive retinal atrophy (PRA), for which a DNA test exists, and hip dysplasia.
