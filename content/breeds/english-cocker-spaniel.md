The English Cocker Spaniel is a British gundog bred to flush and retrieve game birds, especially woodcock, which gave the "cocker" its name. In the 19th century, land spaniels were sorted by size: the smaller ones were used on woodcock and became Cockers, and the larger ones became Springers. The Cocker was recognised as a separate breed in 1893. It is one of the most popular breeds in the UK, loved for its merry, wagging nature, and is still widely used as a working gundog.

### What a Cocker's size means at home

An English Cocker stands about 38–43 cm at the shoulder, just below an adult's knee, and weighs about 12–15.5 kg (26–34 lb). Males are a little bigger than females. It is a compact, sturdy dog that suits most homes, but it has lots of energy. It needs daily walks and play, ideally off the lead somewhere safe where it can use its nose. The [crate size calculator](/calculators/crate-size/?breed=english-cocker-spaniel) suggests a 36″ (91 cm) crate for an average adult. Cockers are too heavy for an airline cabin.

### Working Cockers vs show Cockers

The two types are almost different dogs. **Show Cockers** have a longer, thicker coat, long ears, a more domed head and a calmer nature. **Working Cockers** are smaller and lighter, with less coat and shorter ears, and they have huge energy and drive. They're superb in the field and at dog sports but need much more exercise and mental work. Before you get a puppy, decide which type suits your life.

### English vs American Cocker and Springer

The [American Cocker Spaniel](/compare/english-cocker-spaniel-vs-american-cocker-spaniel/) is a separate breed. It is smaller, with a shorter muzzle, a rounder head and a much fuller coat. The [English Springer Spaniel](/compare/english-springer-spaniel-vs-english-cocker-spaniel/) is the Cocker's bigger, leggier cousin. Cocker Spaniels are also one parent of the popular [Cockapoo](/breeds/cockapoo/).

### Coat and ears

The silky, feathered coat needs brushing a few times a week and regular trimming, especially around the feet and ears. Those long, hanging ears trap warmth and moisture, so check and clean them weekly to prevent ear infections.

### Health

Health issues include eye diseases such as progressive retinal atrophy (PRA), hip dysplasia and familial nephropathy, a kidney disease. DNA tests exist for several of these, so ask breeders for the parents' results. Some solid-coloured Cockers are prone to "rage syndrome" (sudden aggression), which is rare but well documented. English Cockers often live 12–14 years.
