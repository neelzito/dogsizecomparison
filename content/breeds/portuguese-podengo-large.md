The Portuguese Podengo Grande is the largest of the three sizes of Portuguese warren hound. It was used to hunt larger game such as deer and boar, often in packs. It's the rarest of the three sizes, even in Portugal. Like its smaller relatives, it hunts by sight, sound and scent, with large upright ears and a wedge-shaped head. The FCI treats the [Pequeno](/breeds/portuguese-podengo-pequeno/), [Médio](/breeds/portuguese-podengo-medium/) and Grande as one breed in three sizes.

### What a Podengo Grande's size means at home

The FCI standard calls for 55–70 cm at the shoulder and 20–30 kg. A Grande reaches roughly an adult's mid-thigh. It's lean, athletic and strong. Because of its height and big ears, the [crate size calculator](/calculators/crate-size/?breed=portuguese-podengo-large) suggests a 54″ (137 cm) crate for an average dog.

### Podengo Grande vs Saluki

The [Saluki](/compare/portuguese-podengo-large-vs-saluki/) is about the same size, with feathered ears and a finer build.

### Temperament and care

Podengos Grandes are alert, independent and loyal, often reserved with strangers. They have a strong prey drive and need a secure garden and plenty of exercise. The coat can be smooth or wire and is easy to care for. They're generally hardy and often live 12–15 years.

*Sources: FCI standard No. 94 (Podengo Português); Clube Português de Canicultura.*
