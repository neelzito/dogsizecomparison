The Portuguese Podengo Pequeno is the smallest of the Portuguese warren hounds, used for centuries in Portugal to hunt rabbits in rocky, overgrown terrain and to catch rats on farms and ships. Like the Spanish Podencos and the Ibizan Hound, it hunts by sight, sound and scent, with large upright ears and a wedge-shaped head. The FCI treats the Portuguese Podengo as one breed in three sizes: Pequeno (small), [Médio (medium)](/breeds/portuguese-podengo-medium/) and [Grande (large)](/breeds/portuguese-podengo-large/). The AKC recognises the Pequeno as a separate breed.

### What a Pequeno's size means at home

The AKC standard calls for 8–12 in (20.5–30.5 cm) at the shoulder and about 9–13 lb (4–6 kg). A Pequeno reaches around mid-shin on an adult. It's a little longer than tall, sturdy and agile. It suits any home and usually needs a 24″ (61 cm) crate. Check with the [crate size calculator](/calculators/crate-size/?breed=portuguese-podengo-pequeno).

### Flying with a Pequeno

A Pequeno is a good size for cabin travel, staying under the 8 kg (17.6 lb) limit many airlines set, even with a carrier. Check with the [airline cabin check](/calculators/airline-cabin/?breed=portuguese-podengo-pequeno).

### Pequeno vs Chihuahua, Miniature Dachshund and Lundehund

The Pequeno is [taller and heavier than a Chihuahua](/compare/portuguese-podengo-pequeno-vs-chihuahua/), and [taller than a Miniature Dachshund](/compare/dachshund-miniature-vs-portuguese-podengo-pequeno/) of similar weight. The [Norwegian Lundehund](/compare/norwegian-lundehund-vs-portuguese-podengo-pequeno/) is taller and heavier. It comes in two coats: **smooth** (short and dense) and **wire** (rough, with a beard).

### Temperament and exercise

Pequenos are lively, curious, playful and affectionate, devoted to their family and often watchful with strangers. They're true hunting dogs, with a strong prey drive and a love of digging and chasing, so they need a secure garden, and recall can be unreliable near small animals. They need daily walks and games, and they're clever and quick to learn with reward-based training. They can be vocal.

### Care and health

Both coats are easy to care for and need only weekly brushing. The breed is generally healthy and hardy. Known problems include loose kneecaps and some eye conditions. Pequenos often live 12–15 years.

*Sources: AKC breed standard (Portuguese Podengo Pequeno); FCI standard No. 94 (Podengo Português); Clube Português de Canicultura.*
