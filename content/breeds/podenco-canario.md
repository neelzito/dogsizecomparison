The Podenco Canario (Canarian Warren Hound) comes from the Canary Islands, where it has been used for centuries to hunt rabbits, usually in packs and often together with ferrets. Like the other Mediterranean warren hounds, the Ibizan Hound, the Pharaoh Hound and the Spanish Podencos, it hunts by sight, sound and scent, and it's built for speed and agility on rough volcanic terrain. It's recognised by the FCI and is popular on the islands, though rare elsewhere.

### What a Podenco Canario's size means at home

The FCI standard gives heights of 55–64 cm at the shoulder for males and 53–60 cm for females, but no weights. Typical weights are around 20–25 kg for males and 17–21 kg for females. A Podenco Canario reaches roughly an adult's mid-thigh. It's lean, long-legged and fine-boned, light for its height, so its weight puts it in the medium size class on this site. Because of its height and very large upright ears, the [crate size calculator](/calculators/crate-size/?breed=podenco-canario) suggests a 48″ (122 cm) crate for an average male.

### Podenco Canario vs Pharaoh, Ibizan and Valenciano

The [Pharaoh Hound](/compare/podenco-canario-vs-pharaoh-hound/) is very similar in size and shape, but always tan to red. The [Ibizan Hound](/compare/podenco-canario-vs-ibizan-hound/) is taller. The [Podenco Valenciano](/compare/podenco-canario-vs-podenco-valenciano/) is about the same height and a little lighter. The Canario's coat is red and white, in any proportion.

### Living with a Podenco Canario

Podencos Canarios are affectionate, gentle and often sensitive with their families, and they usually get on well with other dogs, having been bred to hunt in packs. Their hunting instinct is very strong, so recall can be unreliable wherever there are rabbits, cats or birds. A long line and a securely fenced area are essential, and fences need to be high, as Podencos can jump and climb. They need daily exercise and a chance to run, but are usually calm indoors.

### Adoption

As with other Spanish Podencos, many Canarios end up in shelters, often after the hunting season. Rescue organisations rehome them across Europe. Ask the rescue about each dog's size, temperament and behaviour with cats.

### Care and health

The short, smooth coat needs very little grooming. Podencos feel the cold, so a coat helps in winter. The breed is generally hardy. Like other sighthound-type dogs, they can be sensitive to some anaesthetics. They often live 12–14 years.

*Sources: FCI standard No. 329 (Podenco Canario); Real Sociedad Canina de España (RSCE).*
