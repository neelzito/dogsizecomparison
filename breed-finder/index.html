Breed finder: common questions

Which dog breeds are best for apartments?

Small, calm breeds usually cope best with apartment life: for example the Cavalier King Charles Spaniel, French Bulldog, Shih Tzu, Bichon Frise, Havanese. Some big but laid-back breeds, such as the Greyhound, also do well. Energy matters more than size: a bored terrier or herding dog is harder to live with in a flat than a sleepy giant.

Which dog breeds shed the least?

Breeds with a continuously growing coat, such as poodles, Bichon Frisé, Maltese, Havanese and the schnauzers, shed very little, as do hairless breeds like the Chinese Crested. No dog is truly hypoallergenic: low-shedding coats need regular clipping or brushing instead.

Which breeds are good for first-time owners?

Breeds known for being eager to please and easy-going, such as the Cavalier King Charles Spaniel, Labrador Retriever, Golden Retriever, Standard Poodle, Papillon. Strong-willed guardians, sighthounds and high-drive working breeds are better with experienced owners.

How are the ratings chosen?

Each breed is rated 1 to 3 for energy, shedding, grooming, how it usually is with children, how well it suits first-time owners and apartment life. The ratings summarise breed-club and kennel-club descriptions. They describe typical dogs of the breed, not any individual dog, so always meet the dog and talk to breeders or rescues.
