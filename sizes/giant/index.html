Giant dog breeds

27 the heaviest breeds, averaging 45 kg (99 lb) and over as adults. Sorted by height with male and female figures.

A selection from tallest to shortest, average males drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person.

Compare these in the tool →

On this site a breed is giant when its average adult weight, males and females together, is 45 kg (99 lb) and over. 27 of the 256 breeds fall in this class. Kennel clubs and vets use slightly different cut-offs, so a breed near the edge may be called large elsewhere; see dog size classes explained.

Typical life expectancy for giant breeds here averages 10.1 years.

The tallest is the Irish Wolfhound (males 82–90 cm (32.1–35.4 in)) and the shortest the Rottweiler (61–69 cm (24–27 in)). The heaviest is the Boerboel at up to 91 kg (200 lb); the lightest the Cane Corso at 45–50 kg (99–110 lb).
