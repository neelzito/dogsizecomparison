Toy dog breeds

22 lap-sized companions, averaging under 5 kg (11 lb) as adults. Sorted by height with male and female figures.

A selection from tallest to shortest, average males drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person.

Compare these in the tool →

On this site a breed is toy when its average adult weight, males and females together, is under 5 kg (11 lb). 22 of the 247 breeds fall in this class. Kennel clubs and vets use slightly different cut-offs, so a breed near the edge may be called small elsewhere; see dog size classes explained.

22 of them all fit, at least as lighter individuals, the 8 kg airline cabin limit for dog and carrier. More in flying with a dog in the cabin.

Typical life expectancy for toy breeds here averages 13.9 years.

The tallest is the Italian Greyhound (males 33–38 cm (13–15 in)) and the shortest the Dachshund (Miniature) (12.5–15 cm (4.9–5.9 in)). The heaviest is the Italian Greyhound at up to 6.4 kg (14 lb); the lightest the Chihuahua at 1.4–2.7 kg (3.1–6 lb).
