Large dog breeds

70 big working and sporting dogs, averaging 25–45 kg (55–99 lb) as adults. Sorted by height with male and female figures.

A selection from tallest to shortest, average males drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person.

Compare these in the tool →

On this site a breed is large when its average adult weight, males and females together, is 25–45 kg (55–99 lb). 70 of the 256 breeds fall in this class. Kennel clubs and vets use slightly different cut-offs, so a breed near the edge may be called the next size up or down elsewhere; see dog size classes explained.

Typical life expectancy for large breeds here averages 12.2 years.

The tallest is the Scottish Deerhound (males 76–82 cm (29.9–32.1 in)) and the shortest the American Staffordshire Terrier (46–49 cm (17.9–19.1 in)). The heaviest is the Otterhound at up to 52 kg (115 lb); the lightest the American Staffordshire Terrier at 25–32 kg (55–71 lb).
