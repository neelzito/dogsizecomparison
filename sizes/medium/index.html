Medium dog breeds

89 mid-sized all-rounders, averaging 10–25 kg (22–55 lb) as adults. Sorted by height with male and female figures.

A selection from tallest to shortest, average males drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person.

Compare these in the tool →

On this site a breed is medium when its average adult weight, males and females together, is 10–25 kg (22–55 lb). 89 of the 256 breeds fall in this class. Kennel clubs and vets use slightly different cut-offs, so a breed near the edge may be called the next size up or down elsewhere; see dog size classes explained.

Typical life expectancy for medium breeds here averages 13.3 years.

The tallest is the Sloughi (males 66–74 cm (26–28.9 in)) and the shortest the Dachshund (Standard) (21–23 cm (8.1–9.1 in)). The heaviest is the Standard Poodle at up to 32 kg (71 lb); the lightest the Maneto at 8.5–12 kg (19–25 lb).
