Small dog breeds

48 small but sturdy dogs, averaging 5–10 kg (11–22 lb) as adults. Sorted by height with male and female figures.

A selection from tallest to shortest, average males drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person.

Compare these in the tool →

On this site a breed is small when its average adult weight, males and females together, is 5–10 kg (11–22 lb). 48 of the 256 breeds fall in this class. Kennel clubs and vets use slightly different cut-offs, so a breed near the edge may be called the next size up or down elsewhere; see dog size classes explained.

35 of them fit, at least as lighter individuals, the 8 kg airline cabin limit for dog and carrier. More in flying with a dog in the cabin.

Typical life expectancy for small breeds here averages 13.7 years.

The tallest is the Bedlington Terrier (males 38–45 cm (15–17.5 in)) and the shortest the Norfolk Terrier (23–26 cm (9.1–10 in)). The heaviest is the Sealyham Terrier at up to 11 kg (24 lb); the lightest the English Toy Spaniel at 3.6–6.4 kg (7.9–14 lb).
