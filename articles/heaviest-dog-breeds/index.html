The 15 heaviest dog breeds

Ranked by average adult weight, males and females together. Mostly mastiffs and livestock guardians.

Updated 2026-11-19 · Data from kennel-club breed standards

The top 5, average males drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person.

Compare the top 10 in the tool →

Breed standards often give only a minimum weight for giant breeds, and individual males can be far heavier than the ranges below. Weights here follow kennel-club standards and breed-club figures; see about the data.

  1. 1

    Boerboel

    ♂ 61–69 cm (24–27 in) · 68–91 kg (150–200 lb)
    ♀ 56–64 cm (22–25 in) · 68–91 kg (150–200 lb) · 9–11 years

    Males weigh 68–91 kg (150–200 lb), females 68–91 kg (150–200 lb). At 61–69 cm (24–27 in) at the shoulder for males, it carries its weight on a relatively compact frame.

  2. 2

    Mastiff (English)

    ♂ 76–91 cm (29.9–35.8 in) · 73–105 kg (160–230 lb)
    ♀ 70–84 cm (27.6–33.1 in) · 55–77 kg (120–170 lb) · 6–10 years

    Males weigh 73–105 kg (160–230 lb), females 55–77 kg (120–170 lb). At 76–91 cm (29.9–35.8 in) at the shoulder for males, it is also one of the tallest breeds.

What weight means in practice

Dogs this size need an XL or XXL crate (see the crate size calculator), fly in the hold if at all, and have the shortest life expectancy: giant breeds here average 10.1 years.
