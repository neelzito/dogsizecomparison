The 15 smallest dog breeds

Ranked by average adult weight, the fairest single measure of how small a dog is. Every one of them fits in an airline cabin carrier.

Updated 2026-11-16 · Data from kennel-club breed standards

The top 5, average males drawn to scale.

Compare the top 10 in the tool →

Height and weight don't always agree: a long, low dog like the Miniature Dachshund is among the shortest breeds but outweighs many toy breeds. This list ranks by average weight of males and females. For height alone, see the toy breeds sorted by height.

  1. 1

    Chihuahua

    12.5–21 cm (4.9–8.1 in) · 1.4–2.7 kg (3.1–6 lb) · 14–16 years

    The smallest breed of all. The Chihuahua weighs 1.4–2.7 kg (3.1–6 lb) and stands 12.5–21 cm (4.9–8.1 in) at the shoulder, about ankle height on an adult. Part of the Toy group, from Mexico.

  2. 2

    Russian Toy

    21–28 cm (8.1–11 in) · 1.4–2.9 kg (3.1–6.4 lb) · 12–14 years

    The Russian Toy weighs 1.4–2.9 kg (3.1–6.4 lb) and stands 21–28 cm (8.1–11 in) at the shoulder, about mid-shin on an adult. Part of the Toy group, from Russia.

  3. 3

    Toy Poodle

    21–26 cm (8.1–10 in) · 1.8–2.7 kg (4–6 lb) · 10–18 years

    The Toy Poodle weighs 1.8–2.7 kg (4–6 lb) and stands 21–26 cm (8.1–10 in) at the shoulder, about mid-shin on an adult. Part of the Toy group, from France/Germany.

  4. 4

    Pomeranian

    15–18 cm (5.9–7.1 in) · 1.4–3.2 kg (3.1–7.1 lb) · 12–16 years

    The Pomeranian weighs 1.4–3.2 kg (3.1–7.1 lb) and stands 15–18 cm (5.9–7.1 in) at the shoulder, about ankle height on an adult. Part of the Toy group, from Germany/Poland.

  5. 5

  6. 6

    Yorkshire Terrier

  7. 7

    Biewer Terrier

    18–28 cm (7.1–11 in) · 1.8–3.6 kg (4–7.9 lb) · 16 years

  8. 8

    Toy Fox Terrier

    22–29 cm (8.5–11.4 in) · 1.8–4.1 kg (4–9 lb) · 13–15 years

    The Toy Fox Terrier weighs 1.8–4.1 kg (4–9 lb) and stands 22–29 cm (8.5–11.4 in) at the shoulder, about mid-shin on an adult. Part of the Toy group, from the United States.

    The Papillon weighs 2.3–4.5 kg (5.1–9.9 lb) and stands 21–28 cm (8.1–11 in) at the shoulder, about mid-shin on an adult. Part of the Toy group, from France/Belgium.

  10. 10

    Bolognese

    26–31 cm (10–12 in) · 2.7–4.5 kg (6–9.9 lb) · 12–14 years

    The Bolognese weighs 2.7–4.5 kg (6–9.9 lb) and stands 26–31 cm (10–12 in) at the shoulder, about mid-shin on an adult. Part of the Toy group, from Italy.

  11. 11

    Chiweenie

    ♂ 15–25 cm (5.9–9.8 in) · 2.3–5.4 kg (5.1–12 lb)
    ♀ 15–23 cm (5.9–9.1 in) · 2.3–5 kg (5.1–11 lb) · 12–16 years

  12. 12

    Affenpinscher

Small, but not fragile

Very small dogs need care around stairs, sofas and children, and they lose heat quickly. They also tend to live longest: the toy breeds here average 13.9 years. See the longest-living breeds.
