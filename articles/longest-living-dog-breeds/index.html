The 20 longest-living dog breeds

Ranked by the middle of each breed's typical life-expectancy range. Small breeds dominate.

Updated 2026-11-18 · Data from kennel-club breed standards

The top 5, average males drawn to scale.

Compare the top 10 in the tool →

Across the 256 breeds on this site, typical life expectancy falls as size rises: toy breeds average 13.9 years, small 13.7, medium 13.3, large 12.2 and giant 10.1. Big dogs age faster, a pattern researchers still try to explain. These figures are breed-club estimates, not guarantees.
