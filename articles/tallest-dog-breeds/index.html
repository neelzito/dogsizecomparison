The 15 tallest dog breeds

Ranked by average height at the withers (top of the shoulder), males and females together.

Updated 2026-11-19 · Data from kennel-club breed standards

The top 5, average males drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person.

Compare the top 10 in the tool →

Height is measured at the withers, not the head, so a tall dog standing up with its head raised looks far bigger than these figures. A male Irish Wolfhound at 90 cm (35.4 in) can rest its chin on a kitchen counter, and many of these breeds can reach one standing on their hind legs.

  1. 1

    Irish Wolfhound

    ♂ 82–90 cm (32.1–35.4 in) · 55–70 kg (120–154 lb)
    ♀ 76–86 cm (29.9–33.9 in) · 48–55 kg (105–121 lb) · 6–8 years

    Males stand 82–90 cm (32.1–35.4 in) at the shoulder, about hip height on a 175 cm adult, and weigh 55–70 kg (120–154 lb). A lean sighthound built for speed, so it is lighter than its height suggests.

Tall vs heavy

Sighthounds like the Irish Wolfhound and Scottish Deerhound are tall but lean; mastiffs are heavier at the same height. For weight, see the heaviest dog breeds.
