Large dog breeds that live the longest

Large and giant breeds (average weight 25 kg / 55 lb and up) ranked by typical life expectancy. The average for large breeds here is 12.2 years.

Updated 2026-11-18 · Data from kennel-club breed standards

The top 5, average males drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person.

Compare the top 10 in the tool →

Want a big dog that's likely to be around for a long time? These large and giant breeds have the longest typical life expectancy among the 97 on this site.
