German dog breeds

27 breeds from Germany, sorted by height.

A selection from tallest to shortest, average males drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person.

Compare these in the tool →

27 of the breeds on this site trace their origin to Germany: 5 toy, 2 small, 6 medium, 10 large and 4 giant. Breeds with a shared or disputed origin are listed under the first country named in their standard.

The tallest is the Great Dane (males 76–82 cm (29.9–32.1 in)) and the shortest the Dachshund (Miniature) (12.5–15 cm (4.9–5.9 in)). The heaviest is the Great Dane at up to 80 kg (175 lb); the lightest the Pomeranian at 1.4–3.2 kg (3.1–7.1 lb).
