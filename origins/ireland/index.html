Irish dog breeds

8 breeds from Ireland, sorted by height.

A selection from tallest to shortest, average males drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person.

Compare these in the tool →

8 of the breeds on this site trace their origin to Ireland: 5 medium, 2 large and 1 giant. Breeds with a shared or disputed origin are listed under the first country named in their standard.

The tallest is the Irish Wolfhound (males 82–90 cm (32.1–35.4 in)) and the shortest the Glen of Imaal Terrier (32–36 cm (12.6–14 in)). The heaviest is the Irish Wolfhound at up to 70 kg (154 lb); the lightest the Irish Terrier at 12 kg (26 lb).
