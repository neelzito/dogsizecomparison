Hungarian dog breeds

7 breeds from Hungary, sorted by height.

A selection from tallest to shortest, average males drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person.

Compare these in the tool →

7 of the breeds on this site trace their origin to Hungary: 4 medium, 2 large and 1 giant. Breeds with a shared or disputed origin are listed under the first country named in their standard.

The tallest is the Komondor (males 70–80 cm (27.6–31.5 in)) and the shortest the Puli (43 cm (16.9 in)). The heaviest is the Komondor at up to 60 kg (132 lb); the lightest the Mudi at 8.2–13 kg (18–29 lb).
