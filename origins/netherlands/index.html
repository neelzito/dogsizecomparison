Dutch dog breeds

5 breeds from the Netherlands, sorted by height.

A selection from tallest to shortest, average males drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person.

Compare these in the tool →

5 of the breeds on this site trace their origin to the Netherlands: 3 medium and 2 large. Breeds with a shared or disputed origin are listed under the first country named in their standard.

The tallest is the Saarloos Wolfdog (males 65–75 cm (25.6–29.5 in)) and the shortest the Nederlandse Kooikerhondje (41 cm (15.9 in)). The heaviest is the Saarloos Wolfdog at up to 41 kg (90 lb); the lightest the Nederlandse Kooikerhondje at 9.1–14 kg (20–30 lb).
