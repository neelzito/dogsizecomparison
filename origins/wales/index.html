Welsh dog breeds

5 breeds from Wales, sorted by height.

A selection from tallest to shortest, average males drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person.

Compare these in the tool →

5 of the breeds on this site trace their origin to Wales: 2 small and 3 medium. Breeds with a shared or disputed origin are listed under the first country named in their standard.

The tallest is the Welsh Springer Spaniel (males 46–49 cm (17.9–19.1 in)) and the shortest the Sealyham Terrier (27 cm (10.4 in)). The heaviest is the Welsh Springer Spaniel at up to 25 kg (55 lb); the lightest the Welsh Terrier at 9.1 kg (20 lb).
