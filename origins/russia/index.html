Russian dog breeds

6 breeds from Russia, sorted by height.

A selection from tallest to shortest, average males drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person.

Compare these in the tool →

6 of the breeds on this site trace their origin to Russia: 2 toy, 2 medium, 1 large and 1 giant. Breeds with a shared or disputed origin are listed under the first country named in their standard.

The tallest is the Borzoi (males 71–85 cm (28–33.5 in)) and the shortest the Russian Toy (21–28 cm (8.1–11 in)). The heaviest is the Black Russian Terrier at up to 59 kg (130 lb); the lightest the Russian Toy at 1.4–2.9 kg (3.1–6.4 lb).
