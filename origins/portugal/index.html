Portuguese dog breeds

5 breeds from Portugal, sorted by height.

A selection from tallest to shortest, average males drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person.

Compare these in the tool →

5 of the breeds on this site trace their origin to Portugal: 1 small, 2 medium, 1 large and 1 giant. Breeds with a shared or disputed origin are listed under the first country named in their standard.

The tallest is the Estrela Mountain Dog (males 62–74 cm (24.4–28.9 in)) and the shortest the Portuguese Podengo Pequeno (21–31 cm (8.1–12 in)). The heaviest is the Estrela Mountain Dog at up to 60 kg (132 lb); the lightest the Portuguese Podengo Pequeno at 4.1–5.9 kg (9–13 lb).
