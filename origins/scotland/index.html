Scottish dog breeds

12 breeds from Scotland, sorted by height.

A selection from tallest to shortest, average males drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person.

Compare these in the tool →

12 of the breeds on this site trace their origin to Scotland: 5 small, 2 medium and 5 large. Breeds with a shared or disputed origin are listed under the first country named in their standard.

The tallest is the Scottish Deerhound (males 76–82 cm (29.9–32.1 in)) and the shortest the Dandie Dinmont Terrier (21–28 cm (8.1–11 in)). The heaviest is the Scottish Deerhound at up to 50 kg (110 lb); the lightest the Cairn Terrier at 6.4 kg (14 lb).
