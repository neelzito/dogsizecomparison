Swiss dog breeds

6 breeds from Switzerland, sorted by height.

A selection from tallest to shortest, average males drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person.

Compare these in the tool →

6 of the breeds on this site trace their origin to Switzerland: 1 medium, 3 large and 2 giant. Breeds with a shared or disputed origin are listed under the first country named in their standard.

The tallest is the Saint Bernard (males 71–76 cm (28–29.9 in)) and the shortest the Entlebucher Mountain Dog (45–52 cm (17.5–20.5 in)). The heaviest is the Saint Bernard at up to 82 kg (180 lb); the lightest the Entlebucher Mountain Dog at 23–30 kg (50–65 lb).
