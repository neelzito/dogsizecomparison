Chinese dog breeds

10 breeds from China, sorted by height.

A selection from tallest to shortest, average males drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person.

Compare these in the tool →

10 of the breeds on this site trace their origin to China: 2 toy, 4 small, 2 medium, 1 large and 1 giant. Breeds with a shared or disputed origin are listed under the first country named in their standard.

The tallest is the Tibetan Mastiff (males 66–76 cm (26–29.9 in)) and the shortest the Pekingese (15–23 cm (5.9–9.1 in)). The heaviest is the Tibetan Mastiff at up to 68 kg (150 lb); the lightest the Chinese Crested at 3.6–5.4 kg (7.9–12 lb).
