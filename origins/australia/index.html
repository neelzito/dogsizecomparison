Australian dog breeds

6 breeds from Australia, sorted by height.

A selection from tallest to shortest, average males drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person.

Compare these in the tool →

6 of the breeds on this site trace their origin to Australia: 1 toy, 2 small and 3 medium. Breeds with a shared or disputed origin are listed under the first country named in their standard.

The tallest is the Labradoodle (Standard) (males 53–61 cm (20.9–24 in)) and the shortest the Silky Terrier (23–26 cm (9.1–10 in)). The heaviest is the Labradoodle (Standard) at up to 30 kg (66 lb); the lightest the Silky Terrier at 4.1–5 kg (9–11 lb).
