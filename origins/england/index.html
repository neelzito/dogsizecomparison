English dog breeds

40 breeds from England, sorted by height.

A selection from tallest to shortest, average males drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person.

Compare these in the tool →

40 of the breeds on this site trace their origin to England: 1 toy, 15 small, 11 medium, 11 large and 2 giant. Breeds with a shared or disputed origin are listed under the first country named in their standard.

The tallest is the Mastiff (English) (males 76–91 cm (29.9–35.8 in)) and the shortest the Yorkshire Terrier (18–21 cm (7.1–8.1 in)). The heaviest is the Mastiff (English) at up to 105 kg (230 lb); the lightest the Yorkshire Terrier at 1.8–3.2 kg (4–7.1 lb).
