French dog breeds

17 breeds from France, sorted by height.

A selection from tallest to shortest, average males drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person.

Compare these in the tool →

17 of the breeds on this site trace their origin to France: 2 toy, 2 small, 8 medium, 3 large and 2 giant. Breeds with a shared or disputed origin are listed under the first country named in their standard.

The tallest is the Great Pyrenees (males 69–82 cm (27–32.1 in)) and the shortest the Toy Poodle (21–26 cm (8.1–10 in)). The heaviest is the Dogue de Bordeaux at up to 65 kg (143 lb); the lightest the Toy Poodle at 1.8–2.7 kg (4–6 lb).
