Spanish dog breeds

4 breeds from Spain, sorted by height.

A selection from tallest to shortest, average males drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person.

Compare these in the tool →

4 of the breeds on this site trace their origin to Spain: 2 medium and 2 giant. Breeds with a shared or disputed origin are listed under the first country named in their standard.

The tallest is the Spanish Mastiff (males 77–88 cm (30.3–34.6 in)) and the shortest the Spanish Water Dog (45–50 cm (17.5–19.7 in)). The heaviest is the Spanish Mastiff at up to 91 kg (200 lb); the lightest the Spanish Water Dog at 18–22 kg (40–49 lb).
