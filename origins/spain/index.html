Spanish dog breeds

11 breeds from Spain, sorted by height.

A selection from tallest to shortest, average males drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person.

Compare these in the tool →

11 of the breeds on this site trace their origin to Spain: 1 small, 7 medium, 1 large and 2 giant. Breeds with a shared or disputed origin are listed under the first country named in their standard.

The tallest is the Spanish Mastiff (males 77–88 cm (30.3–34.6 in)) and the shortest the Maneto (30–35 cm (11.8–13.8 in)). The heaviest is the Spanish Mastiff at up to 91 kg (200 lb); the lightest the Podenco Andaluz (Small) at 5–11 kg (11–24 lb).
