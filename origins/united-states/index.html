American dog breeds

37 breeds from the United States, sorted by height.

A selection from tallest to shortest, average males drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person.

Compare these in the tool →

37 of the breeds on this site trace their origin to the United States: 3 toy, 9 small, 11 medium, 13 large and 1 giant. Breeds with a shared or disputed origin are listed under the first country named in their standard.

The tallest is the Akita (American) (males 66–71 cm (26–28 in)) and the shortest the Chiweenie (15–25 cm (5.9–9.8 in)). The heaviest is the Akita (American) at up to 59 kg (130 lb); the lightest the Toy Fox Terrier at 1.8–4.1 kg (4–9 lb).
