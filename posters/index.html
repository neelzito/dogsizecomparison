Sharing and printing

You're welcome to print these posters for your home, school or club, and to share them in forums, groups and on social media. Please keep the credit line on the poster, and link to dogsizecomparison.com where you can. For commercial use, please ask us first.

The heights are the breed-standard heights at the withers (the top of the shoulder). Individual dogs can be bigger or smaller. See about the data for where the figures come from.
