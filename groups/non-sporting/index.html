Non-Sporting group dog breeds by size

23 breeds, from the Standard Poodle down to the Lhasa Apso.

A selection from tallest to shortest, average males drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person.

Compare these in the tool →

A mixed group of breeds that no longer fit their original job, from the Dalmatian and Poodle to the Bulldog and Chow Chow. Sizes vary more here than in any other group.

By size the group has 11 small, 9 medium and 3 large breeds.

The tallest is the Standard Poodle (males 53–62 cm (20.9–24.4 in)) and the shortest the Lhasa Apso (26–28 cm (10–11 in)). The heaviest is the Chow Chow at up to 32 kg (71 lb); the lightest the Volpino Italiano at 4.5–7.3 kg (9.9–16 lb).
