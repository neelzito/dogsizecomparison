Toy group dog breeds by size

24 breeds, from the Italian Greyhound down to the Chihuahua.

A selection from tallest to shortest, average males drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person.

Compare these in the tool →

Bred as companions, toy breeds are the smallest dogs: most weigh under 5 kg (11 lb). Many fit in an airline cabin carrier.

By size the group has 19 toy and 5 small breeds.

The tallest is the Italian Greyhound (males 33–38 cm (13–15 in)) and the shortest the Pomeranian (15–18 cm (5.9–7.1 in)). The heaviest is the Pug at up to 8.2 kg (18 lb); the lightest the Chihuahua at 1.4–2.7 kg (3.1–6 lb).
