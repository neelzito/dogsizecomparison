Hound group dog breeds by size

45 breeds, from the Irish Wolfhound down to the Dachshund (Miniature).

A selection from tallest to shortest, average males drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person.

Compare these in the tool →

Bred to hunt by scent or by sight. Scent hounds like the Beagle and Bloodhound follow a trail with their nose; sighthounds like the Greyhound and Irish Wolfhound chase by sight and are built for speed.

By size the group has 1 toy, 3 small, 25 medium, 15 large and 1 giant breeds.

The tallest is the Irish Wolfhound (males 82–90 cm (32.1–35.4 in)) and the shortest the Dachshund (Miniature) (12.5–15 cm (4.9–5.9 in)). The heaviest is the Irish Wolfhound at up to 70 kg (154 lb); the lightest the Dachshund (Miniature) at 3.5–5 kg (7.7–11 lb).
