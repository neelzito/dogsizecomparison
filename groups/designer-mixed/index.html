Designer & mixed breeds by size

13 breeds, from the Bernedoodle (Standard) down to the Chiweenie.

A selection from tallest to shortest, average males drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person.

Compare these in the tool →

Popular crosses, mostly between the Poodle and another breed. They have no breed standard, so sizes here are typical ranges, and the size depends heavily on which Poodle (toy, miniature or standard) was used.

By size the group has 2 toy, 5 small, 3 medium and 3 large breeds.

The tallest is the Bernedoodle (Standard) (males 58–74 cm (22.8–29.1 in)) and the shortest the Chiweenie (15–25 cm (5.9–9.8 in)). The heaviest is the Bernedoodle (Standard) at up to 41 kg (90 lb); the lightest the Chiweenie at 2.3–5.4 kg (5.1–12 lb).
