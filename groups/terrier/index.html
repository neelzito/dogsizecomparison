Terrier group dog breeds by size

33 breeds, from the Airedale Terrier down to the Dandie Dinmont Terrier.

A selection from tallest to shortest, average males drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person.

Compare these in the tool →

Bred to hunt vermin and dig after foxes and badgers. Most terriers are small and short-legged, but the group also includes the Airedale and the Bull Terrier.

By size the group has 21 small, 9 medium and 3 large breeds.

The tallest is the Airedale Terrier (males 59 cm (23 in)) and the shortest the Norfolk Terrier (23–26 cm (9.1–10 in)). The heaviest is the Airedale Terrier at up to 32 kg (71 lb); the lightest the Norfolk Terrier at 5–5.4 kg (11–12 lb).
