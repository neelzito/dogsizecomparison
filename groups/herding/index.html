Herding group dog breeds by size

40 breeds, from the Saarloos Wolfdog down to the Lancashire Heeler.

A selection from tallest to shortest, average males drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person.

Compare these in the tool →

Bred to gather, drive and guard livestock, herding dogs are agile, quick to learn and often very energetic. The group runs from compact corgis and heelers to big shepherds.

By size the group has 2 small, 20 medium and 18 large breeds.

The tallest is the Saarloos Wolfdog (males 65–75 cm (25.6–29.5 in)) and the shortest the Pembroke Welsh Corgi (26–31 cm (10–12 in)). The heaviest is the Beauceron at up to 50 kg (110 lb); the lightest the Lancashire Heeler at 4.1–7.7 kg (9–17 lb).
