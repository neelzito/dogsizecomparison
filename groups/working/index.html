Working group dog breeds by size

46 breeds, from the Mastiff (English) down to the Danish-Swedish Farmdog.

A selection from tallest to shortest, average males drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person.

Compare these in the tool →

Bred to guard property, pull sleds and carts, and rescue people. The group includes most of the biggest and heaviest breeds, from the Siberian Husky to the Mastiff.

By size the group has 2 small, 8 medium, 10 large and 26 giant breeds.

The tallest is the Mastiff (English) (males 76–91 cm (29.9–35.8 in)) and the shortest the Danish-Swedish Farmdog (32–37 cm (12.6–14.6 in)). The heaviest is the Boerboel at up to 91 kg (200 lb); the lightest the Alaskan Klee Kai at 2.7–12 kg (6–25 lb).
