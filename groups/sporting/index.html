Sporting group dog breeds by size

35 breeds, from the Irish Setter down to the Sussex Spaniel.

A selection from tallest to shortest, average males drawn to scale next to a 175 cm (5′9″) person.

Compare these in the tool →

Bred to find and retrieve game with hunters: retrievers, spaniels, pointers and setters. Most are medium to large, athletic and friendly.

By size the group has 17 medium and 18 large breeds.

The tallest is the Irish Setter (males 69 cm (27 in)) and the shortest the Sussex Spaniel (33–38 cm (13–15 in)). The heaviest is the Curly-Coated Retriever at up to 43 kg (95 lb); the lightest the Nederlandse Kooikerhondje at 9.1–14 kg (20–30 lb).
